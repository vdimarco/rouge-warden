# Afterlight living worlds verification

## Reproduction

Serve `public/` at `http://127.0.0.1:8765`, then run:

```sh
node qa/afterlight/living-worlds.e2e.mjs
AFTERLIGHT_LIVING_DIAGNOSTICS_ONLY=1 node qa/afterlight/living-worlds.e2e.mjs
```

The test uses the repository Playwright package and system Chromium. `AFTERLIGHT_URL`, `AFTERLIGHT_CHROMIUM` and `AFTERLIGHT_LIVING_SCREENSHOTS` override the defaults.

## Method

The campaign bot walks with the real engine `update` API and normalized directional input. It repeatedly queries moving landmarks rather than teleporting to original coordinates. All restoration and operation decisions use actual rendered buttons or documented keyboard shortcuts. It never changes player coordinates, resources, completion flags, operation answers or unlocks directly.

Six operation types are solved from their displayed cues: lantern bearings, circuit rotations, spatial crew escorting around the reef, matched sonar channels with a deliberately steadied echo, wind-corrected compass bearings, and ordered garden needs. Final restoration landmarks also require their own operation. The first operation in each region is paused and cancelled before retrying; inventory must remain unchanged. An interrupted forest operation is saved and reloaded, preserving supplies while safely discarding the pending operation.

Shortcut checks include Space advancing exactly one forest lock, the correct numeric key advancing exactly one lunar garden stage, and E attaching an escort once. The escort reaches harbor through legal movement and is released through its visible control.

The runtime check requires the actual local Phaser 3.90.0 game, an active `journey` scene, and the Phaser canvas. The layout runs perform a real forest operation at 1440×900, 390×844 and 844×390, check overflow, pagehide pause and game-switcher pause. Diagnostics separately exercise pointer cancellation, weather evolution, physical terrain friction, visible environmental cues, frame stability and a frozen paused composition with reduced motion.

## Evidence

Screenshots and machine-readable reports are generated in `/tmp/afterlight-living-worlds/`. Physical devices and non-Chromium browsers are not covered by these checks.

## Results — 2026-10-09

The full browser campaign passed all six restored worlds through 23 actual operations, including three spatial coastal escorts. Each region’s first operation passed pause/cancel/retry without spending inventory. The interrupted-operation save/reload preserved supplies and safely returned to a deliberate start. A completed save reloaded as complete. All three viewport operation runs passed; the latest UI/renderer received a separate focused rerun with malformed/blocked storage, pagehide and game-switcher pause.

Focused diagnostics passed real Phaser pointer steering, normal-motion camera focus and return, motion-setting runtime replacement without duplicate canvases, touch cancellation, and a frozen reduced-motion paused image. The weather clock advanced and the visible conditions cue matched the physical terrain: woodland mud friction was 0.76 against 1.0 on open ground. An earned terminal-save fixture generated by the legal engine campaign verified restored-world exploration and a fresh journey reset through actual ending/journal controls.

Isolated Chromium browser frame callback intervals were measured over short samples; these are browser responsiveness measurements, not physical-device benchmarks or a claim that every source texture updates at that rate.

Reduced-motion samples at all three viewport sizes averaged 16.67 ms with p95 16.7–16.8 ms. City remains the heaviest animated source in this environment. No uncaught browser errors or local HTTP asset failures occurred in the successful runs.
