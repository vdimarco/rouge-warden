# Hold the handbrake from the phone

## Why
The car locks its wheels on Space, and the phone never sends Space while driving. The brake button is reverse, which the wheel already does in three steps.

## What changes
- While driving, a Lock button holds Space and lets go when the thumb lifts.
- Auto gas stays on during that hold. Brake still drops auto gas, and the brake button still sends S.
- On foot, Lock hides and Jump still sends Space.
- Leaving the car lifts Space.

## Out of scope
A new engine sound, a change to the steering or pedal steps, stream latency, and a ninth story mission.
