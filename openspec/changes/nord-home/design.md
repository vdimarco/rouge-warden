# Stand, then berth, then bank

No new rendering technique is involved, so none of the usual art references fit. The beat uses places that already exist: the bike stand, the pedalo berth, and the dry south bank. No new mesh is spawned.

The stand recover is (34800, -5200), 12 m south of the stand and on the dry east side. The berth recover is 6 m south of the berth, because the bridge deck itself sits inside the 450 cm door. That south point is dry. The bank recover continues away from the float, 12 m past the bank, still dry.

Only the bike at the stand opens the berth. Only the pedalo at the berth opens the walk. Only a dry walker at the bank pays 420. The purse is written by the campaign's own cash grant with chain link -1, so the seven banks do not move. Nothing is saved, and the campaign phase list is not extended.

Sharing the stand with the bike loop, and the berth with boarding, is intentional. Those errands can still move. This beat opens only after mission 9 is banked.
