# Put the day away

## Why
Mission 9 sends the bike east, the pedalo to the buoy, and a swimmer to the float. Nothing asks for those three to be put back.

## What changes
- Mission 10 opens once the tour is banked. It is session-only. The v1 save still stores completed missions 0–2.
- Ride the park bike back to the stand. Paddle the pedalo back to the berth. Walk onto the south bank.
- A later place does not skip the one before it. The wrong way of moving does not advance the step. The purse is 420 session cash, paid once when the walk banks.
- The seven story banks stay seven. This beat does not take a chain link.
- Recover puts the player back on the current leg, outside that leg's reach. A race replay does not rewind the beat.

## Out of scope
A new mesh, an eighth chain link, a save field, a phone button, and a change to the bike, pedalo, or swim speeds.
