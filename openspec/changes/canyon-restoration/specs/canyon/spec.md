## ADDED Requirements
### Requirement: Connected bridge
Midgley SHALL connect to the actual highway on both banks, without duplicate ramps or asphalt on the canyon floor beneath its span.
#### Scenario: Crossing
- **WHEN** the player crosses Midgley
- **THEN** the deck aligns with the highway and its supports stay beneath the roadway
### Requirement: Gabe meeting-site hike
The canyon SHALL have a graded trail from the turnout to a clearing associated with first meeting Gabe and an optional hiking mission.
#### Scenario: Return hike
- **WHEN** the player starts the canyon mission after side activities unlock
- **THEN** objectives lead down the trail, identify the meeting place, and lead back to the turnout
