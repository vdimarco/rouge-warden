# Someone who follows, then stops

No new rendering technique is involved, so none of the usual art references fit. Grey Coat is the existing text caption, and the east door is an existing cube. No new mesh or shader is introduced.

The meet is (29600, 16800) and the door is (29600, 11200), both east of the ponds. The door is 56 m due south of the meet. Recover stands 9 m south of Grey Coat, outside the 450 cm door and inside the 14 m follow. Grey Coat walks at 560 cm/s, between a walk and a sprint, and never closes inside 180 cm. Past 22 m, or on a vehicle or in the water, they plant. Coming back inside 14 m on foot unplants them. A frame longer than a tenth of a second only moves them for that tenth.

Only a dry walker at the meet opens the walk. Only a dry walker, with Grey Coat unplanted and both inside the door, pays 480. The purse is the campaign's own cash grant with chain link -1, so the seven banks do not move.

The slot writer moves from version 3 to version 4. Version 3 still accepts the eight earlier steps and forces the escort to 0. Version 4 accepts an escort step only after the home beat is banked, and only when completed missions are 2. The friend's coordinates are session state, so a load snaps them to the door when the step is banked and to the meet otherwise.

The meet and the door stay off the road centres, the ring, the scooter, the bike stand, the job markers, the stall, and the atelier, so walking up to Grey Coat does not board a vehicle.
