# Walk with Grey Coat

## Why
The day after the race is a sequence of places. The tour and the home beat both send the player through the bike, the pedalo, and a walk, in order. Nothing in the park follows the player, and nothing stops when the player rides off.

## What changes
- Mission 11 opens once the home beat is banked. Grey Coat waits on the east lawn. A walker who reaches them starts the walk.
- Grey Coat follows on foot and keeps a gap. They stop if the player is in a vehicle, is swimming, or gets too far. They walk again when the player comes back on foot.
- Both people on foot at the east door pay 480 session cash once. The seven story banks stay seven.
- Version 4 of the campaign slot stores the escort step with the earlier story steps. Version 3 still loads and starts this walk at the meet. Version 1 still loads and starts the pond over. Version 2 stays unread and is not overwritten.
- Grey Coat's place on the lawn is not saved. A reload during the walk puts them back at the meet while the step stays where it was.
- Recover stands south of Grey Coat, outside the door. A race replay does not rewind the walk and does not write the slot.

## Out of scope
A new character mesh, an eighth chain link, a phone button, and another circuit of the bike, the pedalo, and the bank.
