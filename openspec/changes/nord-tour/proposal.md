# Three ways across the park

## Why
The bike, the pedalo, and the swim each have their own errand. After the blank page, nothing asks the player to use all three in one story.

## What changes
- Mission 9 opens once the blank page is banked. It is session-only. The v1 save still stores completed missions 0–2.
- Ride the park bike to a dry mark east of the stand. Paddle the pedalo to the north buoy. Swim to the south-pond float.
- A later place does not skip the one before it. The wrong vehicle does not advance the step. The purse is 360 session cash, paid once when the swim banks.
- The seven story banks stay seven. This tour does not take a chain link.
- Recover puts the player back on the current leg. A race replay does not rewind the tour.

## Out of scope
A new mesh, an eighth chain link, a save field, a phone button, and a change to the bike, pedalo, or swim speeds.
