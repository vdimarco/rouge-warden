# Bike, then pedalo, then swim

No new rendering technique is involved, so none of the usual art references fit. The tour uses places that already exist: the north-pond buoy, the south-pond float, and a dry point east of the bike stand. No new mesh is spawned.

The east mark is (40000, -4000), 52 m east of the stand at (34800, -4000), on dry ground. Recover for that leg is 12 m south of the mark, outside the 450 cm reach. The boat step uses the buoy the pedalo already laps, and recover returns to the berth. The swim step uses the south-pond float, and recover returns to the dry bank.

Only the bike at the east mark opens the boat. Only the pedalo at the buoy opens the swim. Only a swimmer at the float banks the tour and pays 360. Standing in a later place does not skip. A second pass does not pay. The purse is written by the campaign's own cash grant with chain link -1, so the seven banks do not move. Nothing is saved, and the campaign phase list is not extended.

Sharing the buoy and the float with the earlier errands is intentional. Those errands can still move if they are open. The tour itself opens only after missions 3 through 8 are banked.
