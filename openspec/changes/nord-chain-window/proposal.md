# A held chain lengthens the miss windows

## Why
Difficulty is a 6 the rules can prove. A miss already breaks the chain, but the court ink and the convoy beam stay the windows the first visit taught.

## What changes
- While the chain is held, the court ink and the convoy beam last longer.
- Chain 0 keeps the taught windows.
- A full chain still leaves a clear gap, and the ink and the beam do not cover the warning.
- The card names the clear seconds on the court return and on the convoy gap.
- The ink and the beam marks use that longer window.
