# Proposal: a footfall while walking

The player on foot is silent. The car already has an engine, a tire squeal, a skid chirp, and an impact thud. Rain, the court, the pond, the show, rank, and the story bed already have their own tones. This round adds one original tap that plants with the walk.

A walk of eight seconds plants 18 steps. A sprint of eight seconds plants 32. Standing still, falling, swimming, and sitting in a car stay quiet. The opening camera and the night hour stay.
