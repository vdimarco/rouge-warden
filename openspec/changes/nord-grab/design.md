# Twelve seconds, on foot

No new rendering technique is involved, so none of the usual art references fit. The case and the drop are existing flat cubes and captions. No new mesh or shader is introduced. The side-job clocks and the lap clock are different timers, and this beat does not change them.

The case is (62000, 14000) and the drop is (62000, 7000), 70 m due south. Both are east of the ponds and more than 14 m from a road centre. Recover is 9 m north of the case, outside the 450 cm door and outside the drop. The straight line is 70 m. At 800 cm/s a sprint takes under twelve seconds. At 450 cm/s a walk takes more than twelve. One frame spends at most a quarter of a second, so a hitch does not eat the window.

Only a dry walker at the case opens the run. Only a dry walker at the drop, with time left, pays 560. The purse is the campaign's own cash grant with chain link -1, so the seven banks do not move. If anyone on foot is inside the case or the drop, that person is the one the beat watches, even when a partner is closer to the other mark.

The slot writer moves from version 5 to version 6. Version 5 still accepts the crossing and forces the grab to 0. Version 6 accepts a grab step only after the crossing is banked, and only when completed missions are 2. The elapsed seconds are session state, so a load zeroes them and leaves the step.
