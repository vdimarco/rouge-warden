# Take the case before the window shuts

## Why
The lamp crossing fails a walk that is seen. Nothing in the day fails a walk that is too slow. The case and the drop are a straight sprint apart, and a walk does not arrive in time.

## What changes
- Mission 13 opens once the crossing is banked. A walker who takes the case starts a twelve-second window. The drop pays 560 session cash once, on foot, before the window shuts.
- A walk of the straight line does not make it. A sprint does. A frame longer than a quarter of a second only spends that quarter. A rider cannot take the case or make the drop. When the window shuts, the case is back at the start.
- The seven story banks stay seven.
- Version 6 of the campaign slot stores the grab step. Version 5 still loads and starts this beat over. Version 1 still loads and starts the pond over. Version 2 stays unread and is not overwritten. The clock itself is not saved. A reload during the run gives the full window again.
- Recover stands north of the case, outside it and outside the drop. It does not add time. A race replay does not rewind the grab and does not write the slot.

## Out of scope
A carried mesh, an eighth chain link, a phone button, another follower, another lit path, and another circuit of the bike, the pedalo, and the bank.
