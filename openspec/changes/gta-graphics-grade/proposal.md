# Proposal: a filmic grade

## What

The view uses a full filmic tone curve, a warm gain, and a small warm offset. The offline dusk picture runs the same fitted ACES curve. The shafts and the haze stay.

## Why

The dusk frame clips. Highlights flatten at the sun and the foreground sits in a hard black. A filmic shoulder and a warm lift are the grade the picture is missing.

## Scope

The grade constants, the view post process, and the scorecard. The opening aim stays. The opening weather stays clear. Unreal is not installed, so the editor does not rebuild the post process.
