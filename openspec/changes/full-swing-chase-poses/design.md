# A wider stride from behind

## Source

John Lasseter, "Principles of Traditional Animation Applied to 3D Computer Animation" (SIGGRAPH 1987), the exaggeration principle. The same note is already in the swing pose from round 18. A stride along the facing foreshortens behind the chase, so the lifted knee and the swing trail have to leave the silhouette. No captured clip was rebuilt. The walk and the jog stay on the captured loop.

## Decisions

- The extra knee step still waits for the run clip's weight, above about 2.9 m/s, including the fallback figure when the clips never load. A jog does not move the shin.
- The rope arm is still aimed after the swing pose, so the hand stays on the line to the anchor.
- Pose names, the twelve poses a second, the chase arm and the pitch are unchanged. No bone is scaled.
