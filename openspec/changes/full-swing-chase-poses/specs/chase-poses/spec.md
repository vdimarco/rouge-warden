## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The chase shows a run and a swing
At a full run the lifted foot SHALL step out of the silhouette, and the knee SHALL rise. At the bottom of a swing the feet SHALL sit wider than a quiet hang, with one heel lifted. A jog SHALL keep the knee at rest. The rope hand SHALL stay on the line to the anchor.

#### Scenario: A full run from the chase
- **WHEN** the hero runs at full speed and the chase sits behind
- **THEN** the lifted foot is higher and further to the side than the planted foot

#### Scenario: The bottom of a swing
- **WHEN** a rope is attached and the hero is moving fast across the bottom
- **THEN** the feet are apart on screen and the pose is swing

#### Scenario: A jog
- **WHEN** the hero is jogging and the run clip is not the one in front
- **THEN** the knee stays at its rest position
