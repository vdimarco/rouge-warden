# Open the chase stride and the swing

From behind the chase, a full run still spends the passing frame with the feet nearly on top of each other, and the bottom of a swing keeps the legs inside a narrow trail. The pose names, the captured walk and jog, and the rope hand stay as they are.

## What changes

At a full run the lifted foot and its knee step further out to the side, and the knee rises higher. At the bottom of a swing the trail steps wider and one heel lifts higher. A jog still keeps the shin at rest. A quiet hang stays the mild trail. The rope hand still reaches the anchor after the pose.

## Capabilities

- `chase-poses`: a full run and the bottom of a swing read as those poses from the chase, and a jog does not step the knee out.
