# A short trail behind a fast rope

## Source

None of the wind-field or smoke papers fit. The city already draws one comic particle pool, and a puff is one of its shapes. The trail reuses that puff. Screen speed lines in `comfort.js` stay as they are: the comfortable preset leaves them off, and they ramp in only from 14 m/s.

## Decisions

- The trail runs only with a rope attached, off the ground, off a wall, and faster than 10 m/s. A stand, a run on the street, and a quiet hang spawn nothing.
- One puff about every 0.08 s, born just behind the body and a little to one side, drifting back along the motion. Cream, sometimes gold, with the puff's own ink edge. Gravity off, a little drag, life under a second, so the trail stays short.
- The same pool of 768. No new draw. The King's Z's, the fireworks, and the sludge puffs keep their types.
