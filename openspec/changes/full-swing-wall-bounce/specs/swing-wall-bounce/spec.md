## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Shaded lower walls take a warm bounce
In a chase view that looks away from the sun, shaded wall on the lower floors SHALL read warmer than the same wall up by the roof. Lit wall and window glass SHALL keep their colours. The wall SHALL stay in three cel bands.

#### Scenario: Flat play looks up the avenue
- **WHEN** flat play frames the hero on the avenue, looking away from the sun
- **THEN** the shaded wall beside the street is warmer than that building's upper floors, and the glass is not recolored with the wall
