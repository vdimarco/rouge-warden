# Shaded walls take the street's warm light

A chase view looks away from the sun. The lower floors in that view stay the cold blue shade, with no light from the street under them.

## What changes

Shaded wall below the street air picks up a warm tone. Lit wall, glass, and the floors up by the roofs stay as they are. The cel bands stay three bands.

## Capabilities

- `swing-wall-bounce`: shaded lower walls read warm where the sun does not reach them.
