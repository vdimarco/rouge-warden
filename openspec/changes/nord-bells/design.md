# Three bells, north to south

No new rendering technique. The marks are the same flat slabs the other beats already use. None of the usual art references fit this change.

The bells do not move, do not seek, and do not keep a clock. That is the difference from the cart, the follow, the chase, the finale, and the window. Pace does not change the ring. Order does.

Coordinates, surveyed clear of roads and building shells: X=24800, north Y=22000, middle Y=19000, south Y=16000. Reach 450. Recover is 700 north of the bell this step is waiting for, outside that circle. The column is 4800cm east of the follow and clear of the cart.

A walker already inside the next bell rings it, even if someone else is in another bell. If nobody is in the next bell and a walker is in a different one, the order returns to the north bell and does not write the slot. A second tick in that same wrong bell does not play the warning again. Leaving the bell clears that warning. A car does not ring. The purse is 340 once, only when the south bell follows the other two. Link stays -1.
