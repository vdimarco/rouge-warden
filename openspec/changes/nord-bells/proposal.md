# Ring three bells in order

## Why

The cart moves while you walk and tips if you sprint. Nothing on the lawn cares which mark you touch first.

## What changes

After the cart is banked, three bells sit east of the follow line. Ring the north one, then the middle, then the south. An early bell starts the order over and stays on this beat. A sprint still rings. A car does not. The last bell pays 340 once. Which bell is next is saved on version 12. Version 11 starts this beat over. The seven story banks stay seven.

## Impact

- Affected specs: nord-bells, nord-unreal
- Affected code: `QuebecBells.h`, `QuebecStorySave.h`, `QuebecCampaign`, the bell tests, and the version-12 save readers
