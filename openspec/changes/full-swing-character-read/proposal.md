# Character read

Street people are six boxes, and the hero is a cel-shaded figure with no edge light, so both disappear into the sunset. Spider-Man 2's people read as people at a glance: shoulders, hair, and a rim that lifts them off the street.

## What changes

The instanced crowd keeps one mesh and the same limb pivots, and gains a neck, a hair mass, shoulders, a chest, and a waist in the trouser colour. The hero and the crowd pick up a small warm rim on the sun side and a cool rim in shade, taken from the illustrative wrap in Team Fortress 2. No new art. A headset still draws these materials; they are not a post pass.

## Capabilities

- `swing-character-read`: crowd figures and the hero separate from the street, and a crowd figure has a head, hair, shoulders, and legs.
