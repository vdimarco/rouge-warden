# One thud on a dent

No new rendering technique is involved, so none of the usual art references fit. The cue is a short wave, the same kind of synthesized sting the rank and the lock chirp already use.

The dent rule is the one the body already uses: a wall closing faster than 600 cm/s, or another car faster than 500 cm/s. Under that, the hit is a scrape and stays quiet. The server asks the driver's client to play it. Traffic and a parked car with nobody at the wheel do not play it. The other car asks its own driver, so two players in the crash each hear one thud. The next thud from the same car waits 0.35 seconds. The wave is a 48 Hz body under a short noise crack, quieter than a full-scale sample and louder than the chirp's peak, and it does not sit on the pitches the city already plays. The v1 save is untouched. UpdateMusic and the three feedback sounds are untouched.
