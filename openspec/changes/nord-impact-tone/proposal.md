# A thud when a hit dents the car

## Why
The body takes damage from a hard wall and from another car. The engine hum keeps going. Nothing marks the hit.

## What changes
- A hit that adds body damage plays a short thud for that car's driver.
- A wall starts at 600 cm/s. Another car starts at 500 cm/s. A scrape under that stays quiet.
- A second dent inside 0.35 seconds stays quiet, so a grind does not machine-gun.
- The other driver hears the same crash when they are in the other car.
- The thud is a low crack, off the rank pitches, the lock chirp, the pond tick, the court tones and the theatre page.
- The music path and the three feedback sounds stay as they are.

## Out of scope
A new engine loop, stream latency, a ninth story mission, a comic word, and a change to the rank pitches or the chirp.
