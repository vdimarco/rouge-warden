# An atelier spends cash on a dented car

## Why
Chez Lise sells a kit, a vest, or one star. A wrecked car still has nowhere to put that cash. The bill should grow with the damage, and a police car should not be a customer.

## What changes
- A sign, ATELIER, stands on a sidewalk east of the spawn, eight metres off a mapped road.
- E on foot quotes the nearest hurt car within reach. A wreck costs 400. Half the body costs 220.
- A short purse, a clean car, a driver, a downed player, and a police car are refused. Stars are not cleared.
- The campaign save still writes only Version and CompletedMissions.

## Out of scope
A ninth story mission, a new side job, stream latency, and a new art technique.
