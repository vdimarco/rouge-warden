# Pay for the dent

No new rendering technique is involved, so none of the usual art references fit. The sign reuses the cream, ink, and lime materials the stall already builds. The 3D caption reads ATELIER.

The spot is eight metres off the road centre, and the player's reach is two metres, so someone standing at the sign is farther from a car on that road than the door radius. E repairs the car instead of getting in. The bill is a straight line from 40 dollars at no damage to 400 at a wreck, and a clean car is not charged. Cash is written on the player state. The bay does not call the campaign's private cash grant, and it does not read wanted stars. While the thief job is running, that job's car is skipped so a repair cannot undo the chase.
