# Show the foot dust on the play page

## Why

A walk already leaves five pale puffs at the rear shoe, and the play page never shows them.

## What changes

The play shell draws those same puffs while the walker is scuffing. The puff count, life, interval, and speed stay as they are. No new sound. No new mission.

## Impact

- Affected specs: nord-foot-dust
- Affected code: the puff ages sent to the play shell, and the shell canvas
