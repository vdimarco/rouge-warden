# A buoy lap on the north pond

## Why
The pedalo waits by the bridge and has nowhere to go. The north pond is open water with an island between the berth and the deep end.

## What changes
- A buoy stands at the north-pond swim point.
- Paddle the pedalo to the buoy, then back to the berth.
- The driver is paid 120 session cash once.
- A car does not count. Touching the berth before the buoy does not finish the lap.
- The v1 save is untouched.

## Out of scope
A ninth story mission, a new mesh, a save field, and a change to car handling or the paddle speeds.
