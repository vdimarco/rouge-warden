# The clock follows the diamond

## Why
Pacing is a 6 the rules can prove. The card quotes the next leg between posts and leaves that number in place. The last approach, and every rescue group, name no time at all.

## What changes
- On foot, the card names the walk and the sprint left to the diamond you are following.
- Inside the widest step reach the clock drops, so it does not talk over the work.
- The last approach and a rescue group get that clock.
- In a car the card keeps the stored leg, which is already zero on the drive.

## Out of scope
A ninth mission, a change to the race clock, stream latency, and a new art technique.
