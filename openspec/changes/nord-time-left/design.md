# Time left, not the next post

No new rendering technique is involved, so none of the usual art references fit. The clock uses the walk and sprint speeds the card already uses.

The stored leg stays the distance from this post to the next one. On foot the number on the card is the distance from the player to the current diamond, at 450 cm/s and 800 cm/s. At or inside the pond reach, 280 cm, both times are zero and the suffix drops. A car does not take that path, so a drive does not grow a walk.
