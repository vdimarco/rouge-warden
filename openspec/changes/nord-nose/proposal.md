# Back the car into the stall

## Why

The send-off wants a full stop. The pursuit wants you alongside a car that keeps its lane. Nothing asks you to reverse.

## What changes

After the pursuit is banked, a stall sits on a surveyed road and a line-up sits further toward the far end. A real car inside the stall, reversing at 400 cm/s or more, with its nose toward that far end, banks the beat and pays 440 once. A stop, a forward roll, a sideways reverse, and a walker do not. The step is saved on version 16. Version 15 starts this beat over. The seven story banks stay seven. The send-off and the pursuit are not retuned.

## Impact

- Affected specs: nord-nose, nord-unreal
- Affected code: `QuebecNose.h`, `QuebecStorySave.h`, `QuebecCampaign`, the stall tests, and the version-16 save readers
