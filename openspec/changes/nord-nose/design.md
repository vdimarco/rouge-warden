# Back the car into the stall

No new rendering technique. The stall and the line-up are the same flat cubes the other beats already use. None of the usual art references fit.

The road is the surveyed segment from (44495.179, 23662.179) to (46161.057, 31394.466). The stall is 32 m along that segment. The line-up, which is also the recover point, is 24 m further toward the far end, outside the 9 m stall. Player reverse clamps at -950 cm/s, so 400 cm/s of reverse is reachable and is past the send-off crawl of 80. The nose must point toward the far end within a 0.55 dot. The purse is 440, once. The step index is the only saved field.
