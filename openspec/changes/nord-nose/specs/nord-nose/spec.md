## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Backing into the stall

After the pursuit is banked, the stall is open on a surveyed road. A real car inside the stall, reversing, with its nose toward the far end, banks the beat and pays 440 once. A stop, a forward roll, a sideways reverse, and a walker leave the beat open and pay nothing.

#### Scenario: A reversing car noses into the stall

- **WHEN** the pursuit is banked and a real car is inside the stall, moving backward at 400 cm/s or more, with its nose toward the far end
- **THEN** the beat banks and pays 440 once

#### Scenario: A stop in the stall

- **WHEN** a real car is inside the stall and stopped, rolling forward, reversing sideways, or a walker stands there
- **THEN** the beat stays open and pays nothing

#### Scenario: Recover during the stall

- **WHEN** the player recovers while the stall is open
- **THEN** they return to the line-up and the step stays
