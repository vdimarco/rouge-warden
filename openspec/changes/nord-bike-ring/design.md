# Two marks, then the stand

No new rendering technique is involved, so none of the usual art references fit. Each mark is an engine cylinder in the comic lime, ink, or cream the posts already use. The new part is the order, and that it only moves for the bike.

The lawn is (34800, 16000), 200 m north of the stand. The allée is (14800, -4000), 200 m west of the stand. Both are dry, and so is the straight line between them. Each leg is longer than 50 m, so one reach cannot cover two marks. Only the bike advances the step. The lawn remembers who is riding. That rider has to reach the allée, then the stand. Anyone else leaves the step where it is. The 180 cash is written on that rider's session purse when the step becomes done. Nothing is saved, and the campaign phase list is not extended.
