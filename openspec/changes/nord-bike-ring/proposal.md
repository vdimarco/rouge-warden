# A loop for the park bike

## Why
The park bike pedals, and then the ride is over. The lawn north of the stand and the allée to the west are empty.

## What changes
- One post stands on that lawn, one on the allée, and a ring marks the stand.
- The bike has to tag the lawn, then the allée, then the stand.
- The rider who tagged the lawn is the only one who can finish. They are paid 180 session cash once.
- A car does not count. A later mark does not skip the one before it. A second pass does not pay.
- While the loop is open, the bike's card names the next mark.

## Out of scope
A new mesh, a ninth story mission, a save field, a phone button, and a change to the bike's pedal speeds.
