# Neon Stack in every view, and a grounded runner

Neon Stack had a homepage cabinet and a Lab card, and no machine in the Corridor. All Games left it out, and its Updated date was still the day it was added. This change gives it a machine in every view and makes that the rule for every game (`AGENTS.md`, `.agents/skills/arcade-machine/SKILL.md`).

The runner also stood on the street with no shadow and one flat tone from feet to hood. It gets a contact shadow and a height shade. A browser without WebGL2 now gets a line of text instead of an empty page.
