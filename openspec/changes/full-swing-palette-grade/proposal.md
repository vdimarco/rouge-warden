# The three inks are three hues

The city is one sunset ramp. Shade, the step between, and the sun are darker and lighter versions of the same violet and orange.

## What changes

Cool shade takes a teal. The step between shade and sun takes a rose. Bright lamps, the sun disc, and near-black ink stay as they are. The painted sky strip is not replaced. The dome is graded with the same split, and the lookup the tests read stays the strip.

## Capabilities

- `swing-palette-grade`: shaded city colour reads teal, and the step between shade and the sun reads rose.
