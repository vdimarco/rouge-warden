# Value against the avenue

Jacques Bertin, Semiology of Graphics (1983), the same value variable used for the avenue casing. A mark that shares its ground's value disappears. The avenue center is paper `(0.92, 0.89, 0.82)` and the heading was a paper outline, so the point of the arrow fell into the stroke. The ink wedge is the same triangle grown by 1.55 about its centroid. The paper wedge is the original triangle, drawn after the ink. The three outline strokes stay. The road ranks stay.

Unreal is not installed. The offline picture is the radar disc at scale 1, with one avenue through the centre and the heading along it, then a 3× nearest enlargement. It is not an engine capture. `QuebecGameplay.cpp` was not compiled.
