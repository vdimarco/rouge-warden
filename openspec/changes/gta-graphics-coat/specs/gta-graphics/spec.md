## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Glossy bodywork reflects the sky
The comic post process SHALL mix a sky colour onto a surface whose roughness is in the bodywork range, along the reflected view ray, with a stronger mix at a grazing angle. A rubber surface, a printed crew pixel, a foliage pixel, a sky pixel, and a surface already marked as a smooth reflection SHALL keep their colour.

#### Scenario: A coupe in the walking frame
- **WHEN** the clearcoat is applied to the seventies coupe from the walking camera
- **THEN** the paint and the glass move toward the sky colour, and the tires match the uncoated frame
