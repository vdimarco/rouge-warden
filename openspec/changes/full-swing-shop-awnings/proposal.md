# Shop awnings

The street floor of a shop building is one painted tile. A cloth band already existed for the flat look, and it turned off as soon as that tile came on. The named street camera is close enough that the tile is on, so the shops in that view have no awning.

## What changes

The same band paints after the tile, above the shop glass. One colour per bay, a darker hem, and a seam that fades when it would shimmer. No new mesh, no new draw, no full-screen pass.

## Capabilities

- `swing-shop-awnings`: a shop's street floor carries a cloth band above its windows, including when the painted tile is on.
