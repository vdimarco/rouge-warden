## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The music in a fight
While goons fight the hero in play, the music SHALL add a fight layer in time with the groove: kicks on every beat, an off-beat
bass stab and a tom run into each bar. It SHALL stop when the fight ends.

#### Scenario: A fight starts and ends
- **WHEN** a goon starts to fight the hero, and later the goons are gone
- **THEN** the fight layer is on during the fight and off after it
