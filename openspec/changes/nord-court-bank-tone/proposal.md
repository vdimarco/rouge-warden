# Bank-shot whistle

## Why
The court bank is the mission's joke, and it answered with the same checkpoint cue as every other step. An ink miss was only a line of text.

## What changes
- A clear bank plays a short original whistle on the local player when the step changes from the return gate to the clamp.
- A spoiled bank stays on the return gate, increments a session-only miss count, and the local player hears a rasp. The count is not written to the v1 save.
- The three cooked cues, the pond tick, the page sound, and the two music loops stay as they are.

## Out of scope
A new save field, a tennis simulation, stream latency, and missions 6–8.
