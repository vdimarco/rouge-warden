# A third attack for the King

No new rendering. The ring and the comic words already exist. None of the grass, water, outline, or painted-look sources fit a manhole-sized yank.

Marvel's Spider-Man 2 (Insomniac, 2023) gives a boss a grab you answer by shooting a web, and a ground attack you leave by being in the air. The words stay in this game's voice: PULL, GLUG, HELD, SAFE.

## Decisions

- While every pipe is on, he only throws, so the opening of the fight is unchanged.
- After a pipe is off, attacks cycle throw, stomp, pull. The attack after a throw is still a stomp. The attack after a stomp is a pull.
- A throw that never leaves, because the line closed, does not take a turn.
- The pull drags an airborne hero with no attached rope 14 m toward the perch. Ending within 16 m takes a heart. Farther than that, the drag still happens and the heart stays.
- On the ground the pull does nothing. An attached rope holds, and the pull does not clear that rope.
- During the wind-up the card says he is pulling and to shoot a web. After it, the card names the next pipe again.
