# The same axes, two key sets

No new rendering technique is involved, so none of the usual art references fit. The change is four axis rows in `DefaultInput.ini`.

Unreal already binds MoveForward and MoveRight on both the walker and the car. Up is scale 1 and Down is scale -1, matching W and S. Right is scale 1 and Left is scale -1, matching D and A. The phone wheel's NumPad levels are untouched.

The line does not change CompletedMissions or the v1 save.
