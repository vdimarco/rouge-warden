# Arrow keys walk and drive

## Why
Controls feel is a 4 the input mapping can prove. Walk and drive were WASD only. The route line says left and right, and the arrow keys did nothing.

## What changes
- Up and Down move forward and back at the same scale as W and S.
- Left and Right strafe at the same scale as A and D.
- The arrows are not also actions. The phone's graded steering keys stay as they are.
- The drive hint and the play page name the arrows.

## Out of scope
A handling change, a new mission, stream latency, and a new art technique.
