# Street steam

The avenue has haze and one sun shaft, and no weather a player can see while standing in it. Spider-Man 2's streets carry steam off the walks. This city does not.

## What changes

Grates on the sidewalks put up a short column of comic steam. Each grate keeps three puffs on a loop, so a still always shows one rising. They fade out nearby and past the block, and they thin out in the existing haze. No new post pass, and no new art files.

## Capabilities

- `swing-street-steam`: sidewalk grates put up comic steam that a street view and a play frame can see.
