# Drifting leaf cards

William T. Reeves, "Particle Systems — A Technique for Modeling a Class of Fuzzy Objects" (SIGGRAPH 1983), births a bounded set of cards and carries them with a simple drift. The broadleaf meshes already refused alpha cards on the isosurface, so these cards are a separate set, born under the crown of the nearest tree. The rain-streak shader does not fit: it fills a box around the camera.

Unreal is not installed. The offline picture is the opening walk camera (arm 420, socket Y 70, socket Z 110, pitch −16, horizontal FOV 90) with one sidewalk tree. The before frame is the crown. The after frame adds the cards. It is not an engine capture. `QuebecVegetation.cpp` was not compiled.
