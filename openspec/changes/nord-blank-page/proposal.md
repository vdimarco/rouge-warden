# Mission 8: Leave the Last Page Blank

## Why
Fun is the missions loop's next target, and the story still stops at mission 7 of 8. After the escape, the card drops to side jobs while the last page is still his.

## What changes
- After the convoy is banked, the mission card and the gold diamond lead through three projection gates in order, on dry ground south of the theatre, then a pull that leaves the page blank.
- Crossing a gate does not skip the next one. The pull does not write the save. CompletedMissions stays at most 2.
- Side jobs and T wait until the page is blank. R restores the current gate and does not uncross one that is already crossed.

## Out of scope
Driving the projector, the Curator's page-turn attack, the two retaining clamps, a new save field, a new art technique, a new procedural tone, and stream latency.
