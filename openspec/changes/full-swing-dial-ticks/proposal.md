# A printed dial on the compass

The chase compass is a cream disc and a red arrow. The plate already has a cream lip and a rose hem. The disc is still one colour.

## What changes

The disc keeps its size and its ink rim. A rose ring and four ink ticks are painted on it, in the plate's own hem colour. The ticks do not turn. The arrow stays a second mark and still turns toward the goal. The score row's box does not move.

## Capabilities

- `swing-dial-ticks`: the compass shows a rose ring and four ink ticks, and the row stays where it was.
