# Ticks on the paper the plate already uses

## Source

The lip and the hem from the score plate (round 21). None of the dimensional HUD breakdowns fit this sticker, so the dial uses that same paper: rose `#e7a0b4` and ink `#140a18`.

## Decisions

- The ticks and the ring are a second svg under the arrow. The arrow's own svg is the one that rotates, so a turn does not spin the ticks.
- The dial stays 28 px. The row's box is not part of this change.
- The ring is the plate's hem, not a new colour.
