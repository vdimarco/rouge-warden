# Sun shafts

The golden-hour sky is painted, and the avenue now mirrors it, but the air between the camera and the sun is empty. Spider-Man 2 puts light in that air: shafts that stop at a roof.

## What changes

Flat play gathers a few samples of the far warm sky along the line to the sun and adds them as a capped shaft. Near buildings block those samples. The headset still draws the scene straight. No new art.

## Capabilities

- `swing-sun-shafts`: light shafts on flat views when the sun is in front of the camera.
