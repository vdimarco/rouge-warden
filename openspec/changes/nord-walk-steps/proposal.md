# The on-foot stick walks in steps

## Why
Controls feel is a 5 the tests can prove. The wheel already steers in three steps. The on-foot stick is full speed once the thumb leaves a dead zone.

## What changes
- A short push on the on-foot stick walks at a third of full speed.
- A longer push strides at two thirds.
- The edge of the stick stays full speed, on the same keys as WASD.
- WASD and the arrow keys stay full lock.
- A latched sprint waits for that full step.

## Out of scope
A change to the wheel, a ninth mission, stream latency, and a new art technique.
