# Design and researched basis

Microsoft [XAG108](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/108) recommends reducing barriers through steering/braking/traction assists. Epic documents [speed-dependent steering](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/python-api/class/VehicleSteeringConfig?application_version=5.0) and [input rise/fall rates and curves](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/python-api/class/VehicleInputRateConfig?application_version=5.6). Those are design references; NORD uses its own swept arcade model, not Chaos Vehicles, and the tuning numbers below come from measured local behavior rather than a claimed universal standard.

The retained older native report records 70.7 km/h after 1.5 s; analytical coasting with the former equation from 20 m/s takes 9.55 s / 85.8 m. This is historical native evidence plus a mathematical estimate, not a fresh native before/after run. The wheel sends digital A/D beyond 18% displacement. Gas/Brake/Exit finger drift also sends camera motion and delays the chase camera 1.8 s. Current main includes newer floating/vertical driving and car-impact behavior missing from the dirty shared checkout. Use an isolated current-main checkout and preserve all those changes.

Tune only player simulation: 1,050→700 cm/s² power, 360 cm/s² base lift-off drag, 3.5/s held turn-in and 10/s release/countersteer. The gentler power applies in both forward and reverse; the reverse cap and service-brake-before-reverse precedence remain. While forward Gas is held, steering above 0.2 gives a soft corner-speed target from 2,400 toward 900 cm/s. Above that target, speed approaches it at a bounded 1,200 cm/s² rather than being clamped instantly. Releasing Gas uses lift-off drag instead. Failsafe, service-brake, intentional handbrake, traffic and impact branches remain. Driving action buttons suppress accidental look; walking action-look, deliberate second-finger/gyro look and floating drive controls remain.

There are no reflected header/build-config or asset changes. Exact current-main native source pins match the installed PR404 hotfix, allowing an ordinary native-binary package hotfix with existing cooked content unchanged. Build and verify a separate candidate before promotion; preserve every Saved root and retain a rollback package/service recipe. Do not mutate the shared checkout, source art, maps, cast or other tasks' files. Root owns serialized UE/GPU/service operations and current guards. Browser decode, physical devices and subjective comfort remain separate from numeric tests.

Validation: actual native authority-world motion at 30/120 Hz, measurable tap/full/partial steering and neutral release, useful acceleration and bounded top speed, progressive two-second lift-off slowing, tight/shallow/no assist, retained reverse cap/precedence, traffic/impacts/curbs/walls/input validation; packaged drive/brake/exit and independent peer ownership; rendered and real-stream input/release; browser pedal-jitter and existing gesture regressions. Numbers from a separate CPU model are preparation, not native proof. Browser plugin absent; use the installed repository Playwright fixtures.

The first checkpoint passes both native builds, all 46 native tests with zero errors and existing warnings, all 66 browser unit tests, and local browser UI/gesture checks. Fresh native observations include 1,202.736 cm/s after 1.5 s acceleration; 1.283° for a 100 ms correction; 1,108.916 cm/s after two seconds of lift-off from 2,000 cm/s; and 897.616 cm/s after the full powered-corner scenario. The native test measures a slowing window, not a full stopping distance. Package/rendered/public delivery and physical devices remain pending.
