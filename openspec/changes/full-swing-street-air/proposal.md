# Street air

The avenue is sharp from the kerb to the far crossing. Fog does not start until 180 m, and the print steps hide the thin veil in front of that, so the street has no air. Spider-Man 2's blocks carry a low haze the rooftops rise out of.

## What changes

The existing height fog gains a second, shorter layer. It thickens along the walks and the lower floors, and it thins out by the roofs. The near kerb stays sharp. The four print steps stay. No full-screen pass.

## Capabilities

- `swing-street-air`: the street canyon shows a low haze the upper floors do not.
