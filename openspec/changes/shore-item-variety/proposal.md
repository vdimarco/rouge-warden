# Shore of the Ancients: more varied items

The user asked for items that are more interesting and varied.

## Problem
- **Old item set:** six components, twelve completed items and eight relics.
- **What they do:** most completed items add stats and one hit effect: a burn, a slow, a chain or an aura.
- **What is missing:**
  - No item rewards winning fights over time.
  - No item helps a player push wards, or resists crowd control.
  - No item rewards a skill cast with speed.
  - No item marks a target for the team.
  - No item trades safety for damage.
  - No item supports the team when the ultimate is cast.

## Scope
- **Seven new completed items.** Each has one clear power and uses the existing components:
  - **Drowned doubloon:** stacks from banished heroes.
  - **Wardbreaker maul:** damage against wards, and protection from them.
  - **Duelist's glass:** a risky damage edge.
  - **Seer's eye:** a team mark.
  - **Moonstone charm:** stuns, fears and slows wear off faster.
  - **Riptide sandals:** speed after each cast.
  - **Rallying conch:** a team rally on the ultimate.
- **Three new relics** made from them:
  - **Kraken's hoard:** a larger stack cap, and stacks are kept on death.
  - **Siegebreaker titan:** a stronger ward breaker.
  - **Tidecaller's warhorn:** a rally on every skill.
- **Three new recommended builds** in the Night Market: Plunder, Siege and Vanguard.
- **New icons:** the new items reuse the painted item icons with a hue shift. No new art was generated.
- **Not in scope:** bots keep their current builds, so bot strength and match balance do not change. A player can choose the new builds.

## Capabilities
- Modify `moba-combat`: it adds the item powers.
