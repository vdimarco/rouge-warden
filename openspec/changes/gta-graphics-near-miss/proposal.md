# Proposal: a near miss

The handbrake slide is the only driving stunt the city notices. Variety has been tied for the lowest line since round 83. This round adds a second stunt: passing another car close and fast, without touching it, shows a NEAR MISS banner with the speed.

## Out of scope

No cash is paid. The slide, its grades, its chain, its sounds, traffic behavior, and the crime rules stay as they are. A near miss has no sound this round.
