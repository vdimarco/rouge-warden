# Proposal: dusk windows are not one lamp

## What

A lit window on a dusk wall keeps its frame, muntin and mullion. Its glass is no longer one colour. A hash of the wall, the column and the row picks a dark room or a lit room, and a lit room varies in warmth and brightness.

## Why

The corner shows every occupied window as the same amber square. A street at dusk reads as rooms, some dark and some warmer or cooler than the next.

## Scope

`pane_light` in the window script, the corner picture, and the scorecard. The opening aim, the five-metre floor, and the frame bars stay. Unreal is not installed, so the editor does not apply this.
