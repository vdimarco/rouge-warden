## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The walking camera sits on the right shoulder
The on-foot spring arm SHALL offset the camera 70 cm to the figure's right, at the same height as before. Head and feet SHALL stay inside a wide frame and a tall frame at the allowed pitch limits. Starting to aim SHALL not slide that sideways offset.

#### Scenario: Street ahead of the walker
- **WHEN** the walking camera is shaded down the street at a resting pitch inside the pitch clamp
- **THEN** the figure sits left of center and the street on the figure's right is open beside them

#### Scenario: Pitch limits on a tall screen
- **WHEN** the capsule ends are projected at the upward and downward pitch limits in a 16:9 frame and a 9:20 frame
- **THEN** both ends stay in front of the camera and inside the frame
