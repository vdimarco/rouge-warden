## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: HUD cards carry a paper rule
The mission card, the caption, and the prompt SHALL draw a paper rule inside the fill. The mission title SHALL stay 12 scale units from the left, clear of that rule.

#### Scenario: Read the mission card
- **WHEN** the mission card is shaded at scale 1
- **THEN** a paper rule runs inside the top and bottom of the card, a crop tick sits in the top-right padding, and the title starts to the right of the accent bar and below the rule
