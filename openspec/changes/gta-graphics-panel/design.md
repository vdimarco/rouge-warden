# A rule inside the existing card

Scott McCloud, Understanding Comics (1993), treats the panel border as the line that separates a panel from the scene around it. These cards already have a fill and a left accent. The added mark is a paper hairline 4 scale units in from the top and bottom, plus a 6-unit crop tick in the top-right corner. Type starts 12 scale units in, so the rule and the tick stay in the padding. No shading paper fits a layout line, and this is not one: the cite is the border's job, and the mark is two `DrawLine`s and a tick.

Unreal is not installed, so `QuebecGameplay.cpp` was not compiled. The picture is an offline shade of the mission card at scale 1 on a 1280×720 frame, with the same stand-in words before and after.
