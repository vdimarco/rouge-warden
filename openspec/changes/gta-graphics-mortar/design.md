# A joint, not a darken

Inigo Quilez, "Coding a brick tower", builds bricks as rounded boxes and recesses the mortar so the joint is a second surface. A signed-distance field does not fit this shader. It returns one colour, and the editor pastes that HLSL into the existing facade material.

What carries over is the colour of that joint. The bed is a pale mortar, darker toward the back of the joint, and the top of each course is a thin lit arris under the high afternoon sun. A fine hash mottles the face. Glass, doors, lamps, and rails still replace that colour, so the openings stay clean.
