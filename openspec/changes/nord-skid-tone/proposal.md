# A chirp when the wheels lock

## Why
The phone can hold the handbrake, and the catch only paints the word SKRRT. The engine already hums. Nothing marks the moment the wheels lock.

## What changes
- When the lock engages above 300 cm/s, a short chirp plays.
- A held lock, a lock at a walk, and letting go stay quiet.
- The chirp is a sweep, off the rank pitches and off the tones the city already plays.
- The music path and the three feedback sounds stay as they are.

## Out of scope
A new engine loop, stream latency, a ninth story mission, and a change to the rank pitches.
