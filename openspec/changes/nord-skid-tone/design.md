# One chirp on the catch

No new rendering technique is involved, so none of the usual art references fit. The cue is a short wave, the same kind of synthesized sting the rank already uses.

The car already hums while it moves, and the comic word SKRRT already marks a lock above 300 cm/s. The chirp uses that same catch: the lock was up, it is down, and the car is faster than a walk. Holding the lock does not play it again. The sweep runs from 1860 Hz down to 420 Hz under a little noise, so it does not sit on a rank pitch or on the pond, court, or theatre tones. The v1 save is untouched. UpdateMusic and the three feedback sounds are untouched.
