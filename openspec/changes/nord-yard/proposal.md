# Cross the yard while they look away

## Why

The lamp crossing is a dark pocket. The slip is a sprint against someone who follows. Nothing asks you to wait until a watcher turns, then walk a yard they are not looking at.

## What changes

After the bolt is banked, a yard off the road has a near side and a far side. A watcher stands still and turns. On foot, standing on the near side while they look elsewhere arms the crossing. Reaching the far side before their look swings onto you banks it and pays 540 once. Stepping into that look sends the crossing back to the near side and does not save. A car does not arm it and does not clear it. The step is saved on version 22. Version 21 starts this beat over. Where they are looking, and whether the crossing is armed, stay in the session. The seven story banks stay seven. The bolt, the shade, the slip, and the parcel are not retuned.

## Impact

- Affected specs: nord-yard, nord-unreal
- Affected code: `QuebecYard.h`, `QuebecStorySave.h`, `QuebecCampaign`, the yard tests, and the version-22 save readers
