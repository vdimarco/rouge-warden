## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Crossing the yard

After the bolt is banked, the yard is open. A watcher turns in place. On foot, arm the crossing on the near side while they look elsewhere, then reach the far side before their look reaches you. That banks the beat and pays 540 once. Stepping into the look sends the crossing back and pays nothing. A car does not clear the far side.

#### Scenario: A walk while they look away

- **WHEN** the bolt is banked and the player waits on the near side until the look is elsewhere, then walks to the far side
- **THEN** the beat banks and pays 540 once

#### Scenario: Their look sends the crossing back

- **WHEN** the crossing is armed and the player steps into the look
- **THEN** the crossing goes back to the near side and the beat pays nothing

#### Scenario: A car at the far side

- **WHEN** the crossing is armed and a car stands on the far side
- **THEN** the beat stays open and pays nothing

#### Scenario: Recover during the yard

- **WHEN** the player recovers while the yard is open
- **THEN** they return to the near side and the step stays
