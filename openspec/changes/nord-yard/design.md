# Cross the yard while they look away

No new rendering technique. The near side, the far side, and the watcher are the same flat cubes the other beats already use. None of the usual art references fit.

The near side is (-37668, 59266). The far side is (-36068, 59266). The watcher stands at (-36868, 58166) and turns at 0.35 radians a second. Their look is a cone of 0.55 radians out to 22 m. One frame spends at most a quarter of a second, so a long frame does not sweep the whole turn. The purse is 540, once. The step index is the only saved field. A reload starts the look at the opening angle and clears the armed crossing. The watcher does not move and does not follow.
