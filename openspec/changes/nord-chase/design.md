# A car that follows, on one straight road

No new rendering technique is involved, so none of the usual art references fit. The seat, the hide, and the tail are existing flat cubes and captions. No new mesh or shader is introduced. The lap ghost stays on the race route at its own pace. The police pursuit model stays on wanted cars. This beat does not call either of them, and it does not spawn a car.

The road is the surveyed edge from (55373.3, 15114.2) to (75373.3, 1932.1). The seat is (59548.1, 12362.6) and the hide is (74577.2, 2456.8), 180 m along that edge. The tail waits 45 m behind the seat, still on the edge, and then seeks the player's position at 1100 cm/s. Catch radius is 9 m. A drive at 1800 cm/s along the edge pulls away and reaches the hide. A crawl at 400 cm/s is caught first. A stopped car is caught. The edge from the park to the hide stays more than 2.5 m from every surveyed building shell. The doors sit on the road centre, so a car on that lane is inside the 14 m reach. Recover is (60538.6, 13865.5), off to the side of the seat, outside both doors and off the tail's park. Recover does not move the tail.

Only a real car, ride kind 0, on the seat starts the chase. Only a real car on the hide, with the tail still outside the catch, pays 640. A tie at the hide goes to the tail. If the driver gets out, the tail keeps coming, and the hide does not count until they are back in a car. If anyone in a real car is already inside the seat or the hide, that car is the one the beat watches, even when a partner is closer to the other mark. One frame moves the tail for at most a quarter of a second. With nobody present, the tail does not seek the world origin.

The slot writer moves from version 7 to version 8. Version 7 still accepts the short way and forces the chase to 0. Version 8 accepts a chase step only after the short way is banked, and only when completed missions are 2. The tail's place is session state, so a load puts them back behind the seat and leaves the step.
