## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Some clay bricks carry a salt crust
The facade SHALL paint a pale crust on the lower face of some clay units, clear of the mortar joint. Stone SHALL not take the crust. The lamp apron and the iron trough SHALL stay.

#### Scenario: Look at a brick elevation
- **WHEN** a straight elevation of a clay facade is shaded
- **THEN** some units show a pale deposit above the joint, the mortar stays its own colour, and the glass and the warm apron stay
