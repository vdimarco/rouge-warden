# Side jobs pay on the story chain

## Why
Fun is a 5 the rules can prove. The story chain stops when the last page banks. A side job still pays its flat amount, and failing one does not touch the chain.

## What changes
- A clean side job pays its reward times the chain already held, then the chain grows, up to seven.
- A spoiled chain pays that job's flat reward and starts over.
- A failed side job breaks the chain.
- The card names the stake while a side job is the next thing to do.
- With no chain held, the pay is the same reward as before. The story marks stay.

## Out of scope
A ninth mission, a new side job, stream latency, and a new art technique.
