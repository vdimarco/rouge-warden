## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The chase score row is one printed instrument
In third-person flat play the score row SHALL sit on one paper plate with a printed dot field. The coin and the clog icon SHALL read as faced tokens, and the compass arrow SHALL sit on a dial. The row's border box SHALL stay the box that places the spoken line and the lock arrow.

#### Scenario: Flat play starts
- **WHEN** flat play starts in third person with the mission row showing
- **THEN** the mission, the loonies, the clogs, and the compass share one dotted plate, and the lock arrow's gap under that row is still at least 8 px
