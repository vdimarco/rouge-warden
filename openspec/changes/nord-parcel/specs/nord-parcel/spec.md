## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Crossing with the parcel

After the ditch is banked, the parcel is open off a surveyed street. Picking it up on foot starts a sixteen-second clock. Two traffic cars drive the street and do not steer. Reaching the stoop on foot after the car has passed banks the beat and pays 620 once. Stepping into the lane while a car is close drops the parcel, starts the clock over, and pays nothing. A car does not pick it up. Waiting out the clock drops it.

#### Scenario: A player waits, then walks it across

- **WHEN** the ditch is banked and the player picks up the parcel, waits for the car, and walks to the stoop
- **THEN** the beat banks and pays 620 once

#### Scenario: A rush meets the car

- **WHEN** the player picks up the parcel and walks or sprints into the lane at once
- **THEN** the parcel goes back, the clock starts over, and the beat pays nothing

#### Scenario: A car at the parcel

- **WHEN** a car stands on the parcel
- **THEN** the clock does not start and the beat pays nothing

#### Scenario: Recover during the crossing

- **WHEN** the player recovers while the parcel is open
- **THEN** they return behind the parcel and the step stays
