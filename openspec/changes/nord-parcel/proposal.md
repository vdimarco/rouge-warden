# Cross with the parcel

## Why

The hot delivery is a café and three doors, and the clock grows at each door. Nothing asks you to pick up one parcel, wait for a car that keeps the street, and walk it to the stoop across before a fixed clock dies.

## What changes

After the ditch is banked, a parcel sits off a surveyed street. Picking it up on foot starts a sixteen-second clock. Two traffic cars drive that street and do not steer. Step into the lane while one is close and the parcel goes back, the clock starts over, and that drop does not save. Waiting for the car, then walking or sprinting to the stoop, pays 620 once. A car does not pick it up and does not deliver it. Waiting out the clock drops it. The step is saved on version 20. Version 19 starts this beat over. The seven story banks stay seven. The ditch, the march, the slip, and the hot delivery are not retuned.

## Impact

- Affected specs: nord-parcel, nord-unreal
- Affected code: `QuebecParcel.h`, `QuebecStorySave.h`, `QuebecCampaign`, the parcel tests, and the version-20 save readers
