# Neon Stack bake-off, light first

The exit door sits outside the walkable grid, the glyph light turns the runner's outline on at spawn, and the night read is too flat. This pass keeps the three-draw renderer and spends the budget on readable sign light on the asphalt.

## Scope

- Move the exit and the terminal onto walkable tiles, and test that a search from spawn reaches both.
- Keep every sign more than 2.1 tiles from spawn. Seat noodle and bar on the lower floors without covering the tops of cross and arrow.
- Frame the spawn so the six-storey tower and a skyline strip sit near the top, with the terminal on screen.
- Bake sign pools, wet streaks, and contact shadows into the ground pass. Tint the lots, hood the runner, and redraw the glyphs.
