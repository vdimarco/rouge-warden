# A sixth side job drives a passenger to the quay

## Why
The five side jobs are a delivery, a chase, a fight, an escape, and a poster tour. None of them fails because you stepped out of the car. A fare is a new job, and the clock on foot is a new pace.

## What changes
- A sixth marker, La course, stands on a mapped road south of the park.
- The passenger boards at that marker. The drop is a quay road west of the terrace.
- After boarding, four seconds on foot and the passenger gets out. The job fails.
- The drop counts only for a car that has slowed down. A walker at the quay does not drop them.
- The clock is 100 seconds and does not grow. The pay is 450. The other five pays stay as they were.

## Out of scope
A ninth story mission, a new cash stall, stream latency, and a new art technique.
