# Stay in the car

No new rendering technique is involved, so none of the usual art references fit. The marker and the goal post reuse the yellow discs the other jobs already build. The 3D sign reads LA COURSE.

The pickup and the drop are surveyed road points from the Montréal map. The pickup is far enough from the spawn that the free-roam card still names the escape there, and far enough from the terrace that the card still names the goons. Boarding does not spend the four-second grace; the next tick starts it. A partner who is driving holds the grace for both players. The v1 slot still writes only Version and CompletedMissions. The fare is not a story beat, so it does not bank the chain by itself. Pay still goes through the existing job reward.
