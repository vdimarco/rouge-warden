# The street dust takes the hour

The far avenue stays the same warm rose when the grade has gone to blue hour.

## What changes

The low air keeps its sunset rose. As the hour walks toward blue hour, that dust walks toward the sky's violet. The air above the street layer stays the painted sky. No new mesh.

## Capabilities

- `swing-blue-dust`: down the street, blue hour turns the low haze from rose to violet, and the sunset haze stays warm.
