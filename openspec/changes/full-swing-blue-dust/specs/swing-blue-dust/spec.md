## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Street dust follows the hour

Looking down the avenue, the low haze SHALL be warm rose at the painted sunset and violet at blue hour. Looking up, the haze SHALL stay the painted sky.

#### Scenario: The same street at two hours

- **GIVEN** the street camera
- **WHEN** the hour is the painted sunset, and again when it is blue hour
- **THEN** the low air is rose at sunset and violet at blue hour, and the sky above that air is the painted strip

#### Scenario: The ink stays lines

- **GIVEN** the canyon view
- **WHEN** the facades are drawn
- **THEN** the dust does not flood the picture with ink
