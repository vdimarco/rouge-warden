# Technology pathways and the energy race

Give Breakthrough 2 six technology tracks for the hard parts of net zero, an explicit energy race, and ending copy that treats risk as serious without treating the outcome as already written.

## Scope

- `public/breakthrough2/` and `qa/breakthrough2/` only.
- Do not change `public/breakthrough/` or `public/tellme/`.
- Keep `?seed` and `?fast=1`. Keep the ending-order rules and the existing ending-distribution bands.

## Player-facing change

The player can spend a turn on one of six pathways: aviation fuels, shipping fuels, heavy industry, long-duration storage, grids, and carbon removal. Each track has research, pilot, and scale, a cost, a stall chance, and a bounded breakthrough. Pathways share the same resources as cards. Grids and storage both have to be through pilot before more clean power counts.

Every step shows demand growth against clean additions plus efficiency, and whether fossil generation is rising or falling. That race feeds the warming stock. The trajectory and the ending bars include pathways already started.

Closing copy states real-world context from the UNEP Emissions Gap report and IPCC AR6 WGII. It is labeled as context, not a score from the run. Bad endings describe interacting stress, not a scripted war.
