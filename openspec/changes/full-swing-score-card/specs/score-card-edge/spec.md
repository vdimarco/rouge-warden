## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The score plate has an edge
In flat play the chase score row SHALL show a lighter lip along the top of the plate and a darker hem along the bottom. The plate's box SHALL stay where it was, so the lock arrow still sits below it.

#### Scenario: The chase row
- **WHEN** flat play is up and the score row is on screen
- **THEN** the plate's background paints a light band at the top and a darker band at the bottom, and the row's box is the same height it was
