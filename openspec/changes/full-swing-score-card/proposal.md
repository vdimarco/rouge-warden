# A card edge on the score plate

The chase row is one flat dotted plate. The coin and the compass already have a face. The plate itself is one colour from top to bottom.

## What changes

The plate keeps its box, its ink border, and its magenta dots. A light lip runs along the top and a rose hem along the bottom, painted in the plate's own background. Each sticker takes a light inner edge. Nothing moves, so the spoken line and the lock arrow keep their gap.

## Capabilities

- `score-card-edge`: the chase plate shows a lit lip and a shaded hem, and its box stays where it was.
