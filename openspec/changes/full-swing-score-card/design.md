# A printed card, not a new layout

## Source

None of the dimensional HUD breakdowns fit this row. It is a comic sticker on paper, and the dots are already Benjamin Day's tint, the same field the pause menu uses. The lip and the hem are painted in that paper.

## Decisions

- The edge is a light lip and a rose hem on the plate and on each sticker, painted in the background. The outer offset shadow stays 3 px on the plate and 4 px on a sticker. The element's box does not change.
- The hem is rose, from the same magenta already in the dots, so the plate does not gain a new colour.
- A sticker that already replaces its shadow (the pull mission) keeps that shadow and gains the same inset edge.
