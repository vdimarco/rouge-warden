## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Rain streaks have a readable head
Each rain card SHALL be wide enough that its bright core and round leading head are separate from the tail at street distance. The streak's world-position offset SHALL stay the existing wrap.

#### Scenario: Rain in front of the camera
- **WHEN** rain streaks are shaded from a few metres to the far side of the street
- **THEN** a near streak shows a round bright head and a thinner tail, and a far streak is a shorter mark of the same shape
