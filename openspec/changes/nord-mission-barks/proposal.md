# Spoken lines for the mission givers

## Why
Sound is a 5 the rules can prove. A story beat has its own bed, and the two cooked loops remain. Pressing E on a resident still plays a sting. Nobody speaks.

## What changes
- Pressing E on a park resident plays that resident's own line, spoken.
- There are eleven lines, one per giver, and each giver has a different voice.
- The words are the lines already on the caption. The samples are original speech from espeak-ng.
- The cooked dialogue sting still marks other cues. The feedback list stays three sounds.

## Out of scope
A ninth mission, a change to the cooked loops, stream latency, and a new art technique.
