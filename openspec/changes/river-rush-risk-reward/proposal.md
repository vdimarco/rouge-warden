## Why
Five lanes and varied coin shapes do not yet create meaningful decisions: most alternatives are equally safe, and action rows replace the alternatives with a single obvious reward. The player wants harder judgments about coins and moving enemies without sacrificing speed or fluid controls.

## What Changes
- Add seeded, bounded decision stations offering a modest safe bypass and a richer action route guarded by actual hazards or moving enemies.
- Make guarded coins visibly distinct and worth twice ordinary coin points; perfect unprotected actions retain their separate existing reward.
- Increase route commitment and entry/exit complexity later in the campaign while preserving readable previews, reachable returns, strict pickups and protected tutorial/wave/finish beats.
- Show concise route cues and verify both conservative and aggressive strategies through physical gameplay.

## Capabilities
Modified: river-rush coin rewards, reachable action routes, moving enemies and five-lane layouts. Added: tactical coin decisions.

## Impact
River Rush generator, scoring, both renderers, compact gameplay UI, focused simulations and browser verification. Publish the rebuilt game to the existing arcade through rouge-warden main.
