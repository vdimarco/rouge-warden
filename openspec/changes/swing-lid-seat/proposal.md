# Seat the lid on the eleventh flush

The eleventh flush starts a new job: stand on a rattling street lid until it seats. The card names HOLD IT DOWN and SEAT THE LID, and it does not say to flush the clogs. The King still sleeps. After the lid seats, the clog card returns at 11/12. Flush twelve still wakes him. The first and third flushes stay on the clog card.
