# A grade with more than teal and rose

The avenue's paper is still two inks. Cool shade is teal and the step toward the sun is rose, whichever hour you look at.

## What changes

Deep shade and a cast shadow take violet, the sky's colour. Open shade stays teal. The step stays rose. A face in the sun takes amber. While the city is being played, that family walks from the painted sunset to blue hour and back, over four minutes. Bright lamps, the sun disc, and near-black ink stay. The painted strip is not replaced. The lookup the tests read stays the strip.

## Capabilities

- `swing-grade-hours`: the sunset grade shows violet, teal, rose, and amber, and blue hour moves that family.
