# Proposal: the corner curb is a row

## What

The corner keeps the parked car nearest the Marie-Anne / De Lanaudière sign, and draws the next two surveyed spaces on that same curb. Each car uses the body and paint stored on its slot.

## Why

The street showed one coupe. The curb data already has a wagon and two more cars behind it. A row reads as a street. One car reads as a prop.

## Scope

The row helper, the corner picture, and the scorecard. The opening aim stays. Unreal already fills these slots at runtime. The editor is not here, so this round does not apply a new asset.
