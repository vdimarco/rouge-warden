# Keep walking inside the mark

No new rendering technique. The mark is the same flat cube the other beats already use. None of the usual art references fit.

The mark is (57794.578, -18855.368), off the road. Walking at 90 to 450 cm/s inside 450 cm fills a six-second count. One frame spends at most a quarter of a second, so a long frame does not stand the whole post. The purse is 700, once. The step index is the only saved field. A reload starts the count again and keeps the step. Standing still is not the send-off crawl, and it does not fill the count.
