# Proposal: the slide pay is heard, and so is the chain

The tire scream from round 86 plays while a slide lasts and fades as the tires grip. The graded banner from round 87 and its count from round 88 show when the slide ends, and they make no sound. A driver who watches the road cannot tell a paid slide from a voided one, or x3 from x1. This round plays two rising notes when the banner shows. Each link of a chain adds a short repeat of the high note, up to four.

## Out of scope

The slide rule, the grades, the chain window, the scream, the lock chirp, and the other tones stay as they are.
