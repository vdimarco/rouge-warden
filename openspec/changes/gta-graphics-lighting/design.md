# Cool shadow wash

The comic material is one custom node on the tonemapped colour. A shadow buffer or a sampled sky light does not fit that node. Epic's Sky Atmosphere, already used for the Quebec sky, is the cool blue this wash should agree with, so the addend is a constant on that side of the sky. The wash still lifts shade off black. Outlines stay dark ink. The clear 15:00 sun scale, sky light, exposure, saturation, contrast, and white point are untouched.
