# Job result plate

## Decision

The result sentences live in `QuebecBanner.h`, with no Unreal types, so a system compiler can lock the words. The native plate and the stream both call `Compose`. The play shell paints the head and the line it is given. On a touch page, `R restarts` becomes `Menu · Recover`, which is the phrase the menu already uses.

The wanted plate, the down overlay, resident speech, and the job column stay as they are. This round only unmixes the result plate the player reads when a job ends.

## Rendering

None of the usual art references fit. The plate is the existing payout panel, restated in paper and ink, with a pink fill when the job fails. No new shading, particles, or texture step.
