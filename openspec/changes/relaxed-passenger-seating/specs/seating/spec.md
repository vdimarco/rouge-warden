## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Relaxed passenger arms
Vehicle passengers SHALL sit with upper arms beside their torso and only a slight elbow bend. Drivers SHALL retain their driving pose.

#### Scenario: Open Jeep passengers
- **WHEN** a passenger is seated in a Jeep
- **THEN** their arms rest down beside their body rather than reaching forward
- **AND** the pose remains seated during idle breathing

#### Scenario: Driver unaffected
- **WHEN** an actor occupies seat zero
- **THEN** the existing steering pose is used
