# The card states the length of the beat

## Why
Pacing is the missions loop's next target that tests can prove without Unreal. A beat still played as a sentence and a diamond. Nothing said which stop this was, or how long the next leg was.

## What changes
- While a story beat or the terrace is open, the mission card adds "N of M".
- When the next stop is a walk, it also adds about how many seconds that leg takes at walking speed.
- The ovation's either-order choice and a rescue group do not invent a single walk. Parking the car and releasing the table are not walks. The race keeps its own clock.

## Out of scope
A new mission, a new save field, stream latency, and a new art technique.
