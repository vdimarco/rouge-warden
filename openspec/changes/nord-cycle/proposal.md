# A park bike east of the spawn

## Why
Every ride is a car or the pond pedalo. The dry park east of the spawn has nowhere to pedal.

## What changes
- One bike waits at a stand in that park.
- On land it tops out at 900 cm/s, faster than a walk and slower than a street car, and reverses at 200 cm/s.
- In the water it only creeps at 90 cm/s.
- Letting go coasts to a stop. It does not handbrake-slide, and a bump does not dent it.
- E beside it boards it. E aboard steps off. Cars and the pedalo keep the models they already have.

## Out of scope
A new mesh, a ninth story mission, a save field, a phone button, and a change to car handling.
