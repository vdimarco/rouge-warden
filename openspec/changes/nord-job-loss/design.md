# One sting on a miss

No new rendering technique is involved, so none of the usual art references fit. The cue is a short wave, the same kind of synthesized sting the rank, the lock chirp, and the dent thud already use.

Every side-job failure already ends in `FinishJob` with success false. The server asks each player's client to play the sting from that one branch. A success keeps the complete cue and does not ask. Story-mission misses stay on their own cues. The wave is a fall from 310 Hz to 118 Hz over a fifth of a second, with a little noise under a slow decay, quieter than the thud's peak. It does not sit on the pitches the city already plays, and a sample-by-sample gap keeps it off the thud. The v1 save is untouched. UpdateMusic and the three feedback sounds are untouched.
