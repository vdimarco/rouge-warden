# A sting when a side job fails

## Why
A finished job pays cash and plays the complete cue. A failed job sets the banner and the retry window, and plays nothing.

## What changes
- A failed side job plays one short falling sting for every player in the session.
- A success still plays the complete cue and does not play the sting.
- The sting covers every failure that already ends the job: abandoned, arrested, knocked out, the clock, a crushed delivery, a lost thief, goons that never came, leaving the theatre, and a passenger who got out.
- The wave falls from 310 Hz to 118 Hz. It stays off the rank pitches, the lock chirp, the pond tick, the court tones, the theatre page, and the dent thud.
- The music path and the three feedback sounds stay as they are.

## Out of scope
A new engine loop, stream latency, a ninth story mission, a phone button, a story-mission miss cue, and a change to the rank pitches, the chirp, or the thud.
