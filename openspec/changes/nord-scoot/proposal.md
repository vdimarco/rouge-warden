# A kick scooter on the east lawn

## Why
The park has a pedalo and a bike. A walker who wants something quicker than a walk, and slower than the bike, still has nothing to kick.

## What changes
- One kick scooter waits on the dry lawn east of the ponds.
- It tops out faster than a walk and slower than the bike. In the water it only creeps.
- It does not take body damage, and it does not count as the bike for the loop, the caisse, or mission 9 and 10.
- The v1 save is untouched. This is not a story mission.

## Out of scope
A new mesh, a save field, a phone button, a chain link, and a change to the bike or the pedalo.
