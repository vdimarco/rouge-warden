# Between a walk and the bike

No new rendering technique is involved, so none of the usual art references fit. The scooter uses the car body the bike already uses. The new part is the kick: 620 cm/s on land, 60 cm/s in the water, and a sharper turn than the bike while it is slow.

The stand is (28000, 8000). That lawn is dry, about 44 m from the nearest mapped road centre, and about 138 m from the bike stand. A parked car and a traffic lane both stay more than a car length away. The tailpipe stays on ride kind 0, so the scooter does not grow one.

Nothing is saved, and the campaign phase list is not extended.
