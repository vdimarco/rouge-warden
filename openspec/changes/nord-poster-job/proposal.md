# A fifth side job pastes three posters

## Why
Fun is a 7 the rules can prove. The four side jobs are a delivery, a chase, a fight, and an escape. None of them is a tour you can take in any order.

## What changes
- A fifth marker, Trois affiches, stands on rue Gauthier near Avenue Papineau.
- Three corners, Papineau, Cartier, and Dorion, can be pasted in any order.
- Pasting a corner twice does not count twice.
- The clock is two minutes and does not grow.
- The pay is 350. The other four pays stay as they were.

## Out of scope
A ninth story mission, a cash shop, stream latency, and a new art technique.
