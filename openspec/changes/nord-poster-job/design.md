# Any order, on one street

No new rendering technique is involved, so none of the usual art references fit. The marker and the goal post reuse the yellow discs the other jobs already build.

The corners are the surveyed crossings of rue Gauthier with Avenue Papineau, rue Cartier, and rue Dorion, shifted one sidewalk off the center of the crossing. The marker sits on that sidewalk, short of the first corner, so standing on the M does not paste a poster. The nearest unfinished corner is the one the card names. The v1 slot still writes only Version and CompletedMissions.
