# A pedal boat in the north pond

## Why
Every vehicle is a car, and the pond is only for swimming. The bridge across the south narrows has open water on either side and nowhere to paddle.

## What changes
- One pedalo waits in the channel beside that bridge.
- On the water it tops out at 650 cm/s and reverses at 220 cm/s.
- Off the water it only creeps at 140 cm/s, so it can leave the berth and cannot tour the streets.
- Letting go coasts to a stop. It does not handbrake-slide, and a bump does not dent it.
- E on the deck boards it. E aboard steps ashore. Cars keep the driving model they already have.

## Out of scope
A new mesh, a ninth story mission, a save field, and a change to car handling.
