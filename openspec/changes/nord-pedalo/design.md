# One boat, and the cars stay cars

No new rendering technique is involved, so none of the usual art references fit. The pedalo uses the car body that is already in the world. The new part is the paddle rule, the same kind of engine-free number the walk speed and the fare already use.

The berth is 280 cm east of the footbridge, inside the channel and inside the 450 cm door reach of the deck. The rail would block a normal enter trace, so boarding a pedalo does not require that trace. On the water the paddle tops out at 650 cm/s. Off the water the same hold only creeps, which is how it leaves the berth and why it is not a street car. It does not use the handbrake slide, and body damage does not apply, so a brush with the bank does not wreck it. The v1 save is untouched. Car handling is the branch this rule does not take.
