# COMBAT notes: weapons, pickups, damage, K.-O. and respawn

## What was built

### Pure rules (engine-free, tested here)
- `Source/Quebec/QuebecAimModel.h` (`namespace QuebecAim`): `Choose` picks the target for an aim. A new lock must sit inside a 25° planar half-angle and the weapon range. The previous lock stays preferred inside 35° and 1.15× range for 4 s after it was acquired (the GTA V lock timer); a fresh choice of the same target restarts the timer. Down targets are never chosen; targets more than 45° above or below are ignored. Score = angle/25° + distance/range + penalty − threat bonus (0.35). Cars carry a 0.45 penalty, so people win close calls; police chasing the shooter and goons win close calls over civilians. Only the planar part of the aim counts, because the camera always looks down (pitch −25…−5).
- `Source/Quebec/QuebecCombatModel.h` (`namespace QuebecCombatModel`): the weapon table in `EQuebecWeapon` order (fists 12 dmg/1.6 m/0.45 s; hockey stick 30/2.2 m/0.7 s; pistol 25/0.35 s/12 rounds/45 m; SMG 12/0.09 s/40 rounds/35 m, automatic; shotgun 6×10/0.9 s/8 shells/18 m), shotgun pellets that land by distance (6 point blank, 4 at half range, 2 at full range), armour absorbs first (`Absorb`), the fire gate with 30 ms server slack, ammo caps (4 pickups), pickup kinds, the 10 pickup spots, the QA anchors and the two respawn points.
- Test: `Scripts/aim_model_test.cpp` + `Scripts/test_aim_model.py`, 335 checks, built with c++, g++ and clang++ under `-std=c++20 -Wall -Wextra -Wshadow -Wconversion -Wdouble-promotion -Werror`, plus a pass with the Unreal macro names poisoned.

### Unreal code
- `QuebecCombat.h/.cpp`
  - The `QuebecCombat` contract from `QuebecGTA.h`: `State` (player state of a pawn, controller or player state; a driver's character finds it through the car), `DamagePlayer` (armour first; at zero health K.-O.), `Respawn` (hospital stop after K.-O., Poste de quartier 38 after an arrest; ground trace plus capsule check with two rings of fallbacks, then the district spawn; exits a car without the door check; `EndKnock`; `ResetForRespawn`, which keeps cash).
  - Helpers: `WeaponName`, `PickupName` (Québec French), `RespawnPoint`, `GrantPickup`, `NextOwnedWeapon`, `SpawnPickups`, `BuildWeaponModel`.
  - `AQuebecWeaponPickup`: replicated, always relevant, no collision. The model spins and bobs over a ring of 16 glowing tiles (one instanced static mesh), only while it was rendered in the last 0.25 s and is within 60 m of the local camera. No pickup or held-weapon part casts a shadow. The server checks walking players every 0.1 s within 1.5 m, gives the item, hides it and brings it back after 60 s. A first weapon is equipped; an owned stick or a full gun, full armour and full health are refused, so the pickup stays for someone who needs it. A banner names the item.
  - `AQuebecCombatFX`: a local, unreplicated effect per shot or hit, taken from a per-world pool (idle effects are hidden with their tick off and reused; at most 32 exist): tracers (stretched basic-shape cylinders that thin out over 0.12 s; six spread tracers for the shotgun), a muzzle flash (sphere plus a point light for 60 ms, no shadows), the comic word (PAN!, RA-TA-TA! once per burst, BOUM!, POW!, CLAC!) that pops, rises and faces the local camera, and a procedural bang (24 kHz `USoundWaveProcedural`: a swept decaying low tone plus filtered noise, peaks near 0.3, no new asset). The six bang buffers (five weapons and a bullet thud) are synthesised once and reused; each pooled effect rewinds its one procedural wave, and an effect that goes idle stops its voice and empties its wave. The materials are loaded once in the constructor. Past 120 m from the local camera only the bang plays; past 150 m nothing does, and the volume falls to zero at 150 m. The word pops with its transform scale, so the text mesh is built once per word. POLICE reuses `PlayShot`/`PlayImpact` for officers.
- `AQuebecCharacter` combat members (block at the end of the class in `QuebecGameplay.h`, bodies in `QuebecCombat.cpp`): inputs `StartFire`/`StopFire`/`StartAim`/`StopAim`/`CycleWeapon`/`TurnView`; `ServerFire` (reliable, once per press; an automatic weapon keeps firing on the server until the reliable `ServerStopFire`, so a burst costs two calls; only held automatic fire carries its cadence from shot to shot, and every other press waits a full interval after the last shot, so a double click cannot fire twice; client sends origin and aim; the server sanitises NaN, ignores an origin more than 3 m away, rate-limits, spends ammo, picks the target, traces line of sight on `ECC_Visibility` ignoring shooter and target, applies `QuebecPeople::Hit` or `AQuebecCar::AddBodyDamage` (20% of weapon damage), reports `Gunfire` at most every 3 s when a civilian (not a player, officer or goon) is within 30 m and the shot was not aimed at a hostile (a goon, or police already chasing the shooter; the check is taken before the hit, because a goon knocked down by that shot is no longer hostile; a civilian actually hit is still reported by `QuebecPeople::Hit`), and calls `QuebecPeople::Alarm` within 25 m at most once a second); melee snaps the body up to 90° toward the target and lands 0.15 s (fists) or 0.22 s (stick) later if the target is still within reach + 1 m and in sight; `ServerSetAim`; `ServerCycleWeapon(Steps)` (wheel notches are gathered on the client and sent at most every 0.15 s); multicasts `MulticastShot`, `MulticastSwing`, `MulticastImpact`. An empty gun is put away for the next owned weapon. Aim assist gives hostile police a smaller bonus than goons (penalty 0.2), so goons win close calls with them. Getting into or out of a car releases a held Fire or Aim on both peers (`OnRep_InVehicle` calls `ReleaseCombatInput`).
  - Aim (right mouse): the body turns with the camera (`bUseControllerRotationYaw`), the arm moves to 260 cm with a 70 cm right-shoulder offset, and mouse turn runs at 55%. `KeepCharacterInView` still clamps the pitch to −25…−5. While aiming the server refreshes the lock at 5 Hz and writes `AQuebecPlayerState::LockedTarget` for the HUD reticle; the lock clears 1 s after the player stops aiming and firing, or when the target goes down.
  - Held weapon: basic-shape model on a pivot attached to the crew mesh's `RightHand` socket (the plunger already uses it); if the mesh has no such socket it sits at the hip on the capsule. The pivot takes its rotation from the body, because hand-bone axes differ between rigs. The crew's plunger/tube hides while a weapon is held (`SetVisibility`, separate from swimming's `SetHiddenInGame`). The stick swings, guns kick 14° per shot, fists throw a cream glove sphere out from the chest.
  - Firing is off while driving (the car is possessed), swimming, knocked, down or input-locked (`CanUseWeapons`). Other players are never auto-targeted: the game is co-op.
  - K.-O. at zero health: `DownState = KnockedOut`, `DownUntil = server time + 4 s`, `StartKnock(push, 4 s, bStayDown = true)`, banner "K.-O. / Retour à l'Hôpital Notre-Dame". A player in a car takes half of each hit passed to `DamagePlayer` (an officer's shot, a goon's ink), and the car body takes 60% of it through `AddBodyDamage`. A driver who goes down is put out beside the car first, and the controller's `WalkingCharacter` is cleared as `ExitCar` does (`AQuebecController::ForgetWalkingCharacter`). The character's tick respawns the player when `DownUntil` passes (also for an arrest, so either side can finish it; POLICE also calls `Respawn`). The pawn is never destroyed and stays possessed.
- `QuebecGameplay.cpp` (small, marked lines): includes; `ReleasePawnInput` calls `ReleaseCombatInput`; the character's `Turn` axis goes through `TurnView`; Fire/Aim/NextWeapon bindings; `TickCombat` in `AQuebecCharacter::Tick`; `PlayerStateClass = AQuebecPlayerState::StaticClass()`; `QuebecCombat::SpawnPickups` in `AQuebecGameMode::BeginPlay` after the Toronto return and not under `-NordNoCampaign`. Pickups spawn no cars and have no collision, so population placement and its counts do not change.
- `Config/DefaultInput.ini`: `Fire` = LeftMouseButton, F; `Aim` = RightMouseButton; `NextWeapon` = Q, MouseScrollDown. None was mapped before (Q, F, mouse buttons and the wheel were free).

## Sources
- Aim assist: Liu, Xu & Claypool's latency-compensation taxonomy ("control assistance"); The Astronauts' Witchfire aim-assist write-up (nested cones, magnetism); Rockstar's GTA V lock timer ("every aim mode now has a timer that breaks lock"). Adapted into the planar cone with hysteresis above.
- Comic words and tracers: the project's existing sound-word HUD and `M_ComicInk` look, with original Québec-French words; Spider-Verse-style onomatopoeia as the reference (research-gameplay.md §6). A Niagara beam is the usual technique; it needs an authored system the cloud cannot make, so stretched basic-shape cylinders stand in.
- Procedural bang: the car engine's `USoundWaveProcedural` pattern in `AQuebecCar::UpdateDriveAudio`.
