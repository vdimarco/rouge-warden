# JOBS notes: side jobs, the street HUD and notices

## What was built

### Rules and places: `Source/Quebec/QuebecJobRules.h`
An engine-free header (`namespace QuebecJobRules`), compiled here by `Scripts/test_job_rules.py` with c++, g++ and clang++ under `-std=c++20 -Wall -Wextra -Wshadow -Wconversion -Wdouble-promotion -Werror`, plus a pass with the Unreal macro names poisoned. It holds every job point, the time limits, rewards, wave sizes, the arrival rule, the thief's road choice and speed, the ram bonus and the escape rule.

Places come from OSM centrelines in `Data/LaFontaine/osm.xml`, projected with the generator's formula and moved onto sidewalks or park paths. Z comes from a ground trace at runtime (static geometry only).

### Side-job actor: `QuebecSideJobs.h/.cpp`
- `AQuebecSideJobs` (not a campaign subclass, `NotPlaceable`, replicated, always relevant, 10 Hz). `AQuebecGameMode::BeginPlay` spawns it after the campaign block, so it is after `if (bToronto) return;`, and skips it under `-NordNoCampaign` or `-NordNoSideJobs`. It spawns no cars at BeginPlay, so population placement and counts are unchanged.
- Replicated state: `JobIndex`, `JobState` (Idle/Running/Succeeded/Failed), `JobStep`, `StartedAt`, `DeadlineTime` (server time; clients subtract their own server-time reading), `ObjectiveText`, `TargetLocation`, `bHasTarget`, `JobProgress`, `StarterState`, `TargetActor`, `ResultSerial`, `LastJob`, `bLastSucceeded`, `LastReward`, `LastResult`, `CompletedMask`, `RetryUntil`. The thief job writes `TargetLocation` at 4 Hz only; clients follow `TargetActor`. The goal caption calls `SetText` only when its label changes.
- Markers on every peer: a yellow disc (`M_Cream` with a yellow `Tint`, `M_Gold` fallback), a bobbing halo, a floating `M` and the job name (ASCII captions: the text-render font has no accented glyphs). They hide while a job runs. A goal disc, a 9 m column and a caption mark the café, door, theatre or garage; a paper bag waits at the café; `VOLEUR` floats over the thief's car every frame.
- E: `ServerInteract` calls `HandleInteract` after car entry/exit and the campaign. A walker within 2.5 m of a marker with line of sight starts the job. A running job, the campaign race or worklight aiming refuse with a notice. One job at a time, shared by both players; if the starter leaves, the other player inherits it. In the escape, the heir becomes wanted at `max(current, 3)` stars and must lose them again; stars the first starter lost never pay out.
- R: `ServerRetryCampaign` asks `QuebecJobs::HandleRecover` first. While a job runs, and for 30 s after MISSION RATÉE (`RetryUntil`), R moves the requesting player to the job's marker (a driver to the lane beside it, with ground and clearance checks), clears what the job spawned and starts that job again. The failure banner adds "R réessaie" on keyboard screens. During the campaign race or while that player aims the worklight, R belongs to the campaign, as E does. R from a player who is down, or who stands within 2.5 m of a police officer on its feet while wanted (the dispatcher's arrest hold is private, so this stands in for it), is taken and ignored by `QuebecJobs::HandleRecover` itself, with or without a job and under `-NordNoSideJobs`, so the campaign checkpoint teleport cannot pull such a player away either. A standing, unwanted player's R goes to the campaign as before when no job claims it. (`QuebecGTA.h` still describes `HandleRecover` as "true when an active side job handled the R key"; it now also returns true for a held player.)
- Fail conditions on every job: time out, any player's new K.-O. or arrest (a player already down when the job began does not count until they stand up), no player left. Delivery also fails when the bag carrier's car is wrecked. The thief job fails when the thief gets 300 m from every player for 10 s. The goon job fails when the starter stays 45 m from the arena for 10 s.
- Jobs:
  - Delivery: reach the café (on foot within 3 m, or a car within 5.5 m below 800 cm/s), then the three doors in order; only the player who took the bag delivers, and only that player's own arrival is tested, so a partner at the door never hides it. If the carrier leaves the session, anyone delivers. Each door adds 20 s.
  - Thief: an `AQuebecCar` tagged `SideJobTarget`, painted, always relevant, driven with `SetAutopilot`. It follows the pruned surveyed road graph (no dead ends); at each road end it takes the onward road whose far end is farthest from the players, preferring straight, never a U-turn. It speeds up near a chaser, waits for a distant one, slows for corners and backs out when pinned. A ram from a player's car (velocity change of 450 cm/s or more within 7 m) adds `10 + Δv/50` damage on top of the car's own impact damage. `AQuebecCar::OnCarImpact` (`QuebecDrive.cpp`, marked JOBS) skips the crime report for a car tagged `SideJobTarget`, so ramming the thief gives no stars. `IsWrecked()` wins; so does taking the car. A walker gets a loaner (`SideJobLoan`) at the curb unless a free car waits within 30 m.
  - Goons: waves of 3, 4 and 5 `AQuebecOfficer::SpawnOfficer(..., FactionGoon)` tagged `SideJobGoon`, retargeted each second at the nearest player's current pawn. A downed or removed goon counts. The next wave comes 2.5 s after a wave is down. Every standing player without a gun gets a pistol with 36 rounds at the start (and on R); a partner who is down then gets it on standing up, since the respawn would take it away.
  - Escape: stars are set to `max(current, 3)` at the start and on R, so a more wanted player keeps the higher level. The job wins when the stars clear: the police lose the player, or the police garage clears them, which it does only for a car that no unit sees. Only without a police dispatcher (stars never show) does a car in the garage bay win directly.
- Success pays every player state in the session (`Cash`), plays `Complete` and bumps `ResultSerial`. Steps play `Checkpoint`; starts play `Dialogue`. No new feedback sounds.
- Clean-up on every end and retry: every goon at once. The thief's car stops (kept if a player took it) and the empty loaner waits; each is removed only once no player can see it (more than 150 m away, or out of every view cone and more than 25 m away). For a remote driver, who sends the server no camera updates, the view is the chase camera behind the car (7 m back, 2.5 m up) with a wider cone (cosine 0.17), the same rule as `AQuebecPoliceDispatch::InAnyView`.
- Nothing is saved. Cash and completed jobs last for the session, so no new `.sav` appears and the campaign save is untouched.
- `QuebecJobs::IsJobActive` guards race gate 0 in `AQuebecCampaign::TickRace`: while a job runs and the phase is `RaceReady`, no crossing starts the race. Car positions still update, so a stale position cannot fake a crossing later.
- Logs: `NORD_SIDEJOB ready|start|wave|retry|end`.

### Notices and street HUD: `QuebecGameHUD.cpp`
- `QuebecNotice::Show` sends `AQuebecController::ClientGameNotice(Title, Detail, Seconds)`, a reliable client RPC that sets `GameNoticeTitle/Detail/Start/Until`. It never touches `ResidentCaption`. The sender drops the same notice within 1.5 s. A different notice that arrives while one is under a second old waits and shows after it (`TickGameNotice`).
- `AQuebecHUD::DrawHUD` gains three small hooks: the street prompt before the resident prompt; with `-NordTouchControls` the mission card moves left by `StreetColumnWidth()` (158 units, only while the column shows); and one `DrawStreetHUD(...)` call at the end.
- Status column, only while something is happening (stars, search, a job, a wound, armour, a weapon, or cash that changed in the last 6 s), so it never shows on a calm street:
  - Landscape (desktop and touch): right-aligned at the radar's left edge − 8 units, from the radar's top, 150 units wide. At 1280×720 that is x 940–1090, y 96 to at most about 300. Touch frames keep it clear of the action grid (from y ≈ 352 units) and the combat buttons (from y ≈ 500 units at 844×390 and 667×375).
  - Portrait: right-aligned under `Right`, radar-wide, ending 6 units above the touch area (60 % of the height) and growing upward.
  - Rows: five stars (filled by scanlines, so no texture; they blink at 2 Hz while searching and pop on a change), weapon and ammo (`QuebecCombat::WeaponName`), health bar (red and pulsing under 25), armour bar, job timer (red and pulsing under 10 s), job title, objective (three lines at most), distance with thief damage or goons down, and cash. Floors at phone scale: stars at least 12 px, bars at least 4 px.
- Centre banners, one at a time: the K.-O. / ARRÊTÉ! overlay (dim, ink burst, respawn place and countdown from `DownUntil`), then MISSION RÉUSSIE / MISSION RATÉE for 4 s on `ResultSerial` (a player who is down when the job ends sees it once the overlay is over), then the newest notice, then a RECHERCHÉ plate only for a star rise the police left silent. Notices are hidden while the player is down, so the overlay and COMBAT's or POLICE's K.-O./ARRÊTÉ notices never stack.
- Aim reticle: four red brackets turning over `QuebecPeople::AimPoint(LockedTarget)`.
- Radar blips, in DrawHUD's existing `MapPoint` transform (orientation unchanged): police cars and officers flash red/blue at 4 Hz, goons are black with a white rim, available pickups are white dots within 60 m, job markers are yellow dots within 100 m, and only the running job's target is held on the rim as a pulsing yellow dot. Every QA fixture and the race route are more than 60 m from every pickup and more than 100 m from every marker, so the calm radar in the story, CompactHUD and camera-touch captures should match the accepted baselines; the host should confirm this when it reviews those captures. A screen marker (yellow chevron and distance) sits over the job target.
- A hit flashes the screen edges red for 0.3 s.

### Tests
- `Scripts/job_rules_test.cpp` + `Scripts/test_job_rules.py` (run here): placement and clearance, bounds, route length against the time limit, timers with a client clock offset, arrival rule, waves, the thief's road choice and speed, ram damage, the escape rule.
- `Source/Quebec/QuebecJobsTests.cpp` (host only):
  - `Quebec.Jobs.MarkerClearance`: every job point against the live campaign anchors, the legacy objectives, spawn, car and walk start; the table's race gates against the campaign's; the escape garage against `QuebecPolice::GarageLocation()`.
  - `Quebec.Jobs.TimersAndRetry`: idle R stays with the campaign; start, server-time countdown, R restart with full time; timeout failure and result serial; R within 30 s of MISSION RATÉE restarts the failed job, after 30 s it goes back to the campaign; K.-O. failure, R ignored while down, also with no job and no retry window; a partner's new arrest fails the shared job while one already down at the start does not; pistols for every unarmed player, a downed partner armed only on standing up; success bookkeeping.
  - `Quebec.Jobs.Cleanup`: no cars from the actor itself; the goons are removed when the job ends; the thief's tagged autopilot car is removed once out of sight after the job ends or restarts.
  - `Quebec.Jobs.NoticeChannel`: notices never touch `ResidentCaption`; queueing, promotion and the sender's repeat filter.
