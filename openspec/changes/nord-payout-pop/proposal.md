# Show a story purse

## Why

A banked beat pays, and the only mark is a number on the player. The play page never says the amount, and it never plays a cue for it.

## What changes

A story purse shows a popup with the amount and the word CHING for a few seconds. The play page plays two short notes with that popup. The same popup can be shown with no host, so a frame can be checked. The purse is not saved. No new mission is added, and the three feedback sounds stay three.

## Impact

- Affected specs: nord-payout-pop
- Affected code: the story purse, the play shell popup, and the stream message
