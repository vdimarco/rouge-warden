## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The opening shot includes the nearby surveyed lamps
The opening shade SHALL draw the park furniture within 180 m of the spawn. That set SHALL include a row of street lamps whose heads fall in the frame, between the trees. The opening pitch of −16, the avenue aim, and the walking socket at Y 70 and Z 110 SHALL stay. The pawn SHALL stay where it is.

#### Scenario: Lamp heads line the avenue
- **WHEN** the opening walk camera looks down the avenue from the spawn
- **THEN** at least eight surveyed lamp heads within 180 m sit in the frame, above the road

#### Scenario: The camera stays
- **WHEN** that furniture is drawn
- **THEN** the pitch, the aim, the socket, and the pawn location are unchanged
