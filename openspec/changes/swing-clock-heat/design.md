# One urgent card

Marvel's Spider-Man 2 (Insomniac, 2023) uses one urgent treatment when you are losing the objective, whether the clock, the person, or the thing in the air is the thing getting away. None of the grass, water, outline, or painted-look sources fit. This game already has that treatment: the warm fill and magenta edge on a chase that is pulling away.

## Decisions

- The opening clock is stored when the job starts, after a story rescue has been given time to arrive. The card heats when that opening was at least 12 seconds and the time left is at most 8 seconds or a quarter of the opening, whichever is less. A 3.5 second edge and a 1.2 second slip never heat from the clock, so they are not hot on the first frame.
- The crew's arrival raises the clock to at least 40 seconds and raises the stored opening to match when the new clock is longer. A fresh 40 seconds is not already hot.
- The falling line and a free balloon at 140 m set the same hot flag. 140 m is 30 m under the height where the balloon is gone. The win card and the clog card are not built by the job card, and they do not set the flag.
- The pill repaints when only the hot flag changes. Pulling away still heats the pill from the line, including when the flag is set from that same line.
- The washer's 36 seconds, the rescue's fall rate, and the balloon's slip stay as they are. This round does not add a word or a sound.
