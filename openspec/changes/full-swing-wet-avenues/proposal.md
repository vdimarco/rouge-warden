# Wet avenues

In Full Swing draws its streets and the expressway as flat cel asphalt. Set next to Marvel's Spider-Man 2, the ground is the part of the picture that least reads as a city you could swing over: no darkening where the road is wet, no sky in the pavement, no streak of sun down the avenue.

## Why

A low sun on a city street shows up first in the road. Spider-Man 2's avenues are dark, fresnel-wet, and streaked. This game's roads are one violet tone with lane paint. The buildings, the painted sky, and the ink lines already carry the comic look. The road does not carry any light.

## What changes

- Asphalt (streets, intersections, the expressway deck) darkens in puddle patches and mirrors the game's own sky, stronger at a grazing angle and stronger in the puddles.
- The highlight stretches along the view, so it runs down the avenue whichever way the street points.
- Lit road gets a warm sun glint. Shaded road gets a small warm fill, as if windows bounced.
- Lane paint, kerbs, sidewalks, parks, medians, and the boardwalk stay matte. Alleys are only a little damp.
- Ink lines still draw on top. No new textures and no art from another game.

## Out of scope

- Screen-space reflections of buildings and neon. The mirror is the sky strip only.
- Gameplay, the hero, traffic, and the headset's controls.

## Capabilities

- `swing-wet-avenues`: wet asphalt on streets and the expressway, matte sidewalks, ink preserved.
