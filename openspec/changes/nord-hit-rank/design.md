# One word on the bank

No new rendering technique is involved, so none of the usual art references fit. The word comes from the chain after the bank, and from whether that bank was spoiled.

A spoil reads SCRAPE even when a chain was held. A clean bank reads CLEAN, CHAIN, HEAT, or MAX from the chain it leaves behind. The lap line and the story line both append that word after the pay. The chain rule's pay is unchanged, and the v1 slot still writes only Version and CompletedMissions.
