# A bank names its rank

## Why
Feedback is a 6 the rules can prove. A clean bank and a spoiled bank both end on the pay and the chain count. They do not say how hard the hit was.

## What changes
- A spoiled bank reads SCRAPE.
- A clean bank reads CLEAN at chain 1, CHAIN at 2 or 3, HEAT at 4, 5, or 6, and MAX at 7.
- The lap and the story banks show that word with the pay.
- The pay itself does not change.

## Out of scope
A ninth mission, a new payout, a new sound, stream latency, and a new art technique.
