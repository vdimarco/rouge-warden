## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Ditching the chase car

After the march is banked, the ditch is open on a surveyed road. A real car crossing the line while rolling forward, nose toward the far end, starts a chase car that drives the road and does not steer. Beating that car to the end, then reaching the clear mark on foot, banks the beat and pays 660 once. A stop, a reverse, a sideways nose, and a walker do not start it. A crawl is caught, the line is the line again, and the beat pays nothing. A car at the clear mark does not bank it.

#### Scenario: A drive beats them and the player gets out

- **WHEN** the march is banked and a real car crosses the line rolling forward, stays ahead to the end, and the player then reaches the clear mark on foot
- **THEN** the beat banks and pays 660 once

#### Scenario: A crawl is caught

- **WHEN** the chase has started and the car crawls toward the end
- **THEN** the chase car catches it before the end, the step returns to the line, and the beat pays nothing

#### Scenario: A car at the clear mark

- **WHEN** the chase has been beaten and a car stands on the clear mark
- **THEN** the beat stays open and pays nothing

#### Scenario: Recover during the ditch

- **WHEN** the player recovers while the ditch is open
- **THEN** they return behind the line and the step stays
