# Ditch the chase car

## Why

The tail follows you to a hide. The pursuit is catching a car that keeps its lane. The slip is a sprint on foot. Nothing asks you to roll a car across a line, stay ahead of a chase car that only drives the road, and then leave the car where they will not follow.

## What changes

After the march is banked, a line sits on a surveyed road north of the stall. A real car crossing it, still rolling forward at 500 cm/s or more, with its nose toward the far end, starts a chase car at the near end. That car drives the road at 900 cm/s and does not steer. Beat it to the end. A crawl is caught and the line is the line again, and that catch does not save. At the end, get out. A mark 20 m off the road banks the beat and pays 660 once. A car at that mark does not. They stop at the end of the road. The step is saved on version 19. Version 18 starts this beat over. The seven story banks stay seven. The tail, the pursuit, the stall, the slip, and the march are not retuned.

## Impact

- Affected specs: nord-ditch, nord-unreal
- Affected code: `QuebecDitch.h`, `QuebecStorySave.h`, `QuebecCampaign`, the ditch tests, and the version-19 save readers
