# Proposal: slides that chain

Round 87 grades each slide when it ends: SLIDE, BIG SLIDE, or HUGE SLIDE. A driver who slides corner after corner still sees each slide on its own. This round counts slides that come close together. A slide that pays within 5 s of the last pay adds the count to its grade, for example BIG SLIDE x2, up to x9. A longer gap starts over.

## Out of scope

No cash is paid for a slide. The grades, the end-of-slide timing, the detail line, the tire scream, and the notice channel stay as they are.
