# Proposal: slides that chain

Every slide showed the same SLIDE banner. A driver who slid corner after corner saw nothing more than a driver who slid once. This round counts slides that come close together. A slide that pays within 5 s of the last pay reads SLIDE x2, then x3, up to x9. A pause longer than that starts over at SLIDE.

## Out of scope

No cash is paid for a slide. The slide rule, the notes, the lock chirp, and the notice channel stay as they are.
