# Mission 6: Nobody Left in the Picture

## Why
Fun is the missions loop's next target, and the story still stops at mission 5 of 8. After the curtain, the card drops to side jobs while three groups are still in the pictures.

## What changes
- After the show is banked, the mission card and the gold diamond lead through three short pulls, in any order, on dry ground at the terrace, the north pond and the court.
- Freeing one group does not clear the others. Repeating a freed group does not rewind. The last group plays the existing complete cue. The v1 save still stores CompletedMissions 0–2.
- Side jobs and T wait until every group is out. R restores the current group and does not clear a group that is already out. The terrace recover steps east, away from the water. The pond and the court recover step south.

## Out of scope
NPC escorts, the missing-person walk, missions 7–8, a new save field, a new art technique, a new procedural tone, and stream latency.
