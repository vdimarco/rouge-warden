# Rock the on-foot body when the crew rig is missing

Animation smoothness is the aesthetics score a picture can still move. The crew blend space cannot be posed offline. This round adds a procedural sway: while walking, the stand-in leans up to 12° and shifts 4 cm to the side; while idle, the same motion is about a third of that. The feet do not step. The sway does not run while the crew anim instance, a swim stroke, a knockdown, or a fall owns the mesh. The opening camera stays. No reference-game assets.
