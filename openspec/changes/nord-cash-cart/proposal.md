# A stall spends the cash the jobs already pay

## Why
Fun is an 8 the rules can still lift. Side jobs and story banks pay session cash, and nothing in the district takes it.

## What changes
- Chez Lise stands on the Rue Sherbrooke Est sidewalk, 40 m south of the spawn.
- On foot, E buys one thing: a kit for 80 if you are hurt, else a vest for 120, else one wanted star for 200.
- A short purse buys nothing. A driver and a downed player buy nothing.
- One star is not for sale while Cinq étoiles is running. A kit still is.
- The other pays stay put. The goon pay stays 300.

## Out of scope
A ninth story mission, a second stall, stream latency, and a new art technique.
