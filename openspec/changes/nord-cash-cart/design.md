# One stall, one button

No new rendering technique is involved, so none of the usual art references fit. The stall is engine cubes and a cylinder, painted with the comic materials the job markers already use. The sign is a text render.

The spot is the mapped footway of Rue Sherbrooke Est, 40 m south of the spawn along that sidewalk. It stays more than 8 m from every job point and 15 m from every QA anchor, so E at the stall does not start a job. The v1 slot still writes only Version and CompletedMissions. Cash stays on the player for the session.

The sale calls the existing kit and vest grant. Dropping a star goes through the police star setter, and the cash comes back if that setter does not move the stars. The prompt is the street prompt, not the mission status line.
