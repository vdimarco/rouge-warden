# Postcard blink has a tick

## Why
Sound design is still three one-shots and two music loops. Mission 3 asks the player to watch a blink, and that blink was silent.

## What changes
- While the tag step is open, the local player hears a short original tick each time the decoy beams change state.
- Tagging a blinking beam plays the existing dialogue cue, so a miss does not sound like the blink.

## Out of scope
A new cooked music bed, voice, and the neighborhood and race loops.
