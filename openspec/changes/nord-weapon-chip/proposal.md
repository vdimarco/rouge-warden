# Name the weapon on the play page

## Why

The weapon names are English, and the play page never shows which one is in hand.

## What changes

The play shell shows the current weapon name. Fists, the stick, the pistol, the SMG, and the shotgun keep their English names. Weapon rules do not change. No new mission.

## Impact

- Affected specs: nord-weapon-chip
- Affected code: the weapon name on the stream and the play shell
