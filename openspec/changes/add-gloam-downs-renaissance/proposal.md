# Gloam Downs: the Gilt Lattice

## Why
The user asked: "generate more otherworldly environment with a digital Renaissance theme".

Gloam Downs has five dark places on rolling hills. The user wants more places, with a new look: otherworldly, and digital Renaissance. That is Renaissance architecture and art mixed with digital forms. The Renaissance side is domes, loggias, arcades, the perspective of an ideal city, coffered vaults, marble, gold leaf, sfumato skies, the polyhedra of Pacioli and Leonardo, and armillary spheres. The digital side is floor grids that run to a vanishing point, wireframe solids, voxel breakup, floating fragments and impossible scale.

## What changes
- Three new places for the explorer at `/lab/gloam-downs/` (Wander). Together they are a second set, **the Gilt Lattice**:
  1. **The Vanishing Piazza** (`piazza`): an ideal square of marble. Two arcades close in toward a round temple with a wire dome. The checkered floor turns into a lit grid, and its tiles lift into the air.
  2. **The Broken Cupola** (`cupola`): a huge ribbed dome on a hill at night. One sail of it is broken into floating blocks. A stair climbs a marble rib to the lantern on top.
  3. **The Orrery Isles** (`orrery`): marble islands float over a sea of gold grid lines. Wire polyhedra stand on the grid. A gold armillary sphere floats over the summit isle.
- The places use the same engine and form as the five: Ink Studio splats, the low resolution, the 48-colour palette snap, dither and outlines, chunky forms, rolling ground, a summit landmark that the start can see, five wisps, one beacon and one spawn.
- The Map panel shows two groups: The downs (I to V) and The Gilt Lattice (I to III). The first place of the Lattice is open from the start. Each Lattice beacon opens the next Lattice place.
- The save keeps the Lattice's wisps, beacons and the place where you are, under the explorer's key. The fields of the save do not change.
- The `?place=` address loads a Lattice place when it is open.

## Scope
- The combat run (`?run`) does not change. The new places are not in the run's order, its music, its zones or its save.
- The five places and their baked files do not change.
- The cabinet, the switcher entry and the Corridor piece do not change. Gloam Downs is still one game.
- Names, forms, layouts and pictures are our own. No place copies a named artwork, building, game or artist's piece. Real buildings and books are sources of form only.
