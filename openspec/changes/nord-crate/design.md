# Sidewalk, then the east park

No new rendering technique is involved, so none of the usual art references fit. The caisse and the park post are engine cylinders in the lime and ink the other posts already use.

The caisse is (38004, -12244), 8 m south of Chez Lise. That gap is wider than the stall door plus the caisse reach, and wider than the atelier's car reach. The drop is (60800, -5200), on the dry east side of the pond survey, more than 50 m from the caisse and clear of the shotgun pickup, the portes, the bike marks, and the QA anchors.

Only a walker takes the caisse, and the step remembers who. Only that person, on the bike, banks it at Persillier. The 260 cash is written on that rider's session purse. Nothing is saved, and the campaign phase list is not extended.
