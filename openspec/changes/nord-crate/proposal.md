# A parcel for the east park

## Why
The bike loops the lawn, and mission 9 crosses the pond. Nothing asks a walker to pick something up and pedal it out of the neighbourhood.

## What changes
- A caisse sits on the Sherbrooke sidewalk, eight metres south of Chez Lise.
- One person on foot takes it. That same person finishes on the park bike, at a post in Parc Persillier-Lachapelle.
- The purse is 260 session cash, paid once. A driver does not take it. A walker does not drop it. A later place does not skip. A second pass does not pay.
- While the bike loop is still open, the bike card keeps naming that loop. After it is done, the card names Persillier while the parcel is still aboard.
- The v1 save is untouched. This is not a story mission.

## Out of scope
A new mesh, a save field, an eighth chain link, a phone button, and a change to the bike's pedal speeds.
