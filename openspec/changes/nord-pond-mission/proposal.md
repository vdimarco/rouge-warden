# Mission 3: Still Life, Moving Target

## Why
The story stops after mission 2 of 8. Variety stays low because free roam is the only thing left once the lap is banked.

## What changes
- After the race is banked, the mission card and the gold diamond lead through the north-shore pond scene: watch the postcard, tag the beam that never blinks, set the worklight on two locks, sprint the opened path, and pull the picnic clamp.
- The picnic is session state. The v1 save still stores CompletedMissions 0–2. Loading a finished race offers the pond again.
- While the pond is open, side jobs wait. R (Menu · Recover on touch) restores the current step and does not rewind it. T waits until the picnic is banked, then the side-job guide returns.

## Out of scope
Missions 4–8, a new save field, swimming, driving into the water, and a new art technique.
