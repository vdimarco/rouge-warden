# Stay on the postcard

## Why
The steady beam is a tap. The other two blink, and nothing asks you to stay and watch them.

## What changes
- E on the beam that never blinks starts a four-second hold. The clock runs only while you stand in it.
- One second outside pauses it. More than one second outside drops it, and it can be tried again.
- E on a blinking beam still breaks the chain and does not finish the hold.
- The v1 save still stores only Version and CompletedMissions. The banked step stays 6.

## Out of scope
A ninth story mission, a change to the blink period, the convoy wagons, stream latency, and a new art technique.
