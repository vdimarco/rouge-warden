# Prefer the hoodie when the crew rig is missing

Character models are the aesthetics score a picture can still move. The missing-rig lead is the casual t-shirt, and from the opening camera that back is one flat shirt. The packaged hoodie is the same CC0 family and its garment has a hood, sleeve cuffs, and a hem. When `SK_Body` is missing and the hoodie skeleton, idle, and walk all load, the player mesh plays those clips and keeps the imported cloth colours. The casual walk remains when the hoodie does not load. The opening camera, the socket, and the sway amplitudes stay.
