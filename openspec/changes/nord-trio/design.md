# Three posts, one walker

No new rendering technique is involved, so none of the usual art references fit. Each post is an engine cylinder with the comic lime, cream, or ink the atelier already uses. The new part is the order rule.

The posts sit on sidewalks, past the curb, more than 100 m apart, so one reach cannot cover two of them. Only a person on foot advances the step. Porte 1 remembers who tagged it. That person has to reach porte 2, then porte 3. Anyone else at a later post leaves the step where it is. The 240 cash is written on that player's session purse when the step becomes done. Nothing is saved, and the campaign phase list is not extended.
