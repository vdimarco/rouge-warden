# Three sidewalk posts, in order

## Why
The pond laps are one out-and-back each. A walker still has no errand that asks for a second stop, then a third.

## What changes
- Three posts stand on sidewalks east of the park. Each leg is longer than 100 m.
- Walk them in order: porte 1, then porte 2, then porte 3.
- The person who tags porte 1 is the only one who can finish. They are paid 240 session cash once, at porte 3.
- A driver does not count. A later post does not skip the one before it. A second pass does not pay.
- The v1 save is untouched. This is not a story mission.

## Out of scope
A ninth story mission, a save field, a new mesh, and a change to the pedalo lap, the south-pond wade, or car handling.
