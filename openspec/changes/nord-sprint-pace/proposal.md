# The card names a sprint when it is shorter

## Why
Pacing is a 5 the rules can prove. The card says how long the next leg is at a walk. The player can sprint, and that time is shorter on a long leg.

## What changes
- A leg has a sprint time at the player's sprint speed.
- The card names that time when rounding makes it shorter than the walk.
- A short leg that rounds to the same second keeps the walk line alone.
- The walk time stays the one it already was.

## Out of scope
A ninth mission, a change to sprint speed, stream latency, and a new art technique.
