# Show the clock and the weather on the play page

## Why

The radar already prints the hour and the weather, and the play page never shows them.

## What changes

The play shell shows that same line. The clock still floors the hour. The names stay CLEAR, BREEZY, OVERCAST, FOG, DRIZZLE, and RAIN. The sky does not change. No new mission.

## Impact

- Affected specs: nord-sky-plate
- Affected code: the clock line on the radar and the play shell
