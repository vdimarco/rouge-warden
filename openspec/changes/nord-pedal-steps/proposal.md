# The wheel pedal has three steps

## Why
Controls feel is a 6 the tests can prove. The on-foot stick already walks in three steps. The driving wheel still sends full gas or full brake as soon as the thumb leaves one edge.

## What changes
- A short push on the wheel is a third of gas. A longer push is two thirds. The edge stays full gas, on W.
- Brake uses the same three steps, and the edge stays full brake, on S.
- A short brake drops auto gas.
- Full gas from the button or from Auto wins over a lighter wheel step, so the two do not add.
- WASD and the arrow keys stay full lock.

## Out of scope
A change to steering, a ninth mission, stream latency, and a new art technique.
