# The same three edges, on the pedal

No new rendering technique is involved, so none of the usual art references fit. The pedal uses the edges the wheel already uses for steering: 0.18, 0.45 and 0.75.

The short and middle steps are the same forward and back keys as the on-foot stick. Full gas stays W and full brake stays S. The gas button and Auto stay on W. Any brake step turns Auto off. If full gas and a lighter step are both held, only the full step is sent, because the car adds axis values.

The v1 slot still writes only Version and CompletedMissions.
