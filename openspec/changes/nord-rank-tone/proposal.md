# A bank plays its rank

## Why
Sound design is a 6 the rules can prove. A bank already names scrape, clean, chain, heat, or max. The three cooked stings do not change pitch with that word.

## What changes
- A bank that pays plays a short pitch for the rank it just named.
- Scrape, clean, chain, heat, and max are five different pitches.
- A spoil is the scrape pitch even when the chain it leaves is higher.
- The pitches sit off the pond tick, the court whistle, the court rasp, and the theatre page.
- The cooked stings and the music log stay as they were.

## Out of scope
A fourth cooked sting, a change to the race or neighborhood loops, a ninth mission, stream latency, and a new art technique.
