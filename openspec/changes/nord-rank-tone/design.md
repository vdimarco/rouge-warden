# A pitch beside the word

No new rendering technique is involved, so none of the usual art references fit. This is a short tone, not a shader.

The pitch follows the same thresholds as the rank word. A spoil is 160 Hz. A clean bank that leaves a chain under 2 is 392 Hz. A chain of 2 or 3 is 523 Hz. A chain of 4, 5, or 6 is 659 Hz. A chain of 7 is 880 Hz. Those sit off the pond tick at 740 Hz, the court whistle at 980 Hz and its overtone at 1470 Hz, the court rasp at 210 Hz, and the theatre page at 90 Hz and 1400 Hz.

The sting is a tenth of a second, a sine with a 10 ms attack and an exponential decay, the same shape as the pond tick. Playback is a new audio component. It is not one of the three cooked feedback sounds, and it does not log on the music path. The server asks the owning client to play it, and only when the bank actually paid.

The v1 slot still writes only Version and CompletedMissions.
