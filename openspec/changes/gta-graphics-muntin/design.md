# A bar across the dusk glass

A divided-light window does not fit. The picture is the offline raster, and Unreal is not installed.

The bar is a darker quad across the middle of the glass that is already there, six centimetres above the centre and six below. The sash stays ten centimetres past the glass. A wall shorter than five metres stays blank. The opening pitch and the socket stay.

A wet ribbon under the parked car did not separate from the dark ground at this camera, so that street is not the picture.
