# Design

Use the camera-relative x/z and y/z projection explained in the article. Three luminous rings approach the spacecraft. A relative drag on the canvas steers with one thumb; WASD gives desktop control on the left side of the keyboard. Ring crossing uses the same coordinates as rendering. Passing two rings awards one gravity charge on arrival, capped by the normal charge limit. Award points for collected rings so a full charge inventory still benefits. Skipping and missing rings must never block travel. Reduced motion keeps its short calm transition and receives the charge bonus without a speed challenge.

Steering and travel advance in simulation time. Pause must freeze input, movement and effects. Release/cancel/resize/blur clears held steering. Keep ordinary flippers and field controls suspended in transit. Clip the near plane to avoid projection explosions; bound star/ribbon counts for phones. Use a warm ring target with cool plasma trails and a readable center reticle. Keep the destination caption clear of the play area.

Improve dock rails, comet trails and the shot guide with luminous layered ribbons and travelling energy grains. Preserve exact collision/trajectory centerlines and contrast with the ball.
