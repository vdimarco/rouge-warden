# Desktop command bar in Shore of the Ancients

The player asked for a HUD for the buttons at the bottom centre of the screen, like Dota. On a desktop the skills now sit in a thumb fan at the bottom right, the health bar at the bottom left and the item slots at the bottom centre. The eye must move to three corners.

## Scope
- On a mouse-and-keyboard screen at least 1040 px wide and 600 px high, one framed bar at the bottom centre holds the hero portrait and level, the four skills in a row (Q, E, C, R), the health, mana and experience bars under the skills, and the six item slots in a three-by-two grid with the market and quick-buy buttons beside them.
- Each skill keeps its key cap, rank pips, cooldown, lock state and "+" upgrade badge. The "+" badges and the skill point button sit above the row.
- Touch screens and smaller windows keep the thumb fan and the current layout.
- No change to controls, keys, the simulation or the markup.

## Capabilities
- Modify `moba-skills`.
