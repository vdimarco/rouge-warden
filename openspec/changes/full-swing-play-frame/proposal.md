# Play framing

In flat play the chase camera sits on the centre line, so the hero covers the vanishing point of the avenue. Spider-Man 2 keeps the body low and to one side, and the street ahead owns the middle of the frame.

## What changes

The chase camera slides onto the hero's right shoulder and a little higher. The arm length and the pitch stay, so the settle tests still see the same chase. The slide stops at a wall and eases away in first person. No new art.

## Capabilities

- `swing-play-frame`: in third person the hero sits left of centre and the avenue ahead holds the middle of the frame.
