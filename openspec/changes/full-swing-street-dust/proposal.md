# Street dust

The 18 m layer is the sky's violet, pulled a little darker. Looking down the avenue, the far blocks sink into the same colour as the sky behind them. The near walls already took a teal in the last round, so the air and the wall are one family again.

## What changes

Level views of that short layer take the avenue's warm light: a dusty rose-brown in the air between the buildings. Looking up, the haze stays the sky. The four print steps stay. The grade that paints walls teal sits on the surface, so it does not repaint the dust. No full-screen pass.

## Capabilities

- `swing-street-dust`: the far avenue sinks into warm dust, and the roofs and the sky above it do not.
