# A chain on the story banks

## Why
Fun is a 4 the rules can prove. Every story beat pays the same whether you bank it clean or miss the window. Later beats are not worth more than the first.

## What changes
- Seven story banks pay cash. The base rises from the terrace to the last page.
- A clean bank pays that base times the chain already held, then the chain grows by one.
- A miss spoils the open beat. It pays the flat base, and the chain starts over.
- The card names the stake. A bank names the pay. The cash is shared, the same way a side job pays.
- The chain is this session. The v1 save still stores only CompletedMissions.

## Out of scope
A ninth mission, a change to the side-job rewards, stream latency, and a new art technique.
