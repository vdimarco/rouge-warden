# The King grabs up close

After a pipe comes off, the King throws, then stomps, then pulls, then belches, then sweeps, then grabs, and then throws again. With every pipe on he only throws. During the grab's wind-up the card says he is grabbing and to get back. Within 8 m when it lands takes a heart, on the ground or in the air, and a rope does not stop it. The grab does not move the hero. Farther than 8 m keeps the heart.
