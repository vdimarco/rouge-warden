# Breakthrough trajectory

Show the century Breakthrough 2 is actually heading toward, with more live indicators and motion that follows the sim.

## Scope

- `public/breakthrough2/` and `qa/breakthrough2/` only.
- Do not change `public/breakthrough/` or `public/tellme/`.
- Keep `?seed` and `?fast=1`. Keep the ending-distribution bands.

## Player-facing change

The phone layout gains a warming trajectory from the real stock and lag, per-meter sparklines and deltas, pressure between meters, emissions against removal, ending-likelihood bars, and a turn log. Motion eases numbers, meter fills, the map, and screen changes, and it respects reduced motion.
