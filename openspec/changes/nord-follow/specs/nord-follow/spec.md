## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Keep a gap behind a walker
After the day is banked, one person SHALL walk a fixed line south across the north lawn at a walk. The player SHALL start that walk by standing behind them on foot. A car SHALL NOT start it. While they walk, the player SHALL stay on foot between the close radius and the leash. A matched walk SHALL hold the gap and SHALL pay 400 once at the stop. A sprint SHALL crowd them. A stop SHALL lose them. Either fail SHALL send them back to the corner and SHALL stay on this beat. A partner inside the close radius SHALL crowd them even when another player is in the band. The step SHALL be saved on version 10. A version-9 slot SHALL start this beat over. How far they have walked SHALL stay in the session. The seven story banks SHALL stay seven.

#### Scenario: Walk the gap
- **WHEN** the player stands behind them and walks at their pace to the stop
- **THEN** they reach the stop and the purse is 400 once
- **WHEN** the player sprints, stops, or gets in a car after they have left the corner
- **THEN** they return to the corner, the beat stays open, and the purse is not paid

#### Scenario: Read the gap
- **WHEN** the objective appears at 1280×720, 1920×1080, or a supported tall phone frame
- **THEN** the existing wrapping keeps the title, the gap instruction, and the recover line readable

#### Scenario: Replay during the gap
- **WHEN** the player replays the race while the gap is open
- **THEN** the step stays where it was and the slot is not rewritten
