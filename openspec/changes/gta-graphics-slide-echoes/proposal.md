# Proposal: a chain the driver can hear

Round 87 counts quick slides on the banner, but every slide played the same two notes. A driver who keeps the eyes on the road heard nothing of the count. This round adds one short repeat of the high note for each link after the first, up to four repeats.

## Out of scope

The slide rule, the 5 s chain window, the banner text, the lock chirp, and the other tones stay as they are.
