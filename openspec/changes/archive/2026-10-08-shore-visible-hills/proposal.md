# Visible hills and 3D-only Shore

## Why

Players still see a nearly flat battlefield after the terrain update. Wide road and structure flattening, including repeated attenuation, hides elevation near normal routes. Shore also silently falls back to the removed 2D experience.

## What Changes

Make hills visibly rise beside playable routes, retain safe foundations and crossings, and make 3D the only battlefield. Ignore old renderer preferences and URLs. Explain WebGL startup/context failures instead of switching modes. Preserve the existing art, controls, minimap and simulation.
