# Design

Keep physical lateral contact and .66-second jump/.60-second duck unchanged. Generate jump rewards from the forecast speed and the shared jump curve, with only raised gold along log arcs and compact low ribbons on other beats after the prior jump lands. Render generated coin heights consistently in WebGL and fallback. Check normal early/late human timing rather than only perfect obstacle clears; missed neighboring gold remains missed.

Use brief seeded combinations of uniform mandatory action waves, mixed clear-lane choices and recovery beats. Start action requirements after the three tutorial rows, bound action droughts, vary spacing within safe margins, and prevent long identical formations. Terrain biases its encounters without replacing every row with one hazard. Preserve attainable three-jump objectives and partial-section suppression. Replace the fixed three-terrain cycle with a CPU/GLSL-equivalent nonrepeating seeded ordering.

Validate natural generated runs at 30/60/120 Hz with delayed inputs, action coverage and collectable jump paths, then check actual rendered jump/coin contact and mobile controls. No new art assets, action locks, remote coin collection or speed changes are needed.

The final log route has only its five raised arc coins. Compact low ribbons on other rows avoid premature pickups during earned Rush; finish gold starts 90 m before the finish so the last possible boosted jump can land first. The existing 150 m hazard-free finish approach is preserved.
