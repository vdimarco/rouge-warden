# ASCII Front arcade cabinet

## Why
The user requested a Battle City inspired game with the referenced character-art aesthetic, deeper play, and deployment to Warden Arcade.

## What Changes
Add ASCII Front as an independent tank roguelite at `/ascii-front/`, with an ASCII Scenes cabinet, shared switcher entry and original local character art. Retain the existing arcade's other games and deployment configuration.

## Impact
Adds the static game and its recoverable source, cabinet/catalog registration, focused checks and the `ascii-front` capability specification. No account, backend or new deployment service is required.
