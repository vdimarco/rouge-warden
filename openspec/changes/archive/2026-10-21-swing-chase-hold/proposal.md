# Mission 1 waits out the first swing

## Why

A first run starts the Sludge Run and the gold-ring lesson together. In a play at 1280×800 the card said "Catch the runner, 38 m" and "SLUDGE RUN 75 s" while the training card said "Swing at the gold ring". Three seconds later the clock read 72 s and the runner had moved 17 m. Spider-Man 2 teaches the swing inside the first chase, and the target does not pull away while you are still learning the button.

## What changes

- While the first lesson is open (the training card's gold-ring row, or the spoken tutorial's first line), the runner stands and the bomb clock does not count.
- The card keeps the time and says to swing first. A narrow window says SWING.
- The first rope, or the end of that lesson (the row ticks, or Skip), starts the run and the clock.
- A start after that lesson, including a retry, runs at once. The training rows stay.

## Out of scope

- The eight training rows, their words, and the gold ring stay.
- No change to the runner's path, his speed once he runs, the crew, or the reward.
- No camera change.
