# Why
Afterlight still feels like an abstract checklist over a quiet picture. The user requests a fundamental action-oriented redesign, powerful broad color and an entire screen that feels alive and adapts to play.

# What Changes
- Make a full-screen action-rescue campaign the default Afterlight experience, using locally bundled Phaser and the six original ASCII scenes.
- Move, aim and fire a light weapon, dodge telegraphed enemies, escort stranded survivors to beacons, and survive beacon defense waves and defeat their guardians.
- Use large cinematic weather, color fields, layered scenery, trails, ripples, growth and action-triggered restoration expanding across the screen.
- Replace permanent sidebar puzzles with a compact overlaid HUD and contextual in-world guidance; provide keyboard and touch controls, reduced motion, safe saves and retry.
- Preserve the prior journey and its saves as an explicitly accessible Classic mode.
