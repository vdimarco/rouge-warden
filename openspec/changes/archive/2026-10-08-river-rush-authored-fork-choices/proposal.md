## Why
The physical forks work, but the islands still resemble plain medians and coin chasing repeats a predictable three-guard sequence. The user wants more beautiful splits and deliberate risk/reward choices informed by a deep study of Subway Surfers.

## What Changes
- Research primary SYBO and Unity sources, distinguish documented mechanics from our own recommendations, and record the design rationale.
- Compose small authored opportunities inside both streams: immediate stash versus a wildlife action, an optional landing detour with a return, and a sheltered-stream boulder snatch.
- Vary complete encounter phrases and exits with bounded seeded rules; rewards remain attached to actual hazards and reachable movement rather than random shapes.
- Add themed island shores, clustered landmarks and channel-local flow/eddies in WebGL and fallback.
- Preview one opportunity at a time with honest reward/action/escape cues, preserving urgent hazard priority and unobstructed gestures.
- Preserve speed, steering/actions, strict physical collection, clean-cache qualification, finite maps and rendering budgets; publish to the existing GitHub/arcade deployment.

## Impact
River Rush encounter generation, reward contact/presentation, fork scenery/water, edge cues, behavioral/browser verification and the canonical River Rush specification. Existing title art and ordinary coin/streak values remain.
