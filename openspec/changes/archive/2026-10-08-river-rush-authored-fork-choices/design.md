## Authored choices
Retain the three main guards and the actual clean +400 entitlement over a +200 cache. Replace the invariant inner/outer/inner sequence with a small library of complete, theme-specific phrases whose entry, reward and exit have been checked. An optional pickup never disqualifies clean play by itself: a missed required guard or an actual impact does.

Use a fixed-value gold stash reward distinct from ordinary/premium coins and the final chest. A stash grants its stated points on compatible ground contact inside the existing 0.95m physical radius; it does not change ordinary coin denominations, streak, Gold Boost or Rush charge. It has its own bounded feedback/receipt and is not the final treasure cache.

Families:
- Wildlife choice: a single actual wildlife guard offers action-matched premium gold and a perfect clear; a simultaneous grounded stash in the other water lane offers immediate income. Both complete payoffs cannot be earned at the same contact plane. Bypassing the guard naturally forfeits its clean clear. Never advertise an action-free bypass beside a gate covering both stream lanes.
- Landing detour: one optional stash after a completed guard's jump has landed or duck has cleared, with a visible return to the next required guard. Placement derives from actual action duration, future maximum Rush and physical steering/reversal plus a realistic reaction margin. Reduce the detour value or omit the opportunity when it cannot fit; do not shrink pickup tolerance or fake a return. Taking the stash while honestly clearing all guards preserves the clean bonus.
- Boulder snatch: a stash before one sheltered-stream rock offers exposure followed by a deliberate cutback; staying in the open lane preserves an easier line and the ordinary final cache. Reserve at least roughly 0.5 maximum-future-Rush seconds after the last stash contact and verify delayed-input recovery. One coherent snatch per fork, not a repeated purse at every rock.

The authored movement phrases are `iio`, `oii`, `ioo` and `ooi`, where letters identify the inner and outer water lanes. Wildlife banking and boulder snatches pay a fixed +120; landing detours pay +200. The detour follows an adjacent repeated-lane pair, at 0.50 maximum-future-Rush seconds after its first guard, with at least 0.46 seconds to the next impact. Its return guard is always actual single wildlife, including Canopy's bird instead of its final full-stream branch. The sheltered snatch precedes the second rock of a repeated-lane pair by 0.54 maximum-future-Rush seconds, so coasting the previous opening cannot automatically collect it.

Preview at least a useful planning interval before commitment, then show only the nearest relevant opportunity. All entities have bounded choice metadata: identity, family, entry/alternative/exit, guard identity, reward base and contact/return distances. A collected bank also yields to a following detour offer and its return when both share the source guard and pending exit; the earlier bypass stays clean-ineligible and the actual paid receipt stays in events. If a collected detour returns to a guard with a live bank alternative, promote that next choice while retaining the detour receipt: returning for the guard and clean bonus competes with staying out for another immediate stash. Exit arrows distinguish the guard path from the stash path. Guarded gold is stated as base potential rather than a guaranteed payout; actual contact, streak and boosts determine the receipt. Do not require all mutually exclusive alternatives to be collected.
