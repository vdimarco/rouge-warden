# The toilet erupts and throws you into the lake

## Why

The player asked what happens after you shoot the overflowing toilet: "it erupts and he falls into the lake". The intro
ended with a flush to the start roof.

## What changes

- After the plunge and the pull, the toilet erupts: a column of sludge blasts out of the bowl to the ceiling with a flash,
  a spray and "KABLOOSH!", and the screen fades.
- Play starts with the hero thrown high over the lake shore (60 m up, moving out over the water). He can fall into the
  lake (KASPLASH) and is fished out on the start roof with "KASPLOOSH! Fished out of the lake. Port Loon. Go get him."
- Mission 1, the Sludge Run, starts once he is back on the start roof (or lands anywhere else, or after 12 s), so the
  fall costs none of its time.
- The intro's last line says "Hold on!" in place of "Flush it!".
- Skip during the eruption leads to the same fall.
- The headset intro (the wall crack) does not change.
