# Decisions

- Use the existing hero identities and artwork. Distinct mechanics change decisions, rather than only colours or damage numbers.
- Mana supports a full combination at spawn; regeneration and base recovery create decisions between fights. Failed casts spend nothing. Training stays independent.
- Bots choose combinations by hero, keep distance, dodge hostile fields, conserve escape skills and stop casting while stunned. Delayed offensive bot casts lock their aim and show their real affected shape. Both teams use the same bot rules.
- Nearby heroes receive lane experience; the hero who finishes a wisp earns extra embers. Tower attacks against the same hero grow stronger and reset when that hero leaves range.
- Keep existing automatic basic attacks, rank gates and six-minute match limit. Existing direct casts remain immediate; bot intent warnings create a reaction window.
- Tests cover mechanics and seeded full matches. Use browser checks for the HUD and visible combat. Physical phone checks are reported separately.
