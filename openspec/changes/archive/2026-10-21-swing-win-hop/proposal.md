## Why

Every win hops the hero the same height. A roof fight ends like a pizza delivery. Spider-Man 2 makes a set-piece finish bigger than an errand.

## What changes

- A roof fight, a getaway, a sludge tanker, and the Sludge Run launch the hero higher, and that payoff stays up longer, than a chase, a rescue, or a delivery.
- Reduced motion still does not launch. A miss still does not launch. The comic word and the sting stay as they are.
