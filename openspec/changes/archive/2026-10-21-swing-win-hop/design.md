# A bigger finish for a bigger job

Marvel's Spider-Man 2 (Insomniac, 2023) makes a set-piece finish bigger than an errand: the moment hangs, and the body leaves the ground harder. None of the grass, water, outline, or painted-look sources fit. This game already hops the hero on a win. The hop is one height for every job.

## Decisions

- The roof fight, the getaway, the sludge tanker, and the Sludge Run use the bigger hop: 14 m/s up, a 0.9 s slow moment, and the card stays 1.9 s. Every other win keeps 8 m/s, 0.55 s, and 1.35 s.
- Reduced motion, a headset, and driving still do not launch. A miss still does not. The word and the sting are unchanged.
- The washer's clock, the rescue's fall rate, and the balloon's slip stay as they are.
