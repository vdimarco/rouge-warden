# Shore playability

Implement the first priorities of the Shore UX and fun audit: preserve selected targets during movement, expose Recall, guide players toward the lane they occupy, simplify the first match, explain rejected spells and record meaningful playtest events. Retain the established folklore art, bronze and gold HUD, 3D terrain, camera zoom and top-center Rift Jump.

This change does not adjust combat balance, match timing or cave navigation. Those need separate measured playtests.
