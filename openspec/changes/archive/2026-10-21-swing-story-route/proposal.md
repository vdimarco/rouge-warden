# Missions after the bomb are not another clog

## Why

Winning the Sludge Run opened Mission 2 as twelve of the same rooftop clog. Spider-Man 2 follows its opening with missions that change what you do. The next three missions here should be a chase, a rescue and a fight. The clogs stay in the city and still wake the King.

## What changes

- After the bomb, Mission 2 is Stop, Thief!, Mission 3 is Catch!, and Mission 4 is a rooftop brawl.
- Each starts on its own near the hero, and a failure starts that same mission again.
- While one of them is current, the mission card names it and odd-job markers wait.
- When the three are done, the card returns to the clogs.

## Out of scope

- The twelve clogs, the King fight and the odd jobs' own rules stay.
- Tests that never win the bomb still show the clog card.
