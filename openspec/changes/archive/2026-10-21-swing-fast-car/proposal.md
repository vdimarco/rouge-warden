## Why

The ninth flush is a getaway, and it drives at the same 17 m/s as an odd job. The car before the roof fight is no harder to stay with than a fare across town. Spider-Man 2 makes the chase in front of a finale pull away harder.

## What changes

- The ninth-flush getaway cruises at 24 m/s. Its clock is the road at that pace, plus the same spare.
- An odd-job getaway stays at 17 m/s.
- The card still names STOP THAT CAR. The King still sleeps. The washer's 36 seconds and the rescue's fall rate stay.

### Added Capabilities
