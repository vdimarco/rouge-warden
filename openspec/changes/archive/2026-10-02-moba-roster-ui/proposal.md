# Twelve legends and a readable spellbook

Expand Monster Mash from four to twelve playable folklore heroes. Each new hero has its own statistics, illustrated art, three basic attacks and four learnable spells. Improve the skill selection screen first, then the roster and combat HUD. Keep the corner ultimate and existing level gates.

The new heroes are Kraken, Wendigo, Kitsune, Stone Golem, Banshee, Phoenix, Dryad and Gorgon. The roster shows role filters and move previews. The spellbook separates skill selection from spending a point, shows rank benefits and level gates, and pauses the match.
