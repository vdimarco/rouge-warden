# Why
The user requested the original Battle City mechanics, many distinct maps, visibly evolving tanks, and a musical soundtrack while preserving ASCII Front's faster modern combat.

# What Changes
- 35 original authored maps, stage selection, 20 enemies per stage and four enemy classes.
- Six classic pickups, three star upgrades/four visual tiers, limited lives and shielded respawns, ice, foliage, projectile cancellation, tank blocking and local two-player cooperation.
- Construction mode with safe local map save/load and immediate playtesting.
- Original synthesized chiptune music controlled with the existing sound toggle; music begins after interaction and stops while paused/hidden.
- Preserve responsive mouse/diagonal movement, dash, EMP and between-stage roguelite choices. Modern player armor remains an intentional balance adaptation; HQ returns to the original one-hit vulnerability.

# Impact
Only ASCII Front source/build, its specification and targeted checks change. Existing arcade registration remains intact. Original maps/music/art are authored here; no original game assets are copied.
