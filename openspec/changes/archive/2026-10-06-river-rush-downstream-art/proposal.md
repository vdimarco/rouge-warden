# Downstream rider and richer river art

## Why
The rider faces the camera while approaching scenery implies downstream travel. Duck and jump reuse the same rider pose. The user likes the current speed and wants better graphics.

## What Changes
Create rear-facing paddle frames and distinct tucked crouch and raised-knee jump art, with a chase camera behind the rider. Improve shoreline detail, foliage variation, rock materials, lighting and atmospheric depth while retaining existing Meshy models, water buoyancy, speed and inputs. Apply the same pose/direction correction in the 2D fallback.

## Impact
Rider assets, presentation math, both renderers, visual QA and deployed static game. Engine speed, action windows, fair course, controls and scores remain unchanged.
