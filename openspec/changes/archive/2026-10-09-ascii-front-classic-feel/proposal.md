# Why
The opening overwhelms players before they can enjoy tank progression: eight concurrent enemies, frequent unlimited enemy shells, and random supplies that offer no stars in most stages. The user wants easier play closer to classic Battle City and rewarding tank level-ups.

# What Changes
- Four enemies at once solo, six in co-op; slower, telegraphed arrivals and gradual enemy introductions.
- One active shell per enemy, slower firing and shorter-range player targeting.
- Cardinal movement and facing-based keyboard/touch fire; optional click-to-aim is preserved.
- First two carriers per stage offer stars while a surviving tank needs ranks; supplies appear on reachable nearby ground and last longer. Rank collection gets a clear message, brief shield and chime.
- Campaign clears offer a simple next-stage action, retain stars and repair surviving tanks; existing permanent perk choices stay in Endless.

# Impact
ASCII Front engine, controls, presentation, audio feedback, checks and committed build only. Original ASCII art, music, 35 maps, co-op, construction, six supplies, lives and HQ defense remain. Numerical pacing and guaranteed stars are deliberate forgiving balance choices, not claims of exact original timing.
