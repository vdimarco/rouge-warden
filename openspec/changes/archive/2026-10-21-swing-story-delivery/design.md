# Story delivery

No new rendering. The pizza job, the mission card and the one-panel scenes already exist. None of the grass, water, outline, or painted-look sources fit.

Marvel's Spider-Man 2 (Insomniac, 2023) changes the verb from mission to mission: a chase, a rescue, a fight, then something you carry across the city. This step is an original pizza delivery. There is no new character and no new word.

## Decisions

- Mission 5 is a Pizza Rush, after the roof fight. The card says to deliver it to a roof. The twelve clogs still follow, and they still wake the King.
- The story drop is 70 to 160 m out, a swing or two. An odd-job pizza stays the long run across town.
- Odd-job markers stay hidden for the whole route, including this delivery. `?nocut` and `?skipintro` still do not start the route unless a test asks.
