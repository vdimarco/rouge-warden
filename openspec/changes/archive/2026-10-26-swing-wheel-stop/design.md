# Loose Wheel

## Decision

The wheel is another offer in the existing odd-job list. It rolls along the nearest street axis and turns around at a building or the water. The stop is a ground check against its live position, and the goal beam follows that position. The bump is applied when the job emits `wheel`, using the existing comic word and the existing yank sound.

## Rendering

No new rendering technique. The wheel is the existing goal beam. No outside art source applies.

## Out of scope

No flush changes. The gust and the lid stay as they are. The wheel is not a crime shout.
