# Loose Wheel

An odd job sends a wheel rolling along the street. The card says to get in front of it. Standing on the ground within 4.5 m stops it and bumps the hero along its roll. The air does not stop it. An 18 s clock that runs out misses as rolled off. The wheel is an offer, not a flush. The first flush stays the clog card, and the third flush stays the gust.
