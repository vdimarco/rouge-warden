# Design

Use a fresh integral Meshy rooted leaning tree: one thick gnarled trunk grows
into a crooked over-water limb with broad asymmetric Y-forks and recursive
smaller branches. Preserve native connected topology and proportions through
mostly affine span placement and course registration. Root and collar belong
to the same visible model; remove the old rail core, counted upright prongs and
separate stump under the generated wood. One/two/three-lane variants differ by
reach, not a decorative 3/4/5-fork count. New full/Lite assets are locally hosted
with recorded provenance and optimized embedded maps/meshopt geometry.

Replace procedural fallback with an irregular branching tree skeleton, strong
collar junctions, progressive taper, sideways/downward forks and broad sparse
foliage sprays. Hanging strands must preserve duck clearance and safe lanes.
Keep branchSpan collision footprints and labels unchanged, and prepare fixed
pools before active play. Native geometry tests check roots, coverage/clearance
and resource budgets without forcing the whole canopy into a flattened plane.

Primary acceptance is visual: side-by-side review against the supplied anatomy
in actual phone/desktop chase framing, showing bank roots, junction and complete
limb at near/mid distances. An independent reviewer must reject rail/antenna,
lollipop foliage or detached-anchor silhouettes even if numeric tests pass.
Then verify real controls, pause/resource identity, exact production assets,
merge canonical specs and archive. Physical device frame rate is not measured
by software Chromium; OpenSpec CLI is unavailable and Markdown is checked.
