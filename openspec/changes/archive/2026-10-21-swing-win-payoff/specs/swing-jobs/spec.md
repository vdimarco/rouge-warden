## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A win pays off
A won job SHALL show a comic word wider than a finisher, play a cheer, and hold a short slow moment in play. The Sludge Run's word SHALL be FLUSH. Every other won job's word SHALL be KASPLASH. The next mission card SHALL NOT open during that moment. A failed job SHALL stay a toast, with no payoff. After the moment, the next story mission SHALL start as before.

#### Scenario: Stop, Thief! pays off
- **WHEN** the player catches the purse snatcher
- **THEN** a KASPLASH wider than a finisher shows, the world hangs for a moment, and the Mission 3 card does not show yet

#### Scenario: The next mission follows
- **WHEN** that moment ends
- **THEN** Mission 3 starts

#### Scenario: A miss is not a win
- **WHEN** the purse snatcher gets away
- **THEN** a toast says so, and no payoff word shows
