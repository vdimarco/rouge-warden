## Why
River Rush has responsive controls but repeats generic angular rocks and palms under an empty sky. Study of Subway Surfers' published gameplay, store screenshots and World Tour presentation shows why readable action silhouettes, rewarding route cues and distinctive surrounding landmarks matter more than undirected polygon increases.

## What Changes
- Record a sourced design analysis and translate it into a coherent lush river adventure art direction.
- Generate detailed fal canopy, river pavilion and driftwood models plus a distant valley panorama; locally host optimized full and lightweight variants.
- Add layered background depth, animated waterfalls and wildlife, visually distinct stretches of canopy, falls and temple harbor; preserve clear lanes.
- Improve water and collectible presentation without changing speed, inputs, collisions, action windows or skeletal rider identity.

## Impact
River Rush scene renderer, generated art/models, focused visual QA and committed arcade build. Existing cabinet and route remain available. No change to other games.
