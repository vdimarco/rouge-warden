# A sticker on the job you are chasing

## Why

Mission 1 tells you to catch a runner who is 40 m away, and the chase camera looks at the skyline. In a play of the Sludge Run the runner sat at the bottom edge of the view, while the training card pointed at a gold ring and the compass was still titled "The nearest clog". Spider-Man 2 keeps a readable marker on the chase target, including when he leaves the frame, and says whether you are gaining.

## What changes

- While a job has a goal, a comic sticker names it (RUNNER, CREW, THIEF, CAR, and the rest) and shows the distance.
- The sticker sits on the goal when it is in view. When the goal is off the screen or behind you, the sticker sits on the edge of the safe area and an arrow points the way.
- On a chase (the runner, the thief, the getaway car, the balloon) the sticker says CLOSING or FALLING BEHIND.
- The compass title says "Your job" during a job, and "The next ring" during a trial.

## Out of scope

- The training checklist, the gold ring and the mission card stay as they are.
- No new mission, no change to timers, rewards or the runner's path.
- Headset play has no flat sticker.
