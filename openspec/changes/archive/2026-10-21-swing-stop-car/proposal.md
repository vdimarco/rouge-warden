## Why

The ninth flush is another clog, the same pump as the first and the third, just before the roof fight. Spider-Man 2 puts a different activity in front of a finale. This run has no car to stop.

## What changes

- The ninth flush starts a getaway car to rope. The card names it and does not say to flush the clogs.
- The first, the third, and the eleventh flushes stay clogs. The King still sleeps until twelve, and he does not wake on the ninth.
- When the car is stopped, the clog card returns at 9/12.
