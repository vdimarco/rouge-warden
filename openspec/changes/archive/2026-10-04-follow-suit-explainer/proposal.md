# Follow Suit: a how-to-play explainer

The user played Follow Suit and did not understand it. The start screen gives the rules as text only. Add a short animated explainer that shows the rules with the game's own cards.

## Scope

- An explainer of 7 animated scenes and an end card: build a chain, follow by suit or rank, 8s are wild, Value and Mult, rings, the target, and the run.
- It opens by itself on a player's first visit, unless the address has a seed. The start screen and each stop intro can open it again.
- It plays by itself. The player can go back, go forward, hold a finger on it to pause, and skip it.
- The cards, the numbers and the sounds come from the game: the same CSS cards, the engine's scores and the same notes.
- With reduced motion, cards fade in place and do not travel.
- The arcade copy of the game gets the explainer too.

## Out of scope

- Video or image files. The game stays free of them.
- A guided practice table.
- Changes to the rules or the targets.
