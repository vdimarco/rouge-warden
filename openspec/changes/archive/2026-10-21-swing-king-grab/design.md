# A sixth attack for the King

No new rendering. The sludge ring and the comic words already exist. None of the grass, water, outline, or painted-look sources fit a reach around the King.

Marvel's Spider-Man 2 (Insomniac, 2023) gives a boss a close grab. A jump does not save you, and a web does not save you. The way out is to be farther away. The words stay in this game's voice: GRAB, GLUG, SAFE.

## Decisions

- While every pipe is on, he only throws.
- After a pipe is off, attacks cycle throw, stomp, pull, belch, sweep, grab. The attack after a sweep is a grab. The attack after a grab is a throw.
- A throw that never leaves does not take a turn.
- Within 8 m takes a heart, on the ground or in the air. A rope does not stop it. The grab does not move the hero. Farther than 8 m does not take a heart.
- During the wind-up the card says he is grabbing and to get back. After it, the card names the next pipe again.
