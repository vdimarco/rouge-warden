# Design
A readable rescue expedition inside the existing Phaser ASCII landscapes. You are a courier carrying supplies to a stranded harbor; reconnect all six beacons and rescue the three crews. Warm labels and an outlined YOU marker identify the player, while a distinct current-objective marker and route make the next destination explicit. A short task list explains cause and effect; new players start with the courier pack, then catch fireflies at three groves to power the first beacon. The forest task is spatial catching with a lantern sweep rather than another sidebar dial. Shift/touch dodge provides a short directional burst with recovery and hazard protection, with visible feedback and no harsh failure. Beacon activation is a payoff after local work rather than repeating a completed minigame. Reduced motion preserves essential positions/cues, all actions have keyboard/touch alternatives, and old saves retain inventory and region progress.

Code-native ASCII is intentional to preserve the supplied art direction. Labels and instruments are accessible UI, not replacement scene assets. Verify first-minute comprehension, full campaign, collision/catching/dodge, save compatibility and desktop/phone layouts.
