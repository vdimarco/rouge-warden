# A clear rescue adventure

## Why
The current adventure is too abstract: opaque landmark roles, vague light-network stakes, and repeated sidebar puzzles obscure why the player acts. The requested iteration needs a concrete purpose, navigable missions and more satisfying spatial mechanics.

## What Changes
Make the goal rescuing three boat crews and reconnecting the six-beacon rescue route. Give the opening explicit stakes, a first objective and player identity. Add visible landmark names, player identification and guided next destinations. Replace the forest direction puzzle with spatial firefly catching and delivery, add a directional dodge with cooldown and physical hazard protection, streamline redundant final-beacon operations, and give clear outcomes/rewards. Keep existing saves and the original ASCII art.
