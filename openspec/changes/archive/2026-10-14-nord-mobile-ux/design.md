# Design

Use the existing phone dimensions and WebRTC ceiling negotiation. A small state update in `Browser/config.mjs` owns the four profiles and consecutive sample counters; the player applies changes through its existing SDK commands. Wait five seconds after initial playback or a profile change, require five poor samples (or two below 12 FPS) to lower quality and fifteen healthy samples to restore it. A single freeze counts as one poor sample. Reconnect and slot changes restore the balanced 960/2 Mbps profile. No new dependency or world-rendering technique is introduced.

Scope: config/player, unit and mobile fixtures, browser README, canonical phone-stream requirement and verification evidence. Old output/report destinations remain historical. Runtime saves remain unchanged. Compare the existing low-resolution stream with a candidate at both orientations, run actual stream input/reconnect checks and a 60-second camera sweep, inspect screenshots, then publish the tested frontend through Git/Vercel and verify the public alias. Physical phones/carrier links remain unverified. The larger stream-speed change stays open.

The minimap adds `AQuebecHUD::DrawHUD`, its shared segment clip and native test, browser clearance fixture, and a fresh package. Use the existing Canvas renderer's `FCanvasNGonItem` (local UE 5.8.3 `Engine/Public/CanvasItem.h`) for a 64-sided filled circle and border; quadratic segment-circle clipping preserves actual road intersections without inventing border roads. Diameter 160 logical pixels in landscape and 28% of portrait width; radius always represents 250 metres. Radially clamp goal/home markers inside the rim. Retain nearest-eight car markers and all attribution lines beneath the map. No world-map or art regeneration. Preserve the already merged graphic-novel animation changes during build/package and verify their new test with the native suite.

## Movement and traffic audit

A 600-second fixed-step city replay shows 16 moving cars initially but only one usually within 150 metres after several minutes. Retain eight cars on collision-qualified closed circuits within 250 metres of the starting neighbourhood; keep the other eight on district routes, with the same fleet size, yield rules and art. Replay the full interval and check local circulation rather than only counting startup cars.

Held action pointers now share the existing normalized look path while retaining their key hold; an explicit background look finger takes priority. Double-tapping Move or Sprint latches Shift only while movement is held. Release, menu, cancellation, backgrounding, rotation and car state changes clear the latch. Cover ownership and releases in unit and actual native-input browser checks.

The rendered pond survey has 328 boundary/island vertices and two mapped bridge decks; regenerate a small native header from that same source. A per-world transient water PhysicsVolume and native MOVE_Swimming provide surface-swim movement, with actual polygon/bridge tests and a gradual 1-metre shore transition. Only the swimming capsule ignores the map's flat District ground collider; other actors retain collision. Clearance-check shore exit before restoring ground collision. Keep land, island, bridge and above-water jump behavior. Existing locomotion is reused in a lowered upright treading pose while idle and a centred horizontal swim pose during travel, with the hand prop hidden; dedicated stroke animation remains an explicit limitation. Verify authority and remote motion, buoyancy/stroke, banks/islands/bridges, visuals and recovery without altering map assets or saves.
