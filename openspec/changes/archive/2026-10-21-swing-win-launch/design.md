# Win launch

No new rendering. The comic word, the cheer, and the slow moment already land the win. None of the grass, water, outline, or painted-look sources fit.

The hop follows the mission-complete beat in Marvel's Spider-Man 2 (Insomniac, 2023): a clear throws you back into motion instead of leaving you planted. The move is an original hop. There is no new animation.

## Decisions

- Flat play only. Reduced motion, a headset, and driving keep the word and do not launch the body.
- The hop is 8 m/s up and 3.5 m/s forward, along the flight if it is already moving, otherwise along the view. The feet leave the ground in that same moment, because a grounded step zeroes an upward speed. The view widens by 6 degrees. There is no flip.
- A miss does not change the hero's speed.
