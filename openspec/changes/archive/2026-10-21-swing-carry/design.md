# Swing carry

No rendering technique. The boost is the same velocity add the GO-cue release already uses. None of the art sources fit.

The feel follows Marvel's Spider-Man 2 (Insomniac, 2023): letting go of a moving web keeps you flying into the next one. The perfect window in this game stays the flip. Original move only.

## Decisions

- Outside the cue, a let-go at 8 m/s or faster adds 2.2 m/s along the flight and 1.6 m/s up, and does not cancel a fall or flip the hero.
- Under 8 m/s, the let-go is unchanged.
- The phone already lets go by itself past the bottom of the arc. This change is mouse and pad only.
