# Direction
The user explicitly rejects the current ugly 3D presentation and requests the original dot/pixel aesthetic. Tanks, terrain, vegetation and effects should read as crisp luminous stippled forms in real depth, not smooth solid boxes behind a pixelation overlay. Emerald/cyan foliage and warm gold player contrast against violet/red threats, with dark negative space. Region silhouettes and paths occupy the viewport. Labels, projectiles and warnings stay readable. Real shots change cover, wrecks, atmosphere and local lighting; renderer state follows the deterministic model.

Separate Battle Tanks renderer preserves Afterlight. Independent render tests measure actual images and native movement/aiming, frame timing and three layouts. Encounter updates must reward movement, make relay destruction transform combat, and preserve cover, range limits, extraction and saves. Avoid adding controls simply to create complexity.

Travel charges at4.6/meter; a full charge deals2 damage and pierces one target, stopping at cover. Relay destruction grants5seconds rapid fire, removes nearby enemy shells, drops35-health repair at the relay and leaves a wreck/restored light island. Scouts flank, scatter tanks fire spread pairs, and bruisers are slower with stronger telegraphed attacks. New fields default safely on old saves. Optional sound is gesture-enabled and off by default. The runtime uses fixed60Hz model steps and an independent visual clock paused with play.
