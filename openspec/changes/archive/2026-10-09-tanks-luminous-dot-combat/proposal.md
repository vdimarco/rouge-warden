## Why
Battle Tanks lost the requested dot/pixel aesthetic by rendering solid low-poly shapes, and its encounters provided too little feedback or movement reward.

## What Changes
Replace that presentation with crisp luminous 3D point-built terrain, vegetation, vehicles, cover, trails and warnings. Driving charges piercing shots; distinct enemy roles and relay rewards create tactical choices. Destruction leaves wrecks and restored light patches. Preserve real perspective, independent turret aiming, range limits, physical cover, extraction, existing saves and Afterlight's renderer.
