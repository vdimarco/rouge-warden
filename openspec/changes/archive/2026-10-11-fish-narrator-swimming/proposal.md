# Reel It In: wildlife narration and visible swimming

## Why

The two spoken bite hints sound like generic system speech, and a hooked fish can look stiff while the player reels it through the water. The player should hear a calmer wildlife narrator and see the fish actively swim against the line.

## What Changes

- Give the brief voice hints a measured British nature-documentary delivery using an available device voice, with a clear fallback when that voice is absent.
- Make a hooked fish visibly kick, turn, and respond to its movement and the pull of the reel, while keeping the line attached and its fight path unchanged.
- Check phone and wide-screen play, reduced motion, speech fallback, and store builds.
