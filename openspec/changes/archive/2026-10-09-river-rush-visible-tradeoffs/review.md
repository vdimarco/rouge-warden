# Why the previous tradeoffs were weak

Player feedback: “I'm not really seeing any trade offs.” The independent source/archived-phone audit found:

- Bank +120 fixed score was less than the +140/+160 minimum action reward and also forfeited clean +400. It was a dominated escape for a skilled player.
- A successful landing detour could gain +200 while retaining everything; a sheltered snatch likewise compared additional points with no tangible alternate reward.
- Score stashes never changed visible coin count, coin missions, streak or Rush. Actual guard gold contained only two or three tokens, in a tightly compressed action window.
- Opportunity cost sat in a small sidebar line; the screenshot exposed 7–10px core text and tiny world rewards.

# Current decision contract

| Choice | One benefit | Competing benefit | What physically prevents both |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wildlife bank | 8 actual ordinary coins now: streak, coin goal and Rush progress | Guarded gold and a required unprotected clear toward clean +400 | Opposite lane and ground versus guard/action rewards; bypassed required clear is never earned |
| Landing detour | Fixed +200 points | Gold Boost doubles points of touched coins for 8 seconds | Same exact distance, opposite lanes and .95m grounded contact |
| Sheltered snatch | Fixed +120 points, followed by a boulder cutback | One replacement shield in clear water | Same exact distance, opposite lanes and .95m grounded contact; shields never stack |

This changes the best immediate choice with the player's situation: coin-goal/Rush threshold, streak and forthcoming gold, or an already-lost shield. Gold Boost does not double fixed point stashes or attract adjacent coins. Optional collection by itself does not revoke clean qualification. Risky execution can still preserve clean completion when every required guard is honestly cleared.

The existing [Subway Surfers research](https://github.com/vdimarco/rouge-warden/blob/main/openspec/changes/archive/2026-10-08-river-rush-authored-fork-choices/research.md) remains the source for authored encounters, early previews, several goals and strict separation of gameplay correctness from subjective enjoyment. The research does not claim these are proprietary Subway Surfers algorithms. This revision addresses the player's feedback; automated passing routes do not establish human fun.
