# In Full Swing: city action (fights, cars, jobs, a fast opening, sprint, the dive roll, varied buildings)

## Why

After the street life change (#214) the player asked for:
- "add driving and vehicle entry"
- "make the buildings more interesting, the windows are all the same"
- "when you dive and hit the ground go into a roll"
- "add some element of combat, like fighting"
- "the intro is a bit boring: start with an intro of who the toilet king is and a fast paced mission"
- "shift should make me run faster, with an energy gauge that gets depleted"
- "there has to be more missions than unclogging toilets. We need to save people and do other fun, exciting odd jobs"

## What changes

- **Sprint and energy (flat play).** Shift (a pad's left stick click) on the ground with no rope out makes the hero run about twice as
  fast. An energy gauge drains while you sprint and fills again when you stop. With a rope out Shift still reels in.
- **The dive roll.** A dive now lasts until just before the ground. The hero lands in a forward roll that keeps the speed, then
  runs on. A hard landing at speed rolls too.
- **Fighting.** The King's Sludge Gang stands guard on rooftops near the clogs and turns up in jobs. Goons walk up, wind up and
  punch. In reach, the swing input punches (punch, punch, kick). A rope that catches a goon yanks him off his feet. A dive or a
  roll that lands among goons knocks them down. The hero has health (five hearts) that comes back after a few quiet seconds; at
  zero the hero wakes up on the nearest safe roof.
- **Driving.** Parked cars wait at the kerbs near the player. R (a pad's B, a phone's CAR button) gets in and out. W/S drive and
  brake, A/D steer, Space is the handbrake. The chase camera pulls back behind the car. A car stops on walls, people jump out of
  its way and it knocks goons down. A headset does not drive.
- **A fast opening.** A first run starts with a quick comic about who the Porcelain King is, then the cottage room, then Mission 1,
  "Sludge Run": a 90 s chase after a gang runner with a clog bomb, a fight with his crew, and the bomb defused. The twelve clogs
  become Mission 2 and the King Mission 3.
- **Odd jobs.** Job markers around the city: catch a falling person, carry a stranded window washer to safety, a pizza rush, a
  balloon chase, a rooftop brawl. Each pays Loonies and can be played again.
- **Varied buildings.** Window tiles, window sizes, wall tints, floor bands, balconies, air-conditioner boxes and lit-window tints
  vary by building and by window.
- Version 1.11.0 (Quest APK code 15).

## Out of scope

Headset driving and fighting (the headset keeps its swinging game). Cars that are the moving traffic. Guns. A police response.
