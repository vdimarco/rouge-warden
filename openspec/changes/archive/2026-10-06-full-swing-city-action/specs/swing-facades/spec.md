## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Varied buildings
Neighbouring buildings SHALL look different: the window tile pair, the window size and spacing, the wall tint, floor bands,
balconies and air-conditioner boxes SHALL vary by building, and lit windows SHALL vary in light colour, blinds and curtains. Every
detail SHALL keep the comic look and fade to its average where it would shimmer. Flat play SHALL stay within its draw and triangle
budget (qa/vr/perf.mjs).

#### Scenario: A Market street
- **WHEN** the player looks along a Market street
- **THEN** no two neighbouring buildings share the same window pattern and wall colour, and the lit windows are not all one colour
