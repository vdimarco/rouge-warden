# In Full Swing: the toilet intro, a fair catch, a faster opening

## Why

The player said:
- "The person who fell off the building, I couldn't catch them because they fell into the building before I could get there. Because there was a ledge below."
- "make the initial intro scene faster"
- "The cracks are a bit low-res or bad. I think we can do a better job with the cracks."
- "Make the toilet itself be the thing that overflows, and you have to shoot that" and then (asked) "Flushed into the city".

## What changes

- **Catch!** The faller now slips off an edge with a clear drop to the street: no lower tier, ledge or other building under the
  fall line above 3 m. A roof with no such edge gets no Catch! marker. The faller waits 3.5 s on the edge (was 1.5 s), says
  "Whoa, whoa! I'm slipping!" 1.5 s before the fall, and the card counts the seconds down.
- **The toilet intro (flat screen).** In the cottage room the toilet stands in the middle of the wall. It rattles (1 s), the lid
  flies open and sludge spills over the rim on to the floor (1.4 s), and the toilet is the target. Plunge it and pull: it flushes,
  the sludge swirls down, the screen fades, and you splash out on the start roof ("SPLOOSH! Port Loon. Go get him."). No wall
  crack on a flat screen. The headset keeps the crack and the burst.
- **A faster opening.** The opening comic plays in about 14 s (was about 20 s). The flat-screen cottage reaches its target in
  2.4 s (the crack took about 9 s).
- **A sharper crack (headset).** The crack texture doubles to 2048 px and its line edges are antialiased per pixel.
