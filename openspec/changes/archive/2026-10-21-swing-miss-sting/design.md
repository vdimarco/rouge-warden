# Miss sting

No new rendering technique. The chase clock and the one-shot synth already exist. None of the grass, water, or outline sources fit.

The fail sting follows the mission-fail sting in Marvel's Spider-Man 2 (Insomniac, 2023): a short fall in pitch, separate from the win fanfare. The notes are original. There is no sample.

## Decisions

- While the purse snatcher or the sludge runner is pulling away (heat at least 0.55), the chase clock is the behind clock, even when the distance is holding. Closing still takes the clock away. The first-swing wait still stays quiet.
- A miss plays `miss`: triangle 220 Hz falling to 180, then 146 falling to 110, with a 73 Hz body. It does not play the win's `trialEnd` or `cheer`.
