# Ride the Line

## Decision

The line is another offer in the existing odd-job list. It runs along the nearest street axis, above the street, and the goal beam marks its start. A catch is an airborne speed check against that segment. While the hero is on it, each update places them along the line. The catch and the launch use the existing comic word and the existing rope-release sound.

## Rendering

No new rendering technique. The line is the existing goal beam. No outside art source applies.

## Out of scope

No flush changes. The gust, the lid, the loose wheel, and the kite stay as they are. The line is not a crime shout.
