# Ride the Line

An odd job strings a line across the street. The card says to swing onto it. A fast swing in the air, close to the line, catches it, and the line carries the hero to the far end and launches them off. Standing on the ground, or swinging too slowly, does not catch it. A 16 s clock that runs out before the catch says the line went by. The line is an offer, not a flush. The first flush stays the clog card, the third flush stays the gust, and the wheel and the kite stay offers.
