# Design

Use a fixed, restrained cockpit frame around a clear forward view. Depth-projected stars accelerate from a coherent vanishing point; distant galaxy dust and near streaks move at different speeds. Approach the event horizon, cross the light tunnel, then ease into the destination. Bound particle counts and canvas work. Preserve transition duration, pause, skip, sound and reduced-motion behavior.

Inspect departure, horizon, tunnel and arrival at desktop, phone portrait and landscape sizes. Check the actual canvas, control visibility and browser errors. Reduced motion must keep the existing short fade without high-speed streaks.
