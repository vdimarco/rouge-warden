## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Look around in the opening
In the desktop opening the mouse SHALL turn the view inside the cottage room as well as tilt it, as a headset turns the head. The turn SHALL carry into play at the hand-off.

#### Scenario: Turn in the room
- **WHEN** the player moves the mouse sideways during the opening
- **THEN** the view turns inside the room and the room stays where it is

### Requirement: Music from the first minute
The music SHALL start when play starts, on a first run as well, unless the player turned it off.

#### Scenario: First run
- **WHEN** a new player starts the tutorial with the sound on
- **THEN** the music plays
