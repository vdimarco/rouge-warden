# Design

Store explicit contiguous `branchLanes` on each nonwildlife branch entity and
share one coverage helper between collision, guidance and visual morphology.
New one- and two-lane spans start at the left or right bank; full-width spans
vary banks. Keep the existing reward lane on the entity. Consolidate coherent
branch rows into one rooted tree; preserve an attainable duck or clear route.

Replace the steep dip with a gently sagging low supporting limb and connected
secondary/tertiary offshoots distributed along its length. Keep the optimized
local Meshy bark/geometry and prepared instance batches, and maintain matching
procedural/2D fallback. Low wood stays in covered lanes; a marked contact band
and width label show the correct duck envelope. No texture/model preparation
or unbounded instance allocation during play.

Check physical standing/duck/dodge contact in all covered/clear lanes and
between covered lanes, raised coins and complete delayed-input campaigns.
Review close/mid-distance pictures of all three sizes from both banks on phone
and desktop, including fallback and stopped state. Test real App controls and
exact published bytes. Merge/archive the completed OpenSpec delta.
