## Prioritized visual acceptance matrix

1. **Sherbrooke spawn, facing the park and along the street:** preserve brick scale, cream trim, stairs, entrance depth and readable road/sidewalk. Leaves must show colour and separated crown volume instead of a black noisy screen. Shadows must remain grounded.
2. **Near/mid/far trees, including backlight:** inspect leaf masks, branch attachment, crown continuity and tree-to-tree colour differences. Reject solid rectangular cards, disappearing crowns, checkerboard fallback, aggressive distant holes, new depth halos or ink flooding. Record retained repeated geometry rather than claiming new species.
3. **South/north pond and chalet/theatre context:** park trees and façades behind water must retain readable layers and colour in comic and F2. Preserve pond/path/court/landmark placement and distinguish water from land, without new luminance-band jumps in sky/clouds or smooth pond reflections. Inspect shiny upward props/trim for overbroad treatment. The existing flat banks and simplified terrain are known limits, not permission to move geometry.
4. **Residential/shopfront façades and exterior stairs:** existing richer fronts must not regress. Affected perimeter art must read as masonry, openings and trim rather than uninterrupted generic checkerboard walls. Reject floating trim, duplicated windows, façade planes across gaps, surfaces through stairs and z-fighting.
5. **Panet and Visitation closures, and district edge:** retain readable road closures and existing bounds. Added detail must not imply a new traversable district or obstruct existing pavement. Inspect the visible background wall surfaces, not only a selected shopfront.
6. **Rachel skyline and Le Neuville close-up:** show the corrected survey-derived tall massing from a contextual park/Rachel view and a walking-height modern-façade view, in comic and F2 at both sizes. Confirm no retained low-rise visual shell or generic three-storey façade remains overlaid on it; check material scale, attachment and depth flicker. The source match and unchanged gameplay collider require separate assertions, not visual inference.
7. **École Le Plateau exterior:** show its retained U-shaped plan, taller main walls, lower wing and roof silhouette with readable classroom glazing/tan masonry in comic and F2 at both sizes. Inspect roof material, wall attachment and OSM-footprint seam; reject missing copied survey parts or a duplicate generic low front.

Use matched positions, targets, FOV and comic/F2 state. Required desktop sizes are 1280×720 and 1920×1080; at minimum inspect all seven scene groups at both sizes and both looks where affected. At least one near/far or short moving view is needed to assess foliage stability; static screenshots cannot establish lack of shimmer. New captures live in an ignored, revision-specific evidence directory, with SHA-256, dimensions and actual settings recorded. Keep historical reports and images intact or restore their exact bytes after existing scripts write them.
