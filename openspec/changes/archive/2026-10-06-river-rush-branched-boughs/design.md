# Design

Reuse the prepared bark/leaf textures and curved tube shader. Increase trunk
and structural bough radii, taper to a .42-radius contact, and descend past the
contact without an upward hook. Add four substantial ascending two-part forks
with side twigs, fanning both across and along the course. Move some canopy
leaf clusters onto these forks. Keep counts within 32 wood/32 leaves per tree.

Sample complete Bezier limbs with their tapered envelope to check adjacent
routes remain overhead and low wood stays inside the target lane. Contact
remains at lane centre, course offset zero, height 2.42. Visually inspect the
actual duck pose under the thicker wood on phone, desktop and short landscape,
also exercise 2D/reduced motion and the standard material shader. No new
assets or changes to collision timing are needed.
