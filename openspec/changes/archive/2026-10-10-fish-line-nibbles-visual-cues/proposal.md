# Reel It In: honest line, nibble taps, and visual cues

## Why

Players report winding in much more line than they cast. They also want to feel fish testing the lure, to get useful tactile feedback throughout the fishing experience, and to understand the next move from the top-corner cue without reading a stream of instructions.

## What Changes

Bring the pre-hook retrieve and its line display into agreement with the cast, make each actual nibble perceptible on supported phones, fill meaningful gaps in cast, retrieve, fight, navigation, and outcome haptics, and turn the existing top-corner action card into a compact animated demonstration. Keep the existing controls, optional written guide, screen-reader announcements, and reduced-motion support.
