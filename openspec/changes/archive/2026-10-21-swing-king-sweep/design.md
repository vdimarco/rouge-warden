# A fifth attack for the King

No new rendering. The sludge ring and the comic words already exist. None of the grass, water, outline, or painted-look sources fit a lane on the deck.

Marvel's Spider-Man 2 (Insomniac, 2023) gives a boss a sweep you leave by stepping out of the lane. A jump in that lane still lands. The words stay in this game's voice: SWEEP, GLUG, SAFE.

## Decisions

- While every pipe is on, he only throws.
- After a pipe is off, attacks cycle throw, stomp, pull, belch, sweep. The attack after a belch is a sweep. The attack after a sweep is a throw.
- A throw that never leaves does not take a turn.
- The lane is the heading from the King to the hero when the wind-up begins. It does not follow the hero.
- Within 36 m and within 0.45 radians of that heading takes a heart, on the ground or in the air. Outside the heading, or farther than 36 m, it does not. The sweep does not move the hero.
- During the wind-up the card says he is sweeping and to step aside. After it, the card names the next pipe again.
