# The King sweeps a lane

After a pipe comes off, the King throws, then stomps, then pulls, then belches, then sweeps, and then throws again. With every pipe on he only throws. During the sweep's wind-up he takes the heading from himself to the hero, and the card says he is sweeping and to step aside. That heading does not follow. Still in that lane within 36 m when it lands takes a heart, on the ground or in the air. A step outside the lane, or a place farther than 36 m along the heading, keeps the heart.
