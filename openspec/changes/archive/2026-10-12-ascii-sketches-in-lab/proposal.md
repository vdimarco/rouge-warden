# ASCII sketches in the Lab

The user asked that the small ASCII games, which are not complete, go into one section or into the Lab. They named the set: every ASCII Scenes game except Battle Tanks and ASCII Front.

The eight games are Afterlight, Last Light, Lighthouse Keeper, Echoes Under Ice, Last Train Home, Firefly Courier, Mirage Runner and Orbital Gardener. They move to the Lab:

- In the Old arcade hall, the Lab group holds them after its nine toys. ASCII Scenes keeps ASCII Front and Battle Tanks.
- All Games at `/` leaves them out. The Lab tile covers them. The user also asked for Loon Echo, Take the Plunge and Up the Creek to stand only in the Lab, so All Games leaves those three out too.
- The Lab page lists them in an ASCII sketches section, with a picture, a name, a line of text and a link.
- The switcher inside a game still lists every game, so a player can jump to a sketch from any game. The Crimson Rogue credits leave out every Lab-only game.

`labOnly: true` in `public/arcade/switch.js` marks the eleven Lab-only games.

Out of scope: changes to the games themselves.
