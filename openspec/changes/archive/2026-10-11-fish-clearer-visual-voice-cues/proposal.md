# Reel It In: clearer action cues

## Why

The current corner card is easy to miss during a fight: its gesture is small, some arrows read as decoration, and all action words are hidden. Players want to see the movement more clearly and hear a little guidance at key moments.

## What Changes

Make the illustrated gesture larger and higher contrast, add a short visible action label with an optional brief qualifier, and speak two brief tips for a nibble and a hook set on supported devices. Keep the full accessible instruction, existing controls, sound setting, Calm effects, and reduced-motion behavior. Give players a separate Voice hints switch.
