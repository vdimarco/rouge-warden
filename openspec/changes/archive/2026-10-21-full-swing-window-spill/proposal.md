# Spill the window lamp onto the wall beside it

## Why

A lit window is a sticker of light. The pier next to it, and the cloth above a shop, stay the same colour as a dark window. On the street the avenue still reads as one grade.

## What changes

A lit window throws a warm pool onto the pier around it. The pool falls off within a fraction of the window cell, stays off the glass, and is weaker on a face in the sun. A lit shop warms the hem of its awning, the cloth nearest the glass. The top of the cloth stays red or teal. A dark shop leaves the cloth alone. No new mesh and no new draw.

## Capabilities

- `window-spill`: a near lit window warms the wall beside it, and the city render still passes.
