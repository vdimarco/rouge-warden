# The pump is no longer most of the twelve

Marvel's Spider-Man 2 (Insomniac, 2023) does not spend most of a chapter on one repeated objective. It breaks that objective often enough that the player can name what comes next. None of the grass, water, outline, or painted-look sources fit. This is a pacing change, and the jobs are ones the city already has.

## Decisions

- Three flushes change in this round, because one more beat left the pump as the majority and the pacing score did not move.
- The second flush is the falling rescue, on a roof 36 to 130 m away, card prefix OVER THE EDGE. The first flush stays a clog, so the training card at 1/12 still holds. The fall rate stays as it is.
- The fifth flush is the purse snatcher, 18 to 55 m away, card prefix PURSE RUN. It sits between the balloon and the pizza.
- The seventh flush is the mugging, 18 to 80 m away, card prefix THE ALLEY. It sits between the pizza and the washer.
- After this, the pump flushes are the first, the third, the ninth, the eleventh, and the twelfth. That is five of twelve. No three pumps run in a row.
- The King still roars only at four and eight, and he still wakes at twelve. A miss does not retry a beat. A save that already has that many clogs does not replay the beat, because the beat runs on the flush. A beat waits while a story mission is current, and winning a beat does not move the story route.
