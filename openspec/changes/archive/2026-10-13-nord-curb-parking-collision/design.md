# Design

Reference: supplied Google Street View image (August 2024), retained only in ignored Saved/References; also reviewed public Street View at 1351 Rachel St E, Montréal (45.5280029,-73.5719633). Cars sit parallel to the curb, close to its edge, tires grounded and with bumper gaps. Do not bake Google imagery into art. Preserve original seventies fleet.

Adapt standard Unreal simple convex collision for moving objects: https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/setting-up-collisions-with-static-meshes-in-unreal-engine . Import a hidden proxy for each existing body/cabin profile with bounded convex decomposition; keep visual meshes unchanged. Retain the old box subobject disabled for saved-map compatibility, and explicitly restore the fitted root. Four wheel ground samples support ordinary 18 cm curbs, with bounded slope/step acceptance; wall sweeps and tangent projection prevent tunnelling and sticky glancing collisions. No new vehicle framework.

Parking uses road widths only as a bounded search window. Three complex traces find the actual raised concrete curb across the car length, fit its angle, validate asphalt support, and leave about 10–60 cm body clearance. OSM side restrictions exclude mapped cycle lanes and prohibited parking; one-way tags set facing. Candidate centres start 14.7 m from segment ends and cars retain at least 6 m centre spacing. Keep bumper clearance at intersections (at least 5 m plus half car length, conservative around mapped segment ends), following https://montreal.ca/en/articles/parking-5-metre-clearance-required-intersections-79358 . Survey tags are incomplete: this is visual placement, not a complete legal parking simulation. Runtime collision queries against visible road geometry allow tires to follow the rendered asphalt.

Reach: native car constructor/paint/rotation/movement, population placement, three new collision proxies and bounded importer, native contact tests, population and art fixtures, cooked browser package and verification. No frontend source changes anticipated. Preserve all existing Content files and runtime Saved directories; only add owned proxy assets.


The earlier width-relative implementation was rejected after rendered inspection showed gutter-fill asphalt extending to surveyed sidewalk edges. The final implementation samples the existing Concrete_Collision mesh itself; it does not rebuild the city. Keep the 32-car target as a maximum: the current map yields 19–20 additional qualified spaces, plus the eight relocated original cars and starter. Do not invent curb spaces to meet the old count. Sixteen moving cars, 96 pedestrians and existing decorative street props remain.

A fresh replay of unchanged ed452c1a code established current chalet/pond/spawn-adjacent counts of 5/8/7; an older fixture expected eight chalet residents. The fixture now preserves the current chalet/pond counts and retains its original minimum for nearby street residents, whose safe routes can change around newly parked cars. All 88 mobile people, eight stationary people, sustained traffic, yielding and replicated native entry/exit remain required.


Final corridor validation includes Pawn obstacles so idle mission cars do not block ambient loops. Junction checks consider a reservation released as soon as an entered holder is beyond 11 m, even if that actor ticks later; an added regression assertion reproduces the stale-holder case. Preserve mission car locations. Both checkpoint recovery callers share fitted-hull clearance and retain ignored campaign/player/occupant actors. Browser production code is unchanged; its real-input QA allows twelve short approach attempts for the curb-parked starter.
