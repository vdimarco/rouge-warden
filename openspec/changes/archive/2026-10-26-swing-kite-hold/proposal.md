# Hold the Kite

An odd job sends a kite up from the street. The card says to stay above it. Time in the air above the kite fills a 4 s hold, and the first moment above it tugs the hero up. The ground does not count, and leaving the air slips the hold. Filling the hold catches the kite and launches the hero. A 22 s clock that runs out says the kite rose away. The kite is an offer, not a flush. The first flush stays the clog card, the third flush stays the gust, and the loose wheel stays an offer.
