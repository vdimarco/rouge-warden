# Why
The user reports that the game still looks unlike its concept, lacks 3D depth and remains boring. They request a Battle Tanks variant in parallel.
# What Changes
- Replace flat action presentation with real perspective 3D geometry, depth occlusion, terrain, camera tracking, fog, volumetric-style colored lighting and a restrained ASCII/dot treatment.
- Give Afterlight more tactical action: break survivor tethers, manage weapon heat, interrupt threats with a charged pulse, and engage richer encounters rather than holding fire through a checklist.
- Build a separate playable Battle Tanks variant with vehicle steering, independent turret aim, physical cover, destructible enemy emplacements, shell combat and an extraction objective.
- Keep both variants accessible, preserve existing Classic and action saves safely, and verify actual rendered depth and core combat on desktop/phone before publishing.
