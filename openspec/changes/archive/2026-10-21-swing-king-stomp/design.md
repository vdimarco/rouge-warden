# A second attack for the King

No new rendering. The ring and the comic word already exist. None of the grass, water, outline, or painted-look sources fit.

Marvel's Spider-Man 2 (Insomniac, 2023) gives a boss more than one attack, and a ground attack you can leave by being in the air. The words stay in this game's voice: STOMP, GLUG, SAFE.

## Decisions

- While every pipe is on, he only throws, so the opening of the fight is unchanged.
- After a pipe is off, attacks alternate. The first one after the pipe comes off is still a throw. The next is a stomp.
- The stomp reaches 28 m across the deck. On the ground inside that it takes a heart. In the air it does not.
- During the wind-up the card says he is stomping. After it, the card names the next pipe again.
