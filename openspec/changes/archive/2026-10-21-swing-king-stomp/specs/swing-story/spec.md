## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The King stomps the deck
While every pipe is still on, the King SHALL only throw. After a pipe is off, and before the finale, his attacks SHALL alternate a throw and a stomp, the throw first. During a stomp's wind-up the mission card SHALL say he is stomping and to get off the deck. A stomp SHALL take a heart when the hero is on the ground within 28 m of him. A stomp SHALL NOT take a heart when the hero is in the air.

#### Scenario: A throw, then a stomp
- **WHEN** one pipe is off and the King finishes a throw
- **THEN** his next attack is a stomp and the card says to get off the deck

#### Scenario: The deck and the air
- **WHEN** that stomp lands
- **THEN** a hero on the deck loses a heart, and a hero in the air does not
