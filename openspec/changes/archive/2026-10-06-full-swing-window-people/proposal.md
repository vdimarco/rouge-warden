# Calmer window colours, fewer people, and people who move

## Why

The player said the windows still look bad: "the colored windows don't really make sense", "there are too many people in
the windows", and people in them "should be moving around, not just standing there".

## What changes

- Lit rooms take warm lamp light only (the painted warm light, a warmer orange or a soft white). The cool blue, the
  flickering TV blue and the pink neon go.
- Dark glass is one muted blue-grey in close tones, in place of blue, teal, green, bronze and slate.
- Curtains are cream, beige or a muted red.
- The static person in the procedural rooms goes. Of the 32 painted rooms, the 8 that show people are no longer used as
  rooms; each window shows one of the 24 rooms with nobody in them.
- About one lit room in eight has someone walking across it: a backlit silhouette that walks out, stands, walks back and
  stands again, with swinging legs and arms and a bob in the step, each on its own loop.
- New painted art with Higgsfield could not be made: the account has no credits left.
