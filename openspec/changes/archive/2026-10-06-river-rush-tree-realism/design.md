# Design

Use fal Nano Banana Pro for neutral bark albedo and isolated broadleaf foliage.
Pack local WebP textures with clean alpha and derive a bark normal map. Keep
original local materials as optional-image fallbacks; do not swap late images
during play. Record prompts, request IDs, URLs and hashes.

Replace rigid limb cylinders with a shared instanced curved tube shader,
continuous endpoint tangents and taper, still in two batches. Enlarge and vary
crowns with natural leaf cards. Matching decorative trees remain outside the
river on both banks and share the same batch/resource budget, with hazards
always placed first. Shade and prewarm everything before active simulation.
The 2D renderer uses the same curves and new photographic textures.

Check actual approach and successful duck at phone, desktop and landscape,
2D and reduced motion; compare before/after screenshots, pause pixels, lane
clearance, startup uploads and bounded counts. Exercise WebGL-to-2D recovery
and missing texture fallbacks. Do not infer physical phone FPS from SwiftShader.
