# Design
Actionview writes and uploads every populated model and fallback car color each update. Compare incoming RGB after Math.fround against existing Float32 attribute values; update changed slots with three-component ranges. Reuse Three update-range merging, preserve pending writes across updates before render and hidden frames, never clear pending color ranges on unchanged frames. Initial allocation remains full. Matrix uploads/culling/selection unchanged. Existing populated upload and marker dirty patterns are the source; no shader technique introduced.

The installed Three WebGLAttributes invokes onUploadCallback after both initial allocation and updates; initial bufferData otherwise leaves ranges intact. Color attributes clear completed ranges in onUpload, preserving pending writes until actual upload and preventing initial ranges inflating first repaint. Verified in vendored source; upstream reference https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/master/src/renderers/webgl/WebGLAttributes.js .
