## Sources
- Faraway (Steph Thirion, Annapurna Interactive, 2026), Steam store page, read 2026-11-07: a shooting star with a short life links stars to harvest energy in an endless, procedurally made space, plus 16 handcrafted puzzle stages. Ember Drift borrows that broad idea only. It does not use Faraway's name, art, music, rules text or levels; its stages were designed here without knowledge of Faraway's.
- Glow: every star, ember, comet and beacon glow is one radial gradient pre-rendered to a small offscreen canvas and stamped with `drawImage`, the approach in MDN's "Optimizing canvas" guide (pre-render similar primitives on an offscreen canvas). Particles draw as small squares with `globalCompositeOperation = "lighter"`.
- No other rendering source fit. The sky is plain Canvas 2D: parallax dust, dashed rings for clouds and stage bounds, and lines for chains and shapes.

## Decisions
- **One file.** The game is a single `index.html` with inline CSS and JS and no build step, like the other static games. It loads `/arcade/quiet.js`, `/arcade/switch.js` and `/arcade/analytics.js`. Menus are rebuilt on each open, so each menu calls `GameSwitch.wire()` and adds a Switch game button to its last row. Opening the switcher mid-run pauses the run, and game keys are ignored while the switcher is open.
- **Fuel is the clock.** Endless drain is `6 + 0.4 x depth + min(10, 0.055 x seconds)` per second, plus 24 in a cloud and 11 while boosting. A star gives 5 (12 for a big star, 25 for a flare) plus one per chain link up to 6, so a chain keeps the ember alive and loose flying does not.
- **Comets aim at the ember's future.** A comet starts where it will reach a point near the ember's path in 2 seconds, offset by up to 80 px to the side, so a straight flight gets hit and a turn or boost avoids it. An arrow on the screen edge shows each comet before it arrives.
- **Stages are tuned by an autopilot.** A node harness flies every stage with a greedy pilot that steers round clouds. Each stage's fuel is what the pilot used plus room to fly about 50% slower; par is 1.15 times the pilot's time. All 16 stages clear with the stage fuel.
- **Difficulty measured.** Over 8 endless runs each, a careful pilot that dodges comets lives about 107 to 127 seconds and a careless one about 15 seconds, before this change's harder rules the same pilots lived 139 and 19 seconds.
- **Saves.** `ember-best` (best endless score, also shown on the cabinet), `ember-top` (top five runs), `ember-stage-<n>` (sparks) and `ember-stime-<n>` (best time) in localStorage, each read inside try/catch.
- **Corridor.** Ember Drift is piece 19, after Frostwing, the last Action machine. The marquee, screen and spill light slots move from 41/82 offsets to 42/84 and the uniform arrays from 123 to 126.
