# Point at the King's pipe

Marvel's Spider-Man 2 (Insomniac, 2023) keeps the objective marker on the thing the mission just asked for. The clog card in this game already does that for a district. The King card did not. None of the grass, water, outline, or painted-look sources fit. This is the same compass the clog card uses.

## Decisions

- The nearest pipe that is still on him is the goal, measured the same way as a clog: horizontal distance from the chest, refreshed every frame.
- The card detail is "Next pipe, N m". The count of ripped pipes stays on the title. When none are left, the finale already replaces the card.
- The compass title is "The King's pipe". A goal with kind pipe wins over a leftover clog, so waking him in a test does not leave the compass on a district.
- A job goal and a trial ring are unchanged. The King still wakes at twelve, and this does not change the fight.
