## Why

When the King wakes, the card says to rope his pipes and does not say which one or how far. The compass still hunts a clog. Spider-Man 2 points at the thing you are supposed to do.

## What changes

- While the King is awake, the card says FLUSH THE KING and names the nearest pipe that is still on him, with the distance in metres.
- That distance drops on the next frames when the hero moves toward the pipe.
- The compass title is "The King's pipe" and it points at that same pipe. A clog still in the city does not take the compass.
- The clog card, the job compass, and the ring compass are unchanged.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `swing-jobs`: the King fight points the card and the compass at a pipe.
