# The chase clock has a body

## Why

Falling behind added a click in the same register as the hats. A four-second render of the chase was only a little brighter than the groove with no chase. Spider-Man 2's chase tightens into a pulse you can tell from the stroll.

## What changes

- The chase clock is a wood block under the click.
- Keeping pace ticks on the quarter notes. Falling behind ticks on the eighths and hits harder.
- Closing, and the first-lesson wait, stay quiet. A fight keeps its own pulse.

## Out of scope

- The rest of the groove, the fight layer, and the one-shot stingers stay.
