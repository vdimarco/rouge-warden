## Why
The user finds the motion unfluid and wants the speed and immediate response of Subway Surfers. Current presentation has reversed foam movement, unregistered paddle frames, delayed pose blends and redundant water frame copies.
## What Changes
- Retune speed and action durations, lane settling and rapid swipe chaining while retaining fair courses.
- Align raft anchors and smoothly blend neighboring paddle frames; switch action poses immediately with continuous action movement.
- Correct perspective current direction; synchronize river playback to run speed, add directional speed feedback and reduce foreground clutter.
- Cache video by decoded frame, cap backing-buffer pixel count and avoid redundant background drawing and inactive rendering.
## Impact
Runner engine, canvas presentation, controls, timing/performance verification and existing arcade release. Preserve approved face, hair, loincloth, fal.ai media, title, layout and storage.
