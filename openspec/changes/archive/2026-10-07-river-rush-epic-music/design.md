# Design

Generate one original instrumental orchestral adventure cue using the verified `elevenlabs/music/v2.5` endpoint through fal. The schema supports guaranteed instrumental output, explicit duration and browser-compatible MP3. One 106.667-second generation at the checked price of $0.60/minute has an estimated generation cost of $1.067, excluding unknown billing adjustments. The generic model router did not resolve a music recommendation; its live catalog candidate was verified against the exact endpoint schema rather than submitting the unresolved image/video recommendation.

Prepare a continuous loop offline, normalize its mix, and serve the resulting compressed MP3 from the arcade itself. Do not fetch generation-provider URLs at runtime. Use one reusable streaming media element with a bounded Web Audio graph. Course intensity and Rush smoothly adjust the soundtrack mix without decoding or allocating audio on animation frames.

Sound defaults on for a new visitor only when they start a run; persist their mute preference. No title autoplay. Pause/end/menu/tab hide stop playback; explicit Resume or Retry reuses the graph and continues appropriate playback. Keep effect cues above the score. A failed soundtrack request or rejected play promise must not stop simulation, effects or controls. Clean up the media and context on unmount.

Redstone visibility restores existing procedural-world continuity. The renderer previously treated the engine's `done` crossing flag as destruction, deleting successfully dodged rocks at raft center. Retain resolved physical obstacles until their shared 16 m retirement behind the raft; store destruction on the struck entity for shield/Rush hits so expired effects cannot make it reappear. Apply the same visibility rule in primary 3D and fallback without changing contact or score timing.

Browser geometry inspection also found the canyon horizon shrinking solid mesas to zero while still 127–490 m ahead. Preserve course-anchored solid geometry across those approach distances and recycle only after its full extent has safely passed behind the camera, with bounded prepared instances and clearance from navigable lanes.
