## Why
The camera can orbit underneath characters and the phone action buttons are too small.

## What Changes
Clamp upward look to 30 degrees and keep the camera at back height. Replace the look pad with drag-anywhere only. Put larger action buttons in two bottom-right rows and reserve native HUD space. Preserve rendering quality, gyro and movement controls.

## Impact
Native walking/driving cameras, streaming launcher, HUD, browser markup/styles and their QA consumers.
