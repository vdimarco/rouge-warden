# Design

Adapt Three.js's [responsive drawing-buffer guidance](https://threejs.org/manual/pages/responsive.html). Keep the existing ceiling, multiply its ratio by a bounded session scale. Three-second active windows over25ms mean frame interval with a majority of slow intervals trigger a15% linear reduction, no lower than half the initial ratio. The next three-second window must improve mean frame interval by at least10%; otherwise roll back and stop to avoid sacrificing detail for CPU/browser pacing limits. Inactive/hidden/XR frames break sampling continuity. Resize resets the trial; no saved setting. A successful change can soften fine3D detail, while DOM remains native. This is a trial until matched hardware evidence and visual review justify release; no affected-profile claim.
