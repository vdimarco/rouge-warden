# Afterlight
A journey to restore a broken network of beacons. Keep the existing dense halftone artwork and quiet monospace interface. The player walks/sails/drifts using a consistent dot avatar, explores landmarks, activates tools and interacts with nearby people/devices. A travel map connects six scenes. Restoration opens routes and grants tools used elsewhere; inventory and damage persist across travel. Optional caches/upgrades and alternate routes create decisions beyond a single prescribed score loop. Recovery at safe camps/checkpoints prevents a soft lock.

Forest light restoration supplies seeds and reveals paths. City power repairs grant a sonar instrument and light buildings/routes. Coastal rescues unlock charted fjord passage and illuminate a harbor. Fjord bell recovery clears ice/strengthens aurora and grants a compass. Desert navigation restores an oasis and opens the lunar gateway. Lunar gardens must be planted from seeds transported through the journey; growth alters local gravity and completes the network. Terrain, fog, buildings, vegetation, reflections and wildlife change structurally with restoration, not just HUD counters. World state persists on return; movement/time/weather react during active play. Reduced motion freezes purely decorative animation while discrete state-driven changes remain visible.

Implementation is delegated into a deterministic campaign/world engine, a scene/evolution renderer, and root UI/integration ownership. Agree data contract before coding. No generated raster assets: preserving the explicitly requested original ASCII style is intentional. Original modules remain locally imported with MIT credits. Verification covers complete campaign via legal controls, branch/resource/upgrade decisions, loss/recovery/save/resume, actual before/after scene pixels, pause and keyboard/touch layouts.

Final campaign decisions: forest restoration opens city/coast in either order; harbor progression converges on the city sonar. Groves offer two harvested seeds or one seed plus permanent energy capacity; the initial pack offers salvage or tool efficiency. Shield protection competes for salvage. Camps and emergency salvage keep all branches recoverable. Lunar gardens modify hazard gravity. The ending supports revisiting all restored places.
