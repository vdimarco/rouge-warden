## Why
The six scene games are too simple. The user chose one larger adventure and wants the scenery to evolve with play.
## What Changes
Add Afterlight, a connected exploration campaign through forest, city, coast, fjord, desert and moon. Shared inventory, tools, resources, hazards, branching travel and persistent environmental restoration replace isolated short score rounds.
## Impact
New /afterlight/ static game, arcade entry, shared switcher, campaign QA and evolving adapters for existing locally licensed scenes.
