# Held swing chains the next building

Marvel's Spider-Man 2 (Insomniac, 2023) lets a held swing catch the next web at the release point. The words and the city stay the ones already in this game. None of the grass, water, outline, or painted-look sources fit.

## Decisions

- Flat play with a mouse or a pad, not a phone and not a headset. Toggle hold is unchanged: a press still lets go of its own rope.
- The building rope stays while the button is down until the GO cue has been showing for a third of a second, so the LET GO caption can be read. Then the rope lets go with the cue's boost, and the button still down catches the next building after the short cool that already follows a let-go.
- The next web skips the building just left, and it does not fire at open sky. It looks farther along the swing when the near point is empty. With no other building ahead, nothing dry-fires.
- A clog, a pipe, the crack, and the other special ropes stay while the button is held. Letting the button up still lets the rope go and does not catch another building.
