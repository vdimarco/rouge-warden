# Through the Gust

The third flush starts a new job: swing through a street gust three times. The card names THROUGH THE GUST and SWING THROUGH, and it does not say to flush the clogs. Each pass is an airborne crossing of the column at swing speed. A pass lifts the swing and counts on the card. Three passes end the job. A 28 s clock that runs out misses as blown off. The King still sleeps. After the gust, the clog card returns at 3/12. Flush twelve still wakes him. The first flush stays on the clog card. The gust is not an odd-job offer and it does not move the story route.
