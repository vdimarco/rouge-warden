# Splat Isle: an island quest

## Why
The user asked: "add more objects to break in splat isle and increase the quality and make this a game like the little prince but island version". The toy had one island with eight things to tap. The user wants a game: a small world of tiny islands, each with someone who lives there, and a keeper who tends their own home island.

## What changes
- Five islands on one sea: Home Isle (yours), Orchard Isle, Bell Isle, Wreck Isle and Lantern Isle. You stand on one; the others show on the horizon as quick, sparse copies. The Map sails between them.
- You play the keeper, a small figure with a hood and a lantern. Tap the ground (or use WASD or the arrow keys) to walk; tap a thing (or press E, Space or Act) to use it, talk, or hit it.
- A story chain to relight the dark lighthouse: the lamp keeper sleeps and only the bell can wake them; the bell needs a clapper; a stone from the orchard wall will do once the gardener's thornbushes are cleared; awake, the lamp keeper needs oil; the fisher trades oil for the net rope lost in a crate. An end card shows when the lamp is lit, and play goes on.
- Home chores: water the lantern-bloom from the well each day, pull the thorn sprouts that come up each dawn (left two days they grow into thorn trees that take three hits), and feed fruit to the goat until it follows you and lets you ride it.
- Twelve new kinds of things to break: crates, barrels, clay pots, thornbushes, thorn trees, thorn sprouts, the old stone wall, crystals, mushrooms, fences, statues and hay bales. Some come back at once, some at dawn, some never. Seven shells hide in them.
- A day of four minutes: a sky that turns from day to dusk to a starry night and back, with lamps, windows and the flower still glowing at night, and a turning beam once the lighthouse is lit.
- Higher quality: about a third more strokes, grass strokes on the ground, a cool fill light on the shade side, a soft outline in the pixel pass, a sky dome, and soft shadows under people and the goat.
- The game saves in the browser.
- The cabinet, switcher, Corridor and Lab card subtitles describe the game.

## Removed
New island (random layouts) and the goat's fixed path: the islands are fixed places now, and the goat wanders, follows and carries you. The Pixel, Palette, Goop and Shapes controls move into a Look panel.

## Originality
The game takes the feeling of a small world with one person on each island. It uses no names, words, characters or art from any book.
