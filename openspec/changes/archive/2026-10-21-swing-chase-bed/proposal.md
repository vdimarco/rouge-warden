# A clock on the chase

## Why

The groove plays the same sunset loop whether the runner is getting away or you are catching him. A play of Mission 1 could see CLOSING and FALLING BEHIND on the sticker, and the music did not change. Spider-Man 2 tightens the chase music when the target pulls away.

## What changes

- On a chase (the runner before his crew, the thief, the getaway car, the balloon) the groove gains a clock tick.
- Falling behind makes that tick faster and opens the groove. Closing takes the tick away. Holding pace keeps a slower tick.
- The first lesson, while the runner is waiting, stays quiet. A fight keeps its own pulse instead of the clock.

## Out of scope

- No new melody, no voice, no change to the city ambience or the one-shots.
- The sticker, the bomb clock and the training rows stay as they are.
