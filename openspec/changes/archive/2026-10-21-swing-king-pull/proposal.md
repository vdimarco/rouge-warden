# The King pulls you in

After a pipe comes off, the King still throws, then stomps, then pulls, and then throws again. With every pipe on he only throws. During the pull's wind-up the card says he is pulling and to shoot a web. In the air with no rope he drags you 14 m toward him, and that takes a heart when you end within 16 m. On the ground, or with a rope attached, you stay put and lose no heart.
