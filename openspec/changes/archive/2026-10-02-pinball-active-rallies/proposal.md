# Active celestial rallies

Players currently watch long orbits instead of making repeated flipper shots. In a six-seed baseline, Pulse alone completed every voyage without a flipper press. Make the existing celestial adventure depend on timing and reaction.

Scope: Full Tilt adventure physics, moving asteroid encounters, shot feedback and focused gameplay checks. Preserve the six worlds, celestial upgrade console, existing controls, gravity fields, reverse scoop and Warp Surf. No new paid art request.

The new loop is launch or flip, fly through a shifting field, then return to the dock for the next shot. Timed flipper strikes power the ball to smash asteroids and earn a bounded skill multiplier. Pulse bends momentum instead of replacing it with an aimed shot. Asteroids drift, deflect ordinary balls, break under power, and visibly warn before reappearing.
