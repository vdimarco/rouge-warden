# The sixth flush is a delivery

Marvel's Spider-Man 2 (Insomniac, 2023) breaks a run of the same objective with a different activity. The pizza, the card and the city stay the ones already in this game. None of the grass, water, outline, or painted-look sources fit.

## Decisions

- The sixth flush uses the existing pizza job. The card prefix is HOT PIE, so the drop uses the close roof band (a swing, the same reach as Mission 5) rather than the long odd-job drop.
- The pickup is on a street 18 to 80 m away, the same band as the balloon. If that band is empty, the wider fallback already used by the other beats applies.
- The fifth and the seventh flushes do not start a job. The fourth stays the balloon and the eighth stays the washer at 36 seconds.
- The King still roars only at four and eight. This beat does not add a roar. It still waits while a story mission, a win card, or a miss card is up.
