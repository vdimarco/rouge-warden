## Why

After the balloon, four clogs in a row are the same job again before the washer. Spider-Man 2 does not leave a repeated objective running that long. The sixth flush should be a different thing to do.

## What changes

- The sixth flush starts a pizza delivery. The card says HOT PIE and names the roof, and it does not say to flush the clogs.
- The fifth and the seventh flushes stay on the clog card. The fourth flush is still the balloon. The eighth is still the washer, still 36 seconds when the tower is a swing away.
- The King still roars only at the fourth and the eighth. A beat still waits out a story mission.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `swing-jobs`: the sixth flush is a pizza delivery.

`public/vr/js/main.js` (`CLOG_BEAT`), `qa/vr/clogbeat.e2e.mjs`.
