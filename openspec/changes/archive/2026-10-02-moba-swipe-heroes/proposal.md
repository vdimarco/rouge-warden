# Swipeable legends and uninterrupted controls

Replace the vertically scrolling hero grid with an animated horizontal lineup. Show three heroes on phones, four on tablets and five on wide screens. A swipe, card tap or arrow selects a hero. Every menu must fit the current viewport without vertical scrolling. A second finger must be able to cast or upgrade a skill while the movement finger remains held.

Scope includes selection, compact skill inspection and viewport-contained panels, GIF assets, and independent movement/ability pointer handling. Preserve twelve heroes, role filters, four unique spells, progression gates, match controls and the Fal citadel art.
