# Chase pressure

No new rendering technique. The card and the runner speed already exist. None of the grass, water, or outline sources fit.

The pace is adapted from the pursuit missions in Marvel's Spider-Man 2 (Insomniac, 2023): a target you are on stays catchable, and a target you lose pulls away. The lesson lines are original. The comic sheet has no miss word, so a miss stays a card and a toast, with no payoff word.

## Decisions

- The purse snatcher jogs at 6.3 m/s inside 28 m and rises toward 9.2 m/s past that gap, over 1.2 s. Back inside 28 m he eases to the jog over 0.6 s. The card says he is pulling away once that rise is most of the way there.
- The Sludge Run runner does the same, milder: 5.4 m/s inside 46 m, toward 7.4 m/s over 2 s. The first-swing wait still holds him at speed 0.
- A miss shows a lesson card for 1.7 s (GOT AWAY tells you to stay close with a swing; IT BLEW tells you to catch the runner before the drain). The toast still says what happened. No comic word, no cheer, no slow moment. The Sludge Run and the current story mission start again about 0.4 s after that card. Other jobs do not restart themselves.
