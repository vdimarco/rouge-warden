# A fourth attack for the King

No new rendering. The sludge ring and the comic words already exist. None of the grass, water, outline, or painted-look sources fit a circle painted on the deck.

Marvel's Spider-Man 2 (Insomniac, 2023) gives a boss a ground tell you leave before it lands. The words stay in this game's voice: BELCH, GLUG, SAFE.

## Decisions

- While every pipe is on, he only throws.
- After a pipe is off, attacks cycle throw, stomp, pull, belch. The attack after a pull is a belch. The attack after a belch is a throw.
- A throw that never leaves does not take a turn.
- The spot is the hero's place on the deck when the wind-up begins. It does not follow the hero.
- Still on the ground within 8 m of that spot takes a heart. Farther out, or in the air, it does not.
- During the wind-up the card says he is belching and to leave the spot. After it, the card names the next pipe again.
