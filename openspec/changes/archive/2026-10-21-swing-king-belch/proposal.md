# The King belches on a spot

After a pipe comes off, the King throws, then stomps, then pulls, then belches, and then throws again. With every pipe on he only throws. During the belch's wind-up he paints the spot under the hero, and the card says he is belching and to leave the spot. Still standing on the ground within 8 m of that spot when it lands takes a heart. A step outside that circle, or being in the air, keeps the heart.
