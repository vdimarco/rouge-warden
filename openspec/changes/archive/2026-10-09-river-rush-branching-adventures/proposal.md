## Why
Repeated coin chains and abstract layout shapes still feel mechanical. The player wants compelling reward encounters and a river that sometimes splits into two actual streams, then rejoins.

## What Changes
- Replace routine geometric coin scatter with sparse clues, action rewards, landing pockets and visible treasure objectives.
- Add seeded, organically shaped islands that divide the river into two traversable streams and taper back into the five-lane river.
- Give each stream a coherent multi-beat encounter and different risk/reward; vary the side and encounter theme.
- Share topology across steering/contact, moving enemies, water, bank/island terrain and both renderers.
- Preserve speed, continuous controls, strict physical pickups, finite maps, natural branch anatomy and fixed resource bounds.

## Impact
River Rush course generation, world topology, scoring/treasure encounters, WebGL and fallback presentation, compact route cues/help, meaningful simulation/browser verification, and the existing GitHub/arcade publication.
