## Why

Every won job plays the same brass fanfare a finished ring trial plays. Spider-Man 2 marks a finished mission with a sting that fits the mission, and keeps a longer fanfare for a timed run.

## What changes

- A won flush plays a wet sting. A won fight plays a hit. A won chase or delivery plays a dash. A won rescue plays a lift.
- A won job does not play the ring-trial fanfare. A finished ring trial still does.
- A failed job plays none of the win stings. The hop, the comic word, and the cheer stay.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `swing-sound`: a job win has its own sting.
