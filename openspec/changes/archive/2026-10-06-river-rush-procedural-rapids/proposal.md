## Why
The straight, level water strip and evenly repeated banks feel like a corridor. The player requests wider water, a smaller raft in the landscape, organic procedural variation, lively rapids and a clear downstream descent.

## What Changes
- A seeded continuous course profile drives winding centerlines, changing channel width, pools and downhill chutes.
- Shared CPU/GPU surface equations align water, buoyancy, hazards, banks and scenery, including downhill pitch.
- Replace broad cloudy water with directional flow, broken whitewater crests, bank eddies, shoal foam and rapid-dependent spray.
- Pull back the chase view, vary bank spacing/scale/placement and keep all three action lanes readable.
- Preserve accepted speed, immediate controls, collision windows, identity, skeletal paddling and distinct duck/jump.

## Impact
River Rush hydrodynamics, scene renderer, procedural scenery, focused QA and committed arcade bundle. Existing fal art is reused. No new media generation is needed for procedural geometry and water.
