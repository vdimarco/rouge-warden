# Splat Isle: sound

## Why
The user asked: "add sound to splat isle". The game had no sound.

## What changes
- The sea and a little wind under everything, more wind and crickets at night.
- Slow, soft notes in each island's own key.
- Steps, knocks and cracks by what a thing is made of, a burst when it breaks, a glide when it pops back.
- A chime for a shell, a fruit, the rope or the stone; blips under each line a person says, at that person's pitch.
- The bell (and a hum with no clapper), a splash at the well, the flower watered, the goat, setting sail, the lamp lit, and dawn.
- A Sound button in the controls row. It is the arcade's one sound switch.
- `/arcade/quiet.js` loads first, and `qa/arcade/quiet.mjs` starts the page's sound the way a player does.
