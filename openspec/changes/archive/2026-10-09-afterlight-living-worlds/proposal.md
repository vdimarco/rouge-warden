# Living worlds

## Why

The connected adventure still resolves restoration as immediate contextual actions on largely passive maps. The requested iteration needs changing terrain and spatial activity, richer tool interactions, and a real game engine while retaining the ASCII direction and browser deployment.

## What Changes

- Move scene lifecycle, input, cameras, transitions and texture composition to locally bundled Phaser 3.90.0.
- Add regional weather, terrain effects, moving crews, currents and continuous restoration growth.
- Replace instant restoration with six distinct multi-step operations, including physical boat towing.
- Preserve the campaign, save compatibility, recovery, motion controls and responsive layouts.
- Verify complete journeys, operation controls, save/lifecycle behavior and exact incremental-render fidelity.
