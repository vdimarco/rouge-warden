## Why

After the washer, flushes 9, 10, and 11 are three pumps in a row before the King. Spider-Man 2 does not leave a repeated objective running that long.

## What changes

- The tenth flush starts a rooftop brawl. The card says ROOF CREW and names the wave. It does not say to flush the clogs.
- The ninth and the eleventh flushes stay on the clog card. The King still sleeps. He still roars only at the fourth and the eighth, and he still wakes at twelve.
- The fourth flush is still the balloon, the sixth is still the pizza, and the eighth is still the washer at 36 seconds.
