# A fight before the King

Marvel's Spider-Man 2 (Insomniac, 2023) breaks a repeated objective before it runs through the last stretch into a boss. The words and the city stay the ones already in this game. None of the grass, water, outline, or painted-look sources fit.

## Decisions

- The tenth flush is the break. Nine and eleven stay clogs, so the three pumps in a row become one pump, a fight, one pump, then the King.
- The fight is the rooftop brawl already in the game, on a roof a swing away (24 to 140 m), with the card prefix ROOF CREW. It is not a new gang and not a new move.
- The King still roars only at four and eight, and he still wakes at twelve. A miss of the brawl does not retry it. Reloading a save that already has ten clogs does not replay it, because the beat runs on the flush.
