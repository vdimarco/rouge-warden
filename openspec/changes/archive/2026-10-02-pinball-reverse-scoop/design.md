# Design

Use a fresh press of the matching flipper to trigger a localized gravity scoop when the ball is below the dock flippers. Give the scoop a visible reverse-sweep effect and an upward recovery impulse. Limit the reachable area and recovery rate; holding a button must not repeatedly rescue the ball. Extend the drain beneath the recovery area so a player can react. Normal flipper shots and classic-table physics stay unchanged.

Validate both flippers, misses, held input, pause/resume, and subsequent normal shots. Exercise touch controls in portrait and landscape as well as Z/X on desktop. Keep the existing space art and musical score.
