# One button starts the swing

## Why

On a computer the first swing asked for a forward hold as well as the swing button. A press on its own attached the rope, slid the hero across the start roof, and friction stopped him there. Spider-Man 2 starts a web swing from the swing button. The stick still steers, and it is not required to leave the roof.

## What changes

- On the ground, a swing input with no move input walks the hero toward that web at walking speed.
- A rope on a clog, a pipe, or the crack does not walk the hero. A move input still steers.
- The hint strip, the training row, the spoken line, the title note and How to play name the swing button and not a forward hold.

## Out of scope

- Phone play already launches off a roof on a tap. That launch stays.
- The hop stays at 0. The release cue, the gold-ring row and the eight training rows stay.
