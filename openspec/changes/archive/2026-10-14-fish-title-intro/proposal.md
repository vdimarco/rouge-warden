# Painted title sequence on Reel It In

## Why

The painted title at Loon Lake is a still picture. A finished title sequence already exists as two web videos, portrait and landscape, and the title should play it without getting in the way of fishing.

## What Changes

- Ship the portrait and landscape clips, with a poster still of each, in `public/fish/clips/`.
- On the title at Loon Lake in the Painted style, play the matching clip muted in the logo's space. Portrait screens get the vertical film. Landscape screens get the horizontal film.
- Skip closes it. Play starts it when it is not running. Unmute turns its sound on. The menu, including Go fishing, stays usable the whole time.
- Reduced motion and Calm effects show the poster until the player chooses Play.
- Starting a game dismisses the sequence. Opening Settings, How to play, the Journal, or Places only pauses it.
