# Clog beats

No new rendering. The balloon, the washer, and the clog card already exist. None of the grass, water, outline, or painted-look sources fit.

Marvel's Spider-Man 2 (Insomniac, 2023) breaks a long checklist with a different activity, then hands you back to the list. These two beats are the jobs already in the city. There is no new character.

## Decisions

- The fourth flush starts UP FOR AIR, a balloon to bring back. The eighth starts GET HIM DOWN, a washer to carry to the street. They are not the same job.
- The card names that job until it ends, then FLUSH THE CLOGS returns with the count. One flush, and the flushes in between, stay on the clog card. All twelve still wake the King.
- A beat waits while a story mission is running. It does not start again on its own if it is missed.
