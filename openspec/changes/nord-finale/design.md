# One day, five short stages

No new rendering technique is involved, so none of the usual art references fit. Each mark is an existing flat cube or caption. No new mesh or shader is introduced. The lap ghost stays on the race route at its own pace. The police pursuit model stays on wanted cars. This beat does not call either of them, and it does not spawn a car.

The five earlier beats stay as they are. This is one mission in a new place, shorter than each of them, with one purse at the end. A miss returns to the stage that failed.

The foot marks share X = 32800, on open lawn. Meet (32800, 19000), pocket (32800, 16200), south dark (32800, 13700), page (32800, 10500) to drop (32800, 7000), line (32800, 5200) to finish (32800, 2200). The lamps are (32300, 14950) and (33300, 14950), radius 6.5 m. The straight line passes 5 m from each lamp, inside the light. A point 9 m west of the west lamp is dark. Foot reach is 4.5 m. Every foot mark is more than 14 m from a road centre.

The page is 35 m centre to centre, and 26 m from the south edge of the case to the north edge of the drop. The clock starts anywhere inside the case, including that south edge. Five seconds still loses a walk from that edge (26 m at 450 cm/s) and still beats a sprint from the north edge (35 m at 800 cm/s). One long frame only spends a quarter of a second. The clock runs if they mount, and a rider cannot take or drop the page.

Their road bends through (34600, 3700) and is longer than the 30 m of grass. They run at 1100 cm/s, faster than a sprint. The grass is 21 m from the south edge of the line to the north edge of the finish. Their time still loses a walk from that edge and still loses to a sprint from the north edge. A sprint on their road does not beat them. A tie at the finish goes to them. A rider cannot start the line or take the finish.

The car stage is the surveyed base-map edge from (36384.939, -13092.345) to (38188.081, -4938.155), not the mission-15 road and not the radar copy. The seat is 38 m along that edge and the hide is 45 m farther. The tail waits 12 m behind the seat and seeks at 1100 cm/s. Catch radius is 6 m. Car reach is 11 m, short of the comic stand. A drive at 1800 cm/s pulls away. A crawl at 400 cm/s is caught. A stop is caught. Getting out does not freeze the tail. A tie on the hide goes to the tail. With nobody present, the tail does not seek the world origin. The drive stays more than 2.5 m from every surveyed building shell.

If anyone is already inside the current mark, that person is the one the beat watches, even when a partner is closer to the other mark. On the dark, any person in a lamp burns the crossing, including a partner who is not the one being watched.

The slot writer moves from version 8 to version 9. Version 8 still accepts the chase and forces the finale to 0. Version 9 accepts a finale stage only after the chase is banked, and only when completed missions are 2. The burn, both clocks, and the tail's place are session state, so a load keeps the stage and clears them. Recover on the dark clears the burn and does not rewind the stage. Recover on the page and the cut leaves the clocks. Recover beside the seat does not park the tail.
