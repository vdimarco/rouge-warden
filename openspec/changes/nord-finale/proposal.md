# Once more, shorter

## Why
The walk, the dark, the page, the grass, and the tail are five separate days. Nothing asks the player to carry all five in one run, and a miss on one of them does not throw away the others.

## What changes
- Mission 16 opens once the tail is lost. Walk up, cross a shorter dark, sprint a five-second page, cut the grass, then drive a short hide.
- A fail returns to that stage. The meet, the pocket, the page, the line, and the seat stay where they were. Nothing is paid until the hide.
- The hide pays 800 session cash once. The seven story banks stay seven. The lap ghost and the police chase stay as they are. No extra car is spawned.
- A walker does the first four stages. A real car does the last. A bike, a pedalo, a scooter, or a rider cannot clear a foot stage or take the seat or the hide.
- A straight walk through the two lamps burns the dark. The pocket clears it. A sprint beats the page and the grass, including from the far side of the trigger. A walk does not, including from the south edge. A crawl on the last road is caught. Getting out does not stop that tail.
- Version 9 of the campaign slot stores the stage. Version 8 still loads and starts this day over. Version 1 still loads and starts the pond over. Version 2 stays unread and is not overwritten. The burn, the page clock, the rival's clock, and this tail's place are not saved. A reload keeps the stage and clears those.
- Recover stands off the current stage. On the dark it clears the burn. It does not rewind the stage, the page clock, the rival, or the tail. A race replay does not rewind the day and does not write the slot.

## Out of scope
An eighth chain link, a phone button, a second copy of any one of those five beats, a new mesh, and any change to the police pursuit or the lap ghost.
