# Proposal: a slide that pays

Fun factor was still "not played." The car already slides: the handbrake brakes at 2,300 cm/s², yaws harder above 450 cm/s, and drops sideways grip from 8 to 1.15. Nothing told the player the slide had happened. This round adds one banner when a shove at speed has held for 0.35 s, and a scripted drive that earns it.
