# Streak core

The rain volume is already the camera-centred box from Sarah Tariq, "Rain", NVIDIA SDK 10 (2007). That chapter's textured-quad falloff does not fit here: the streak material has no texture sample, and the displacement file is locked by the rain tests. The bright core, soft skirt, and leading knot are multiplies on the existing line-and-sine mask. The vertex offset, wrap, and visibility mask stay as they are.
