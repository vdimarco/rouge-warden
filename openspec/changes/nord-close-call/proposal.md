# A close call pays on top of the chain

## Why
Fun is a 6 the rules can prove. The chain pays the same amount whether the bank is early or late in the clear window.

## What changes
- The first quarter-second the ball spends on the middle panel, once the ink is clear, pays a close bonus.
- The first quarter-second after the convoy beam pays the same kind of bonus.
- The bonus is 50 times one more than the chain held. A spoil pays nothing.
- A long chain pushes the court ink over the taught sample, so that sample stops being a close call. A later clear bank still lands.
- The card names the close bonus on the return and on the gap.
- The story bank's own pay does not change.

## Out of scope
A ninth mission, a new fail, a change to the chain's pay, stream latency, and a new art technique.
