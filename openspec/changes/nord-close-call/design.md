# The front of the clear window

No new rendering technique is involved, so none of the usual art references fit. The bonus is a rule on the windows the beats already have.

On the court, a close call is the first 0.25 seconds the ball spends on the middle panel, and only while the ink is clear. The taught sample, 0.2 seconds onto that panel, is a close call at chain 0 and ink at chain 6. A bank 0.4 seconds onto the panel still lands at both.

On the convoy, a close call is the first 0.25 seconds after the beam. The 1.5 second pull stays a normal bank at chain 0.

The bonus is 50 times one more than the chain held, and it stops at a chain of six. A spoil pays nothing. The cash is added beside the story bank, which still pays what it paid before. The v1 slot still writes only Version and CompletedMissions.
