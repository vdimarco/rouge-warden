# A painted trough

No shading paper fits. The facade shader returns one colour, and the trough is another mark in that colour, the same way the gallery rail is. It sits under the sill (`Cell.y` near 0.105), wider than the pane by a small margin, and it is multiplied by `1 - Opening` so a door does not grow a bar across the leaf. The lip is a thinner, lighter strip at the top of the trough. It is drawn after the lamp apron, so the iron covers the apron only where the trough is, and the apron remains on the brick beside it.

Unreal is not installed here. The offline picture is the same straight elevation as the palette round, with the trough added. It is not an engine capture.
