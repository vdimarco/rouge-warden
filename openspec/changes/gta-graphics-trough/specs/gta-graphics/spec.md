## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Windows carry an iron trough
The facade shader SHALL draw an iron trough under each window and SHALL leave the door opening clear of that trough. The gallery rail and the lamp apron SHALL keep their colours.

#### Scenario: An elevation with windows and a door
- **WHEN** the facade shader shades a wall that has windows and a ground-floor door
- **THEN** each window has a dark trough with a lighter lip, and the door opening has neither
