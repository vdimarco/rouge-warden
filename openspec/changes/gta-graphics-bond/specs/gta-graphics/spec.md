## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Odd courses read as headers
The facade SHALL split odd courses with a second vertical joint and a separate tint on each half. Even courses SHALL stay one stretcher wide. The salt crust and the lamp apron SHALL stay.

#### Scenario: Look at the bond
- **WHEN** a straight elevation of the facade is shaded
- **THEN** odd courses show shorter units than the even courses, and the glass and the warm apron stay
