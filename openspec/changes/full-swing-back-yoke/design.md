# The upper back panel

## Source

Mitchell, Francke, and Eng, Illustrative Rendering in Team Fortress 2 (2007). The cel flattens the colour map, so a material break has to be painted back onto the albedo after the bands. The cream belt in round 17 is the same idea. This panel is the next break on the surface the chase camera sees.

## Decisions

The test is in the hips' frame: up the spine, out from that axis, and toward the face. The model faces +z, so the back is the negative of that axis. A roll carries the panel with the jacket. A world-up band would stay horizontal through the roll.

The panel is centered 42 cm above the hip joint, about 22 cm tall before it fades, and it stays inside the torso. The chest side is left red. The belt stays at 8 cm above the hip. The colour is a dark wine, mixed after the cel, so the bands do not lift it back to the jacket red.

No new mesh. The draw stays the body, the ink hull, and the hair and collar.
