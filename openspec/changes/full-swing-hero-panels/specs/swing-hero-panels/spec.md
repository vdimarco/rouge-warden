## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The chase jacket keeps its painted panels
At chase distance the hero's jacket SHALL show more than one flat red. The painted light and dark of the colour map SHALL remain visible inside the cel bands. The skinned triangle count SHALL stay inside 10,000 to 14,000.

#### Scenario: Play starts on the avenue
- **WHEN** play starts and the hero stands on the north-south avenue, seen from behind
- **THEN** the jacket is not one flat red, the chase distance stays 4.5 m, and the pitch stays about −0.35

### Requirement: The hero stays at four draws
The panel read SHALL NOT add a mesh.

#### Scenario: The hero is in the frame
- **WHEN** the chase view draws the hero
- **THEN** the hero's draws are still the body, the ink hull, the hair forms, and that forms' outline
