# The tab

## Why

The last beats are one verb. A complete errand needs three: walk, drive, and stand, in that order, with a slip that does not throw away the step already banked.

## What changes

Mission 32, THE TAB, opens after the post. Walk to the counter. Drive the only car through the lane inside a speed band, nose down the road. Stand still for the receipt. Too fast, too slow, or the wrong nose drops the lane and keeps the counter. Moving starts the receipt count again and does not save. One purse, 760, when the tab closes. Version 25 writes the step. Version 24 starts the tab over. The receipt count and the lane warning stay in the session.

## Impact

- Affected specs: nord-tab
- Affected code: the tab rules, the campaign tail, and the story slot
