# Proposal: the slide pay is heard

Round 85 gave a held handbrake slide one SLIDE banner. The banner was silent. The lock chirp marks the catch, about 0.35 s before the pay, so nothing the player heard said the slide had paid. This round plays two quick rising notes on the same frame as the banner.

## Out of scope

The slide rule, the banner text, the lock chirp, the engine hum, the rank pitches, and the music path stay as they are.
