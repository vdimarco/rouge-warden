# Two lamps, and the straight line is seen

No new rendering technique is involved, so none of the usual art references fit. Each lamp is an existing cylinder and caption, and each pocket is an existing flat cube. No new light, shadow, or shader is introduced. The wanted stars are a different system and this beat does not touch them.

The north pocket is (50700, 14500) and the south dark is (50700, 9000), 55 m due south. The lamps are (50000, 12000) and (51400, 12000), each 8 m across. The straight line between the pockets passes through both lights. A west path at x=48500 stays outside them. Recover is 9 m north of the pocket, outside the 450 cm pocket and outside both lamps. Every one of those points is east of the ponds and more than 14 m from a road centre.

Only a dry walker in the north pocket opens the crossing. A lamp, or anyone else in a lamp, burns it. Only a dry walker in the south dark, with a clean attempt, pays 520. The purse is the campaign's own cash grant with chain link -1, so the seven banks do not move.

The slot writer moves from version 4 to version 5. Version 4 still accepts the escort step and forces the crossing to 0. Version 5 accepts a crossing step only after the walk is banked, and only when completed missions are 2. The burn is session state, so a load clears it and leaves the step.
