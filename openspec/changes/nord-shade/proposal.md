# Stay out of the lamps

## Why
Grey Coat follows and stops. Nothing in the park watches a path and fails you for being seen. The straight walk from one dark pocket to the other is the obvious way, and it is lit.

## What changes
- Mission 12 opens once the walk is banked. Two lamps stand on the east lawn. A walker who steps into the north dark pocket starts the crossing.
- A lamp burns the attempt, whether the player is on foot or riding. The south dark pays 520 session cash once only when the attempt is still clean. Going back to the north pocket clears the burn. Another person standing in a lamp burns it too.
- The seven story banks stay seven.
- Version 5 of the campaign slot stores the crossing. Version 4 still loads and starts this beat over. Version 1 still loads and starts the pond over. Version 2 stays unread and is not overwritten. The burn itself is not saved.
- Recover stands north of the pocket, outside it and outside the lamps, and clears the burn. A race replay does not rewind the crossing and does not write the slot.

## Out of scope
A new mesh or a light shader, an eighth chain link, a phone button, another person who follows, and another circuit of the bike, the pedalo, and the bank.
