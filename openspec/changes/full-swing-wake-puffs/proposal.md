# A fast rope leaves one fleck

On a fast rope the air behind the body is one cream puff every so often.

## What changes

The same rope still spawns only while it is off the ground and faster than 10 m/s, about every 0.08 s. Each birth is three puffs along the wake, the nearer one a little larger, so the air behind the body is a short streak. A stand and a quiet hang still spawn nothing. The puff and the pool are the ones the city already draws.

## Capabilities

- `swing-wake-puffs`: a fast rope leaves a short cream streak behind the body, and a stand leaves none.
