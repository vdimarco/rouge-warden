# A short wake behind a fast rope

## Source

William T. Reeves, Particle Systems — A Technique for Modeling a Class of Fuzzy Objects (SIGGRAPH 1983). A puff is born, lives, and dies. The trail is births along the path the body just left. None of the wind-field papers fit this cel pool, so the streak reuses the puff the city already draws.

## Decisions

The gate stays the one from round 19: off the ground, a rope attached, faster than 10 m/s, and a birth about every 0.08 s. A stand and a quiet hang still spawn nothing. Each birth lays three puffs back along the velocity, stepped to either side, the nearer one larger. Life stays under a second so the pool of puffs does not fill. The colour stays cream, with the same occasional gold fleck on the middle puff.
