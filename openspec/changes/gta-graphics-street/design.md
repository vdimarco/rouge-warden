# Design: look down Sherbrooke

## Source

None fit. The lane offset is already `QuebecTraffic::CurbLane` (145 cm). The sidewalk offset is already half the road width plus 150 cm in the population spawn. The picture uses those offsets on the surveyed road.

## Choice

Layout road 63 is Rue Sherbrooke Est at the starter, 14 m wide, heading about 77.5 degrees. The eye sits just past the starter, 4.2 m toward the near sidewalk, 155 cm up, pitched 12 degrees down that heading. Cars use lateral ±145 cm. Walkers use lateral ±850 cm. One lane faces the camera and one faces away, so the still reads as traffic rather than a parked row. The opening look stays at pitch −16 and the socket at (0, 70, 110). The Marie-Anne corner stays where it is.
