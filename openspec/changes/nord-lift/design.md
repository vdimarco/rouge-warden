# Stop the car and let them walk to it

No new rendering technique. The stop and the person are the same flat cubes the other beats already use. None of the usual art references fit.

The stop is (-25683.074, 18644.430), on the road. They start at (-24169.643, 17669.994), off the road, and walk the straight line at 450 cm/s. The car counts as stopped at 60 cm/s or slower, inside 6 m of the stop. One frame spends at most a quarter of a second, so a long frame does not walk them the whole way. The purse is 680, once. The step index is the only saved field. A reload puts them back at the door and keeps the step.
