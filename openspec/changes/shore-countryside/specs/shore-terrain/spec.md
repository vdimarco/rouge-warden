## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Varied countryside on open ground
The 3D Shore battlefield SHALL show varied land use on open ground: vineyards with rows on tilled soil, open meadows,
cypress trees, grey rock outcrops with heath, and rocks along the stream bed. The layout SHALL come from the match
seed, SHALL mirror between the teams, and SHALL be the same in both realms except that vines grow only in the town
realm. Land use SHALL be visual only and SHALL stay clear of lanes, water, cover, objectives, bases and river crossings.

#### Scenario: Fly over the jungle
- **WHEN** the player moves the camera over the open ground between lanes in 3D
- **THEN** they see vineyard rows, meadows with lone trees, cypress lines and rock outcrops, and no tall tree stands in a vineyard.

#### Scenario: Cross the river
- **WHEN** the player walks along the river away from a bridge
- **THEN** pale rocks line the banks and shallows, and the bridge approaches stay clear.

#### Scenario: Same rules as before
- **WHEN** a hero walks through a vineyard, meadow or outcrop
- **THEN** movement, sight and targeting work as on any other open ground.
