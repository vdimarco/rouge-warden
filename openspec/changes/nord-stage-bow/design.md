# Twelve seconds in one light

No new rendering technique is involved, so none of the usual art references fit. The light is a cylinder and a disc, painted with the gold and cream the markers already use.

The spot sits on the theatre lawn, toward the arena centre from the goon marker, far enough that standing in the light is not standing on the M. The hold is tighter than the fight's lawn. The v1 slot still writes only Version and CompletedMissions. The pay is written onto each player in the session, the same way a job pays, and it does not touch the chain.
