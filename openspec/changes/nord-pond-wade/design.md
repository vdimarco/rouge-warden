# One wade, paid once

No new rendering technique is involved, so none of the usual art references fit. The float and the bank post are engine cylinders with the comic ink, cream, and lime materials the atelier already uses. The new part is the wade rule.

The float is the south-pond swim point. The bank is 21 m away on dry ground, so the two reaches do not overlap. Only a walker or a swimmer advances the leg, and the person who tags the float is the only one who can finish it. A friend on the bank does not collect the purse. A driver is not on foot, because the car or the pedalo holds the controller. Bank becomes Buoy at the float, and Buoy becomes Done at the bank. Done stays Done, and the 80 cash is written on that player's session purse. Nothing is saved. The north-pond pedalo lap is a different rule and is not changed.
