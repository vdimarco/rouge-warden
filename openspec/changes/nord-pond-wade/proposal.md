# A wade across the south pond

## Why
The south pond is only for swimming. The pedalo circuit is on the north pond, and a walker has no reason to get in this water.

## What changes
- A float stands at the south-pond swim point, and a post stands on the dry bank 21 m away.
- Swim to the float, then walk back to the bank.
- The swimmer is paid 80 session cash once.
- A driver does not count. Standing on the bank before the float does not finish the wade.
- The v1 save is untouched.

## Out of scope
A ninth story mission, a new mesh, a save field, and a change to the pedalo lap or to car handling.
