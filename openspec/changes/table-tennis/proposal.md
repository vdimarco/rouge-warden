# Table Tennis

Add a portrait table-tennis game at `public/pingpong/` and put its cabinet where Lawn Tennis stood in the Cottage row. The phone is the bat. A swing's speed, timing, aim and wrist roll choose the shot. The first pull request is casual only: one game to 11, topspin and backspin, a swipe and mouse fallback, and the opening point that a player can win inside five seconds.

Lawn Tennis stays on disk. Its homepage cabinet and its switcher entry move to Table Tennis.

Out of scope: sidespin, a pro tier, smash replay, a handedness toggle, and serving by toss. The Neighbour serves every point.
