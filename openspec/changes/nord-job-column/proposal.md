# Show the side-job column in English

## Why

The job column still names the work in French, and the play shell never shows that column.

## What changes

A running side job shows its English name and a distance, plus damage for the thief and a down count for the crew. The play shell shows that column with no host. The prompt to start or restart a job uses the same English. The 3D signs, the objective sentences, and resident speech stay as they are. Job rules do not change.

## Impact

- Affected specs: nord-job-column
- Affected code: the job titles, the column line, the start prompt, and the play shell
