# Job column

## Decision

The six names live in `QuebecBanner.h` with the other English chrome. The column and the start prompt use them. The stream sends the name and the distance line while a job is active. The play shell paints that pair on the right.

The 3D signs stay the ASCII captions the text font can draw. Objective sentences and resident speech are not this column.

## Rendering

None of the usual art references fit. The column reuses the mission card's ink and gold. No new shading, particles, or texture step.
