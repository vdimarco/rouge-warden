# Hold the Kite

## Decision

The kite is another offer in the existing odd-job list. It rises in place above the street where the offer stands, and the goal beam follows that height. The hold counts only while the hero is in the air, above the kite, and within 8 m. The tug and the catch are applied when the job emits `kite`, using the existing comic word and the existing rope-release sound.

## Rendering

No new rendering technique. The kite is the existing goal beam. No outside art source applies.

## Out of scope

No flush changes. The gust, the lid, and the loose wheel stay as they are. The kite is not a crime shout.
