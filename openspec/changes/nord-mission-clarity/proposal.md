# Mission card leads to the next side job

## Why
After the two story scenes, the mission card says both scenes are done and offers only a race replay. The four side jobs stay off the radar until the player is within 100 m, and the nearest one is farther than that from the terrace and from the spawn. A phone player who fails a job never sees how to retry, because the failure line mentioned the R key and then hid that line on touch.

## What changes
- Once the campaign is complete, the same gold diamond, distance and mission card name the nearest unfinished side job. A finished set points at the nearest replay. A running job replaces that hint with its objective, timer and restart hint.
- During the story the campaign objective keeps the diamond. The 100 m radar dots for idle markers stay as they are.
- On a touch screen, MISSION RATÉE says Menu · Recover, which is the button already in the game menu.

## Out of scope
New story missions, marker positions, rewards, the campaign save, and the calm-radar capture rule.
