# The curator buys the last page

## Why
Leave the Last Page Blank ends on one pull. The curator already wants the moment kept, and nothing lets you take his offer.

## What changes
- On the last page, a second post stands southeast of the blank page.
- E there sells the page for 200, ends the beat, and does not bank the chain.
- E on the blank page still banks the chain. The sale pays less than that bank at chain zero.
- The v1 save still stores only Version and CompletedMissions.

## Out of scope
A ninth story mission, a change to the chain bases, stream latency, and a new art technique.
