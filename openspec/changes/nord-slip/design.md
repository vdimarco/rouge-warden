# Lose the pursuer on foot

No new rendering technique. The door, the gate, and the pursuer are the same flat cubes the other beats already use. None of the usual art references fit.

The lawn is the column at X=12800, from the door at Y=14000 south 72 m to the gate at Y=6800. Recover stands 7 m north of the door, outside the 4.5 m door. They wait 16 m north of the door and catch at 5 m. Close speed is 640 cm/s, between a walk at 450 and a sprint at 800. One frame spends at most a quarter of a second. The same step outside does not also clear the gate. The closest person on foot is the one being chased. A partner already at the gate does not clear it. The purse is 500, once. The step index is the only saved field. Their place stays in the session, and a reload during the run puts them behind the door again.
