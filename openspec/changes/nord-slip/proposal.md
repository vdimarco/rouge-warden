# Lose the pursuer on foot

## Why

The tail follows a car. The finale's last stage follows a car. Nothing chases a person, and nothing makes a walk fail where a sprint succeeds.

## What changes

After the stall is banked, a pursuer waits behind a door on the lawn west of the cart. Stepping outside starts them. They close at 640 cm/s. A walk is caught before a gate 72 m south, and the door is the door again. A sprint reaches the gate and pays 500 once. A car does not start the run and does not clear the gate. A catch plays the checkpoint and does not save. The step is saved on version 17. Version 16 starts this beat over. The seven story banks stay seven. The tail, the finale, and the stall are not retuned.

## Impact

- Affected specs: nord-slip, nord-unreal
- Affected code: `QuebecSlip.h`, `QuebecStorySave.h`, `QuebecCampaign`, the slip tests, and the version-17 save readers
