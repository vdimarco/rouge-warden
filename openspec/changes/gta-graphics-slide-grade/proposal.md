# Proposal: a slide gets its grade when it ends

Feedback was tied for the lowest line. Every slide got the same banner, "SLIDE · Through the corner", 0.35 s into the slide. A gentle turn and a full turn at top speed read the same. Since round 86, the tire scream gives the live feedback. So the banner can now give the result: it comes when the slide ends and grades it by its peak sideways speed. SLIDE, BIG SLIDE or HUGE SLIDE, then "Held for 0.4 s".
