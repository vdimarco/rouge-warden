# Open broadleaf crowns

The GTA graphics loop is pushing NORD toward cinematic city fidelity with original art only. The street trees read as one flat green volume. This round gives the eight broadleaf crowns a shaded underside and a sunlit cap, and keeps the lobes from fusing, without new leaf cards or any reference-game assets.

Unreal is not in this environment, so the imported map meshes stay as they are until the environment apply runs. Source tests and an offline shade of the OBJs are the check for this round.
