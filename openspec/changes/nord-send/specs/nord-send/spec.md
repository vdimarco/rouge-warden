## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Send the day home
After the block is banked, the send-off SHALL offer four stages on one lawn. A walker at the door SHALL start the drive, and a car SHALL NOT. A car under the speed cap that covers the drive and reaches the gate SHALL open the bay. A car faster than the cap SHALL start that distance over, keep the stage, and SHALL NOT write the slot. A stopped car in the bay SHALL open the walk, and a rolling car SHALL NOT. A walker at the finish SHALL bank the beat and pay 520 once, and a car SHALL NOT. The stage SHALL be saved on version 14. A version-13 slot SHALL start this beat over. How far the drive got SHALL stay in the session. The seven story banks SHALL stay seven.

#### Scenario: Bring it home
- **WHEN** the player walks to the door, drives gently to the gate, stops in the bay, and walks the last stretch
- **THEN** the day is home and the purse is 520 once
- **WHEN** the car is faster than the cap during the drive
- **THEN** the drive starts over, the stage stays, and the purse is not paid

#### Scenario: Read the send-off
- **WHEN** the objective appears at 1280×720, 1920×1080, or a supported tall phone frame
- **THEN** the existing wrapping keeps the title, the stage instruction, and the recover line readable

#### Scenario: Replay during the send-off
- **WHEN** the player replays the race while the send-off is open
- **THEN** the stage stays where it was and the slot is not rewritten
