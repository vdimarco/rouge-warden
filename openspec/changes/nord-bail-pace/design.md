# A short run-out on the way out of the car

No new rendering technique is involved, so none of the usual art references fit. The pace rule is a plain number, the same kind of engine-free rule the walk speed and the fare already use.

The player already steps out to the side of the car. After that step, if the car is moving faster than a walk, the body keeps that planar pace: the car's forward speed and its sideways slip, capped so a highway exit is a run and not a cannon. The pace eases off. A movement key, a jump, or the water ends it, and the walk or the sprint key takes over. Getting out of a slow car still plants the player. The blocked-door line is unchanged. A combat knockdown that leaves the car does not use this path. The v1 save is untouched.
