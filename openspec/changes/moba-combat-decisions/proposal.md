# MOBA combat decisions

Make the existing twelve-hero MOBA reward aim, positioning and timely responses. Restore independent control effects, expose combo states, and add readable attack commitments and objective patterns while retaining the painted art, automatic basic attacks and four active abilities per hero.

## Scope
- Cursor-directed desktop skills and touch ground-placement distance with deliberate cancellation.
- Roots restrain movement while permitted spells and attacks remain available; stun, silence and disarm keep separate rules.
- Selected strong casts lock aim, show a warning and have a short commitment. Quick defense stays responsive.
- Visible combo marks and result feedback, with distinct hero attack rhythms.
- Bounded bot reactions, shared objective pressure, and readable neutral attacks with earned recovery openings.
- Simulation regression checks and rendered desktop, portrait and landscape interaction checks.

## Capabilities
- Modify `moba-combat` and `moba-skills`.
