# Mission 5: Standing Ovation

## Why
Fun is the missions loop's next target, and the story still stops at mission 4 of 8. After the court is banked, the card drops to side jobs before the show that the rescues were building toward.

## What changes
- After the court is banked, the mission card and the gold diamond lead through a short rehearsal on dry ground south of the west pond: walk to the loading edge, set the followspot and hang the backdrop in either order, then call the curtain from that same edge.
- The curtain is the page turn. Four comic panels and a souvenir appear around the stage mark. A local procedural page sound plays once, on the step into the banked show. The three cooked cues stay Checkpoint, Complete and Dialogue.
- The show is session state. The v1 save still stores CompletedMissions 0–2. Side jobs and T wait until the curtain. R restores the current step and does not rewind a station that is already set.

## Out of scope
Missions 6–8, mouse-aimed followspot control, a server-gated multi-pawn regroup, the escape after the page, a new save field, a new art technique, and stream latency.
