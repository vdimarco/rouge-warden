# Proposal: Sherbrooke crowns are the clustered canopies

## What

The trees in the Sherbrooke frame keep their trunks. The ellipsoid crown is replaced by the checked-in canopy mesh for that tree: leaf masses on the limbs, with a shaded underside, a mid mass, and a sunlit cap.

## Why

An ellipsoid reads as one balloon. The canopy files already break the crown into masses and leave gaps. That is the foliage the park was prepared with.

## Scope

The crown helper, the Sherbrooke picture, and the scorecard. The falling-leaf cards stay at ten slots. The opening aim stays. Unreal is not installed, so the editor does not instance the meshes.
