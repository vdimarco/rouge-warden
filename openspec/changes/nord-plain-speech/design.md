# Plain speech

## Decision

The remaining sentences live in `QuebecBanner.h` with the other English chrome. Notices, prompts, 3D captions, weapon names, and resident lines read them. The stream sends the running job's objective and the resident line while that caption is up. The play shell paints both.

Place names stay names, including the streets on a delivery and Hôpital Notre-Dame. The copyright line stays. The 3D captions stay ASCII because the text-render font has no accented glyphs.

## Rendering

None of the usual art references fit. The objective sits in the existing gold column. The resident line reuses the mission card's paper. No new shading, particles, or texture step.
