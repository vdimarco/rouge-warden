# Finish the English sentences

## Why

The job column is English, and the sentences around it still mix languages: objectives, signs, resident lines, weapons, and the small notices.

## What changes

Those player-facing sentences become English. Place names stay names. The play shell shows the job objective and a resident line with no host. Job rules, rewards, heat, and the save slot do not change.

## Impact

- Affected specs: nord-plain-speech
- Affected code: the banner lines, the notices that used them, and the play shell
