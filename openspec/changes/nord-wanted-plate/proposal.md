# Show wanted and down in one language

## Why

The star plate and the down plate still mix French chrome with the English result plate. The play shell never shows them, so that mix cannot be checked without Unreal.

## What changes

A silent star rise says WANTED and a count of stars. Going down says ARRESTED or KNOCKED OUT, and names the station or the hospital. The play shell shows that plate with no host, and the stream sends the same state. The native plates and the police notices use those words. Heat, arrest timing, and the respawn places do not change.

## Impact

- Affected specs: nord-wanted-plate
- Affected code: the star plate, the down plate, the police notices, and the play shell
