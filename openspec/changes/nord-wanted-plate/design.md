# Wanted and down plates

## Decision

The words live next to the job-result lines in `QuebecBanner.h`. The native plates, the police notices, and the knockout notice call those lines. The stream sends a star count while the silent plate is up, and `arrested` or `knocked` while the player is down. The play shell turns those into the same English sentences. A down plate hides the star count, matching the native order.

Place names stay names. Poste de quartier 38 and Hôpital Notre-Dame are not translated. The job column and resident speech stay as they are.

## Rendering

None of the usual art references fit. The plate reuses the job-result panel. Wanted is paper on cyan. Down is the same pink fill as a failed job. No new shading, particles, or texture step.
