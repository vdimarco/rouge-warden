## Why
The previous height lift pushed the character out of frame, and mobile streaming still stutters. The user now prioritizes streaming performance.

## What Changes
Use a character-centered orbit bounded to -25..-5 degrees; preserve independent worklight aiming. Reduce phone stream pixels and bandwidth and investigate the relay/hardware encoding path. Retain two-row controls and scene graphics.

## Impact
Native camera and regression checks, frontend stream negotiation, native streaming recipe and performance observations.
