# Stay inside the march

No new rendering technique. The join, the step-out, and the march are the same flat cubes the other beats already use. None of the usual art references fit.

The lawn is the column at X=13600, from the join at Y=16800 south 64 m to the end at Y=10400. The step-out is 9 m further south, outside the 3.8 m circle. Recover stands 7 m north of the join. The march walks at 450 cm/s, the same pace as a walk. One frame spends at most a quarter of a second. Arriving does not also step out. The closest person on foot is the one inside the march. A partner already at the step-out does not finish it. The purse is 580, once. The step index is the only saved field. Where they have walked stays in the session, and a reload during the march puts them back at the join.
