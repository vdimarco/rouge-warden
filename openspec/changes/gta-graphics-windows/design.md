# Dusk windows

A new window model does not fit. The blocks are the city footprints the avenue shade already draws, one colour per face. At dusk the street lamps are on, and a long wall takes a grid of warm panes: up to eight across and four up, on both sides of the face so the winding does not hide them. A wall under five metres, or under four and a half metres tall, stays blank. The afternoon leaves the grid off.

The panes are flat emissive rectangles in the offline shade. They are not an engine capture. Unreal is not installed. The opening pitch and the socket stay.
