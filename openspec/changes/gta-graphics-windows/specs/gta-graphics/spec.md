## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Warm panes on dusk walls
A wall at least five metres long and four and a half metres tall SHALL carry a grid of warm panes when the street lamps are on, and SHALL stay dark when they are off. The opening pitch of −16 and the walking socket at Y 70 and Z 110 SHALL stay.

#### Scenario: A long wall is lit only after dusk
- **WHEN** a twenty-metre wall twelve metres tall is scored
- **THEN** it has panes with the lamps on, and none with the lamps off

#### Scenario: The opening camera stays
- **WHEN** the avenue is framed
- **THEN** the look stays at pitch −16 and the socket stays at Y 70 and Z 110
