# A higher socket

No shading paper fits. The walking camera is a spring arm. Its socket is already 70 cm to the figure's right and 55 cm up. Raising the up component to 110 cm moves the camera along its own up axis, so the orbit target sits lower in the frame while the view direction stays the player's pitch. Aim uses the same height and keeps its 7 cm lift, so aiming does not jump the figure vertically. The sideways 70 cm, the pitch clamps at −25 and −5, the car socket `(0, 0, 80)`, and `TargetOffset.Z` stay.

The portrait feet at a −5° pitch land at 0.853 of the half-frame, inside the 0.95 test. A 90 cm shoulder is still past 0.90. Unreal is not installed here, so `Quebec.Camera.LookBounds` did not run. The check is `Scripts/test_camera_shoulder.py`, and the picture is a perspective shade of the walking camera at pitch −12 before and after the raise.
