## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The walking figure sits low in the frame
The on-foot camera SHALL sit high enough that the figure is lower in a 16:9 frame than it was at a 55 cm socket, and the head and feet SHALL stay inside the frame. The sideways shoulder offset SHALL stay.

#### Scenario: Look down the street on foot
- **WHEN** the walking camera is shaded at pitch −12 in a 16:9 frame
- **THEN** the figure is lower than it was at a 55 cm socket, still left of center, and the head and feet remain inside the wide and tall framing limits
