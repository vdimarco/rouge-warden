# In Full Swing: people on the streets, neon shop signs, a bloom setting and a dive

## Why

The player gave a list of features from another city game (a dark isometric cyberpunk district) and asked to add some of
its ideas to Full Swing, "like people". The list named streets with shops, signage and street props; neon lighting and
adjustable bloom; pedestrian navigation, crossings, civilian activities and responses; ambient sound and volume controls.
The player then said: "the falling animation needs to be improved. Like a diving effect like Spider-Man."

The city has cars, streetcars, trees and street lamps, but nobody walks on its sidewalks. Its shop floors have no signs.
Nothing glows past its edge. A fall shows the hero with arms and legs flailing.

## What changes

- **People on the sidewalks.** Up to 160 people walk the sidewalks near the player, in both directions, at their own pace
  and in their own clothes. At a corner they turn or cross. They cross a street on its zebra only while its walk light is
  on. The walk lights of the two directions take turns. Some people stand at shop windows, look at a phone or talk in
  pairs.
- **People see the hero.** When the hero lands near them, they stop, turn to face the hero and cheer. A hard landing close
  by makes them jump back and run a few steps first. When the hero swings low over them, they look up and point. When
  the hero walks into a person, that person steps aside.
- **Shop signs and neon.** The street floors of the Market, Old Town and Warehouse districts and the avenues get blade
  signs and boards over the shop windows. They glow in neon colours, and some of them flicker.
- **Bloom.** On a computer or a phone, the bright parts of the view (neon, lit windows, the sun, the lamps) glow past
  their edges. The Comfort menu has a Bloom row: Off, Low or High. The default is Low on a computer and Off on a phone.
  A headset has no bloom.
- **Crowd sound.** A murmur rises when people are close. A cheer or a gasp comes from the people who see the hero land.
- **The dive.** A fall with no rope turns into a head-first dive: the body pitches down along the flight, the arms sweep
  back, the legs stay together and the toes point. Near the ground, or when a rope catches, the hero flips back upright.
- Version 1.10.0 (Quest APK code 11). The new files are in the offline cache.

## Out of scope

Driving, getting in and out of cars, vaulting, ladders and crouching. Cars that stop for people. A security or police
response. Meshy characters and eight-direction sprites (the game is 3D and its people are built in code). A volume
slider (the game keeps its Sound and Music toggles).
