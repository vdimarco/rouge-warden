# Opening pitch

Joseph V. Mascelli, *The Five C's of Cinematography* (1965), calls any downward tilt a high angle, and says a modest one is how you show the layout of the ground. A Dutch tilt does not fit: the street stays level. The spring-arm socket is already locked, so the pitch is the remaining lever.

Unreal is not installed. The offline picture is the walking camera (arm 420, socket Y 70, socket Z 110, horizontal FOV 90) on the brick street, with a stand-in figure. The before frame is pitch −5, the clamp of an unset pitch. The after frame is pitch −16. It is not an engine capture.

`FinishRestartPlayer` writes the start rotator after possess, so the game mode sets the pitch again after that write. Getting out of a car, a respawn, and a side-job recovery on foot use the same pitch. The car's possess pitch stays −12, and so does the drive look target. `QuebecGameplay.cpp` was not compiled.
