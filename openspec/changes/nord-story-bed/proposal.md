# A third bed for an open story beat

## Why
Sound design is a 4 the rules can prove. The city has two cooked loops, neighborhood and race. Missions 3 through 8 still play the neighborhood bed. Nobody is voiced.

## What changes
- While a story beat is open and the race is not running, a third bed plays: an original four-bar loop at 84, about 11.4 seconds, stereo, and it restarts on the downbeat.
- A race keeps the race loop even if a beat is also open.
- With no beat and no race, the neighborhood loop is unchanged.
- The two cooked loops and the selection log stay as they are. The story bed is its own component.

## Out of scope
Voiced lines, a ninth mission, a change to the cooked assets, stream latency, and a new art technique.
