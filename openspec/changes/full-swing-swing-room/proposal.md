# The fast swing leaves room for the avenue

On a fast rope the camera rises, and the body still fills the frame. The arm stays near 4.5 m.

## What changes

While the hero is on a rope, off the ground, and moving fast, the chase arm lets out past the ordinary speed length. The figure sits smaller, and more of the avenue is in the frame. At a stand, and at the hand-off, the arm stays 4.5 m and the pitch stays the chase default. No new rig.

## Capabilities

- `swing-chase-room`: a fast rope pulls the camera back so the body is smaller and the street ahead keeps the frame.
