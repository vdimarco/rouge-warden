# Swing room

## Source

Mark Haigh-Hutchinson, Real-Time Cameras (2009). Lead room: at speed the subject takes less of the frame so the space ahead of the motion stays readable. The shoulder slide and the fast-rope rise already follow that book. Nothing is taken from Spider-Man 2.

## Decisions

- The extra length is only while a rope is attached, the hero is off the ground, and horizontal speed is above 8 m/s. It eases in by 16 m/s, up to about 2.6 m past the arm the speed already asks for.
- A stand, a walk, and the hand-off stay on the 4.5 m arm. Looking up still shortens the arm the way it does now.
- The pitch is not moved by this. The rise from the fast rope stays.
- A wall still stops the arm. The ray that already shortens the room is unchanged.
