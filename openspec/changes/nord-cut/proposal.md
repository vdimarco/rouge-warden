# Beat the faster runner by cutting the grass

## Why
The lap already rides a ghost along its own route, at a fixed pace, and that ghost does not care which way you go. Nothing else in the day is a person you have to beat to a finish.

## What changes
- Mission 14 opens once the grab is banked. Step on the line and a faster runner takes the long road. The grass to the finish is shorter.
- A sprint on the grass arrives first and pays 600 session cash once. A walk on the grass does not. A sprint on their road does not. A rider cannot start the race or take the finish. When they arrive first, the line is back and nothing is paid.
- A frame longer than a quarter of a second only spends that quarter, so a hitch does not hand them the race.
- The seven story banks stay seven. The lap ghost and its route stay as they are.
- Version 7 of the campaign slot stores the short-way step. Version 6 still loads and starts this beat over. Version 1 still loads and starts the pond over. Version 2 stays unread and is not overwritten. Their clock is not saved. A reload during the race puts them back on the line.
- Recover stands north of the line, outside it and outside the finish. It does not add time. A race replay does not rewind the short way and does not write the slot.

## Out of scope
An eighth chain link, a phone button, another follower, another lit path, another timed grab, and a second copy of the lap.
