# A faster runner, a shorter line

No new rendering technique is involved, so none of the usual art references fit. The line, the finish, and the runner are existing flat cubes and captions. No new mesh or shader is introduced. The lap ghost stays on the race route at its own pace. This beat does not move that ghost.

The line is (24000, 18000) and the finish is (24000, 11000), 70 m due south across the grass. The runner's road bends through (26800, 14500), so it is longer. They move at 900 cm/s, faster than a sprint at 800 cm/s. A sprint on the grass arrives first. A walk on the grass does not. A sprint on their road does not, because they are faster and the road is longer. The straight grass and both legs of their road stay more than 2 m from every surveyed building shell. The line, the bend, the finish, and the north recover stay more than 14 m from a road centre. Recover is 9 m north of the line, outside both doors.

Only a dry walker on the line starts the race. Only a dry walker on the finish, before the runner arrives, pays 600. A tie at the finish goes to the runner. The purse is the campaign's own cash grant with chain link -1, so the seven banks do not move. If anyone on foot is inside the line or the finish, that person is the one the beat watches, even when a partner is closer to the other mark. One frame spends at most a quarter of a second.

The slot writer moves from version 6 to version 7. Version 6 still accepts the grab and forces the short way to 0. Version 7 accepts a short-way step only after the grab is banked, and only when completed missions are 2. The elapsed seconds are session state, so a load zeroes them and leaves the step.
