# Drive, then the seeker, then the gate

No new rendering technique. The end, the bay, the gate, and the seeker are the same flat cubes the other beats already use. None of the usual art references fit.

The road runs from (-152.082, 21026.122) to (-16062.937, 31202.996). The drive ends 22 m short of the far end. The seeker waits 18 m behind that end and then steers. The bay is 20 m off the end. The gate is 18 m past the bay. A crawl at 600 cm/s is caught. A drive that holds 1200 cm/s reaches the bay. One frame spends at most a quarter of a second. Arriving at the end does not also clear the bay. Reaching the bay does not also clear the gate. The purse is 720, once. The step index is the only saved field. Where the seeker is, and the escape clock, stay in the session. A reload during the chase puts the seeker behind the end again and keeps the step. A reload during the escape starts the clock again.
