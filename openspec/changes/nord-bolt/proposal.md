# Drive, then the seeker, then the gate

## Why

The ditch is a car that stays on its road. The tail follows you to a hide. Nothing asks you to arrive fast, then outrun a seeker that steers, and then leave the car for a gate they will not follow.

## What changes

After the parcel is banked, a road north of the park asks for a real car at the far end, still at 1200 cm/s, nose down the road. That starts a seeker 18 m behind. They steer toward the car at 1050 cm/s. Reach the bay off the road before they close. A crawl is caught and the chase starts again. The drive stays banked, and that catch does not save. At the bay, get out. A gate further off the road pays 720 once if you reach it on foot before a ten-second clock. A car at the gate does not. The clock running out starts it again and leaves the chase banked. The step is saved on version 21. Version 20 starts this beat over. The seven story banks stay seven. The ditch, the tail, the pursuit, and the parcel are not retuned.

## Impact

- Affected specs: nord-bolt, nord-unreal
- Affected code: `QuebecBolt.h`, `QuebecStorySave.h`, `QuebecCampaign`, the bolt tests, and the version-21 save readers
