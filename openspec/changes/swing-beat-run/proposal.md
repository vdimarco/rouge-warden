## Why

Eight of the twelve flushes are still the same pump. Spider-Man 2 does not leave a repeated objective as most of the run into a boss.

## What changes

- The second flush starts a falling rescue. The card says OVER THE EDGE. It does not say to flush the clogs. The King still sleeps.
- The fifth flush starts a purse snatcher. The card says PURSE RUN. After it, the clog card returns at 5/12.
- The seventh flush starts a mugging. The card says THE ALLEY. After it, the clog card returns at 7/12.
- The first, third, ninth, and eleventh flushes stay on the clog card. The balloon, the pizza, the 36 second washer, and the roof fight are unchanged. The King still roars only at the fourth and the eighth, and he still wakes at twelve. The fall rate of the rescue is unchanged. A beat does not move the story route.
