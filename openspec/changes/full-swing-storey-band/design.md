# Storey band

## Source

Francis D.K. Ching, Architecture: Form, Space, and Order. A string course marks the floor and separates a shopfront from the storey above it. Nothing is taken from Spider-Man 2.

## Decisions

- The course is painted in the facade shader. No extra mesh and no extra draw.
- It is mixed on after the grade, the way the awning is, so the violet and teal paper do not recolor the stone.
- The storey directly above the shop (the first floor cell) gets a course about a quarter of the floor high, a dark lip, a sill, and a window pulled up so wall shows under the glass.
- Floors above that get a thinner course, about a seventh of the floor, so the stack is not one continuous tile.
- `detail` fades the band before the pixels shimmer. Glass towers are left alone.
- The stone stays lighter than the ink line in both the sun and the shade.
