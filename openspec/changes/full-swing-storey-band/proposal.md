# The floor above a shop is its own storey

Above the shop cloth, every floor is the same window tile.

## What changes

A shop building gets a limestone course at each floor. The storey just above the shop is taller stone, with a sill and a shorter sash, so it is not the tile of the floors above it. The stone is printed after the grade. It fades before it can shimmer. No new mesh.

## Capabilities

- `swing-storey-band`: the street shows a stone course on the floors above a shop, and a shorter window on the first of those floors.
