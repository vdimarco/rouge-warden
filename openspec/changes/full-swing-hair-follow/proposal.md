# Hair follows through a turn

The hair and the collar are glued to the head. A turn of the view moves them on the same frame as the skull, so the figure has no follow-through.

## What changes

The hair keeps the head's position and arrives at the head's turn a little later, with a short sideways drag while it is behind. At rest it sits on the skull again. The skinned mesh, the draw count, and the chase distance stay as they are.

## Capabilities

- `swing-hair-follow`: during a look, the hair trails the head and then settles.
