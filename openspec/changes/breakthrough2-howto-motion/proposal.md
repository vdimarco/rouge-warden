# Visual how to play

## Why

How to play is a stack of paragraphs. The century, the turn, the heat lag, the path, the race, and the pathways are easier to learn if they move.

## Scope

- `public/breakthrough2/` and `qa/breakthrough2/` only.
- Do not change `public/breakthrough/`, `public/tellme/`, or the sim.
- Keep the existing rules in the sheet, including the UNEP, IPCC, and Ember context.

## Player-facing change

Opening How to play shows a motion stage: six short scenes the player can step through. The stage plays on its own when motion is allowed. `fast=1` and reduced motion leave a still frame, and the dots still change scenes.
