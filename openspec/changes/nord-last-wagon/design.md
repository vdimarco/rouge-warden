# Two posts, one bank

No new rendering technique is involved, so none of the usual art references fit. The called wagon is a berry post and a caption, the same parts the feeder already uses. The clear gate stays the lime post.

`Apply` grows an optional wagon argument that defaults to the clear gate, so every existing Use on the escape step still banks. The called wagon is a miss, the same way a pull during the paint is a miss: dialogue, `BreakChain`, no bank, no rank sting. `GetStatusText` stays "R recovers this wagon". The called spot is 14 m north of the clear gate, more than 8 m from the three posts and their recovers, and past the 15 m QA line. The v1 slot still writes only Version and CompletedMissions.
