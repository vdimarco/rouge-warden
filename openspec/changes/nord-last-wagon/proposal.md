# The last wagon is a choice

## Why
Can't Tow Tomorrow ends by walking up to one gate. The lines already argue about two wagons, and only one of them exists.

## What changes
- On the escape step, a second wagon stands north of the clear gate.
- E on the clear gate banks the convoy. E on the wagon he called does not advance, and the chain breaks.
- The card marks the clear gate. The prompt names which wagon you are standing at.
- The v1 save still stores only Version and CompletedMissions.

## Out of scope
A ninth story mission, a change to the beam timing, the pond beam, stream latency, and a new art technique.
