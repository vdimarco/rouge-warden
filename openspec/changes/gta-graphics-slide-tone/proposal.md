# Proposal: a slide that screams

Round 85 gave the handbrake slide a SLIDE banner, and the slide stayed nearly silent. The lock chirp lasts 0.1 s. The drive loop's tire hiss peaks at 0.045, under the engine. This round adds a tire scream to the drive loop. It starts on the banner's live line, follows the sideways shove, and fades in about 0.3 s when the slide ends.
