# Wood pole at the corner

A new pole model does not fit. `utility_pole` in `Scripts/blender/plateau_props.py` is a 10.5 m wooden shaft, radius about 15 cm at the base, ending at 1053 cm. City pole 13707 stands at (-38893.9, 46509.5, 18). The street blades and the cobra head are mounted on it and leave out their own posts.

The corner shade had been drawing a 12 cm grey cylinder at the sign origin, 4 m tall. That cylinder goes. The shaft is the surveyed wood pole. The blades stay where they were, so the names still read. The cobra head is near 8 m and this camera looks under it. The opening pitch and the socket stay. Unreal is not installed.
