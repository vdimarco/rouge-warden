# gta-graphics

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The curb frame holds a bag pile and a hydrant

The scored curb look stands on layout road 61. A surveyed bag pile and a surveyed hydrant, about 4 m apart, both project inside the frame and sit closer than 12 m. The opening camera stays at pitch −16 with the socket at (0, 70, 110).

#### Scenario: The pair is in the frame

- **WHEN** the curb look is drawn
- **THEN** the bag pile and the hydrant are both in front of the eye and inside the horizontal field

#### Scenario: The opening aim stays

- **WHEN** the player spawns on foot
- **THEN** the look is pitch −16 and the socket is (0, 70, 110)
