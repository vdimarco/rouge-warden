# Design: stand where the pair already is

## Source

`Scripts/blender/plateau_props.py`, `hydrant` and `bin_garbage`. The hydrant is the Montréal barrel: octagonal body, two hose outlets, a front pumper, and the red marker post. The bin is three tied bags. No other street-furniture reference fit better than the prop the game already builds.

## Choice

The furniture placements put a bag pile at (35394.46, −24048.46) and a hydrant at (35624.11, −23688.59), about 4 m apart, on layout road 61 (width 1400). The eye sits 420 cm back along that road and 860 cm toward the props, at height 140, pitch −14. The Sherbrooke eye stays where street life left it. The opening look stays at pitch −16 and the socket at (0, 70, 110).
