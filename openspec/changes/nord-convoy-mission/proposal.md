# Mission 7: Can't Tow Tomorrow

## Why
Fun is the missions loop's next target, and the story still stops at mission 6 of 8. After the groups are out, the card drops to side jobs while the border is still being drawn.

## What changes
- After every group is out, the mission card and the gold diamond lead along the street ring: walk to the ink feeder, pull its clamp in the beam's gap, invert the projector plate, then take the escape gate.
- A pull while the beam is painting stays on that step and plays the dialogue cue. A pull during the warning still lands. Finished disruptions stay finished. The v1 save still stores CompletedMissions 0–2.
- Side jobs and T wait until the escape. R restores the current wagon and does not rewind a disruption. The recover points step off the carriageway, away from the race gates.

## Out of scope
Moving wagons, driving the escape, co-op drivers and runners, mission 8, a new save field, a new art technique, a new procedural tone, and stream latency.
