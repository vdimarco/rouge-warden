# Mission 4: Match Pointless

## Why
Variety is still the missions loop's next target. Three of eight story missions exist. After the picnic is banked, the card drops straight to side jobs.

## What changes
- After the picnic is banked, the mission card and the gold diamond lead through the exhibition court: walk on, score the gold panel once, bank the ball into the return gate while the ink is off the line, and pull the clamp.
- The court is session state. The v1 save still stores CompletedMissions 0–2. Loading a finished race offers the pond again, and the court only after that picnic is banked in the session.
- While the court is open, side jobs and race replay wait. R (Menu · Recover on touch) restores the current rally and does not rewind it. Scoring the gold panel again does not rewind the checkpoint. After the clamp, the side-job guide returns.

## Out of scope
Missions 5–8, a tennis simulation, a new save field, a new art technique, and stream latency.
