# The carry

## Why

The tab is a walk, a lane, and a still count. The next errand should ask for a different pace: pick something up, walk it in, then roll the car slower than that walk.

## What changes

Mission 33, THE CARRY, opens after the tab. Pick up the case on foot. Walk it to the stoop inside a walk band. A sprint, a creep, or a car at the stoop drops that delivery and keeps the case. Roll a real car across the line, slower than a walk and faster than a crawl. A stop or a bolt drops the roll and keeps the stoop. The purse is 740, once. Version 26 writes the step. Version 25 starts the carry over.

## Impact

- Affected specs: nord-carry
- Affected code: the carry rules, the campaign tail, and the story slot
