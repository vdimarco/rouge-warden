## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Printed crew keeps source hue
The comic grade SHALL shade printed crew with a scalar, and a warm-dark shirt SHALL stay warmer than it is blue. The city's cool shadow wash SHALL stay off printed crew. The silhouette rim SHALL stay.

#### Scenario: Shade the black tee
- **WHEN** the warm-dark tee albedo is graded as printed crew in full shade
- **THEN** red stays above blue, and the same albedo under the coloured blue key puts blue above red

#### Scenario: Skin stays warm in the light
- **WHEN** the skin albedo is graded as printed crew on the lit side
- **THEN** red stays above green and green stays above blue
