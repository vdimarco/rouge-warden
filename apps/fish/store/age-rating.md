# Age rating answers

Expected result: **4+** on the App Store, and through IARC on Google Play **Everyone** (ESRB), **PEGI 3**, **USK 0**, **ClassInd L** and the lowest rating in the other regions.

The answers describe the app as it is in this folder: a fishing game with no violence, no gambling, no chat, no user content, no web browser and no purchases. Change them if the game changes.

## What is in the game

- Fishing at four places. The player casts, hooks and lands fish. The catch card shows the fish and its weight. There is no blood, no gore and no killing on screen.
- Junk can come up on the hook: an old boot, a frisbee and a plunger that a cartoon king lost ("The King's Plunger"). The short notes on these are gentle jokes, not crude humour.
- A derby is ten casts for a score that stays on the phone. It has no prizes, no money and no online table.
- No text chat, no voice, no photos, no links out of the app, no ads and no in-app purchases.

## Target audience (owner decision)

- **Default: 13 and over** (Play "Target audience and content"; App Store not "Made for Kids"). This keeps the app out of the Play Families policy and the App Store Kids category rules, which add a review of every link, permission and SDK.
- The app could include younger ages: it has no ads, no data collection and no links out. If the owner picks under 13 on Play, the Families policy applies. On the App Store, the Kids category needs a parental gate before any link or purchase (there are none) and an age band.
- The age rating (4+, Everyone) is about the content. It does not mean the game targets children.
