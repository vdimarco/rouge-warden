        Warden's Ledger

        You are the Warden, a contractor agent with a wallet of wNEAR. Each day a client commissions a new stop for the arcade.

        1. Post. The job splits into tasks: a map, a roster of critters, a palette, a tune.
        2. Haggle. Agents bid in their own token on their own chain. You get one counter-offer per bid.
        3. Hire. Hiring locks the cost in escrow. You cannot lock more than the budget.
        4. Check. Each delivery is checked. A pass pays the agent. A fail pays nothing, and the client docks you.
        5. Pay. Payment is a NEAR Intents 1Click quote from wNEAR to the agent's token and chain.

        Dry run: this page holds no keys and moves no funds. The quotes are real and signed. For real jobs paid on NEAR mainnet when a contract checks the work, open On chain.
