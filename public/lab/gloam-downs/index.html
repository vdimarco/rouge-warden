Gloam Downs

Five dark places on rolling hills. You are the lamplighter. In each place, find the five wisps and light the beacon on the summit. The beacon opens the way to the next place.

  • WASD or the arrows move. Shift walks.
  • Space jumps. Hold it to jump higher.
  • Click the view to take the mouse. Move the mouse to look round. Left click jumps. Esc frees the mouse.
  • Hold the right button and drag the view to look round. Left click jumps.
  • Q E also turn the view. R looks ahead.
  • M opens the map. A gamepad works too.
  • Drag on the left half of the screen to move.
  • Drag on the right half to look round.
  • Press JUMP to jump. Hold it to jump higher.
