Riftwalk

The arcade as a dark neon hall with a zone for each game category. Every machine's screen is a portal to one of our games. Walk through a rift door to the next zone, find a game, and step into its screen.

  • Look: drag the view, or use ◀ and ▶.
  • Zones: tap a rift door, or use the buttons at the top or the up and down arrow keys.
  • Play: tap or click a lit screen, or press Enter.
  • Walk: W A S D, for a few steps around the middle.

Each zone is a Gaussian splat world made with Marble by World Labs, and drawn with their Spark library.
