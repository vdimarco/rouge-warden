Riftwalk

The arcade as a neon hall. A ring of portals stands around you: one for each collection, then one for each game. Step through a portal to play.

  • Look: drag the view, or use ◀ and ▶.
  • Open: tap or click a portal, or press Enter.
  • Back: the Back portal, the Back button, or Esc.
  • Walk: W A S D, for a few steps around the middle.

The hall is a Gaussian splat world made with Marble by World Labs, and drawn with their Spark library.
