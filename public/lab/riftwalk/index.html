Riftwalk

The arcade as a neon alley at night. Each storefront is a portal to a world for one game category: a meadow, a temple, a diner, a space station, the Great Wall and an arcade hall. In each world the games float in portal frames.

  • Look: drag the view, or use ◀ and ▶ to turn from portal to portal.
  • Storefronts: the buttons at the top, or the up and down arrow keys, take you to a shop.
  • Go in: walk into the shop's door, tap it, or press Enter or R. The portal behind you, the Alley button or Esc brings you back.
  • Play: tap or click a game's frame, or press Enter.
  • Walk: tap or click a spot on the street, or use W A S D.

The alley and the worlds are Gaussian splat worlds made with Marble by World Labs, and their Spark library draws them. The music was made with ElevenLabs Music.
