Loon Echo

Same lake, wrong dimension. Bring your growing family of little weirdos home.

Play

  • Drag or use the arrow keys to steer. Rescued chicks echo your turns.
  • CALL (or Space) gathers the flock and braces against boat wakes.

Try this

  • Take a detour for a chick, then get the whole tail through the next gap.
  • Bring all eight home without losing anyone to the shore.

A 72-second crossing. Your best home count stays in this browser.

Take the Plunge

You are a loon, and winter is coming. Dive into the lakes for speed and fly south.

Play

  • Hold anywhere (or Space) to tuck and dive. Let go to glide.
  • Hit a lake steep for a rip. A flat landing is a belly-flop.

Try this

  • Dive through the fish and burst out of the water.
  • Share your run, and race a friend's ghost.

Play

Up the Creek

Your phone is the canoe paddle. Run the rapid and catch the eddies behind the rocks.

Play

  • Phone: tip it to a side, then rock the top to stroke on that side. Twist at the end for a J-stroke. Tip it hard and hold to brace.
  • Touch: drag down on a side to stroke. Keys: A and D stroke, Q and E J-stroke, Z and C brace.

Try this

  • Paddle straight with J-strokes.
  • Lean into an eddy and sit still while the rapid roars past.

Play

Full Tilt

A bare pinball table. The question is the flippers: do they feel like a real machine?

Play

  • Hold the left or right half of the screen to flip. To launch, slide a finger down on the right half and let go.
  • Keys: Z and / flip. Hold Space to pull the plunger. Jolt the phone, or push A or L, to nudge.

Try this

  • Cradle the ball on a raised flipper, then pass it across with a quick tap.
  • Launch softly into the green top lane for a skill shot.
  • Nudge a ball back from the outlane. Nudge too hard and it tilts.

House Rules

You are the Cottage. Build a Down the Drain layer, clear it yourself, and send it to the crew.

Play

  • Dig tunnels, then paint rock, sand, water, lava and more. Place critters and propane tanks.
  • Press Test it to play your layer in Down the Drain. Reach a drain to unlock Share.

Try this

  • Hold a lake up with a thin sand dam, and watch what happens when someone digs.
  • Put a propane tank near lava, and press Settle to see which one wins.
