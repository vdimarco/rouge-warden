HOW TO FLY

Two squadrons, four minutes. A downed ship scores 10. Every few minutes a meteor shower falls: shoot the rocks, then fly through the amber energy they drop. Each orb scores 5, and the team that gathers more energy in a shower earns 25 more. Red orbs are bombs. Green orbs repair your shield.
