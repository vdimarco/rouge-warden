The Warden's Hand

The Warden waits for you at the door.

Drag to move. Tap to throw your lantern. Flick to dash.

Warden

Each floor has six rooms and then the Warden's hand. Beat the hand and go deeper. The floors never end, and the run ends when you fall. When a room is clear, walk to the reward in the middle. Then two doors open, and each shows what waits behind it.

The Warden designs every room zone by zone, and picks your rewards, doors, and foes. Its decisions pop up as it makes them. Tap the eye at the top to see why it chose what it did.

Move with WASD. Attack with J or the left mouse button, and hold it for a three-hit combo. Fire your Special with K or the right mouse button. Throw your lantern with L, then walk over it to pick it up. Dash with Space. Dash through a blow at the last moment and your next swing crits. M turns sound on or off.

On a phone, one hand is enough. Drag anywhere to move, and you attack on your own when a foe is close. Tap where you want to throw your lantern. Flick your thumb to dash.

    How to fight

    Foes flash and show a red wedge on the floor before they strike. Shieldbearers block hits from the front, so hit them from behind or stun them with the lantern. Blocks stop shots, and spike tiles hurt foes too.

    Mood

    Watching

    The Warden picks a mood before each room. The mood changes what it offers you and what it sends at you.
