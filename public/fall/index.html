The Warden melted its halls. Now every grain falls.

You carry the lantern down a well of sand, stone, and liquid. Every pixel moves. Fire spreads, water flows, acid eats rock, and oil waits for a spark. Find the glowing well at the bottom of each layer to rest at the landing and change your wand.

The Warden pours each layer. It picks the stone, the liquids, the gold, and the foes, and it watches you while you fall. When it sees fit, it rains water, drips lava, or opens a rift. Its decisions pop up as it makes them. Tap the eye to see why.

Move with A and D. Jump with W or Space, and hold it in the air to float on your lantern. Aim with the mouse and hold the left button to cast. With keys only, cast with J toward where you move, and hold S to aim down. Q shows or hides the map. M turns sound on or off.

Lost? The gold arrow by your lantern points down the route to the well. The map shows what your lantern has lit, and the well always glows on it.

On a phone, drag on the left half of the screen to move. Push up to jump and float. Drag on the right half to aim and cast, or tap to cast at a spot.
