# BREAKTHROUGH — Art Direction

## Core style
Hand-painted 2.5D climate-future animation.

The world must feel painterly, soft, warm, atmospheric, human, cinematic, and tactile. Use watercolor and gouache cues, soft outlines, diffuse light, organic forms, and retro-futurist infrastructure.

## Visual rules
- Natural palettes with moss green, warm paper, faded teal, ochre, clay, and soft sky blue.
- Avoid hard-edged low-poly forms, shiny generic 3D, excessive neon, sterile sci-fi HUDs, and mobile-game gradients.
- Technology belongs inside landscapes. Solar, geothermal, wind, transmission, cities, farms, and restoration projects should feel built into a living world.
- Major player actions must change the world scene.
- Climate damage must be visible through haze, dryness, fire scars, degraded forests, and dense infrastructure.
- Ecological recovery should visibly bring back water, forests, canopy, and richer color.

## World states
The living view blends four stops. Damaged is brown haze, dry ground, and wildfire scar. Strained keeps the heat but still has structure. Transitioning is the mixed century: infrastructure arriving, color coming back. Thriving is moss, cream, clear teal water, and clean infrastructure in the landscape.

Clean-energy deployment adds wind, solar, transmission, geothermal, and electrified transit. Ecology adds forest, water, and green ground. Emissions and warming add haze, dryness, scars, and heat shimmer.

## Breakthroughs and endings
Meta-breakthroughs (Planetary Grid, Carbon Mining, Land Dividend, Electric Everywhere) fill the screen with a warm light, a large title, and a scientist's line. The six endings do not share one picture: abundance is gold and clear, regeneration is green, the managed transition is teal and orderly, hot growth is amber, the long emergency is dark and scarred, and the fractured century is split down the middle.

## Camera
Use broad cinematic landscape compositions with a mild 2.5D parallax feel. Human-scale infrastructure can sit in the middle distance. Avoid top-down map presentation as the default view.

## UI
UI sits on warm translucent paper panels. Typography is editorial, readable, and restrained. Decisions should feel like illustrated story cards rather than spreadsheet controls.

## Character portraits
Scientists and operators use expressive painted portraits with soft linework and practical contemporary clothing. Avoid exaggerated cartoon proportions.

## Prompt template
"Hand-painted 2.5D climate-future animation scene, watercolor and gouache texture, soft organic outlines, warm diffuse light, lush natural environment integrated with plausible clean infrastructure, retro-futurist but grounded technology, cinematic landscape composition, atmospheric depth, human scale, tactile painterly finish, restrained natural color palette, no glossy generic 3D, no neon sci-fi HUD, no text."
