How to play

The Porcelain King clogs the city from its rooftops. Plunge the twelve green clogs and collect Loonies. Then rip the pipes off the Needle and flush the King.

Headset controllers

  • Point at a building and pull the trigger to shoot a plunger. Hold the trigger to stay on the rope.
  • Let go of the trigger to let go of the rope.
  • Squeeze the grip to reel in.
  • Pull your hand back hard to yank. A yank gives you speed and pumps a clog.
  • Push the right stick to turn. Push the left stick to walk or to steer in the air.
  • Press A or X to jump. Press B or Y for the menu.

Hands

  • Point your hand at a building and pinch to shoot. Keep pinching to hold on.
  • Open your fingers to let go.
  • Make a fist to reel in.
  • Pull your hand back hard to yank.
  • Tap the arrows on your left wrist to turn. Tap the round button on your wrist for the menu.

Keyboard and mouse

  • The game picks a building for you. Look toward the buildings you want. A ring marks the target.
  • Hold the left mouse button, or E, to swing. On a roof, hold W too, so you run off the edge. Let go when the ring says GO.
  • Hold the right mouse button, or Q, to add a second rope.
  • Hold Shift or turn the wheel to reel in.
  • Press F to yank. Three yanks plunge a clog.
  • W A S D or the arrow keys walk and steer in the air. The mouse looks around.
  • Space jumps. In the air it yanks. On a wall it jumps off.
  • Fly or walk into a wall to hold on. W and S climb. A and D go along it. At the top you step on to the roof. A rope swings you off.
  • V switches between the view behind the hero and the first-person view. In first person the crosshair aims the rope exactly.
  • Tab opens the map. Press it again to close it. M turns the sound off and on. Esc pauses.

Game pad

  • This is for a standard pad, such as Xbox or PlayStation. Press PLAY with the mouse, a tap or Enter. The pad works after that.
  • Hold the right trigger (RT, R2) to swing. On a roof, push the left stick up too. Hold the left trigger (LT, L2) to add a second rope. Let go when the ring says GO.
  • Hold the left bumper (LB, L1) to reel in. Press the right bumper (RB, R1) or X (Square) to yank.
  • The left stick walks, steers and climbs. The right stick looks around.
  • A (Cross) jumps. On a wall it jumps off.
  • Y (Triangle) switches the view. Start (Options) pauses and resumes.

Phone and touch

  • Tap a building to swing from it. Or press SWING, and the game picks a building. A ring marks the target.
  • The rope lets go by itself and flings you on. Tap again while you fly.
  • A tap on a clog plunges it.
  • Drag to look around. VIEW switches the view.
  • Hit a wall to hold on. Hold the arrows to climb. Tap JUMP to jump off.
  • Motion aim is optional. Center sets it straight again.

Comfort
