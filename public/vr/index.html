How to play

The Porcelain King clogs the city from its rooftops. Plunge the twelve green clogs and collect Loonies. Then rip the pipes off the Needle and flush the King.

Controllers

  • Point at a building and pull the trigger to shoot a plunger. Hold the trigger to stay on the rope.
  • Let go of the trigger to let go of the rope.
  • Squeeze the grip to reel in.
  • Pull your hand back hard to yank. A yank gives you speed and pumps a clog.
  • Push the right stick to turn. Push the left stick to walk or to steer in the air.
  • Press A or X to jump. Press B or Y for the menu.

Hands

  • Point your hand at a building and pinch to shoot. Keep pinching to hold on.
  • Open your fingers to let go.
  • Make a fist to reel in.
  • Pull your hand back hard to yank.
  • Tap the arrows on your left wrist to turn. Tap the round button on your wrist for the menu.

Keyboard and mouse

  • Hold the left or right mouse button to shoot a rope from that hand. Let go of the button to let go of the rope.
  • Hold Shift or turn the wheel to reel in.
  • Press F to yank. Press Space to jump, or to yank in the air.
  • W A S D walk. The mouse looks around.
  • Tab opens the map. Esc pauses.
  • A game pad works too.

Comfort

Swinging moves you fast. Pick how much help you want. You can change it at any time in the pause menu.
