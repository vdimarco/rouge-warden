In Full Swing: privacy policy

Last update: 28 September 2026

In Full Swing is a VR game for Meta Quest. It is part of the Cottage Arcade. This page tells you what data the game uses, where that data stays, and how you delete it.

Summary

  • The game has no accounts. You do not sign in.
  • The game shows no ads. It uses no analytics and no tracking tools.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your headset.
  • Data about your room and your hands stays on your headset. The game does not keep it and does not send it.

Data that our web host receives

The game is a web page. When you start it, your headset downloads the game files from our web host, Vercel. Like every web server, Vercel receives standard request data. This data includes your IP address, your browser type (the user agent), the time, and the files that your headset asks for. Vercel uses this data to send the files and to keep the service safe. We do not use this data to find out who you are.

Data that stays on your headset

The game saves your progress and your settings in the browser storage of your headset. This includes the clogs that you clear, the coins that you collect, your best trial times and your comfort settings. The game does not send this data to us or to other persons.

The game also keeps a copy of its own files on your headset, so that it can start without a network connection.

Room data and hand data

In mixed reality, the game uses data about your room from your headset. This data includes the positions of your walls, floor and furniture, and the points where your view hits a surface. With hand tracking, the game uses the positions of your hands and fingers.

The game uses this data only on your headset and only while you play. It uses the data to put the game into your room and to show your real room when you come near a wall. The game does not save this data and does not send it to a server.

If you use "Scan your room" in the menu, your headset opens its own Space Setup. Meta Horizon OS keeps that scan, not the game.

Microphone and camera

The game does not use your microphone. The game cannot see the camera images of your headset. Passthrough shows your room to you, but the game gets no image of it.

Delete your data

You can delete your data in one of these ways:

  • In the game, open the menu and select Reset progress. This deletes your progress.
  • In the Quest Browser, clear the site data for this website. This deletes your progress, your settings and the saved game files.
  • If you installed the app, uninstall it. This deletes all data that the app keeps.

We keep no copy of your game data, so there is nothing to delete on our side.

Meta

Your headset, Meta Horizon OS, the Quest Browser and the Meta Horizon Store are services of Meta. Meta collects data under its own privacy policy. This policy does not cover the data that Meta collects.

Children

The game is not for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

If you have a question about your data, open an issue at github.com/vdimarco/rouge-warden/issues.
