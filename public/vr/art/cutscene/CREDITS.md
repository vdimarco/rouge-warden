# Comic scene pictures

The seven pictures here are the painted panels of the comic scenes (`js/cutscene.js`). They were made on 2026-10-05 with
Higgsfield (model GPT Image 2.5, high quality, 2K, 16:9) for this game, from three screenshots of the game itself as
references: the city and the Needle, the Porcelain King on his perch, and the hero on the start roof. Each was then scaled
to 1280 px wide and saved as WebP (quality 0.78) in a browser canvas.

| File | Panel |
| --- | --- |
| `city.webp` | Opening 1: Port Loon and the Needle, the city's water tower |
| `king.webp` | Opening 2: the Porcelain King asleep in the tank |
| `clogs.webp` | Opening 3: the rooftop drains back up with sludge |
| `hero.webp` | Opening 4: the hero with the plunger, the Needle behind |
| `swing.webp` | Opening 5: the hero swings toward the Needle (the Mission 1 card) |
| `king-awake.webp` | The King wakes (Mission 2) |
| `finale.webp` | The King goes down the drain |

The prompts ask for a bold comic style with ink outlines and halftone shading, the game's sunset colours, and no words in
the picture (the scenes draw their own captions and balloons). A panel whose picture does not load shows the live city.
