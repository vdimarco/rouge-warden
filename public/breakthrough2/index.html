How to play

Twelve turns carry the world from 2026 to 2100. A step can be 6 years or 10. The longer step moves the climate more.

Each turn, play one breakthrough. Three are dealt, plus the Idea Lab when it is open.

Warming chases a target set by emissions and the land. It does not jump to the target. You always see the heat now and the heat projected for 2100.

Costly energy drags prosperity and stirs resistance. Growth pushes emissions unless you break that link. A living landscape stores carbon. Low trust softens a law and raises its political price.

The Idea Lab is one wild card every three turns. There is no reroll. A card you cannot pay for stays dim.

The run ends in an alternate history, with the timeline of what you chose.
