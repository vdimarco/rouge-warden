Reel It In: privacy policy

Last update: 3 October 2026

Reel It In is a fishing game for phones. You can play it in a web browser or in the Android app. Both load the game from our web host. This page tells you what data the game uses, where that data stays, and how you delete it.

Summary

  • The game has no accounts. You do not sign in.
  • The game shows no ads. It uses no analytics and no tracking tools.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your phone. The game sends them to nobody.
  • The motion sensors work on your phone only. Their data never leaves it.

Data that our web host receives

The game is a web page. When you start it, your phone downloads the game files from our web host, Vercel. Like every web server, Vercel receives standard request data. This data includes your IP address, your browser type (the user agent), the time, and the files that your phone asks for. Vercel uses this data to send the files and to keep the service safe. We do not use this data to find out who you are.

Data that stays on your phone

The game saves your progress and your settings in the browser storage of your phone. This includes the fish that you land, your records, the places that you open, your best derby scores, and your settings for sound, buzz, easy mode, controls, art style and graphics. It also remembers if you hid the animated guide. The game does not send this data to us or to other persons.

The game also keeps a copy of its own files on your phone, so that it can start without a network connection.

Motion sensors

The game reads the motion sensors of your phone, so that the phone works as a fishing rod. The game uses this data only on your phone and only while you play. It does not save the data and does not send it to a server. You can play with touch if you do not allow the sensors.

Vibration and the screen

The game can make your phone buzz, and it can keep the screen on while you fish. You can turn the buzz off in Settings. These features need no account and send no data.

Microphone, camera and location

The game does not use your microphone, your camera or your location.

Delete your data

You can delete your data in one of these ways:

  • In the game, open Settings and select Reset progress. This deletes your fish, your records and your settings. It keeps the sound switch, which other games on the same website also use.
  • Clear the site data for this website in the settings of your browser. The Android app shows the game through Chrome, so the app keeps its data in Chrome as site data for this website.

If you uninstall the Android app, the site data can stay in Chrome. Use Reset progress first if you want to be sure that it is gone.

We keep no copy of your game data, so there is nothing to delete on our side.

Children

The game is not for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we update this page and the date at the top.
