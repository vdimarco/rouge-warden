REEL IT IN

Privacy

Last changed: 4 October 2026

Reel It In collects no personal data. It sends nothing off your phone.

It has no ads, no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking. The app does not connect to the internet. On the web, your browser gets the game files from the web site, as for any web page. After that, the game sends nothing.

What the game keeps

The game keeps your fish, the places you opened, your best scores, and your settings. It keeps them on this phone only, in the app's own storage. When you play on the web, it keeps them in your browser's storage on that device.

We cannot see this data.

Motion sensors

The game reads the motion sensors of your phone only to play the cast and the reel. It keeps no record of the readings, and it sends them nowhere. You can play with touch and not use the sensors.

Backups

The backup of your phone (iCloud or Google) can include the game's progress. Your account settings control this backup. We cannot see it.

Delete your data

To delete everything, delete the app. On the web, clear the site data for this game in your browser.

Children

The game collects no personal data from anyone, at any age.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new text will be on this page and in the app.
