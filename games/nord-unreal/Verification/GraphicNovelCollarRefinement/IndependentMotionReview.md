# Fixed-fixture pilot motion review

All 85 selected decoded frames were visually inspected across Standard and Comic, including 12 contact sheets and 2 full-resolution gameplay images. Both fixed-motion jobs and native reports pass at 1920×1080, with eight unpaused phases at 1×. Encoded 60 fps is not native 60 fps proof.

The sampled poses retain player/camera/HUD framing and coherent clothing, cuffs, shoes and attached prop through walking, sprinting, turning, jump, landing and recovery. Standard fast feet and plunger blur; rigid hands, pinched shoulders, indigo comic colour shift and noisy scene ink remain. Ground relation is plausible at the sampled jump/landing points, with no claim of exact continuous foot planting.

Both raw videos last 9.516667 s/571 encoded frames. Gameplay remains visible at the sampled 7 s point, editor UI appears at 8 s, and Comic is black at 9 s. Standard still shows editor UI at 9 s. The CsvProfiler debug counter remains visible during gameplay. These are diagnostic captures rather than clean trailers.

Normal-speed continuous playback was not viewed by this reviewer. Sampled-frame pose coverage is retained for the draft; fluidity, continuous line stability and final graphics acceptance remain open.

Skipped: audio, physical controls, co-op and packaged performance. Sparse frames cannot establish full-cycle contact, subtle timing or native frame rate.
