# Neighbourhood visual review — 2026-10-10

The corrected **1280×720 and 1920×1080 matrices pass a bounded visual review of the implemented neighbourhood changes**. All 42 raw PNGs were inspected at their native dimensions: seven retained park/street cameras, seven neighbourhood close-ups and the same seven after actual F2 at each size. Both [720p](EnvironmentRender1280.json) and [1080p](EnvironmentRender1920.json) reports pass completion; an independent read verified every PNG signature, matching IHDR dimensions and terminal IEND. Camera position, target, FOV and comic/F2 definitions match between sizes. These integrity checks are separate from the observations below. Native environment inspection uses temporary streaming/VSM limits: 1024 MiB/2048 pages at 720p and 512 MiB/1024 pages at 1080p. Packaged Epic settings are unchanged; these are visual captures, not profiling. Current package play and all eleven packaged lifecycle checks pass; all six current packaged performance scenes fail their p95 budgets, recorded separately in [current verification](README.md). Overall quality acceptance remains open.

This review covers the current 190 cm portal surrounds, capped/filtered municipal frontage art, replacement fine tennis nets, two estimated sand courts and staggered resident hubs. The first 720p attempt completed automatically but failed visually: shell-intersecting StreetCorner/Parc cameras, an obstructed terrace, floating Rachel trim, misaligned portal/window frames and coarse tennis nets. The corrected frames supersede that visual verdict for the changes actually visible here; they do not establish exact Montréal likeness.

No blank capture, unsupported floating cornice or gross geometry explosion remains in these reviewed corrected frames. This is limited to the visible sample, not every façade edge or possible player position. The existing comic filter is retained; its thin-line aliasing is disclosed. [Primary references and access limits](NeighbourhoodReferences.md) distinguish mapped data from estimates. Google Street View pixels were inaccessible; no direct Street View match, photorealism, live construction reconstruction, performance pass or whole-city visual acceptance is claimed.
