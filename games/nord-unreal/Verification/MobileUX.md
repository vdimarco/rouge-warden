# Mobile UX audit — 14 October 2026

The candidate fixes the reported mobile interaction and navigation issues across the browser player and native game. The native package is installed and the tested frontend is live at [nord-montreal.vercel.app](https://nord-montreal.vercel.app/). [PR 351](https://github.com/vdimarco/rouge-warden/pull/351) merged as `38ad7494`; production deployment `dpl_Gi5bvxeg4CDNYCMN3Mnsm2RyAfox` is READY. Machine-readable identities and evidence are in [MobileUX.json](MobileUX.json).

- Double-tap Move and hold the second touch to sprint, or double-tap Sprint while moving. Stopping, menu, cancellation, rotation, backgrounding, disconnection and car transitions release the latch. Held action buttons can turn the view without dropping their action; an existing background look finger retains priority. Steering remains independent.
- Sprint pressed feedback follows actual held keys. Accessibility activation can cancel latched sprint, and inactive menus cannot inject keys. Small finger jitter stays within the tap tolerance; an actual look drag returning to its start cannot latch sprint. Instructions now describe dragging and double-tapping. Native swimming changes Jump to Stroke and restores Jump/Brake correctly.
- Native pond swimming follows the same mapped outlines, island and bridge geometry as the rendered water. Characters float, stroke and leave at a clear bank. Only a swimming capsule ignores the flat collider under the pond. Idle uses a lowered treading pose and travel uses a centred horizontal pose, with the hand prop hidden. Dedicated swimming clips remain future work.
- The circular radar is 160 logical pixels across in landscape and 28% of portrait width. Its 500-metre diameter shows more streets; road clipping and distant markers follow the circle. Attribution remains beneath it.
- Traffic existed but drifted away from the starting neighbourhood. Eight cars now keep circulating within 250 metres; eight retain district routes. A ten-minute fixed-step replay improved the median number within 150 metres after minute three from one to six, without adding actors or changing yield/collision rules.
- Phones start at a balanced 960-pixel long edge/2 Mbps and recover up to 1280/4 Mbps. Five-second settling and consecutive pressure counters prevent one hiccup from permanently reducing quality. Continuing pressure can still use 800/1.5 Mbps and emergency 640/1 Mbps; healthy playback restores quality one step at a time. Native graphics and 30 FPS cap remain unchanged.

## Limits

Physical phones, carrier/off-LAN access, audible playback, subjective handling and a new full campaign/race replay were not checked. Surface swimming uses existing crew locomotion. Emergency streaming can still look grainy on a constrained connection. OpenSpec CLI validation was unavailable; spec structure and observable scenarios were checked manually.
