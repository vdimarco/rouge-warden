# Toronto Path shadow comparison

**Accept only `Toronto Path.cast_shadow=False`.** All 12 candidate 1080 PNGs were independently viewed against the corrected-sign 1080 baseline at identical six camera fixtures and Epic 3/100% screen percentage. The candidate native report/process and restored managed window pass; the map-only trial receipt preserves geometry, materials, collision and every other actor. Exact image/report/window/receipt SHA256 values are in `PathShadowReview.json`.

At Corner the large black sidewalk wedges and long strip-end shadows disappear in Standard and Comic, making the paving read continuously. Euclid’s background angled strip shadows and several long left-side Grace seams also disappear. Northwest’s right pavement is cleaner. Building and character shadows, signs, façade, prop, car and readable native HUD remain; no new obvious regression was seen across all 12 images.

Broad black roadway-edge bands and some thin overlap/depth seams remain. This flag change does not join path geometry, change pavement heights, fix coplanar overlap or establish collision quality. Generic empty corridors, the Grace horizon and Comic trim dot/line clutter remain. Native idle poses vary; body pixels are not matched. This candidate was reviewed at 1080 only, with no continuous playback, so no 720 candidate, motion/FPS/audio or final district/package acceptance is implied.
