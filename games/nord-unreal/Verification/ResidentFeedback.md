# Resident and activity feedback — 2026-10-14

Implementation targets the current integrated NORD checkout, not the retired three-objective prototype. This pass adapts interaction/feedback ideas inspected in Grand Theft Argon using original native code and original synthesized audio. No Argon code or media was copied.

## Behavior

- Authority checks every player's nearby residents, including remote controllers whose server `PlayerTick` does not run. Calm walking approaches can trigger a private Québec French greeting. Fast occupied cars approaching a resident's travel corridor can trigger a warning. Hidden player-crew residents, slow/parked/departing cars and blocked sight lines are excluded.
- Residents pause and turn for 2.4 seconds. Shared route-clock compensation preserves their position when resuming, including overlapping manual E conversations. They remain on existing routes; this is pause/turn behavior, not fleeing or obstacle-avoidance navigation.
- Ambient captions yield to existing private messages and story briefings. Manual E dialogue keeps its original content and receives a short sound. New French ambient lines are captions, not synthesized spoken dialogue.
- Accepted campaign steps and ordered checkpoints trigger built-in owning-client sound RPCs with original short SoundWaves. Completion has a distinct cue. Recovery, reload and late join do not replay past success sounds.
- Native race finishes replicate last elapsed time, whether recovery was used, and the session's best clean lap. T preserves these results and completed story progress. Version-1 mission-boundary saves are unchanged; session lap times are not persisted.
