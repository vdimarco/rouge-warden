## Native integration

`Scripts/test_story_slice.py` exercises ordinary controller input callbacks through two-player listen-server PIE, with authority-only fixture positioning. It observes public campaign state rather than advancing it directly. The suite targets invalid range/future actions/vehicle state, valid rescue sequence, ordered driven race gates, retries, peer join/departure, cosmetic C/private F2 preservation and actual mission-boundary save/reload. Client RPC input runs after Python's execution guard exits, matching the existing native smoke harness. Every fixture relocation and native action window is traced separately.

Persistence uses the isolated `NordCampaign_StorySliceQA_v1` slot. The suite restores any pre-existing QA-slot bytes after PIE ends, verifies all other `.sav` hashes and restores editor play settings on failure as well as success. It reloads partial mission 1 to the opening, partial race to the completed mission-1 boundary and completed mission 2 to completion. T replays the race while retaining both completed missions and saved boundary 2. Explicitly disabled persistence cannot count as a passing save/reload check.

The final scenario seeds an isolated future-version save using Unreal's native `SaveGameToSlot` format (Version 2, completed boundary 1), then actually replays the rescue. It requires a fresh opening and warning on both peers, successful session-only mission completion and byte-identical future-save contents after the attempted boundary write. This is an unsupported-version preservation check; it does not test every corrupt-file format.

## Physical play and visual scope

Play from a fresh slice with physical keyboard/mouse, first solo and then with a peer. Record findings and reproducible confusion rather than converting automatic assertions into a fun/quality verdict.

- At the chalet, follow the opening mystery without prior instructions. Confirm the displaced shadow/worklight teaches how light reveals a clamp, the plunger action has readable feedback, and the two action types feel distinct. Try E around the parked light car; entry/exit must not silently perform a clamp action. Check that C preserves tools and story state and F2 changes only the local view.
- Finish the rescue and follow the handoff to the race. Confirm the restored terrace, collector mark and souvenir provide a comprehensible consequence without revealing the later twist prematurely. Dialogue and objectives should remain readable, brief and recoverable if missed.
- Drive the circuit using acceleration, braking, corners and a controlled handbrake turn with ordinary traffic/curbs present. The tempting ideal line and safe alternative must be readable in time; the alternate route should reward observation, not memorization of an invisible trigger. No required passenger or destructive bystander strategy is acceptable.
- Miss a gate, retry and recover. Check safe placement, clear checkpoint guidance, retained completed story work and no duplicate completion/reward. Depart and rejoin mid-encounter; the remaining player must be able to proceed and the joiner must understand the current state.
- [Completed visual inspection](StoryVisualReview.md) covers all 18 native states at both sizes: opening/tool prompt, exposed clamp, restored terrace, race/retry/completion and future-save warnings. Continue checking these cues during physical play; static frames and PNG integrity do not establish moving-scene readability or comprehension.

Automatic fixture relocation does not prove physical driving enjoyment or a complete naturally traversed race. Native callback checks do not prove physical input delivery. Static screenshots do not establish pacing, audio quality, co-op comprehension, complete campaign implementation or passing performance. The separate current [package performance measurements](Performance.json) pass all six static reference-view p95 budgets; they do not establish worst-case driving/co-op performance.
