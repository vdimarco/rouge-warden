# BlackCap cheek/frame diagnosis — 17 October 2026

The hanging cheek/frame shapes are real protruding geometry, not only black pixels painted on the skin. The actual-source [front clay](clay-face-front.png) and [three-quarter clay](clay-face-threequarter.png) remove the imported material **only on a scratch copy**, exposing both downward branches from the outer frame. Their geometry hash remains identical to the [original-material review](../../BodyReview/BlackCap/Review.json); no source repair or bitmap edit was made.

They are **not safely removable disconnected components**. The raw GLB contains 3,486 components because its geometry is split at UV/normal boundaries. On a temporary BMesh transformed into world space, welding coincident positions within **1 micrometre** yields **one component containing all 103,225 triangles and 51,579 vertices**, with 48 boundary edges. The same result holds at 10 micrometres. No polygons are removed at either tolerance. Thus deleting a whole geometric component would remove the intended frames, face and body together. Treating the raw UV-chart components as loose accessory parts would also be unsafe.

[TopologySummary.json](TopologySummary.json) records exact body/head bounds, raw versus welded counts, unchanged input hash and three measured focus volumes around the two cheek appendages and upper frame. All three belong to geometric component 0. The approximate volumes select 3D evidence; **they are not deletion masks** and include adjacent intended surfaces. The raw base/evaluated positions differ by at most 0.241 micrometres; the evaluated hash matches the original body review exactly. This representational difference does not change the component result.

There is no verified safe corrective derivative at this checkpoint. Keep paid-rig acceptance on hold for this exact source. The smallest possible next trial would be a carefully isolated local eyewear edit on a separate derivative, preserving the intact rim/temples and closing the cut boundaries, followed by fresh geometry/UV/material/skin and visual checks. Its cut locations have not been proven safe here; no automatic component deletion, whole-body remesh or forced patch is recommended.
