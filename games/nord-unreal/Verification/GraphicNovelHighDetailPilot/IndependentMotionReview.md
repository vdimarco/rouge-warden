# High-detail pilot native motion review

All 82 selected decoded frames were visually inspected in 12 contact sheets plus 3 full-resolution gameplay frames. Both final motion jobs/reports passed and the window restored the demo. Interrupted 083947Z motion is excluded. Continuous playback was not viewed.

Camera/HUD framing, advancing walk/sprint poses, turn, braking, jump, landing, clothing/shoe coverage and attached prop remain coherent in the samples. Native selected-state checks verify 90-degree turns, approximately 183.6 cm jump peaks, zero horizontal jump drift and recovered idle. These do not establish full fluidity or exact planting.

Detached ground-shadow fragments remain visible, clearest in Standard through stop/jump/landing/recovery; isolated soft slashes also appear in Comic sprint/turn samples. Cause is unproven. Static hands, companion-still raised shoulder lobes, motion blur, indigo comic cast and noisy environment ink remain. Shadow polish and final graphics/motion are unaccepted.

Raw videos contain gameplay at sampled 7 s, editor UI at 8 s, black at 9 s, and CsvProfiler debug text during gameplay. Standard is 9.016667 s/541 frames; Comic 9.516667 s/571, both encoded 60 fps. They are diagnostic captures rather than clean trailers.

Skipped: continuous playback, audio, physical controls, co-op and packaged performance. Keep the high-detail pilot isolated; no production or cast promotion.
