# City and character correction — 2026-10-10

This pass addresses unreadable faces, missing locomotion poses, empty streets and generic environment art in the existing Linux Unreal game. It preserves the comic direction, eight selectable characters, native controls, objectives and shared geographic origin.

**Status: quality remediation remains active.** The official saved-asset player matrices pass all eight bodies and six poses at both supported sizes, with 96 fresh captures. Independent review accepts all eight face close-ups at both sizes and confirms intact, distinct sampled poses. Population, headless gameplay, final packaged gameplay and environment/HUD capture checks also pass within their recorded scenarios. Final post-atlas performance fails the required p95 frame budget in five of six views, and the documented Montréal art limits remain. Other applications remained active on the shared GPU; isolated performance and cause attribution are not claimed. See [the audit](QualityAudit.md), [face review](FaceVisualReview.md) and [environment visual review](EnvironmentVisualReview.md).

## Characters

All eight supplied designs have their own idle, walk, run and jump sequences. Running comes from the already completed Meshy jobs; no new paid generation was submitted. The original jump poses are authored on each retained skeleton. Native character movement controls the capsule; ascent, descent and landing select matching parts of the pose sequence. Walking/running remain root locked. The implementation integrates locomotion phase and holds comic poses toward 12 updates per world second while movement stays continuous; official eight-body native pose/cadence matrices pass at both supported sizes. Native control/replication checks pass, including C cycling independently for each peer while on foot and driving.

The face correction masks character interiors from halftone, print offset and normal-edge ink while retaining silhouette outlines. Generated normal detail is reduced and source corner normals/full UV precision are retained, but those changes alone did not resolve the triangle-colored faces and weak eyes. The source atlases contain tiny disconnected UV islands; reduced mip levels blend their neighboring colors. The saved correction sets only the eight base-color atlases to NoMipmaps, NeverStream and zero LOD bias, with matching importer settings. `MeshyFaceMipsApplied.json` passes all eight saved textures with 2048×2048 BC7 and unchanged original TextureSource RGB. Its preceding 32×32 size readings may be asynchronous default-texture placeholders; they do not prove the earlier native face captures always sampled 32-pixel textures. Protected meshes, default lit materials, animations and retained sources remain unchanged. Both official saved-asset matrices pass without diagnostic explicit-mip or mesh-LOD overrides. Independent review accepts coherent painted detail on all eight faces at both sizes, including restored eyes on styles 3 and 5. See [the face review](FaceVisualReview.md).

Base-color imports remain BC7; exported 2048×2048 TextureSource pixels match the retained source PNGs. Source atlases, geometry and lit materials are preserved by the texture correction. Unlit materials are not the selected correction. Generated anatomy, fingers and lettering remain approximations; matching source pixels does not establish exact photographic likeness.

The official [1280 player report](PlayerQuality/PlayerQuality1280.json) and [1920 player report](PlayerQuality/PlayerQuality1920.json) each pass eight bodies × idle/face/walk/run/airborne/landing, or 48 captures per size, with native movement, clip, root and pose-cadence checks. All 96 PNGs independently pass dimensions/completion and were captured after the saved atlas correction. No diagnostic candidate sampler/LOD overrides are used. Independent visual review covers all sixteen face close-ups plus run/airborne/landing for styles 3, 5 and 9 at both sizes; bodies and sampled poses remain intact and distinct. A foreground court rail partly occludes heads in some run/landing frames, so facial acceptance relies on the unobstructed face/airborne views. The fixture uses a clear soccer pitch and disables input to prevent idle axis events overriding automated movement; slowed observation resolves pose holds and landing recovery. Physical keyboard delivery and exact photographic likeness are outside that harness.

## Street population

The map adds 32 parked cars, 16 traffic cars and 96 walkers (64 on mapped park paths and 32 alongside streets), in addition to the saved fleet and 14 original residents. Placement uses ground/collision queries and fixed bounded counts. Eight supplied designs populate the crowd; distance culling bounds animation and draw costs. The minimap shows nearby parked cars without filling with traffic markers.

Traffic uses a connected subset of mapped roads, validates both full lanes against static collision and prunes dead ends. This corrects reproduced stops against building and tree collision. Cars reserve junction space before turning and yield to players and other vehicles. A stopped or sufficiently slow unoccupied traffic car can be entered with E; possession disables its traffic control. Parking uses asphalt at least 12 m wide, with full footprint clearance. Eight old fleet cars move to clear wide-road spaces at runtime; the farthest moves 627 m. The starter shifts 1.45 m into its forward lane, avoiding the original centerline position that blocked oncoming traffic and the first drive.

The final `Population.json` repeat passes thirteen checks for two-player counts, movement over time, route clearance, obstruction recovery, pedestrian replication, traffic yielding, and entry/exit of added cars. It includes a minute of circulation: 15 of 16 traffic cars move in the final eight-second window, while the other safely follows the starter. All sixteen resume after the obstruction clears. These bounded scenarios do not establish jam-free traffic under every player interaction.

## Parc La Fontaine and Sherbrooke

The district retains 1,161 municipal building shells and roof geometry from the preserved 2016 survey. The park retains its mapped two-pond arrangement, paths, landmark footprints and 4,586 municipal inventory tree locations. Tree species and diameters are source data; heights, crown dimensions and seasonal colors are estimated. Terrain remains flat and the ponds share a level, omitting the documented banks and cascade.

Six asymmetric broadleaf variants replace the old repeated crowns, with blended bough clusters, forks and gaps; a seventh variant handles conifers. They use smooth normals and Nanite. Estimated broadleaf spread ranges from 7.08–20 m, median 10.25 m. Opaque foliage volumes remain stylized; individual leaves are not modeled.

Masonry uses varied brick/limestone, basement courses, narrower sash windows, lintels and sills. Along Sherbrooke, 81 selected survey edges across 37 buildings gain decorative cornices and estimated entrances; 35 include shallow stoops and iron rails. The chalet gains terrace-side glazing and the theatre a light perimeter frame. These details have no collision or usable interiors. Pond shading uses quieter unequal-direction ripples instead of the old regular grid.

Primary references: the [City park page](https://montreal.ca/en/places/parc-la-fontaine), [City heritage guide](https://ville.montreal.qc.ca/pls/portal/docs/PAGE/CONS_PAT_MTL_FR/MEDIA/DOCUMENTS/GUIDE%20PARC%20LA%20FONTAINE_DEPT_CPM_15JUIN2012.PDF), [Sherbrooke heritage assessment](https://ocpm.qc.ca/sites/default/files/pdf/P62/5c2.pdf), [Lemay theatre project](https://lemay.com/projects/theatre-de-verdure/) and [ékm chalet renovation](https://www.ekmarchitecture.com/data-projects/1021-lafontaine-eng). No reference photographs are copied into textures. The Google share resolves to 1334 Rue Sherbrooke Est, but Street View could not be inspected with the available tools. Exact building façades and present-day construction are not reconstructed.

The current [City tennis listing](https://montreal.ca/en/places/parc-la-fontaine-tennis-and-pickleball-courts) distinguishes seven carpet and seven acrylic courts; the retained map uses a shared surface. The [volleyball listing](https://montreal.ca/en/places/beach-volleyball-courts-parc-la-fontaine) describes two courts, whereas the retained combined polygon has one illustrated net. Those are remaining representation gaps, not verified matches.

`EnvironmentRefresh.json` records 4,586 trees, 24 crown batches, 81 façade edges and 19 preserved gameplay actor transforms. Source hash guards for park and municipal city data live in `Data/EnvironmentRefresh/layout.json`; they do not cover the OSM file. Tree XY and inventory diameters remain preserved; trunk heights and collision extents change with the estimated canopy. Seven fixed-camera views in each of `Preview/Environment1280/` and `Preview/Environment1920/` support visual inspection; source and placement assertions do not establish photographic likeness. See [environment provenance](../Data/EnvironmentRefresh/README.md) for regeneration and limits.

Both environment capture reports pass, and [independent visual review](EnvironmentVisualReview.md) inspected all fourteen fresh views plus seven final packaged screenshots. It observes broader irregular crowns, visible forks/gaps and quiet green-grey pond shading without the old ripple grid. Resemblance remains partial: opaque cloud-like crowns/repeated forks, flat banks, generic institutional/landmark façades and blank basement stretches remain visible. The Sherbrooke camera does not convincingly demonstrate the selected doors/stoops. No missing-material fallback or gross broken mesh was observed in those frames; that is not a declaration of exact architectural fidelity or complete artistic acceptance.
