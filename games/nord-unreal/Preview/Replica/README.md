# Plateau replica previews

These are Blender previews of the generated replica layers, not Unreal captures. The layers have not been imported or checked in Unreal yet, so the editor captures listed in `Data/Plateau/ReplicaGeometry/README.md` are still owed.

## The game frame, not the real city

The game world is a mirror image of the real Plateau (`openspec/changes/nord-gta-level-up/notes-mirror.md`). These images show it the way the player will see it in Unreal, not the way the street looks in Montréal. For example:

- Facing Montréal-north on Papineau, the park is on your right. In the real city it is on your left.
- Signs that stand on the right of the lane in Montréal stand on the left of the lane here, as the ARRÊT in `corner.jpg` does.
- Text still reads left to right: the props are modelled for the mirrored world (`Data/Plateau/ReplicaGeometry/Props/README.md`).

When the mirror fix lands, every view flips left to right.

## How they are made

`Scripts/blender/plateau_replica_preview.py` builds each view. Run it with a Python that can `import bpy` (Blender 5.1), from `games/nord-unreal`:

```bash
<blender-python> Scripts/blender/plateau_replica_preview.py --list
<blender-python> Scripts/blender/plateau_replica_preview.py --view fabre --samples 24 --threads 4           # fabre.jpg
<blender-python> Scripts/blender/plateau_replica_preview.py --view fabre --before --samples 32              # fabre_before.jpg
<blender-python> Scripts/blender/plateau_replica_preview.py --view ruelle_mentana --probe 950,520 220,300   # what two pixels show
```

The other views are `mentana`, `rachel`, `corner`, `ruelle`, `ruelle_mentana`, `papineau` and `aerial`. The first run indexes the OBJ bounds and saves the tree model into `Saved/ReplicaPreview/`. `python3 Scripts/test_plateau_replica_preview.py` checks the material graphs and the yard-tree transforms the script builds, against a small fake `bpy`, so it runs without Blender.

- **Geometry.** Every OBJ is placed as written: (x, −y, z) of a game-frame point. Blender is right-handed and Unreal is left-handed, so this shows the mirrored world the way Unreal does. Each view loads the faces inside its camera wedge, usually out to 170 m.
- **Existing layers** are the ones visible in the game: the background masses, `OSM Road`, `OSM Path`, `OSM Glass`, the ground and park lawn (`GroundGeometry`), and the park, perimeter, context and landmark batches. The script leaves out what the game hides or replaces: the `OSM Brick/Cream/Sage/Roof` masses, the old canopies, and `OSM Grass`, whose mesh `plateau_ground.py` replaces with `ParkGrass`. `OSM Marking` appears only in *before* images, because the replica apply step hides it.
- **Replica layers** are `Streets`, `Plex` and `Soul`, read from their manifests. The Furniture and Soul `placements.json` rows are instanced from the `Props` meshes at position (x, −y, z) and yaw −yaw. The street-name text that the apply step adds as `TextRender` is drawn as flat black text in the same `text_slots`.
- **Trees.** The 4,571 mapped trees are the Poly Haven `tree_small_02` instanced at the OSM tree nodes, with the scale and yaw from `plateau_rendering.py`. The 573 Plex yard trees in `Plex/yard_trees.json` stand on the same model with the transforms the apply step gives its four instanced actors (`apply_plateau_replica.plan_trees`): position (x, −y, z), yaw −yaw, scale (scale × crown, scale × crown, scale), where the crown factor is 1.15 for maples, 0.9 for ash, 1.05 for elms and 1 for small trees. Both use the leaf material from `refine_plateau_trees.py`, which the yard-tree actors keep: it desaturates the leaves 35 %, blends each tree from green to gold by its per-instance random value, and cuts out at 1/3.
- **Colours and textures.** Replica materials use their manifest colours. Asphalt and brick follow `apply_plateau_replica.py`: they multiply their Poly Haven texture by the target colour divided by the texture mean, capped at `TINT_LIMIT` (8). Every key that takes a replica texture set (the apply step's `base_material` decides: `Stone`, `Slate` and the 15 shared keys of the rear pass) is wired the way `apply_plateau_replica.textured_graph` wires it:
  - the set's `diff`, `nor_dx` and `rough` maps from `Textures/`, with the tiling from `textures.json` (1 / tile size on metre UVs, 1 / tile length by 1 on the card UVs of `Rail_Baluster`, `Rail_Lattice` and `Fence_Boards`);
  - base colour = diffuse × (colour / `mean_linear`) capped at 8, roughness from the red channel of `rough`, the manifest's metallic, and the normal map read with the Normal Map node's DirectX convention, as Unreal reads it;
  - for the masked sets (`Rail_Baluster`, `Rail_Lattice`, `Fence_Boards`, `Leaf_Creeper`), a texel is kept only where the diffuse alpha reaches the set's clip (0.5), through a Mix Shader with a Transparent BSDF. The alpha is read channel-packed, so the colour under a hole is never premultiplied. Cycles shades both faces, as the two-sided masked materials do;
  - `Leaf_Creeper` also transmits light: a Translucent BSDF of base colour × 0.65 is added, the foliage parent's `SubsurfaceColor`.

  Every other replica material is a flat colour, including `Stone_Cast`, `Wood_Deck`, `Trim`, the wood paints and the Streets parterres (`yard_grass`, `yard_soil`). Existing materials use the colours and textures that `upgrade_plateau.py` gives them. Daylight emissive lenses are dimmed to 8 % of their Unreal strength.
- **Light.** An autumn afternoon: a Hosek-Wilkie sky and a warm sun from the south-south-west, 31° up, unless the view says otherwise.
- **Probe.** `--probe X,Y ...` builds the view and, instead of rendering, casts a camera ray through each pixel and prints the batch, material, game-frame point (cm), normal and whether the face is seen from behind. Rays pass through the clear texels of the masked cards, as the clipped material does. The batch and material names in the review below come from it.
- **Technique sources.** The cut-out wiring follows the usual Cycles setup for clipped transparency: image alpha into the factor of a Mix Shader with a Transparent BSDF, clipped at 0.5 (Katsbits, *Cycles, Transparency & Blender 4.x+*, https://www.katsbits.com/codex/?p=8373). The image's *Channel Packed* alpha mode keeps the colour and alpha channels independent (Blender Manual, *Image Settings*, https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/editors/image/image_settings.html). The creeper's added translucency stands in for Unreal's two-sided foliage model, where the subsurface colour sets how much light passes through (Epic, *Shading Models*, https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/shading-models-in-unreal-engine). No code was taken from them.

## Review against the research

Reference: `research-plateau.md` and the owner's "soul" brief.

**What reads as the Plateau.** Rows of plexes with steep black exterior stairs, wooden balconies, bracketed cornices, stone sills and lintels and raised ground floors. Parterres with low iron fences. Grey saw-cut sidewalks with a real curb. Street trees, blue bins, locked bikes, R-PF signs, a red hydrant, the ARRÊT with its stop bar, white street-name blades, green bollards on Rachel, orange chantier cones. From above, the plex roofs are now white membrane and gravel with chimneys, hatches and party copings, and the yards have lawns, patios, beds and board fences. In the ruelles the fences have boards and gaps, the guards have balusters, and the creeper is red.

**Fixed since the last set** (checked in these images):

- **Flat roofs were slate.** They are white membrane, white gravel ballast or tar now (`aerial.jpg`, the plex roofs across the top half; probe `Roof_Membrane` at (120, 230), `Roof_Gravel` at (950, 300), `Roof_Tar` at (250, 270)).
- **Siding read as render.** The lap siding shows its courses (`rachel.jpg`, the grey wall at x 1137–1190, y 175–420).
- **The long blank box.** The Mentana block 356220851 now has 90 windows on each long wall (`aerial.jpg`, right half).
- **Galleries read as concrete; fences were flat panels.** Most guards are baluster cards, and the fences are boards with gaps, posts and cap rails (`ruelle_mentana.jpg`, right half and both fences).
- **Bare yards.** Yards have lawn, patio, beds and a fence (`aerial.jpg`, x 175–490, y 560–700).
- **Window cards outside the layer.** The park-perimeter row (`aerial.jpg`, top) and the background mass on the right of `ruelle.jpg` now have skins with windows.
- **Floating stoop steps.** The buff plex's stoop in `corner.jpg` (x 0–205, y 445–540) has solid steps with risers.

**Defects in this set.** Positions are pixel boxes in the named image.

1. **`mentana.jpg` – a street tree stands in the parking lane.** The trunk at x 690–730, y 300–540 (probe (705, 500): the tree 7 cm from OSM tree node 12034342064, game (−37694, −5171)) stands on the replica carriageway. Across the district, 664 of the 4,571 mapped trees stand on Streets carriageway asphalt. 500 of them are within 1 m of the sidewalk edge, which points at the curbs that are clamped out to at least 550 cm from the centreline (Mentana is 11.0 m from curb to curb instead of about 10 m), past trees mapped near the old curb; 74 stand more than 3 m out. The mapped trees have no collision, so traffic and parked cars will drive through them. The tree nodes need moving to the new sidewalk or the curb needs to give way around them.
2. **`fabre.jpg` – bare floor and a gap in the sidewalk.** The front of the near-left plex is bare district floor (x 0–520, y 505–720; probes (300, 650), (100, 680), (470, 560) hit the ground plane), which is what the spec's "no taupe paver surface between the curb and the building line" forbids. A dark strip crosses the sidewalk (x 520–770, y 613–630): the replica sidewalk is missing there, and the probes find the old `OSM Path` at z 5 and the ground plane under it. Both were already in the last set.
3. **`aerial.jpg` – siding 31 m up a 9-storey wall.** The Saint-André end of block 356220851 is cream lap siding to the top (x 1180–1280, y 450–720; probe (1230, 550): `Siding_Lap_Cream` at 27.2 m). It is a 4.5 m `side` wall beside the block's Saint-André corner front. The "never within 6 m of the front line" rule measures only from the main front, not from corner side fronts, and no height limit stops siding on a tall block. It reads as a smooth dark sheet.
4. **`aerial.jpg` – bare floor beside the 9-storey block.** About 50 m by 5–10 m of bare tan floor runs along the block's long wall and the ruelle (x 440–760, y 400–720; probes (650, 520) and (480, 690) hit the ground plane 5.0 and 5.2 m from the ruelle centreline). The block has no rear wall and more than four storeys, so it gets no yard, fence or ground cover.
5. **`aerial.jpg` – the 9-storey block reads as a warehouse.** A 49 m by 31 m brick box with small windows, no balconies and a single 14 x 49 m gravel roof with one chimney and one hatch (x 650–1280, y 90–720). The roll gives it 9 storeys and the year 1885, and the year picks a 19th-century crown and brick; a 9-storey block is more likely a later apartment building with balconies. The ballast's 1 m tile also shows as a regular diagonal grid across that roof.
6. **`aerial.jpg` – background roofs stay dark.** The background and perimeter masses keep the dark `Roof` colour (x 0–650, y 50–260; probe (300, 120): `BackgroundGeometry Roof` at 9.6 m) next to the white plex roofs, and their bright red skins with one small window per 4.5 m bay read as blank boxes (x 15–350, y 150–270).
7. **`rachel.jpg` – a tower lost its windows and its brown brick.** Tower 358275482 (x 695–970, y 155–390; probes (800, 250) and (920, 200): `Brick_Red` 20–26 m up, 105 m away) is now a flat orange-red box with 130 windows over 77 m of wall and 10 storeys, about one per 6 m of wall per floor; the old window cards were far denser (the previous set's `rachel.jpg`, in git history). `Plex/README.md` says towers get buff or brown brick, but three of the eight (357948906, 358275482, 359275176) are `Brick_Red`.
8. **`aerial.jpg`, `ruelle_mentana.jpg` – yard trees read as summer, and too heavy.** The yard trees are mostly green with a few gold leaves (`ruelle_mentana.jpg` x 280–780, y 0–400; `aerial.jpg` x 370–640, y 410–610 and x 480–940, y 555–720). In late October Plateau maples and ash are red, orange and yellow. Because one 4.55 m model is scaled up to 18 m, a mature tree gets a trunk 1.1–1.6 m thick near the ground (`ruelle_mentana.jpg` x 150–290, y 0–390, the maple at game (−16350, −22750), scale 3.96), long bare limbs, a sparse crown and a crown that leans off the trunk by up to 2 m (the model's crown centroid is 0.49 m off its trunk at scale 1, and the direction depends on how the importer turns the glTF axes). From the lane the maple's crown spreads over the ruelle.
9. **`ruelle_mentana.jpg` – solid guards and gates are flat colour.** The solid-board guards on the near-left galleries are flat `Wood_Grey` with no board joints (x 0–280, y 0–90 and y 190–300; probe (60, 225) at 4.74 m), so they still read as concrete; about a third of the guard runs (2,072 of 6,587) are solid. The gate leaf is a flat `Wood_Green` panel (x 925–985, y 410–650; probe (950, 520)).
10. **`ruelle_mentana.jpg`, `ruelle.jpg` – the ruelle-verte plants look like folded paper.** The perennial mounds are low-poly faceted shapes (`ruelle_mentana.jpg` purple x 770–830, y 495–560, probe `plant_purple`, and orange x 805–860, y 575–600; `ruelle.jpg` x 30–270, y 540–580, probes `plant_gold` at (215, 565) and `plant_green` at (100, 560)).
11. **`ruelle.jpg` – rear windows read as black holes.** The applied windows on the buff rear have dark green `Wood_Green` frames around dark glass, so only the sill shows (x 1150–1260, y 285–470 and x 1130–1245, y 0–95; probes at (1151–1157, 380) and (1205, 288) hit `Wood_Green` at 2.0–2.7 m, (1200, 380) hits `Glass`).
12. **Buff brick reads as a speckled mix.** `#CCB07F` multiplies the red brick photo by up to 8, and its light and dark bricks keep their contrast, so the buff fronts read as cream, olive, red and dark speckle rather than buff (`corner.jpg` x 0–440, y 0–450 and x 800–880, y 150–400; `ruelle.jpg` x 870–1280, y 0–570, probe `Brick_Buff`; `mentana.jpg` x 790–980, y 80–420).
13. **`rachel.jpg` – siding faces the street.** A grey lap-siding side wall stands in full view of Rachel between two fronts (x 1137–1190, y 175–420; probe (1160, 250): `Siding_Lap_Grey` at 6.8 m). It is more than 6 m from its own front line, so the rule allows it.
14. **`aerial.jpg` – tar roofs read flat black, sheet metal reads brown.** `Roof_Tar` (`#2C2B29`) scales the tar texture by about 0.22, so no gravel shows (x 200–297, y 240–303). The flat `Sheet_Metal` gallery roofs read dark brown in shade (x 435–495, y 425–450; probe `Sheet_Metal` at 9.45 m), because metallic 0.6 mirrors the brick around them; galvanised sheet reads light grey.
15. **`papineau.jpg` – parterres are flat colours.** The Streets parterres are flat `yard_grass` and `yard_soil` (x 1190–1280, y 420–720; probes (1230, 500) and (1240, 650)), a saturated green and a flat brown that do not match the textured Plex yards.
16. **`corner.jpg` – a crack seal looks like paint.** The glossy black crack seal at x 1050–1098, y 515–527 (probe `crack_seal` at z 7.1) catches the sky and reads as a white painted hook. It was in the last set too.
17. **No laundry in view.** The lane-life pass moved the laundry from the utility poles to the galleries over the yards (`Data/Plateau/ReplicaGeometry/README.md`), so `ruelle.jpg` no longer has a line across the lane. In `ruelle_mentana.jpg` the nearest lines are behind the camera or hidden behind galleries 48 m away, so no view in this set shows laundry.

**Lighting, not geometry.** In `ruelle.jpg` the crossing ruelle reads pure black (x 150–575, y 585–720). Ray casts from that asphalt find open sky and no second surface; it is in the shadow of the left wall with the sun low ahead (24°) and exposure 0.35. The lower half of `ruelle_mentana.jpg` is nearly black for the same reason. The game's exposure and sky fill will decide how dark these read.

**Still open from earlier sets.**

1. **One tree for everything.** Every street, park and yard tree is the same small Poly Haven tree, so the park in `papineau.jpg` (x 0–1000, y 200–440) reads as an orchard of clones, and the per-kind crown factors (0.9–1.15) do not change that.
2. **Empty streets.** There are no parked cars or people here. The game adds 32 parked cars and traffic at runtime.
3. **Low autumn sun on narrow streets.** At 24–31° the sun leaves most of Mentana and the ruelles in shade. That is true to late October.
4. **Mirror.** Until the mirror fix, signs stand on the left of the lane as the player sees it, and the park sits on the wrong side of Papineau and Rachel.
5. **Street names are text actors.** These previews draw them in Blender; the apply step's `TextRender`s have not been seen in the editor.
6. **Texture checks owed on the host.** Slate orientation (the previews assume the OBJ importer flips V, so image row 0 is up), course joints through the 23 cm `Stone` lintels, the card alpha-coverage mips at a distance, and the creeper's two-sided foliage shading all need a look in Lumen.

## Limits of these previews

- Shading is Cycles path tracing, not Lumen. There is no comic post-process, wet street, exposure adaptation or fog.
- Cycles shades every face from both sides. Unreal culls the back of one-sided materials, so a face wound the wrong way shows here but not in the game; `--probe` reports `seen_from_behind` for the faces it hits.
- Cycles samples the full-resolution card textures many times per pixel, which stands in for Unreal's alpha-coverage mips but is not the same filter.
- Only faces inside each camera wedge are loaded, so the far side of the park in `papineau.jpg` and the ends of long streets and lanes stop at bare ground or sky (`ruelle_mentana.jpg` x 600–655, y 400–430).
- Unreal's Nanite, LOD, cull distances and shadow settings are not reproduced. Small props keep their shadows here.
