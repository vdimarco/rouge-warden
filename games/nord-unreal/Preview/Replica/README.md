# Plateau replica previews

These are Blender previews of the generated replica layers, not Unreal captures. The layers have not been imported or checked in Unreal yet, so the editor captures listed in `Data/Plateau/ReplicaGeometry/README.md` are still owed.

## The game frame, not the real city

The game world is a mirror image of the real Plateau (`openspec/changes/nord-gta-level-up/notes-mirror.md`). These images show it the way the player will see it in Unreal, not the way the street looks in Montréal. For example:

- Facing Montréal-north on Papineau, the park is on your right. In the real city it is on your left.
- Signs that stand on the right of the lane in Montréal stand on the left of the lane here, as the ARRÊT in `corner.jpg` does.
- Text still reads left to right: the props are modelled for the mirrored world (`Data/Plateau/ReplicaGeometry/Props/README.md`).

When the mirror fix lands, every view flips left to right.

## How they are made

`Scripts/blender/plateau_replica_preview.py` builds each view. Run it with a Python that can `import bpy` (Blender 5.1), from `games/nord-unreal`:

```bash
<blender-python> Scripts/blender/plateau_replica_preview.py --list
<blender-python> Scripts/blender/plateau_replica_preview.py --view fabre --samples 24 --threads 4           # fabre.jpg
<blender-python> Scripts/blender/plateau_replica_preview.py --view fabre --before --samples 32              # fabre_before.jpg
<blender-python> Scripts/blender/plateau_replica_preview.py --view ruelle_mentana --probe 640,600 552,650   # what two pixels show
<blender-python> Scripts/blender/plateau_replica_preview.py --view gallery_closeup --camera=X,Y,Z,TX,TY,TZ  # another camera
```

The first run indexes the OBJ bounds into `Saved/ReplicaPreview/`, and the first `--before` run also saves the old tree's model there. `python3 Scripts/test_plateau_replica_preview.py` checks the script against a small fake `bpy`, so it runs without Blender (see *Checks*).

- **Geometry.** Every OBJ is placed as written: (x, −y, z) of a game-frame point. Blender is right-handed and Unreal is left-handed, so this shows the mirrored world the way Unreal does. Each view loads the faces that reach its region: a disc of 45 m around the camera plus a wedge 22° wider than the field of view on each side, usually out to 90–170 m. A face is kept when a corner is in the region, when an edge crosses it, or when it holds the camera, so a long lane quad whose corners lie behind the camera and past the far distance stays. The OBJ index forgets files a generator has removed. It never caches tree positions; see *Fixed* for why.
- **Existing layers** are the ones visible in the game: the background masses, `OSM Road`, `OSM Path`, `OSM Glass`, the ground and park lawn (`GroundGeometry`), and the park, perimeter, context and landmark batches. The script leaves out what the game hides or replaces: the `OSM Brick/Cream/Sage/Roof` masses, the old canopies, and `OSM Grass`, whose mesh `plateau_ground.py` replaces with `ParkGrass`. `OSM Marking` appears only in *before* images, because the replica apply step hides it.
- **Replica layers** are `Streets`, `Plex` and `Soul`, read from their manifests. `Collision_Only` batches (the invisible walls on the gallery guards, open stair sides and stair-head rails) are never read or drawn: the apply step spawns them hidden in game. The Furniture and Soul `placements.json` rows are instanced from the `Props` meshes at position (x, −y, z) and yaw −yaw, each part in the colour variant the apply step picks from the row's `variant_seed` (`[material] + variants`, indexed by the seed modulo their count). The street-name text that the apply step adds as `TextRender` is drawn as flat black text in the same `text_slots`.
- **Trees.** Every tree stands where and how the apply step places it (`apply_plateau_replica.plan_trees`):
  - The 4,707 street and park trees of `Streets/street_trees.json` (the city inventory, CC BY 4.0, and the OSM trees it lacks) each stand on their kind's species mesh from `Trees/manifest.json`. The kinds are `norway_maple`, `silver_maple`, `honey_locust`, `linden`, `ash`, `elm` (the vase form, for elm and hackberry) and `small`. A tree under `YOUNG_BELOW` of its kind's mature height stands on the kind's young mesh, which wears the kind's bark and leaves; that is 1,520 trees. Each tree sits on the ground under its trunk (pit soil, sidewalk or lawn), with its yaw and the height and trunk-spread scale of `street_tree_scale`.
  - The 565 Plex yard trees of `Plex/yard_trees.json` stand on their species meshes, scaled uniformly to their row's height. The old kind `maple` maps to `norway_maple`.
  - Each species OBJ splits into its `bark` and `leaves` groups (`apply_plateau_replica.tree_slot`). It keeps the normals the generator bent to the crown, and takes the textured bark and the masked, two-sided autumn leaf atlas. A mesh is read only when one of its trees is in the view.
  - The old summer tree (Poly Haven `tree_small_02`) appears only in `--before` images, or when `street_trees.json` does not exist. That matches the game: once the street trees stand, the apply step clears `QuebecVegetation`'s instances. The old tree then stands at the current `plateau_rendering.tree_positions()`, with that file's scale and yaw and the summer leaf material `refine_plateau_trees.py` gives it.
- **Colours and textures.** Replica materials take the apply step's own plan row for their key (`apply_plateau_replica.material_row`). Asphalt and brick multiply their Poly Haven texture by the target colour divided by the texture mean, capped at `TINT_LIMIT` (8). Every key that takes a replica texture set is wired the way `apply_plateau_replica.textured_graph` wires it. That covers `Stone`, `Slate`, the 22 shared keys (among them `Brick_Buff_Even` and the fourth pass's `Concrete_Lane_Aged`, `Leaf_Cedar`, `Plant_Grass_Fountain`, `Plant_Pit_Flowers`, `Iron_Wrought` and `Cloth_Laundry`), the plan's other textured keys such as `Rail_Baluster_Teal` and `Concrete_Exposed`, and the trees' bark and leaves:
  - the set's `diff`, `nor_dx` and `rough` maps from `Textures/` (or `Trees/` for the bark and the leaf atlas), with the row's tiling (1 / tile size on metre UVs, 1 / tile length by 1 on the card UVs, 1 on the atlases);
  - the set's address modes (`apply_plateau_replica.address_modes`). Tiling sets wrap. The laundry and leaf atlases clamp both axes (the image's *Extend* mode). Cards wrap U and clamp V. Blender has one extension for both axes, so for a card V is clamped to the centres of the first and last texel rows before a *Repeat* lookup, which is what a clamped V samples: the top of a fence, grass, flower or iron card never blends in its bottom row;
  - base colour = diffuse × the row's tint: colour / `mean_linear` capped at 8, or white for a set that owns its colour (`Roof_Tar`, `Cloth_Laundry`, `Plant_Pit_Flowers`);
  - the macro variation of `add_macro` on the sets that have one (`Brick_Buff_Even`, `Concrete_Lane_Aged`, the roofs and the crushed stone): the tint × lerp(1 − s, 1 + s, mask R at a 17.3 m tile) × lerp(1 − s, 1 + s, mask G at a 5.3 m tile) from `T_Replica_Macro_mask`;
  - roughness from the red channel of `rough`, the row's metallic (the manifest's, capped by the set: `Sheet_Metal` at 0.3), and the normal map read with the Normal Map node's DirectX convention, as Unreal reads it;
  - for the masked sets (`Rail_Baluster`, `Rail_Lattice`, `Fence_Boards`, `Leaf_Creeper`, `Leaf_Cedar`, `Plant_Grass_Fountain`, `Plant_Pit_Flowers`, `Iron_Wrought`, `Cloth_Laundry` and the tree leaves), a texel is kept only where the diffuse alpha reaches the set's clip (0.5), through a Mix Shader with a Transparent BSDF. The alpha is read channel-packed, so the colour under a hole is never premultiplied. Cycles shades both faces, as the two-sided masked materials do;
  - the two-sided foliage sets (`Leaf_Creeper`, `Leaf_Cedar`, `Plant_Grass_Fountain`, `Plant_Pit_Flowers`, the tree leaves) also transmit light: a Translucent BSDF of base colour × 0.65 is added, the foliage parent's `SubsurfaceColor`.

  Every other replica material is a flat colour, including `Stone_Cast`, `Wood_Deck`, `Trim`, the wood paints and the Soul leaf drifts. Existing materials use the colours and textures that `upgrade_plateau.py` gives them. Daylight emissive lenses are dimmed to 8 % of their Unreal strength.
- **Light.** An autumn afternoon: a Hosek-Wilkie sky and a warm sun from the south-south-west, 31° up, unless the view says otherwise.
- **Probe.** `--probe X,Y ...` builds the view and, instead of rendering, casts a camera ray through each pixel and prints the batch, material, game-frame point (cm), normal and whether the face is seen from behind. Rays pass through the clear texels of the masked cards and leaves, read with the set's address modes, as the clipped material does.
- **Checks.** `Scripts/test_plateau_replica_preview.py` (stdlib, about 4 s) checks:
  - every replica-set material against the apply step's plan row: maps and their folder, the address modes (the exact V clamp on cards, *Extend* on the atlases), tiling, tint, macro variation, metallic, mask and transmission. Each of the six fourth-pass keys is read from the manifest that uses it;
  - the yard trees on their species meshes, and the bark and leaves slots and triangle counts of every species OBJ, mature and young;
  - every one of the 4,707 street trees on its kind's mature or young mesh with the plan's transform, the per-kind counts of `street_trees.json`, no old summer tree beside them, and in a `--before` build the old tree at the current positions;
  - that the young mesh is read from the kind's young OBJ and wears the kind's materials;
  - that the OBJ index forgets removed files and drops cached tree positions;
  - the face-keeping rule, including the real alley face under the `ruelle_mentana` camera;
  - that no `Collision_Only` batch is read or drawn;
  - that every placement's parts take the apply step's colour variant;
  - that the `gallery_closeup` camera stands in its lot's yard, and that the `hlm_mentana` camera stands on the far sidewalk past the end wall's corner, looking into the block;
  - that no street-level camera has a street tree within 2.5 m ahead in its frame.

  Each of ten deliberately broken copies of the script fails it: the old summer tree for the street trees, the mature mesh for young trees, cached tree positions, removed OBJs kept, card V wrapping, the atlas wrapping, the probe ignoring the address modes, the old `mentana` or `hlm_mentana` camera, and `Collision_Only` drawn.
- **Technique sources.**
  - The cut-out wiring follows the usual Cycles setup for clipped transparency: image alpha into the factor of a Mix Shader with a Transparent BSDF, clipped at 0.5 (Katsbits, *Cycles, Transparency & Blender 4.x+*, https://www.katsbits.com/codex/?p=8373). The image's *Channel Packed* alpha mode keeps the colour and alpha channels independent (Blender Manual, *Image Settings*, https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/editors/image/image_settings.html).
  - The per-axis clamp copies what Unreal's `TA_Clamp` does, the OpenGL clamp-to-edge rule. Coordinates are held between the centres of the edge texels, [1/(2N), 1 − 1/(2N)], so a linear filter never reaches past the edge (SGI, *SGIS_texture_edge_clamp*, https://www.opengl.org/registry/specs/SGIS/texture_edge_clamp.txt; Khronos forum, *Understanding of GL_CLAMP_TO_…*, https://community.khronos.org/t/understanding-of-gl-clamp-to/75971). Blender's image node clamps both axes at once with *Extend* (Blender Manual, *Image Texture Node*, https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/render/shader_nodes/textures/image.html), so a card clamps V with a Clamp node and keeps *Repeat* for U.
  - The foliage translucency stands in for Unreal's two-sided foliage model, where the subsurface colour sets how much light passes through (Epic, *Shading Models*, https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/shading-models-in-unreal-engine).
  - The face-keeping rule is the per-primitive frustum test made exact for large faces. A corner-only test misses faces that span the view (Paul Bourke, *Frustum Culling*, https://paulbourke.net/miscellaneous/frustum/; Cesium, *Tighter Frustum Culling*, https://cesium.com/blog/2017/02/02/tighter-frustum-culling-and-why-you-may-want-to-disregard-it/). So each edge is clipped to the wedge's two half-planes the way Liang and Barsky clip a line parametrically (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liang%E2%80%93Barsky_algorithm; Liang, Barsky and Slater, UC Berkeley 1992, https://www2.eecs.berkeley.edu/Pubs/TechRpts/1992/6271.html). A face that holds the camera is found with the crossings (even-odd) test (Haines, *Point in Polygon Strategies*, Graphics Gems IV, 1994, https://erich.realtimerendering.com/ptinpoly/).
  - The macro variation, the leaf-card normals and the species trees follow the apply step, the texture generator and the tree generator, which name their own sources (`Data/Plateau/ReplicaGeometry/Textures/README.md`, `Trees/README.md`).

  No code was taken from any of them.

## Review against the research

Reference: `research-plateau.md`, the owner's "soul" brief and the last critics' reports (`fidelity3`).

**What reads as the Plateau.** Rows of plexes with steep black exterior stairs, wooden balconies, bracketed cornices, stone sills and lintels and raised ground floors. Front lawns with fallen leaves behind low iron fences. Grey saw-cut sidewalks with a straight curb. Street trees of the city's inventory in late-October colour, in sidewalk pits with low wrought-iron fences and planted beds. Blue bins, locked bikes in several colours, R-PF signs, a red hydrant, the ARRÊT with its stop bar, white street-name blades, green bollards on Rachel, orange chantier cones. From above, white membrane and gravel roofs with chimneys, hatches and party copings, and yards with lawns, patios, beds and fences. In the ruelles, asphalt from fence to fence, fences with red creeper, baluster guards, lap-siding guards, fountain grasses and autumn trees. Behind the plexes, gallery stacks with chairs, plants and laundry. The OMHM block on Mentana has the form of the 2009 photo: stacked balconies with teal railings, brick bays between concrete piers, blank concrete end walls and a recessed ground floor.

**Fixed since the last set** (checked in these images):

- **The street and park trees were one green summer tree, copied 4,571 times** (critic item 1). Every street and park tree now stands on its kind's species mesh in autumn colour. That is 4,707 inventory and OSM trees, 1,520 of them young street trees. Examples: `papineau.jpg` x 0–1280, y 0–560; `rachel.jpg` x 0–700, y 0–560; `mentana.jpg` x 440–1280, y 0–450; `fabre.jpg` x 450–1110, y 0–490; `aerial.jpg` x 0–700, y 0–470. Its OBJ index had also kept the 4,571 OSM positions from its first run, so even the old tree stood where no tree is any more; tree positions are no longer cached.
- **A curb extension bulged round nearly every street tree** (critic item 2). On Mentana and Papineau the curb runs straight and the trees stand in sidewalk pits behind it (`mentana.jpg` x 490–620, y 300–610; `papineau.jpg` x 870–1010, y 510–575).
- **Tree pits were bare black squares** (critic item 8). Each pit has a low wrought-iron fence and a planted bed (`mentana.jpg` x 490–620, y 530–610; `papineau.jpg` probe (920, 560): `Iron_Wrought`; `rachel.jpg` x 360–580, y 660–720).

## Limits of these previews

- Shading is Cycles path tracing, not Lumen. There is no comic post-process, wet street, exposure adaptation or fog.
- Cycles shades every face from both sides. Unreal culls the back of one-sided materials, so a face wound the wrong way shows here but not in the game; `--probe` reports `seen_from_behind` for the faces it hits.
- Cycles samples the full-resolution card and leaf textures many times per pixel, which stands in for Unreal's alpha-coverage mips but is not the same filter.
- Only faces that reach each camera's region are loaded, so the far side of the park in `papineau.jpg` and the ends of long streets and lanes stop at bare ground or sky.
- The existing materials' colours are the `upgrade_plateau.py` palette or plausible stand-ins (the park lawn, `ParkGrass`, reads tan in `papineau.jpg` x 0–1050, y 380–440), not the Unreal materials themselves.
- Unreal's Nanite, LOD, cull distances and shadow settings are not reproduced. Small props keep their shadows here, and every tree is drawn at full detail.
