# Plateau replica previews

These are Blender previews of the generated replica layers, not Unreal captures. The layers have not been imported or checked in Unreal yet, so the editor captures listed in `Data/Plateau/ReplicaGeometry/README.md` are still owed.

## The game frame, not the real city

The game world is a mirror image of the real Plateau (`openspec/changes/nord-gta-level-up/notes-mirror.md`). These images show it the way the player will see it in Unreal, not the way the street looks in Montréal. For example:

- Facing Montréal-north on Papineau, the park is on your right. In the real city it is on your left.
- Signs that stand on the right of the lane in Montréal stand on the left of the lane here, as the ARRÊT in `corner.jpg` does.
- Text still reads left to right: the props are modelled for the mirrored world (`Data/Plateau/ReplicaGeometry/Props/README.md`).

When the mirror fix lands, every view flips left to right.

## How they are made

`Scripts/blender/plateau_replica_preview.py` builds each view. Run it with a Python that can `import bpy` (Blender 5.1), from `games/nord-unreal`:

```bash
<blender-python> Scripts/blender/plateau_replica_preview.py --list
<blender-python> Scripts/blender/plateau_replica_preview.py --view fabre --samples 24 --threads 4           # fabre.jpg
<blender-python> Scripts/blender/plateau_replica_preview.py --view fabre --before --samples 32              # fabre_before.jpg
<blender-python> Scripts/blender/plateau_replica_preview.py --view ruelle_mentana --probe 640,600 625,405   # what two pixels show
<blender-python> Scripts/blender/plateau_replica_preview.py --view gallery_closeup --camera=X,Y,Z,TX,TY,TZ  # another camera
```

The first run indexes the OBJ bounds and saves the mapped trees' model into `Saved/ReplicaPreview/`. `python3 Scripts/test_plateau_replica_preview.py` checks the script against a small fake `bpy`, so it runs without Blender (see *Checks*).

- **Geometry.** Every OBJ is placed as written: (x, −y, z) of a game-frame point. Blender is right-handed and Unreal is left-handed, so this shows the mirrored world the way Unreal does. Each view loads the faces that reach its region: a disc of 45 m around the camera plus a wedge 22° wider than the field of view on each side, usually out to 90–170 m. A face is kept when a corner is in the region, when an edge crosses it, or when it holds the camera. The last set kept a face only when a corner was inside, so a long quad whose corners all lay behind the camera or past the far distance vanished: the 188 m lane quad under `ruelle_mentana.jpg` was dropped and the lane showed bare district floor. With the new rule the views also keep the large `YardGround`, `ParkGrass`, `Road` and `Path` faces they used to drop (1–37 faces a view).
- **Existing layers** are the ones visible in the game: the background masses, `OSM Road`, `OSM Path`, `OSM Glass`, the ground and park lawn (`GroundGeometry`), and the park, perimeter, context and landmark batches. The script leaves out what the game hides or replaces: the `OSM Brick/Cream/Sage/Roof` masses, the old canopies, and `OSM Grass`, whose mesh `plateau_ground.py` replaces with `ParkGrass`. `OSM Marking` appears only in *before* images, because the replica apply step hides it.
- **Replica layers** are `Streets`, `Plex` and `Soul`, read from their manifests. `Collision_Only` batches (the invisible walls on the gallery guards, open stair sides and stair-head rails) are never drawn: the apply step spawns them hidden in game. A scan of the ten images finds no magenta pixel. The Furniture and Soul `placements.json` rows are instanced from the `Props` meshes at position (x, −y, z) and yaw −yaw, each part in the colour variant the apply step picks from the row's `variant_seed` (`[material] + variants`, indexed by the seed modulo their count). The street-name text that the apply step adds as `TextRender` is drawn as flat black text in the same `text_slots`.
- **Trees.** The 572 Plex yard trees in `Plex/yard_trees.json` stand on the species meshes of `Trees/manifest.json`, as the apply step places them (`apply_plateau_replica.plan_trees` with the species): the old kinds `maple` and `elm` on `norway_maple` and `linden`, each tree scaled uniformly to its row's height, at position (x, −y, z) and yaw −yaw. Each species OBJ splits into its `bark` and `leaves` groups (`apply_plateau_replica.tree_slot`), keeps the normals the generator bent to the crown, and takes the textured bark and the masked, two-sided autumn leaf atlas. The 4,571 mapped street and park trees keep the game's shared Poly Haven `tree_small_02` at the OSM tree nodes, with the scale and yaw from `plateau_rendering.py` and the summer leaf material `refine_plateau_trees.py` gives them by default (desaturated 35 %, each tree blended from green to gold by its per-instance random value, cut out at 1/3), because that is what `QuebecVegetation` still draws.
- **Colours and textures.** Replica materials take the apply step's own plan row for their key (`apply_plateau_replica.material_row`). Asphalt and brick multiply their Poly Haven texture by the target colour divided by the texture mean, capped at `TINT_LIMIT` (8). Every key that takes a replica texture set (`Stone`, `Slate`, the 16 shared keys including `Brick_Buff_Even`, and the trees' bark and leaves) is wired the way `apply_plateau_replica.textured_graph` wires it:
  - the set's `diff`, `nor_dx` and `rough` maps from `Textures/` (or `Trees/` for the bark and the leaf atlas), with the row's tiling (1 / tile size on metre UVs, 1 / tile length by 1 on the card UVs, 1 on the atlas);
  - base colour = diffuse × the row's tint: colour / `mean_linear` capped at 8, or white for a set that owns its colour (`Roof_Tar`);
  - the macro variation of `add_macro` on the sets that have one (`Brick_Buff_Even`, `Roof_Membrane`, `Roof_Gravel`, `Roof_Tar`, `Yard_Gravel`): the tint × lerp(1 − s, 1 + s, mask R at a 17.3 m tile) × lerp(1 − s, 1 + s, mask G at a 5.3 m tile) from `T_Replica_Macro_mask`;
  - roughness from the red channel of `rough`, the row's metallic (the manifest's, capped by the set: `Sheet_Metal` at 0.3), and the normal map read with the Normal Map node's DirectX convention, as Unreal reads it;
  - for the masked sets (`Rail_Baluster`, `Rail_Lattice`, `Fence_Boards`, `Leaf_Creeper`, `Tree_Leaves_Autumn`), a texel is kept only where the diffuse alpha reaches the set's clip (0.5), through a Mix Shader with a Transparent BSDF. The alpha is read channel-packed, so the colour under a hole is never premultiplied. Cycles shades both faces, as the two-sided masked materials do;
  - the two-sided foliage sets (`Leaf_Creeper`, the tree leaves) also transmit light: a Translucent BSDF of base colour × 0.65 is added, the foliage parent's `SubsurfaceColor`.

  Every other replica material is a flat colour, including `Stone_Cast`, `Wood_Deck`, `Trim`, the wood paints and the cedars. Existing materials use the colours and textures that `upgrade_plateau.py` gives them. Daylight emissive lenses are dimmed to 8 % of their Unreal strength.
- **Light.** An autumn afternoon: a Hosek-Wilkie sky and a warm sun from the south-south-west, 31° up, unless the view says otherwise.
- **Probe.** `--probe X,Y ...` builds the view and, instead of rendering, casts a camera ray through each pixel and prints the batch, material, game-frame point (cm), normal and whether the face is seen from behind. Rays pass through the clear texels of the masked cards and leaves, as the clipped material does. The batch and material names in the review below come from it.
- **Checks.** `Scripts/test_plateau_replica_preview.py` (stdlib, about 4 s) checks every replica-set material against the apply step's plan row (maps and their folder, tiling, tint, macro variation, metallic, mask, transmission), the yard trees on their species meshes and each species OBJ's slots, the face-keeping rule (a lane quad, a wall run across the wedge, a face holding the camera, three faces outside, a batch box crossing the wedge sideways, and the alley face under the `ruelle_mentana` camera), that no `Collision_Only` batch is read or drawn, that every placement's parts take the apply step's colour variant (34,463 parts, 7,808 of them in a variant), and that the `gallery_closeup` camera stands in its lot's yard and the `hlm_mentana` camera on the far sidewalk. Each of seven deliberately broken copies of the script (the old corner rule for faces or for batch boxes, `Collision_Only` drawn, no variants, the shared tree mesh, no macro, the manifest tint for the tar) fails it.
- **Technique sources.** The cut-out wiring follows the usual Cycles setup for clipped transparency: image alpha into the factor of a Mix Shader with a Transparent BSDF, clipped at 0.5 (Katsbits, *Cycles, Transparency & Blender 4.x+*, https://www.katsbits.com/codex/?p=8373). The image's *Channel Packed* alpha mode keeps the colour and alpha channels independent (Blender Manual, *Image Settings*, https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/editors/image/image_settings.html). The foliage translucency stands in for Unreal's two-sided foliage model, where the subsurface colour sets how much light passes through (Epic, *Shading Models*, https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/shading-models-in-unreal-engine). The face-keeping rule is the per-primitive frustum test made exact for large faces: a corner-only test misses faces that span the view (Paul Bourke, *Frustum Culling*, https://paulbourke.net/miscellaneous/frustum/; Cesium, *Tighter Frustum Culling*, https://cesium.com/blog/2017/02/02/tighter-frustum-culling-and-why-you-may-want-to-disregard-it/), so each edge is clipped to the wedge's two half-planes the way Liang and Barsky clip a line parametrically (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liang%E2%80%93Barsky_algorithm; Liang, Barsky and Slater, UC Berkeley 1992, https://www2.eecs.berkeley.edu/Pubs/TechRpts/1992/6271.html), and a face that holds the camera is found with the crossings (even-odd) test (Haines, *Point in Polygon Strategies*, Graphics Gems IV, 1994, https://erich.realtimerendering.com/ptinpoly/). The macro variation and the leaf-card normals follow the apply step and the tree generator, which name their own sources (`Data/Plateau/ReplicaGeometry/Textures/README.md`, `Trees/README.md`). No code was taken from any of them.

## Review against the research

Reference: `research-plateau.md`, the owner's "soul" brief and the last critics' reports.

**What reads as the Plateau.** Rows of plexes with steep black exterior stairs, wooden balconies, bracketed cornices, stone sills and lintels and raised ground floors. Front lawns with fallen leaves behind low iron fences. Grey saw-cut sidewalks with a real curb, and curb extensions with tree pits where the trees stand. Blue bins, locked bikes in several colours, R-PF signs, a red hydrant, the ARRÊT with its stop bar, white street-name blades, green bollards on Rachel, orange chantier cones. From above, white membrane and gravel roofs with chimneys, hatches and party copings, and yards with lawns, patios, beds and board fences. In the ruelles, asphalt from fence to fence, board fences with red creeper, baluster guards, lap-siding guards and autumn trees. Behind the plexes, gallery stacks with chairs, plants and laundry. The OMHM block on Mentana reads as a 1970s social-housing slab, not a warehouse.

**Fixed since the last set** (checked in these images):

- **The ruelle had no asphalt.** The `ruelle_mentana.jpg` lane is asphalt from fence to fence with cracks and the centre gutter (x 140–970, y 430–720; probe (640, 600): `alley` at z 6.8). Two fixes meet here: the preview now keeps faces whose edges cross the view, and the Streets layer now paves the lane in pieces of 20 m or less.
- **A street tree stood in the parking lane.** On Mentana it now stands in a pit on a curb extension (`mentana.jpg` x 650–760, y 440–545; probe (700, 530): the trunk at z 21.8 cm over the 18 cm extension). Papineau's trees stand in pits on extensions too (`papineau.jpg` x 830–960, y 530–565).
- **Fabre's bare front yard and sidewalk gap.** The yard is lawn with a walk to the sidewalk, which is continuous (`fabre.jpg` x 0–580, y 490–720; probe (450, 560): `yard_path`).
- **The Mentana block read as an 1885 warehouse.** It has the post-1945 kit: balcony slabs with steel panel guards on every bay, patio doors, panels under the windows, the entrance canopy, a parapet, two brick lift penthouses and vents; no siding (`hlm_mentana.jpg` x 140–860, y 0–680; `aerial.jpg` x 640–1280, y 90–720). Its yardless side has asphalt parking with stall lines and a lawn (`aerial.jpg` x 555–760, y 400–620; probe (700, 600): `Asphalt_Lot`).
- **Background roofs were dark** and their skins blank. They are white membrane and gravel with denser windows (`aerial.jpg` x 0–650, y 30–300). Tar reads dark grey with its gravel (x 135–355, y 105–225; probe (250, 170): `Roof_Tar`).
- **The Rachel tower was an orange-red box.** It is buff with balconies on every second bay (`rachel.jpg` x 650–975, y 145–300; probe (830, 200): `Brick_Buff_Even`).
- **Buff brick was a speckled cream, olive and red mix.** It reads even yellow-tan with grey mortar (`corner.jpg` x 0–440, y 0–450; `ruelle.jpg` x 860–1280, y 0–580; `mentana.jpg` x 760–990, y 100–420; `gallery_closeup.jpg` x 400–1280, y 0–720).
- **Rear windows read as black holes.** They have white frames and blinds (`ruelle.jpg` x 1020–1140, y 285–470, probe (1080, 370): `Trim`; `gallery_closeup.jpg` x 860–960, y 255–360 and x 880–960, y 505–630).
- **Solid guards read as concrete.** They are lap siding (`ruelle_mentana.jpg` x 0–275, y 190–300; probe (100, 240): `Siding_Lap_Cream`).
- **Yard trees were summer clones with 1.1–1.6 m trunks.** They are autumn species with trunks to scale (`ruelle_mentana.jpg` x 0–680, y 0–420: the 18 m Norway maple's trunk at x 195–260 is 59 cm thick, probe (230, 200): `Bark_NorwayMaple`; `aerial.jpg` x 400–870, y 560–720; `gallery_closeup.jpg` x 0–500, y 0–560).
- **Parterres were flat colours.** They are the textured lawn with fallen leaves and the soil bed (`papineau.jpg` x 1190–1280, y 420–720; `fabre.jpg` x 0–580, y 490–720).
- **No laundry in any view.** `gallery_closeup.jpg` shows lines with garments running from the deck guards to the fence poles (x 0–700, y 0–410).
- **Galleries were empty.** The ground deck in `gallery_closeup.jpg` has a patio chair and potted plants (x 455–585, y 615–690).
- **The preview drew invisible walls.** No `Collision_Only` face is loaded, and no image has a magenta pixel.

**Defects in this set.** Positions are pixel boxes in the named image, worst first.

1. **The street and park trees are still the one summer clone.** Every mapped tree is the green `tree_small_02` on a bent trunk, beside autumn yard trees on the species meshes, so the street and the yard read as different seasons: `papineau.jpg` x 0–1000, y 190–440 (the park, an orchard of clones) and x 560–1190, y 0–550; `mentana.jpg` x 470–1280, y 0–480; `rachel.jpg` x 0–700, y 0–720, the near trunk at x 240–500 (probe (680, 300) on a far one: `tree_small_02`); `fabre.jpg` x 480–1280, y 0–430; `corner.jpg` x 630–770 and x 950–1140, y 205–410; `hlm_mentana.jpg` x 430–1280, y 0–720; `aerial.jpg` x 0–700, y 0–110. The apply step puts only the 572 yard trees on `Trees/` meshes; the 4,571 mapped trees stay on `QuebecVegetation`'s shared mesh with the summer leaves (`refine_plateau_trees.py` shifts them to autumn only with `-NordTreeAutumn`). They need a species per tree (the city's inventory, critic item 1) and the species meshes, which means the apply step or the vegetation actor, and the autumn option at least.
2. **No laundry reads from the lane.** In `ruelle_mentana.jpg` the lines in frame are 28–48 m out and sit behind the yard trees' crowns and the galleries: the nearest, at 28.5 m, runs from (469, 327) to (552, 301) with no garments, and the next, at 41 m, from (457, 368) to (576, 360) with 6. The 9 mm rope is under a pixel wide. The spec's lines crossing overhead in a ruelle still show in no lane view.
3. **Sheets hang as stiff white boards.** In `gallery_closeup.jpg` the white garments are flat rectangles with no sag or folds, seen edge-on they read as signs (x 390–460, y 25–150; x 615–690, y 225–370; x 620–680, y 0–70; probes (420, 90) and (650, 300): `cloth_a`). The Soul cloth batches need a sag along the line and a few folds, or a cloth texture.
4. **Cedars are dark faceted cones.** `gallery_closeup.jpg` x 75–280, y 400–720 (probe (200, 600): `Wood_Green` 5.4 m away): `plateau_plex.py` draws a cedar as six flat `Wood_Green` triangles and a hedge as a `Wood_Green` box. They need crossed foliage cards in a cedar green, as the creeper and the ruelle plants have.
5. **Ornamental grass is a thin straw cone.** `ruelle.jpg` x 150–175, y 505–575 (probe (160, 545): `plant_straw`). The other ruelle plants are crossed cards now; the grass clumps are not.
6. **Gallery props hide behind the guards from the yard.** Lot 356349231 has 23 gallery props; from the yard only the ground deck's chair and pots read. The props of the upper decks stand behind the baluster cards and the deck edges (`gallery_closeup.jpg` x 380–1000, y 60–480; at the barbecue's spot, probe (781, 158) hits the `Wood_Deck` edge of its deck, and probe (785, 95) a `Rail_Baluster` card). That is true to eye level, but no view yet shows an upper deck's chairs, barbecue or dish clearly.
7. **Bare floor beside a yard.** `aerial.jpg` x 200–480, y 650–720 (probe (300, 690): `GroundGeometry YardGround` at z 0.5, 11 m from the ruelle centreline and inside no Plex yard). The yards around it have cover; this patch between lots has none.
8. **A crack seal looks like paint.** `corner.jpg` x 1050–1098, y 515–527: the glossy black seal catches the sky and reads as a white painted hook. It is in its third set.
9. **Siding faces Rachel.** A grey lap-siding side wall stands in full view of the street between two fronts (`rachel.jpg` x 1140–1185, y 180–420; probe (1160, 300): `Siding_Lap_Grey` 33.7 m away). It is more than 6 m from its own front line, so the rule allows it.
10. **The Rachel tower's end wall is blank.** Its side facing the camera is plain buff brick with no window (`rachel.jpg` x 700–810, y 150–300; probe (750, 230): `Brick_Buff_Even` 22.7 m up); the balconies are only on the long face. Many slab towers have blind end walls, so this needs a photo of 1565 Rachel Est before anyone changes it.
11. **Tower balconies in painted wood green.** The brown tower's balcony guards are `Wood_Green` (`aerial.jpg` x 1060–1200, y 120–230; probe (1170, 140): `Wood_Green` 23 m up). A 1960s tower's guards are aluminium or steel; `Iron` or `Sheet_Metal` would read truer.
12. **A side wall with few windows across from the OMHM block.** The dark brown plex wall right of the block (`hlm_mentana.jpg` x 840–1100, y 430–650; probe (950, 550): `Brick_BrownDark` 43 m away) has seven small windows over three storeys and reads as a blank box.

**Lighting, not geometry.** In `ruelle.jpg` the dark blocks on the crossing ruelle (x 170–590, y 570–720) are the left wall's shadow on the same `alley` asphalt as the lit strips (probes (300, 610), (100, 620), (300, 700) all hit `alley` at z 6.8). The lower half of `ruelle_mentana.jpg`, most of `mentana.jpg` and the yard in `gallery_closeup.jpg` are in shade with the sun low at 24–31°. The game's exposure and sky fill will decide how dark these read.

**Still open from earlier sets.**

1. **Empty streets.** There are no parked cars or people here. The game adds 32 parked cars and traffic at runtime.
2. **Low autumn sun on narrow streets.** At 24–31° the sun leaves most of Mentana, the ruelles and the yards in shade. That is true to late October.
3. **Mirror.** Until the mirror fix, signs stand on the left of the lane as the player sees it, and the park sits on the wrong side of Papineau and Rachel.
4. **Street names are text actors.** These previews draw them in Blender; the apply step's `TextRender`s have not been seen in the editor.
5. **Texture checks owed on the host.** Slate orientation (the previews assume the OBJ importer flips V, so image row 0 is up), course joints through the 23 cm `Stone` lintels, the card alpha-coverage mips at a distance, the leaf atlas's clamped addressing, and the creeper's and the trees' two-sided foliage shading all need a look in Lumen.

## Limits of these previews

- Shading is Cycles path tracing, not Lumen. There is no comic post-process, wet street, exposure adaptation or fog.
- Cycles shades every face from both sides. Unreal culls the back of one-sided materials, so a face wound the wrong way shows here but not in the game; `--probe` reports `seen_from_behind` for the faces it hits.
- Cycles samples the full-resolution card and leaf textures many times per pixel, which stands in for Unreal's alpha-coverage mips but is not the same filter.
- Only faces that reach each camera's region are loaded, so the far side of the park in `papineau.jpg` and the ends of long streets and lanes stop at bare ground or sky (`ruelle_mentana.jpg` x 615–655, y 380–428; probe (625, 405) hits nothing).
- The existing materials' colours are the `upgrade_plateau.py` palette or plausible stand-ins (the park lawn reads tan in `papineau.jpg` x 0–900, y 380–440, probe (200, 415): `ParkGrass`), not the Unreal materials themselves.
- Unreal's Nanite, LOD, cull distances and shadow settings are not reproduced. Small props keep their shadows here.
