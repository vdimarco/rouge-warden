The WAV files in this folder are original deterministic procedural synthesis,
generated by `Scripts/plateau_life.py` using only Python's standard library.
They contain no downloaded audio, recordings, samples, or recorded conversation.

- `ParkWindBirds.wav`: 40-second loop of filtered noise gusts and quiet synthesized chirps.
- `OccasionalCycleBell.wav`: 47-second loop with sparse synthesized metallic bell strikes.

Both are mono 16-bit PCM at 22,050 Hz. Unreal's level importer makes them looping,
auto-activating, spatialized SoundWave/AmbientSound pairs at low volume. The park
source fades over 135 m beyond a 32 m inner radius; the bell fades over 36 m beyond
a 4 m inner radius. These are an ambience sketch, not location field recordings.

Regenerate: `python Scripts/plateau_life.py`.
Validate: `python Scripts/plateau_life.py --self-check`.
