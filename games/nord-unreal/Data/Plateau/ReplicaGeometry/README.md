# Plateau replica streetscape

Not yet imported or verified on the host. Everything here was generated and checked on the CPU, without Unreal and without a GPU. The host import, the fresh-load verify, the curb-parking, car-collision and traffic checks, the captures and the frame-time profile are all still open.

This folder holds the layers that make the La Fontaine map read as the Plateau at street level. Every layer sits in its own folder with a `manifest.json` that uses one schema: `layer`, `generator`, `frame`, `batches[]` (`file`, `mesh`, `material`, `collision`, `triangles`, `bounds_cm`, `sha256`), `materials{}` (`base_color_srgb`, `roughness`, `metallic`, optional `texture` and `emissive_*`), `counts` and `source_sha256`. The frame is the existing map frame: x east, y north, z up, centimetres (`prepare_lafontaine.xy`). The world is mirrored (see `openspec/changes/nord-gta-level-up/design.md`), and these layers keep that frame so they line up with gameplay.

| Layer | Generator | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Streets/` | `Scripts/plateau_replica.py` | Carriageways cut to curb lines measured from the mapped sidewalks, Québec paint, grey concrete sidewalks with an 18 cm curb, parterres to the building line with low iron fences, the city inventory's street and park trees with their sidewalk pits (low iron guards and planting cards) and the few curb extensions the data or a corner calls for (`street_trees.json`: every tree the game shows, the extensions and the trees left for the host), ruelles paved from fence to fence with a sunken aged-concrete centre gutter with a weedy edge, bike-track bollards |
| `Furniture/` | `Scripts/plateau_replica.py` | `placements.json` only: lamps, hydrants, ARRÊT signs, white street-name signs, one-way and parking signs, BIXI docks with bikes, benches, bins, mail boxes |
| `Soul/` | `Scripts/plateau_replica.py` | Seeded wear and life: asphalt patches, sealed cracks, potholes, late-October leaf litter (`Leaf_Litter` patches under the street and park crowns, drifts at the curbs and in the ruelle gutters), weeds, rust streaks, original colour-block murals, ruelle wires; the lane life (gallery laundry, gallery props, gate props, ruelles vertes, tags, see *Lane life*); `placements.json` for cones, the chantier fence, utility poles, curb and gate bins, flower boxes and locked bikes, and the gallery props (patio chairs, potted plants, barbecues, bikes, bins, window AC units, rail planters, satellite dishes); `lane_life.json` with every lane-life record; and the committed `ruelles_vertes_clip.json` (the city's ruelles vertes over the district) |
| `Plex/` | `Scripts/plateau_plex.py` | Plex fronts on the plain buildings (see `Plex/` files) |
| `Props/` | `Scripts/blender/plateau_props.py` | The prop meshes that Furniture and Soul instance (see `Props/README.md`) |
| `Trees/` | `Scripts/blender/plateau_trees.py` | The species tree meshes in late-October dress (nine kinds, the vase elm, the conifer and the larch among them, and a young tree for each kind but small) that the street, park and yard trees stand on (see `Trees/README.md`) |

The OBJ files are generated and git-ignored (`ReplicaGeometry/**/*.obj` in `games/nord-unreal/.gitignore`). The manifests, placements and READMEs are committed. Generation is deterministic: two builds are byte-identical, and every OBJ hash is in its manifest.

## Regenerate and check (any machine, stdlib Python)

From `games/nord-unreal`:

```bash
python3 Scripts/blender/plateau_trees.py --no-render   # about 15 s: the species tree OBJs (git-ignored), textures
python3 Scripts/plateau_plex.py            # about 2 min: the Plex OBJs (git-ignored), fronts.json, yard records
<blender-python> Scripts/blender/plateau_props.py --no-render   # 2 s, Blender 5.1 (Props/README.md): the prop OBJs
python3 Scripts/plateau_replica.py         # about 30 s: Streets, Furniture, Soul
python3 Scripts/test_plateau_replica.py    # about 2.5 min: builds twice into temp dirs, then checks
python3 Scripts/apply_plateau_replica.py --dry-run   # the import plan, from the manifests only
```

On a fresh checkout run all three builds before `plateau_replica.py`, as both blocks do. Every OBJ is git-ignored while the manifests are committed, so a checkout has none: without the tree OBJs the replica test, the dry run and the apply step stop with the missing OBJ's path under `Trees/`; without the Plex OBJs `plateau_replica.py` cannot read the `Glass` batches; and without the prop OBJs the dry run stops at the first missing file under `Props/`. `plateau_trees.py` and `plateau_plex.py` are stdlib Python; the props build needs a Python that imports bpy (Blender 5.1, `Props/README.md`), and rebuilds byte for byte with that version. The replica layers read `Props/manifest.json` for the BIXI slot positions, the utility-pole wire anchors, the locked-bike lean point and the prop bounds, and fall back to the agreed defaults when it is absent. The Soul layer's lane life reads the Plex yard records in `Plex/fronts.json` (galleries and their props floor, guards, flights, fences, gates, sheds, pads, yard trees), the Plex layer's `Glass` batches (through `Plex/manifest.json`, for the rear windows the AC units sit in) and `Soul/ruelles_vertes_clip.json`; without `fronts.json` it is skipped, and without the clip a seeded 30% of the ruelles is green. The washing and the leaf litter read the crowns the game draws: `Trees/manifest.json` (each kind's crown width, crown base, height and leaf retention) with `Plex/yard_trees.json` and the street trees; the litter also reads the park lawn and what covers it in `Data/Plateau/BaseGeometry` (`Grass.obj`, `Path.obj`, `Water.obj`, `Court.obj`, `Road.obj`). Rerun `plateau_replica.py` whenever `plateau_plex.py` rewrites `fronts.json`, `yard_trees.json` or its batches, `plateau_trees.py` rewrites `Trees/manifest.json`, or the props build rewrites `Props/manifest.json`: the laundry anchors, gallery props, gate props, beds and tags follow their records, and the manifests hash all three. The other way round, the Plex yard trees keep clear of the street and park trees in `Streets/street_trees.json` (through `plateau_rendering.tree_positions`), and the Plex manifest hashes that file: when the street trees move, rerun `plateau_plex.py` and then `plateau_replica.py`, until neither output changes (one round reaches that today). The full order is `plateau_trees.py --no-render`, `plateau_plex.py`, the props build, `plateau_replica.py`, then `plateau_textures.py --check`. Rebuild the clip from the city's file with `python3 Scripts/plateau_replica.py --clip-ruelles ruelles-vertes.geojson`; the build reads only the clip. A build takes about 1.5 minutes (the leaf litter's ground checks take a third of it), the test about 5 minutes.

## Import on the host (editor Python)

`Scripts/apply_plateau_replica.py` is additive and refuses to run twice: it stops if any actor label starts with `Plateau replica ` or if `/Game/Plateau/Replica` exists. Run it in a fresh `-NullRHI` editor with the saved map loaded and no unsaved packages:

```bash
ue_root=/home/von/.local/share/Epic/UE_5.8.3
blender_python=/path/to/python-with-bpy-5.1   # any Python that imports bpy 5.1 (Props/README.md)
cd games/nord-unreal
python3 Scripts/blender/plateau_trees.py --no-render   # the git-ignored species tree OBJs the plan and import need
python3 Scripts/plateau_plex.py                        # the git-ignored Plex OBJs (the replica layers read Glass)
"$blender_python" Scripts/blender/plateau_props.py --no-render   # the git-ignored prop OBJs
python3 Scripts/plateau_replica.py && python3 Scripts/test_plateau_replica.py
backup="$(pwd)/Saved/Recovery/plateau-replica-$(date +%Y%m%d-%H%M%S)"
"$ue_root/Engine/Binaries/Linux/UnrealEditor-Cmd" "$(pwd)/Quebec.uproject" /Game/Maps/LaFontaine \
  -NullRHI -Unattended -NoP4 -NoSound -ExecutePythonScript="$(pwd)/Scripts/apply_plateau_replica.py" \
  -NordPlateauReplicaBackup="$backup"
# Verify in a new process with the same backup path:
"$ue_root/Engine/Binaries/Linux/UnrealEditor-Cmd" "$(pwd)/Quebec.uproject" /Game/Maps/LaFontaine \
  -NullRHI -Unattended -NoP4 -NoSound -ExecutePythonScript="$(pwd)/Scripts/apply_plateau_replica.py" \
  -NordPlateauReplicaBackup="$backup" -NordPlateauReplicaVerify
```

Add `-NordPlateauReplicaDryRun` (no backup needed) to log the plan without changing anything. The log lines to look for are `PLATEAU_REPLICA_OK` and `PLATEAU_REPLICA_PLAN`.

What the apply step does:

- **Preflight.** Checks the OBJ hashes against the manifests, the OSM source hash, the base materials, the required editor APIs, the native class `/Script/Quebec.QuebecReplicaInstances` and the fresh backup path inside `Saved/Recovery`. The Quebec module must be rebuilt with `Source/Quebec/QuebecReplicaInstances.h/.cpp` before the apply step.
- **Backup.** Copies `Content/Maps/LaFontaine.umap` and its sidecars into the backup folder, with `original-actors.json` and `backup-manifest.json`.
- **Materials.** It never rebuilds an existing `M_*`; rebuilding strips the NordSky wet-street and night wrappers. Each manifest material becomes `/Game/Plateau/Replica/Materials/M_Replica_<Layer>_<key>`, a duplicate of an existing parent with its tint set. The tint is the parent's only `Constant3Vector` that is not a NordSky wrapper node; the rain pass adds a flat-normal one to `M_Asphalt`. Tints may reach 8× the texture mean, enough for buff brick `#CCB07F`.
  - Asphalt duplicates `M_Asphalt`, keeping the world-space texture and the wet wrapper. Its tint is the target colour divided by the texture mean.
  - Ground surfaces (sidewalk, curb, yard, patches) duplicate `M_ConcreteDark`, which keeps the wet wrapper.
  - Brick duplicates `M_BrickRed`.
  - Emissive prop materials (`emissive_srgb`, `emissive_strength` in `Props/manifest.json`: the 3000 K lamp lens, the park globe, the BIXI screen, the amber flasher and the light-bar lenses) duplicate the emissive-capable `M_Glow`. The duplicate's emissive becomes colour × an `EmissiveStrength` scalar parameter. That product replaces the tint on the NordSky `WindowGlow` multiply, so night glow is kept. A parent without that wrapper gets `plateau_atmosphere.night_window`.
  - Everything else duplicates `M_Trim`.
  - If a parent does not have exactly one tint node, a fresh constant material with the same colour, roughness and metallic is created instead.
  - The fourth pass's contract keys (`CONTRACT_KEYS` in the script) take their `Scripts/plateau_textures.py` sets: `Concrete_Lane_Aged` (`lane_concrete`, a textured ground duplicate of `M_Gravel` with macro variation and no shadow), `Leaf_Cedar` (`cedar`) and `Plant_Grass_Fountain` (`fountain_grass`) as masked two-sided foliage cards clamped in V, `Plant_Pit_Flowers` (`pit_flowers`, masked two-sided foliage cards in their own colours), `Iron_Wrought` (`iron_wrought`, masked two-sided cut-out cards) and `Cloth_Laundry` (`laundry`, a masked two-sided atlas in its own colours, clamped in U and V). The fifth pass adds `Leaf_Litter` (`leaf_litter`, masked two-sided fallen leaves in their own colours on metre UVs that tile both ways, with macro variation and no shadow). The plan refuses a set that drifts from the contract, the dry run lists each key's set, parent, mode, addressing and the layers that use it, and verify checks the built material and maps again.
- **Order.** Textures and the masked parents first, then the tree materials and the species meshes, each mesh checked at once (its two slots named bark and leaves from the OBJ's `usemtl` lines, their materials, Nanite with Preserve Area), and only then the other materials and the layer meshes, so a slot problem stops the step before about 1,800 meshes are imported and saved.
- **Meshes.** Each batch is imported with the `upgrade_plateau.import_mesh` options into `/Game/Plateau/Replica/<Layer>/SM_<batch>`, with Nanite on (exact fallback), 64 Lumen cards, and complex-as-simple collision on collision batches. Each prop's main part (pole, post, body...) gets the `colliders_cm` boxes from `Props/manifest.json` as simple box collision. Props land in `/Game/Plateau/Replica/Props`, where `QuebecLife.cpp` looks for the companions. That folder is in `DirectoriesToAlwaysCook` (`Config/DefaultGame.ini`), so the packaged game contains the dogs, strollers and bags.
- **Actors.** One `StaticMeshActor` per batch, labelled `Plateau replica <Layer> <batch>`, in the `Replica/<Layer>` folder, at identity. Collision batches get `BlockAll`; the others get `NoCollision`. Paint, decals and flats have shadows off.
- **Instances.** Furniture and Soul placements become one `AQuebecReplicaInstances` actor per prop part, colour variant and collision class. The class is new in `Source/Quebec/QuebecReplicaInstances.h/.cpp`; its root is a static `HierarchicalInstancedStaticMeshComponent`, like `AQuebecParkInstances`, which the `upgrade_plateau.py` guard rejects on the live map. Cull distance is 90–120 m. The variant material is picked per placement from its seed. Only the main part of a colliding placement gets `BlockAll`.
- **Trees.** Each species mesh of `Trees/manifest.json` (and each kind's young mesh) is imported once into `/Game/Plateau/Replica/Trees/SM_Tree_<kind>[_young]`, Nanite with Preserve Area, no collision, its bark and leaves slots set by name. The Plex yard trees (`Plex/yard_trees.json`) stand on them as before, the old `maple` on `norway_maple` and `elm` now on the vase elm. Every street and park tree of `Streets/street_trees.json` (4,682: the city inventory and the OSM trees it lacks) stands on its kind's mesh, one `AQuebecReplicaInstances` actor per kind, mesh and late-October colour (`Plateau replica Street trees <kind>[ young][ <fall>]`, folder `Replica/Street trees`, with the actor tag `NordStreetTree` that the dogs' sniff search in `QuebecLife.cpp` reads), NoCollision, casting shadows, culled at 400–650 m as `QuebecVegetation` was: the height scale is the row's height over the mesh's (0.55–1.65 on the mature meshes), a tree under 55% of its kind's mature height stands on the kind's young mesh (0.6–1.6 of 5 m; 1,600 of them, the inventory's new plantings), and the crown and trunk take the height scale × √((DHP / mesh trunk) / height scale), within 0.8–1.25 of it. A row's `fall` picks its leaves. A tree of its kind's own colour (yellow broadleaf, evergreen `conifer`, gold `larch`) wears the kind's leaves. The red maples and russet oaks stand on their own actors on the same meshes (7 actors, no new triangle), whose leaves slot takes `M_Replica_Trees_Tree_Leaves_Autumn_<kind>_<fall>`: a duplicate of the atlas material whose tint brings that kind's cells, weighted by cards and cover, to the fall's mean colour (4 materials). No trunk stands within 300 cm of a traffic centreline of `Data/LaFontaine/layout.json` unless it is in a pit, or at a ruelle's edge where every centreline that near is a 380 cm service lane, and none within 200 cm of any; the plan stops otherwise.
- **Street names.** Black `TextRenderActor`s on both faces of each blade, at the `text_slots` of `Props/manifest.json`, with the small generic over the large specific. If a text renders mirrored, turn it 180° about Z (see `Props/README.md`).
- **Existing actors that change.** `OSM Marking` is hidden in game, because its old centre dashes would poke through the new asphalt. Its collision and transform are unchanged, and the change is recorded in `apply-report.json`. The Streets layer re-authors the 41 dashes that no replica asphalt covers (the Panet/Visitation route roads), so those roads keep their centre line. Once the street trees stand, no old summer tree remains: the `Plateau mapped vegetation` actor (`QuebecVegetation`, the mapped trees on `tree_small_02` that `plateau_rendering.py` placed) keeps its label, class and transform but loses every instance (`clear_instances`, the count recorded in `apply-report.json`), and the seven `Plateau detail street tree <n>` actors on the Panet/Visitation route are hidden in game. A later `upgrade_plateau.py` run respawns the vegetation empty when the replica street trees exist (`plateau_rendering.planned_vegetation`).
- **Save and report.** Saves the level and writes `apply-report.json` to the backup folder. `verify` reloads in a fresh process and writes `verify-report.json`. It checks:
  - every pre-existing actor against `original-actors.json`: only the hidden flag of `OSM Marking` and the seven route trees may change, and every new actor carries the replica prefix;
  - every actor's mesh path, triangle count (within 0.1 %), Nanite, collision profile and material;
  - every instanced actor's count, variant material and collision profile, and the box collision on colliding meshes;
  - asphalt and ground duplicates still carry the texture and the `Wetness` wrapper, and emissive duplicates their `EmissiveStrength` and `WindowGlow`;
  - the street-name text count, the hidden `OSM Marking` and route trees;

## Rules the Streets layer keeps (runtime couplings)

- **Road meshes.** Road surfaces are `SM_Asphalt_Replica_*` (carriageways) and `SM_Asphalt_Replica_Alley_*` (ruelles), with no collision. `QuebecGameplay.cpp` promotes `SM_Asphalt*` to Visibility-only at BeginPlay, so cars and people still stand on the z 0 floor. The asphalt is at z 7.0, 1 cm over `OSM Road`; ruelles are at z 6.8.
- **Sidewalks.** Sidewalks, corner rings and the raised curb extensions are `SM_Concrete_Collision_Replica_*` with complex collision and the top at 18.0 cm, inside the 17–25 cm curb-parking window. Curb ramps drop to 9 cm within 1.1 m of each crossing. Where a building stands inside the sidewalk band (narrow streets such as Napoléon), the sidewalk runs from the curb to its wall instead of stopping.
- **Curb extensions and parking.** An extension's raised edge is a curb like any other, so runtime curb parking (`CurbParking` samples the curb at −235, 0 and +235 cm along the car) does not park on one, and `CanFitAt` keeps cars off its collision. Extensions closer than 5.4 m of clear curb merge, so the curb between separate ones still takes a car.
- **Curb distance.** The nearest curb of every traffic segment is at least 550 cm from its centreline; runtime parking needs 540 cm. Measured offsets are clamped up to that, so Mentana is 11.0 m from curb to curb instead of about 10 m. Residential offsets are capped at 625 cm, so plex streets stay within the spec's 12.5 m: Fabre is 12.3 m and Garnier 12.5 m.
- **Traffic band.** No collision lies within 300 cm of any traffic segment. Sidewalk pieces that would intrude, for example at driveways or at ruelles in the traffic graph, become visual-only dropped-curb aprons at z 9, and so does any part of a curb extension in the band (18 of them) or across a crossing ramp (3); a parterre slab in the band is laid in the non-colliding lawn, soil or curb key. Props whose base point or collider boxes reach into the band keep their position but lose collision; the ones that sat in a carriageway are moved to the curb first.
- **Curb faces face the street.** Curb faces, back walls and end walls are wound outward from each slab. With one-sided materials, the 18 cm curb is therefore visible from the road.
- **Visual-only geometry.** Paint, bollards (flexible, as on the real street), fences, tree pits and their low iron guards and planting cards, the ruelle paving, its gutter and weedy edge, and every Soul mesh have no collision. Parterre yards are collision slabs at 18 cm, so feet do not sink into the lawn. The trees keep no collision, as before; the curb extension round a trunk is what a car meets.

## Method and sources

- **Curb lines.** Every drive way inside the district is clipped to the boundary. Junctions are kept, and the polylines between them are simplified, because OSM splits ways at every crossing node. Every 6 m a perpendicular ray finds the parallel `footway=sidewalk` and the nearest façade. The curb offset per way side is the median sidewalk-centre offset minus half the sidewalk width: 1.9 m residential, 2.6–3.2 m arterial. The offset is capped by class, so a park promenade is not taken for roadway. Unmeasured sides use class defaults. Results: Rachel 14.0 m, Papineau 17.1, Sherbrooke 21.0, Fabre 12.3, Garnier 12.5, Mentana 11.0.
- **Junctions.** Arms are sorted by angle. Each corner gets a curb fillet: 3.6 m on residential corners and 5.2 m on arterials, shortened on short arms. The sidewalk fills to the sharp property corner. Near-straight joins use a mitre. Junction pavement is a star polygon around the node.
- **Paint** (Québec, *Marques sur la chaussée*):
  - Yellow centre line on two-way streets, double where there are 4 or more lanes; white lane lines.
  - White parking edge lines where OSM tags parking lanes and on arterials.
  - White 40 cm stop bars at the mapped `highway=stop` nodes and on signalised approaches.
  - Crosswalks: white blocks or two white lines where signals or stops control the crossing, yellow blocks on uncontrolled marked crossings. A two-line crosswalk is generated at a mapped stop with no crossing mapped near it.
  - Green conflict zones and white bike symbols on on-street cycle tracks. 1.16 m green `VC-BA-1` bollards every 4 m on the traffic side of the Rachel, Brébeuf and Cherrier tracks.
  - Dash lengths and widths are estimates, because MTQ Tome V was not retrieved.
- **Ground detail.** Sidewalks have 1.5 m saw-cut joints and a light 15 cm curb band. Manholes and storm drains are placed per block. Parterres are cut per 7.6 m lot. The building line is sampled every 60 cm along the lot, so a parterre reaches a stepped or set-back façade (up to 15 m behind the sidewalk; 2.5 m where no building is that close) and stops 3 cm short of it, at a street, another sidewalk, the park or a ruelle's paving; past a ruelle met at a slant it fills the ground to the next wall. Each lot gets lawn or a soil bed (the shared `Yard_Grass` and `Yard_Soil` keys, textured as the Plex yards are) and a walkway; plex streets get low picket fences with a gate gap, along the parterre only (never across a ruelle mouth). Lawn edges spill jittered grass over the sidewalk edge, so the seam is not a ruler line, but not where the sidewalk below drops to an apron or a ramp, where a tuft would float. Beside a ruelle mouth met at a slant the parterre fills the wedge up to the paving. Tree pits follow the city's tree inventory, not the OSM tree nodes the first pass used (see *Street and park trees* below).
- **Side passages.** Where the building line steps back at least 1.5 m for at most 9 m between two façades (the passage between two plexes that leads to the yards), a concrete walk runs from the parterre to where the passage opens out, a Plex yard, a ruelle, a street or a building, at most 30 m back. It is laid in 25 cm rows across the passage from a seed that follows a slanted passage, merged where the rows run straight, and a lead-in quad, found to 1 cm at both ends of the first row, closes the gap to the parterre's far edge (118 passages, about 5,700 m²). No outside technique was adapted.
- **Risers.** A raised sidewalk piece gets an end wall wherever the next piece is a dropped apron, a gap or the strip's end, and so do corner rings; a parterre or walk gets one along an edge over lower ground (an apron, the foot of a ramp, a sliver of bare floor); the part of a curb extension behind the curb gets one where no sidewalk lies under it; and a dropped apron's edge over bare floor gets a 9 cm one. This closes the gap where the old `OSM Path` showed between a sidewalk and the dropped apron at a ruelle mouth on Fabre. A sidewalk piece that a building corner cuts across is laid in six parts, so the part clear of the wall stays. The walls are the skirts that terrain renderers hang under tile edges to hide cracks (Ulrich's chunked level of detail, as the TU München terrain-rendering tutorial describes it, https://www.cs.cit.tum.de/fileadmin/w00cfj/cg/Research/Tutorials/Terrain.pdf).
- **Street and park trees.** Every tree the game shows comes from the Ville de Montréal's public tree inventory (CC BY 4.0, clipped into `Data/Plateau/Inventory/`, see its README): its position, species and trunk diameter (DHP). The OSM `natural=tree` nodes were imported from the same inventory (4,394 of the 4,571 lie within 50 cm of an inventory tree), so an OSM tree within 3 m of an inventory tree is that tree, and the 87 others are kept as OSM trees of unknown species. `Streets/street_trees.json` lists all 4,707, one line each: id, position, ground height, species, the Trees manifest kind (`TREE_SPECIES` and `TREE_GENERA` in `plateau_replica.py`: silver and Freeman maples `silver_maple`; other maples and plane `norway_maple`; elm, hackberry, zelkova and willow the vase kind `elm`; honey locust, Kentucky coffee tree and black locust `honey_locust`; ash, Amur cork tree, hickory and walnut `ash`; linden, oaks, buckeye, catalpa, ginkgo and beech `linden`; the small flowering and understorey trees `small`; spruces, pines, firs, cedars, hemlocks and yews `conifer`; larches `larch`; an unknown species by crown size from the trunk), its late-October colour (`fall`: yellow, red, russet, gold or evergreen, from `TREE_FALL`; the apply step and the preview tint each kind and colour group), the trunk, a height from the trunk (research-plateau.md §10.4: 10 cm about 6 m, 30 cm 13 m, 60 cm 20 m, 100 cm and over 24–28 m; the small kinds 70% of it, under 11 m), the source, the inventory's placement, whether it stands in a pit, and where it moved from. Lane life: the tags follow the Graffiti Markup Language's model of a tag as strokes of points with no width (F.A.T. Lab, 2010, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graffiti_Markup_Language), drawn as ribbons after one pass of Chaikin's corner cutting (Chaikin 1974; the UNC notes at https://www.cs.unc.edu/~dm/UNC/COMP258/LECTURES/Chaikins-Algorithm.pdf); the letters are original skeletons, not real tags. The creeper is the foliage-card patch the Plex layer uses (the CG Cookie foliage-card thread, https://cgcookie.com/community/11178-collab2021-wip-raisonhomme), and the perennials and shrubs are crossed foliage cards, the grass-object idea of Pelzer, *Rendering Countless Blades of Waving Grass* (GPU Gems, chapter 7, https://developer.nvidia.com/gpugems/gpugems/part-i-natural-effects/chapter-7-rendering-countless-blades-waving-grass). Cut-out cards are masked and two-sided (Epic, *Material Blend Modes*). The beds, planters, arches and lane closures follow the borough's *Guide d'aménagement d'une ruelle verte* (https://portail-m4s.s3.montreal.ca/pdf/guide_ruelles_vertes_2022_final.pdf) and the dataset's own description (beds, furniture, ground marking, full or partial closure). The gallery props are laid out the way Germer and Schwarz's furniture agents furnish a room (*Procedural Arrangement of Furniture for Real-Time Walkthroughs*, Computer Graphics Forum 28(8), 2009, https://diglib.eg.org/handle/10.2312/CGF.v28i8pp2068-2078): each kind seeks its parent surface (the wall, the guard, a window, the rail, a guard cap) and keeps its clearance, on the free floor Plex leaves; here one seeded pass per deck rather than agents moving over time. The washing adapts two procedural-clothesline ideas: garments spaced along the rope by each one's own width, as J. Fuchs's *Procedural Clothesline* Houdini Digital Asset for Unreal places its items (ArtStation, https://fuchs_johanna1.artstation.com/blog/oArnq/procedural-clothesline-hda-for-unreal-engine; her rope comes from a Vellum simulation, which this stdlib build replaces with a closed-form curve), and a rope set by its sag rather than simulated, the fixed-sag mode of the Maya catenary generator 80.lv describes (https://80.lv/articles/fully-procedural-catenary-curves-generator-in-maya; that tool uses a parabolic approximation, the old code's too). The curve is the catenary itself, its parameter and vertex from the height difference and length relations in Wikipedia, *Catenary*, "Determining parameters" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catenary). The folds at the hem follow Weil, *The Synthesis of Cloth Objects* (SIGGRAPH 1986: cloth hung from points falls in folds that radiate from its supports), whose drawn folds are already in the atlas (`plateau_textures.cloth_folds`). The ornamental grass takes the same crossed-card clump as the perennials (Pelzer, above; K. Quinn's God of War Ragnarök plant cards, https://kevinquinn.artstation.com/projects/QXzrW4) on the `fountain_grass` drawing. The leaf litter's patches use the `Leaf_Litter` set, a splatter of the tree atlas's leaf shapes after Adam Sharp, *Making a Fallen Leaves Material in Substance 3D Designer* (80.lv, 2022, https://80.lv/articles/making-a-fallen-leaves-material-in-substance-3d-designer), and carry world metre UVs so separate patches tile as one surface, the idea of Unreal's `WorldAlignedTexture` material function (Epic, *Texturing Material Functions*, https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/texturing-material-functions-in-unreal-engine) baked into the UVs. The washing's clearance from the crowns is a rule on the data, from no outside technique. The test's sight check walks a uniform grid along each sight line (Amanatides and Woo, *A Fast Voxel Traversal Algorithm for Ray Tracing*, Eurographics 1987, https://www.eecs.yorku.ca/~amana/research/grid.pdf) and tests triangles with Möller and Trumbore's ray-triangle intersection (*Journal of Graphics Tools* 2(1), 1997). No code was taken from any of them.
- **Data.** `Data/LaFontaine/osm.xml` (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0) and the read-only traffic graph in `Data/LaFontaine/layout.json`. Ruelles vertes: Ville de Montréal, CC BY 4.0, clipped into `Soul/ruelles_vertes_clip.json` with the source, licence and the file's SHA-256. Dimensions and palette come from `research-plateau.md` (Plateau heritage sheets, the Ville de Montréal sign inventory, Québec marking rules).

## Limits

- **Mapped stops only.** OSM maps only 26 stop nodes inside the district (21 on the replica network), not the 77 of the whole snapshot. Most Plateau junctions are all-way stops in reality, but only mapped stops get ARRÊT signs and bars.
- **Missing props.** There is no traffic-signal prop yet, so the 236 signal nodes have no poles. Parking-sign text is part of the prop mesh; it is not per pole.
- **The Panet/Visitation route is skipped.** The route within 64 m of `prepare_plateau.ROUTE` keeps its own detailed streets.
- **Avenue du Parc-La Fontaine.** It has few mapped sidewalk samples, so most of its sides use the clamped default. The inventory's 91 median trees stand on its median; where the clamped carriageway covers the median, 47 of them stand in raised lawn verges (27 extensions).
- **Trees left out** (`street_trees.json`, `host_review`): 25 trees the inventory maps on the carriageway have no seat and are not placed: twelve median trees on Rue De Champlain (Ville-Marie; the city's median there is not mapped in OSM, which draws one carriageway), nine median trees on Avenue du Parc-La Fontaine where OSM maps a single carriageway, and four sidewalk trees on Roy Est whose side has 40–160 cm of sidewalk between the clamped curb and the walls. Check each against the street; mapping the medians or narrowing the curb would bring them back.
- **Coverage residue.** On the capture streets, 4 of 135,123 curb-to-building samples miss, all within 35 cm of a building line: three on Papineau, in slivers a few cm wide where a ruelle's weedy edge or a façade corner meets the parterre at a slant, and one in a 2 cm wedge at a building corner 14.5 m back on Rachel.
- **Open steps.** 25 of about 174,000 raised edges still have no wall over lower ground (41 before the fourth pass dropped most curb extensions): parterre and lawn-edge slabs in the traffic band beside an apron, pit soil, a curb-band end over a ramp, and slivers a few cm wide by a parterre, a ruelle or an apron. The longest is a 1.7 m parterre edge over the foot of a ramp. Three lie within 25 m of a capture street.
- **Parking.** Curb extensions take 0.8% of the parking curb's length.
- **No terrain.** The ground is flat; curb faces drop to z 0.
- **Mirror fix.** When the mirror fix lands, negate y in every batch and placement. For prop instances, negate only position y and yaw, never scale (see `Props/README.md`).
- **Text only in the editor.** The street-name `TextRender`s are not visible in the top-down CPU previews.
- **Lane life.** Laundry hangs only from Plex galleries over Plex yards: 195 modules drawn to have a pole find no room (a yard under 2.6 m past the gallery, or a shed, pad, gate, tree or wall in the way), and 239 more have room but every line would pass through a crown. On the Mentana and Saint-André ruelle the lots near the fourth pass's camera spot (39,500 cm along OSM way 204859680) all stand under 15 to 18 m ashes and maples, so its line with washing is gone; 40 m further along the same lane (43,500 cm, same height and heading) lot 356252971's line with 14 garments stands 12 m ahead, 5 garments in sight over the fence. The preview camera has to move there for the laundry view check to pass. Nothing forces a line near a camera. A different seed would show a different stretch; nothing forces a line near a camera. A 0.9 cm rope is under a pixel wide past 10 m, so it is the poles and the washing that read. 226 drawn gate props find no room, most on ruelles in the traffic graph, where the lane side is inside the 300 cm band. Garments are single cards on the atlas, folded only at the hem (one row of quads): seen along the line they still read thin, and they do not move; the wind's swing is a fixed seeded offset, not animation. A garment does not sag the rope more than an empty line. The plants are crossed foliage cards and grass tufts, and the ruelle-verte beds stop at gates, sheds and gate props. Gate props, plants, planters, arches, laundry and the gallery bikes, pots, AC units, planters and dishes have no collision. AC units sit in the windows Plex draws flat on the wall (the wall is not cut), so the unit's 4 cm inside part ends in the skin, and its side panels (92 cm across) stand a few cm past the 80 cm window frame. A small cedar the Plex layer may plant is not in its records, so yard-side gate props keep clear of everywhere one can stand.
- **Leaf litter.** The litter is flat cut-out patches: the leaves do not move, blow or pile up in depth, and each patch's edge is a line through the texture, softened only by its ragged outline and the strays. A conifer's patch shows broadleaf leaves, since the set has no needles. The Plex yards keep their own ground and get no litter, and 355 street and park trees whose trunk and nearby ground are no flat surface the litter knows (a Plex yard, a pond edge, a path) get none of their own. The leaves lie on the parterres and sidewalks a uniform 0.75 cm up; on the park lawn the Park layer's older lobed leaves still lie round 90 OSM trees, under and through the patches.
