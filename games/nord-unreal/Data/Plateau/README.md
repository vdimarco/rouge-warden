# La Fontaine / Panet / Visitation — Plateau environment

`layout.json` and `Geometry/*.obj` come from `python3 Scripts/prepare_plateau.py`. The walking route follows mapped park paths and crossings, the Panet sidewalk, OSM alley 347132484 and Visitation. Coordinates retain the base map's centimetre-based east/north frame. The current detailed environment contains **721 mesh batches and 1,070,013 triangles**, with masonry façades, recessed glazing, curtains and blinds, exterior stairs and rear galleries. The planted ruelle adds individual botanical leaves, climbing plants, laundry and recycling/compost/refuse bins. Parked cars, bicycles and street furniture are original procedural geometry.

The surrounding city extends **200 m beyond the original district**: 742 context batches represent 1,722 mapped buildings outside or crossing the boundary, plus 235 continued road ways. Their complete footprints are retained; heights use tagged storeys or conservative estimates. The original district's simpler buildings remain represented by 418 local background batches. Spatial grouping supports Lumen lighting, and all 4,571 mapped trees use the shared detailed Nanite mesh at surveyed positions.

Parc La Fontaine follows its concave surveyed boundary, covering 360,035 m² inside the district. Its 284 park batches contain 166,872 triangles, two surveyed ponds with the northern pond's island hole, and **234 solid benches at mapped locations**. Shoreline coping, autumn leaves and textured paths soften the ground transitions. Two French orientation boards use original cartography derived from the same survey. The Théâtre de Verdure receives an illustrative closed timber-and-metal envelope. Two fictional `RUE BARRÉE` closures explain the road exits while the four original hidden boundary actors retain their collision.

Road/path geography guides sidewalks, ramps, crossings and alley paving; paint is flat and shadowless. The façade designs, storefront names and theatre treatment are interpretations, not measured reconstructions or records of actual businesses or city works. No bicycle lane is added where the source lacks one.

Geography derives from the checked-in OpenStreetMap snapshot: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0. Source and geographic derivations follow [the existing attribution and license](../LaFontaine/README.md). Surface textures reuse the existing [CC0 Poly Haven sources](../Art/README.md). Ambience is [original procedural synthesis](../Art/Plateau/Audio/README.md). Residents use six [CC0 clothed Quaternius characters and original animations](../Art/Plateau/Civilians/README.md), with matte cloth/skin materials. Their native actor follows authored routes on the server, yields to obstacles and replicates walking state and movement.

## Warden integration and regeneration

This environment was imported from GTA-NORD commit `eddc54c` into Warden's newer gameplay project. The exact baked Plateau map is the environment base. `Scripts/merge_plateau.py` restores the 14 original Warden resident routes and eight driveable parked cars from a temporary original-map sublevel; the sublevel is removed before saving. Warden retains its campaign, eight playable crew, period vehicles and native population logic. Original municipal comic assets remain available for recovery but their old scene layers are omitted, avoiding overlapping buildings, benches and trees.

`Data/Plateau/BaseGeometry/` preserves the incoming project's 16 base OBJ inputs. Keep Warden's `Data/LaFontaine/layout.json`, `LaFontaineMap.h` and road graph: gameplay traffic and missions use these coordinates. Plateau regeneration imports its road/background meshes from BaseGeometry. Running Warden's base generator does not reconstruct the imported Plateau map by itself.

For environment changes, back up map/assets first, run `python3 Scripts/prepare_plateau.py` followed by `python3 Scripts/plateau_perimeter.py`, and execute `Scripts/upgrade_plateau.py` only with the **already merged baked Plateau map** loaded. Its preflight rejects the old Warden map and its overlapping park-instance/building/tree layers before writing assets or saving the level. It refreshes generated Plateau layers, including the [additional park-facing fronts](PerimeterGeometry/README.md), restores the 4,571-tree surveyed layer and checks that Warden residents, cars, starts and mission markers are unchanged. Reopen and run `Scripts/verify_plateau.py` and `plateau_perimeter.verify(unreal)` to validate the saved environment plus retained Warden routes/cars. The guard and geometry/verifier CPU tests pass; a full end-to-end regeneration has **not** yet been run on this integrated revision. `Scripts/capture_plateau.py` provides walking-height environment reference images with a 20 FPS capture cap; it is not an uncapped performance check. Rebuild/cook the Linux package after changing the map: the launcher prefers the package.

Use Unreal's `-nullrhi` option for bulk asset authoring, retaining the same import, material and Nanite settings. Then close that process and reopen with the normal Vulkan renderer for fresh-load verification and rendered captures; offline authoring does not satisfy those checks. During the 2026-10-11 pass, interleaved material recompiles and thumbnail saves in the live renderer triggered a UE 5.8 RHI uniform-buffer reference-count assertion; the observed stack and recovery are recorded in [the building graphics evidence](../../Verification/GraphicsBuildingSources.md).

The landmark corrections also use `python3 Scripts/plateau_landmark_visuals.py` to prepare exact filtered visual copies and `python3 Scripts/plateau_facade_finish.py` to prepare the final corner/cornice layer after the perimeter preparation. Full `upgrade_plateau.py` explicitly refreshes the landmark source bindings after recreating background actors; the standalone narrow landmark apply instead requires the recorded current actor/component identities. Hidden original collision geometry remains in place. Run `plateau_landmark_visuals.verify(unreal)` and `plateau_facade_finish.verify(unreal)` on a fresh normal-renderer load in addition to the environment checks above.

The incoming source project's previous QA is historical. Current unified-release checks and limitations are recorded in [UnifiedPlateau.md](../../Verification/UnifiedPlateau.md), rather than inferred from those older reports.

Mapped building masses block entry, including complete shells at the district boundary. Decorative façade skins have small offsets from those collision masses; stairs, gallery decks, parked-car bodies, park benches and map-board steelwork have separate solid geometry. Garden, shoreline and water overlays are visual. The theatre is closed and not enterable, and building interiors are not implemented. Terrain is flat, many heights are estimated, and the architecture, vehicles and residents remain stylized. Local recognition, audio listening and performance on other GPUs still need review.
